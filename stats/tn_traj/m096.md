## 题目
Carbon is allowed to diffuse through a steel plate 10 mm thick.  The concentrations of carbon at the two faces are 0.85 and 0.40 kg C/m³ Fe (maintained constant).  If the diffusion pre-exponential factor and activation energy are D₀ = 5.0×10⁻⁷ m²/s and Q = 77 000 J/mol, respectively, compute the temperature at which the diffusion flux is J = 6.3×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m²·s).The unit of the answer is K.

## 过程
## Solution

Using **Fick's first law** at steady state with a linear concentration gradient:

$$J = -D\frac{dC}{dx} \quad\Rightarrow\quad J = D\frac{C_1 - C_2}{\Delta x}$$

**Step 1 — Find the required diffusivity D:**

$$D = \frac{J\,\Delta x}{C_1 - C_2} = \frac{(6.3\times10^{-10})(0.010)}{0.85 - 0.40} = \frac{6.3\times10^{-12}}{0.45} = 1.4\times10^{-11}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Step 2 — Relate D to temperature via the Arrhenius equation:**

$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT}\right)$$

Solving for T:

$$T = \frac{Q}{R\ln(D_0/D)} = \frac{77\,000}{8.314\,\ln\!\left(\dfrac{5.0\times10^{-7}}{1.4\times10^{-11}}\right)}$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate:**

$$\ln\!\left(\frac{5.0\times10^{-7}}{1.4\times10^{-11}}\right) = \ln(3.571\times10^{4}) \approx 10.483$$

$$T = \frac{77\,000}{8.314 \times 10.483} \approx 883.5\ \text{K}$$

$$\boxed{883.4}$$

## 最终答案
883.4
