## 题目
Iron titanate, $\mathrm{FeTiO}{3}$, forms in the ilmenite crystal structure that consists of an HCP arrangement of $\mathrm{O}^{2-}$ ions.
(a) What fraction of the total tetrahedral sites will be occupied?
(b) What fraction of the total octahedral sites will be occupied?

Give your answer as a tuple (fraction of the total tetrahedral sites be occupied,  fraction of the total octahedral sites be occupied).

## 过程
Looking at this problem, I need to analyze the ilmenite crystal structure of FeTiO₃.

## Reasoning

**Oxygen sublattice:** FeTiO₃ has an HCP arrangement of O²⁻ ions. For an HCP lattice with *N* anions per unit cell:
- Tetrahedral sites = 2*N*
- Octahedral sites = *N*

**Ilmenite structure:** Ilmenite (FeTiO₃) is structurally related to corundum (Al₂O₃). In corundum, the Al³⁺ cations occupy **2/3 of the octahedral sites** in the HCP oxygen lattice. Ilmenite has the same oxygen framework, but instead of one cation species, there are two ordered cations — Fe²⁺ and Ti⁴⁺ — that *each* occupy 1/3 of the octahedral sites (ordered alternately in layers), giving a total of:

$$\frac{1}{3}(\text{Fe}) + \frac{1}{3}(\text{Ti}) = \frac{2}{3}$$

of the octahedral sites occupied.

**Tetrahedral sites:** In the ilmenite structure, both Fe²⁺ and Ti⁴⁺ are in **octahedral** coordination with oxygen. No cations occupy tetrahedral sites.

## Answers

(a) Fraction of tetrahedral sites occupied = **0**

(b) Fraction of octahedral sites occupied = **2/3**

$$\boxed{\left(0,\ \dfrac{2}{3}\right)}$$

## 最终答案
\left(0,\ \dfrac{2}{3}\right)
