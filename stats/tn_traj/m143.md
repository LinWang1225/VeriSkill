## 题目
A thick steel sheet of area 100 in. ${ }^{2}$ is exposed to air near the ocean. After a one-year period it was found to experience a weight loss of 485 g due to corrosion. To what rate of corrosion, in both mpy and $\mathrm{mm} / \mathrm{yr}$, does this correspond? The density of steel is $7.9 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple (the rate of corrosion in mpy, the rate of corrosion in mm/yr).The units of each required answer are (mpy, mm/yr), respectively.

## 过程
Looking at this problem, I need to find the corrosion penetration rate from the weight loss data.

## Step-by-step solution

**Given:**
- Weight loss: $W = 485\text{ g}$ over $t = 1\text{ yr}$
- Area: $A = 100\text{ in}^2$
- Density: $\rho = 7.9\text{ g/cm}^3$

**Step 1 — Volume of metal lost:**
$$V = \frac{W}{\rho} = \frac{485}{7.9} = 61.39\text{ cm}^3$$

**Step 2 — Convert area to cm²:**
$$A = 100\times(2.54)^2 = 645.16\text{ cm}^2$$

**Step 3 — Penetration depth (corrosion rate) in cm/yr:**
$$d = \frac{V}{A} = \frac{61.39}{645.16} = 0.09516\text{ cm/yr}$$

**Step 4 — Convert to mm/yr:**
$$0.09516\times 10 = 0.9516\text{ mm/yr}$$

**Step 5 — Convert to mpy (mils per year), where $1\text{ mil} = 0.00254\text{ cm}$:**
$$\frac{0.09516}{0.00254} = 37.46\text{ mpy}$$

**Verification** with the standard formula $r = KW/(DAt)$ using $K=534$ (mpy) and $K=87.6$ (mm/yr), $W$ in mg, $t=8760$ hr:
- mpy: $534\times485000/(7.9\times100\times8760) = 37.42$ ✓
- mm/yr: $87.6\times485000/(7.9\times645.16\times8760) = 0.9516$ ✓

Both methods agree.

$$\boxed{(37.4,\ 0.95)}$$

## 最终答案
(37.4,\ 0.95)
