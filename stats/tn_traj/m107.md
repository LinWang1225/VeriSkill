## 题目
A cylindrical specimen of stainless steel having a diameter of 12.8 mm ( 0.505 in.) and a gauge length of 50.800 mm (2.000 in.) is pulled in tension. Use the load-elongation characteristics shown in the following table. Compute the modulus of resilience.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1: Cross-sectional area and stress-strain data**

With diameter \(d = 12.8\ \text{mm}\):
\[
A_0 = \frac{\pi d^2}{4} = \frac{\pi(12.8\times10^{-3})^2}{4}=1.287\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2
\]
Stress \(\sigma = F/A_0\), strain \(\epsilon = \Delta L/L_0\) with \(L_0 = 50.800\ \text{mm}\).

**Step 2: Modulus of elasticity (linear region)**

The first five data points (up to 50 800 N) are linear. A regression gives
\[
E \approx 196.6\ \text{GPa}
\]
(The secant modulus is ~196–200 GPa through point 5, then drops to ~173 GPa at point 6 — marking the proportional limit.)

**Step 3: Yield strength by the 0.002 strain-offset method**

Construct the line \(\sigma = E(\epsilon - 0.002)\) and find its intersection with the stress–strain curve. This occurs at
\[
\sigma_y \approx 744\ \text{MPa}
\]

**Step 4: Modulus of resilience**

The modulus of resilience is the elastic strain-energy density up to yielding:
\[
U_r = \frac{\sigma_y^2}{2E}
     = \frac{(744.3\times10^6)^2}{2(196.6\times10^9)}
     \approx 1.41\times10^6\ \text{J/m}^3
\]

\[
\boxed{1.41\times10^{6}}
\]

## 最终答案
1.41\times10^{6}
