## 题目
If the atomic radius of lead is 0.175 nm, calculate the volume of its unit cell in cubic meters.The unit of the answer is m^3.

## 过程
Lead has an FCC crystal structure. For FCC, the relationship between the lattice parameter $a$ and the atomic radius $R$ is:

$$a = 2R\sqrt{2}$$

**Step 1:** Convert the atomic radius to meters.
$$R = 0.175 \text{ nm} = 0.175 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 1.75 \times 10^{-10} \text{ m}$$

**Step 2:** Compute the lattice parameter.
$$a = 2(1.75 \times 10^{-10})\sqrt{2} = 4.95 \times 10^{-10} \text{ m}$$

**Step 3:** Compute the unit cell volume ($V = a^3$).
$$V = (4.95 \times 10^{-10})^3 = 1.21 \times 10^{-28} \text{ m}^3$$

$$\boxed{1.21 \times 10^{-28}}$$

## 最终答案
1.21 \times 10^{-28}
