## 题目
Zinc undergoes corrosion in an acidic solution according to the reaction:
\[
\mathrm{Zn} + 2\mathrm{H}^{+} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Zn}^{2+} + \mathrm{H}_{2}
\]

The rates of both oxidation and reduction half-reactions are controlled by activation polarization.

(a) Compute the rate of oxidation of Zn (in $\mathrm{mol/cm^{2}\cdot s}$), given the following activation polarization data:

\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|}
    \hline
    \textbf{For Zn} & \textbf{For Hydrogen} \\
    \hline
    V_{\left(\mathrm{Zn}/\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\right)} = -0.763\,\mathrm{V} & V_{\left(\mathrm{H}^{+}/\mathrm{H}_{2}\right)} = 0\,\mathrm{V} \\
    \hline
    i_{0} = 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} & i_{0} = 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} \\
    \hline
    \beta = +0.09 & \beta = -0.08 \\
    \hline
\end{array}
\]


(b) Compute the value of the corrosion potential.

Give your answer as a tuple (rate of oxidation in (a), the value of the corrosion potential in (b)).
The units of each required answer are (mol/(cm${}^{2}$s), V), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Mixed-potential theory:** At the corrosion potential, the anodic (Zn oxidation) and cathodic (H⁺ reduction) current densities are equal to $i_{corr}$.

**Activation polarization (Tafel) equations:**

$$V_{corr} = V_{Zn} + \beta_{Zn}\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{i_{corr}}{i_{0,Zn}}\right) = V_{H} + \beta_{H}\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{i_{corr}}{i_{0,H}}\right)$$

**(a)** Solving the equality gives $i_{corr} \approx 1.19\times10^{-4}\,\text{A/cm}^2$. The oxidation rate is

$$r = \frac{i_{corr}}{nF} = \frac{1.19\times10^{-4}}{2\times 96485} \approx 6.18\times10^{-10}\,\text{mol/cm}^2\!\cdot\!\text{s}$$

**(b)** Substituting $i_{corr}$ back:

$$V_{corr} = -0.763 + 0.09\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{1.19\times10^{-4}}{10^{-7}}\right) \approx -0.486\,\text{V}$$

$$\boxed{(6.18\times10^{-10},\ -0.486)}$$

## 最终答案
(6.18\times10^{-10},\ -0.486)
