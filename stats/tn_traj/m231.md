## 题目
A 4-in.-diameter sphere of liquid copper is allowed to solidify, producing a spherical shrinkage cavity in the center of the casting. Compare the volume and diameter of the shrinkage cavity in the copper casting to that obtained when a 4 in. sphere of liquid iron is allowed to solidify. Give your answer as a tuple: (diameter_copper_in, diameter_iron_in).The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Step 1: Original sphere volume**

For a 4-in. diameter sphere:
$$V = \frac{\pi}{6}d^3 = \frac{\pi}{6}(4)^3 = \frac{32\pi}{3} \approx 33.51 \text{ in}^3$$

**Step 2: Solidification shrinkage values** (standard tabulated values)

| Metal | Solidification Shrinkage |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Copper | 4.5% |
| Iron | 3.0% |

**Step 3: Cavity volumes**

The shrinkage cavity volume = (shrinkage fraction) × (sphere volume):
- $V_{\text{cav,Cu}} = 0.045 \times 33.51 \approx 1.508 \text{ in}^3$
- $V_{\text{cav,Fe}} = 0.030 \times 33.51 \approx 1.005 \text{ in}^3$

The copper cavity volume is **1.5×** the iron cavity volume (ratio = 4.5/3.0).

**Step 4: Cavity diameters**

Since each cavity is spherical, $d_{\text{cav}} = \left(\frac{6V_{\text{cav}}}{\pi}\right)^{1/3} = d_{\text{sphere}} \times (\text{shrinkage})^{1/3}$:

- $d_{\text{cav,Cu}} = 4 \times (0.045)^{1/3} \approx 1.42 \text{ in}$
- $d_{\text{cav,Fe}} = 4 \times (0.030)^{1/3} \approx 1.24 \text{ in}$

The copper cavity is larger because copper has a greater solidification shrinkage (4.5% vs. 3.0%).

$$\boxed{(1.42,\ 1.24)}$$

## 最终答案
(1.42,\ 1.24)
