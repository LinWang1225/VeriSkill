## 题目
(a) Calculate the saturation magnetization ($\mu_{0} M_{\mathrm{s}}$) for $\mathrm{Fe}_{3} \mathrm{O}_{4}$ if the lattice constant ($a_{0}$) of the larger unit cell, which consists of eight smaller unit cells, is $8.37 \AA$.The unit of the answer is T.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
Fe3O4 (magnetite) is an inverse spinel: [Fe3+]_A[Fe2+Fe3+]_B O4. Fe3+ (5 μB) on tetrahedral A sites is antiparallel to the two B-site cations (Fe2+ = 4 μB, Fe3+ = 5 μB), so the net moment per formula unit is 4+5−5 = 4 μB. The given 'larger unit cell' of a0 = 8.37 Å is the conventional cubic spinel cell, which contains Z = 8 formula units of Fe3O4 (it is explicitly described as built from eight smaller sub-cells). Per g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling, the cell moment is therefore Z × 4 μB = 32 μB (not 4 μB). The saturation magnetization is M_s = (cell moment)/(cell volume), and μ0 M_s = μ0 · (32 μB)/a0^3. Using μ0 = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A, μB = 9.274009994×10⁻²⁴ A·m² (CODATA, stated source), a0 = 8.37×10⁻¹⁰ m: μ0 M_s = 0.63599 T. Independent verification in cgs (moment in emu, volume in cm³, then 1 emu/cm³ = 1000 A/m) gives 506.1 emu/cm³ → 0.63599 T, matching the SI path. a0 has 3 significant figures, so the result is rounded to 3 sig figs: 0.636 T. This is consistent with the known room-temperature saturation magnetization of magnetite (~0.6 T).

（执行证据）
Inverse-spinel cancellation gives 4 μB per Fe3O4 formula unit; the conventional spinel cell holds Z=8 formula units, so the cell moment is 32 μB. With a0 = 8.37 Å, μ0 Ms = μ0·32μB/a0³ = 0.63599 T (SI) and 506.1 emu/cm³ → 0.63599 T (cgs independent path). Rounded to 3 sig figs (limited by a0): 0.636 T.

## 最终答案
0.636 \text{ T}
