## 题目
Consider the freezing of pure aluminum. Estimate the temperature gradient in the solid near the liquid-solid interface if the interface moves at $5 \mathrm{~mm} / \mathrm{min}$. For aluminum, the thermal conductivity $k=4.8 \mathrm{Jm} /(\mathrm{Ks})$, the latent heat of freezing is $397 \mathrm{~kJ} /$ kg , and the density is $2.7 \mathrm{mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$. Give your answer as the value of temperature_gradient.The unit of the answer is K/m.

## 过程
## Reasoning

At the moving solid–liquid interface, the latent heat released by solidification must be removed by conduction through the solid. The Stefan-type heat balance gives:

$$k\frac{dT}{dx} = \rho L V \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dT}{dx} = \frac{\rho L V}{k}$$

**Values used:**
| Quantity | Value | SI |
|---|---|---|
| $\rho$ | $2.7\ \mathrm{Mg/m^3}$ (the "mg" is a typo for Mg) | $2700\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}$ |
| $L$ | $397\ \mathrm{kJ/kg}$ | $397{,}000\ \mathrm{J/kg}$ |
| $V$ | $5\ \mathrm{mm/min}$ | $8.333\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ |
| $k$ | $4.8\ \mathrm{J/(s\,m\,K)}$ | $4.8\ \mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ |

**Calculation:**
$$\frac{dT}{dx} = \frac{(2700)(397{,}000)(8.333\times10^{-5})}{4.8} = \frac{89{,}325}{4.8} \approx 18{,}609\ \mathrm{K/m}$$

$$\boxed{18609.375}$$

## 最终答案
18609.375
