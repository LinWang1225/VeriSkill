## 题目
Calculate the heat (in calories and joules) required to raise the temperature of 1 kg of the following materials by $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ : (a) lead, (b) $\mathrm{Si}_3 \mathrm{~N}_4$

Give your answer as a tuple: (lead_heat_cal, Si3N4_heat_cal).The units of each required answer are (cal, cal), respectively.

## 过程
Using the specific heat capacities from Callister's standard table (Table 17.1):

- **Lead:** $c = 0.128\ \mathrm{J/(g\cdot °C)}$
- **Si₃N₄:** $c = 0.71\ \mathrm{J/(g\cdot °C)}$

With $Q = mc\Delta T$, $m = 1000\ \mathrm{g}$, $\Delta T = 50^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$, and $1\ \mathrm{cal} = 4.184\ \mathrm{J}$:

**Lead:**
$$Q = (1000)(0.128)(50) = 6400\ \mathrm{J} = \frac{6400}{4.184} \approx 1530\ \mathrm{cal}$$

**Si₃N₄:**
$$Q = (1000)(0.71)(50) = 35{,}500\ \mathrm{J} = \frac{35{,}500}{4.184} \approx 8485\ \mathrm{cal}$$

$$\boxed{(1530,\ 8485)}$$

## 最终答案
(1530,\ 8485)
