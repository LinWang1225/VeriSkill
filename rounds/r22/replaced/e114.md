## 题目
Nitrogen from a gaseous phase is to be diffused into pure iron at $675^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. If the surface concentration is maintained at $0.2 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{~N}$, what will be the concentration 2 mm from the surface after 25 h ? The diffusion coefficient for nitrogen in iron at $675^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is $2.8 \times 10^{-11} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$.

## 过程
## Solution

For non-steady-state diffusion into a semi-infinite solid (pure iron, $C_0 = 0$) with constant surface concentration $C_s$:

$$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = 1 - \text{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given values:**
- $C_s = 0.2$ wt% N, $C_0 = 0$
- $x = 2 \text{ mm} = 2\times10^{-3}$ m
- $D = 2.8\times10^{-11}$ m²/s
- $t = 25 \text{ h} = 90{,}000$ s

**Compute the argument $z$:**

$$z = \frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}} = \frac{2\times10^{-3}}{2\sqrt{(2.8\times10^{-11})(9\times10^{4})}} = \frac{2\times10^{-3}}{2\sqrt{2.52\times10^{-6}}} \approx 0.630$$

**Find $\text{erf}(z)$:** Interpolating in the table between $\text{erf}(0.60)=0.6039$ and $\text{erf}(0.65)=0.6420$:

$$\text{erf}(0.63) \approx 0.6039 + \frac{0.63-0.60}{0.65-0.60}(0.6420-0.6039) \approx 0.627$$

**Solve for $C_x$:**

$$C_x = 0 + (0.2 - 0)(1 - 0.627) = 0.2 \times 0.373 \approx 0.0746 \text{ wt% N}$$

$$\boxed{0.075}$$

## 最终答案
0.075
