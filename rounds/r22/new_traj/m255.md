## 题目
An aluminum casting is made using the permanent mold process. In this process, the liquid aluminum is poured into a gray cast iron mold that is heated to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. We wish to produce an aluminum casting that is 15 in . long at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Calculate the length of the cavity that must be machined into the gray cast iron mold.The unit of the answer is m.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-param-lookup-consistent-source
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
Cavity machined at 25°C has length L0. Mold heated to 350°C → cavity length L0[1+α_Fe·325]. Liquid Al solidifies conforming to cavity at 350°C, then cools to 25°C → final Al length = L0[1+α_Fe·325][1−α_Al·325]. Set equal to 15 in and solve. Using α_Al=23.6e-6 /°C and α_Fe=11.4e-6 /°C (Callister Appendix property tables, same source for both materials): denom=(1−0.00767)(1+0.003705)=0.9960066; L0=15/0.9960066=15.06014 in = 15.06014×0.0254 = 0.38253 m. Linear thermal expansion is valid here (small strains ≈0.4–0.8%, far below any phase boundary; both 25°C and 350°C are well below Al melting 660°C and gray-iron service limits, no Curie/phase transition crossed for the thermal-expansion linear law in this range).

（执行证据）
denom = (1 − 23.6e-6·325)(1 + 11.4e-6·325) = 0.9960066; L0 = 15/0.9960066 = 15.06014 in; converted L0 = 0.38253 m. Coefficients α_Al=23.6×10⁻⁶ and α_Fe=11.4×10⁻⁶ taken from Callister Appendix property tables (consistent single source).

## 最终答案
0.3825 \text{ m}
