## 题目
For an aluminum alloy, the activation energy for crystal growth is $120 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$. By what factor would the rate of crystal growth change by dropping the alloy temperature from $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to room temperature $\left(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$ ? Give your answer as the value of growth_rate_factor.

## 过程
The rate of crystal growth follows an Arrhenius relationship, $\text{rate} \propto \exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q}{RT}\right)$. Dropping from $T_1=773\ \text{K}$ to $T_2=298\ \text{K}$ with $Q=120\ \text{kJ/mol}$:

$$\text{factor}=\exp\!\left[-\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)\right]\approx 1.19\times10^{-13}$$

The growth rate decreases by roughly $10^{13}$.

$$\boxed{1.19\times10^{-13}}$$

## 最终答案
1.19\times10^{-13}
