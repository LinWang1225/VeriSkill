## 题目
An aluminum casting is made using the permanent mold process. In this process, the liquid aluminum is poured into a gray cast iron mold that is heated to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. We wish to produce an aluminum casting that is 15 in . long at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Calculate the length of the cavity that must be machined into the gray cast iron mold.The unit of the answer is m.

## 过程
The cavity must be slightly longer than 15 in. because the aluminum contracts more (higher CTE) than the gray iron mold when cooling from 350°C to 25°C, so we need extra length to compensate.

- $L_{\text{mold}} = 15.062$ in $= 0.3826$ m

$$\boxed{0.3826}$$

## 最终答案
0.3826
