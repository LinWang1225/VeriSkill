## 题目
Compare the diffusion coefficients for hydrogen and nitrogen in FCC iron at $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and explain the difference. Give your answer as a tuple: (diffusion_coefficient_H, diffusion_coefficient_N).The units of each required answer are (cm²/s, cm²/s), respectively.

## 过程
Using the standard interstitial diffusion data for FCC iron (austenite), $D = D_0\exp(-Q_d/RT)$, with $T = 1000^{\circ}\mathrm{C}=1273\ \mathrm{K}$:

- **H in FCC Fe:** $D_0 = 6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$, $Q_d = 43{,}100\ \mathrm{J/mol}$
- **N in FCC Fe:** $D_0 = 3.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$, $Q_d = 144{,}900\ \mathrm{J/mol}$

$$D_H = 6.0\times10^{-7}e^{-43100/(8.31\cdot1273)} \approx 1.02\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{m^2/s} = 1.02\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{cm^2/s}$$

$$D_N = 3.0\times10^{-7}e^{-144900/(8.31\cdot1273)} \approx 3.38\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{m^2/s} = 3.38\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{cm^2/s}$$

**Explanation of the difference:** Hydrogen diffuses roughly $10^5$ times faster than nitrogen in FCC iron. This is because hydrogen is a much smaller atom, so it fits through the interstitial channels of the FCC lattice far more easily and has a much lower activation energy for jumping between interstitial sites ($Q_d \approx 43$ kJ/mol for H versus $\approx 145$ kJ/mol for N). The smaller the interstitial solute, the lower the lattice distortion required for each jump and the higher the diffusion coefficient.

$$\boxed{(1.02\times10^{-4},\ 3.38\times10^{-9})}$$

## 最终答案
(1.02\times10^{-4},\ 3.38\times10^{-9})
