## 题目
A current density of $100,000 \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{cm}^2$ is applied to a gold wire 50 m in length. The resistance of the wire is found to be 2 ohm. Give your answer as a tuple: (diameter_cm, voltage_V).The units of each required answer are (cm, V), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Given:**
- Current density $J = 100{,}000\ \mathrm{A/cm^2}$
- Length $L = 50\ \mathrm{m} = 5000\ \mathrm{cm}$
- Resistance $R = 2\ \Omega$
- Gold resistivity $\rho = 2.44\times10^{-6}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{cm}$

**Step 1 — Cross-sectional area from resistance:**

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{A} \;\Rightarrow\; A = \frac{\rho L}{R} = \frac{(2.44\times10^{-6})(5000)}{2} = 6.1\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{cm^2}$$

**Step 2 — Diameter:**

$$d = 2\sqrt{\frac{A}{\pi}} = 2\sqrt{\frac{6.1\times10^{-3}}{\pi}} \approx 0.0881\ \mathrm{cm}$$

**Step 3 — Voltage:**

Current $I = JA = (100{,}000)(6.1\times10^{-3}) = 610\ \mathrm{A}$

$$V = IR = (610)(2) = 1220\ \mathrm{V}$$

$$\boxed{(0.0881,\ 1220)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.0881,\ 1220)
