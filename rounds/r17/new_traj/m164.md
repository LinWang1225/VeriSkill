## 题目
A rotating steel shaft is supported by a sleeve bearing. Compare the rate of wear when the sleeve is (a) made of the same steel and (b) made of copper. Give your answer as the value of relative_volume_loss_ratio.The unit of the answer is 20.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-wear-two-body-archard

## 过程
Archard adhesive wear, single surface: V = K·F·s/H. This is a two-body shaft-on-sleeve sliding pair, so total wear is shared by BOTH surfaces: V_total = K·F·s·(1/H_1 + 1/H_2). The problem's hardness basis is H_steel/H_copper = 20 (the figure given in the statement). Case (a) steel shaft + steel sleeve (same material, equal hardness H_s): V_a = K·F·s·(1/H_s + 1/H_s) = 2·K·F·s/H_s. Case (b) steel shaft + copper sleeve: V_b = K·F·s·(1/H_s + 1/H_c). The relative volume-loss ratio (copper-sleeve case over steel-sleeve case) is V_b/V_a = (1/H_s + 1/H_c)/(2/H_s) = (1 + H_s/H_c)/2 = (1+20)/2 = 10.5. The naive single-surface treatment (V_a = K·F·s/H_s, V_b = K·F·s/H_c, ratio = H_s/H_c = 20) drops the surface-sharing factor of 2 for the same-material pair; the two-body skill flags this and corrects 20 → 10.5. Code confirms: naive = 20.0, two-body = 10.5.

（执行证据）
Two-body Archard gives V_a = 2KFs/H_s (steel-steel, factor 2 from surface sharing) and V_b = KFs(1/H_s + 1/H_c) (steel-copper). With H_s/H_c = 20, the ratio V_b/V_a = (1+20)/2 = 10.5. The single-surface trap gives 20; the skill's factor-2 correction yields 10.5.

## 最终答案
\dfrac{21}{2} = 10.5
