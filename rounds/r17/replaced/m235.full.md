## 题目
A eutectoid steel has been heated to $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 1 h , quenched to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and held for 750 s , and finally quenched to room temperature. What is the hardness?The unit of the answer is HRC.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-chart-lookup-read
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Eutectoid steel austenitized at 800°C (fully γ). Quench to 350°C puts it in the bainite region (Ms≈220°C < 350°C < 550°C). Reading the published Callister isothermal-transformation diagram (Fig. 10.22) for eutectoid Fe–C at 350°C: the 50%-transformation curve passes through ≈750 s (start≈10² s, finish≈2×10³ s), so a 750 s hold converts 50% of austenite to bainite; the remaining 50% austenite transforms to martensite on the final quench to RT. Microstructure = 50% bainite(350°C) + 50% martensite. Hardness values from the same diagram (HRC, 100%-transformed): bainite 400°C→48, 300°C→55 ⇒ 350°C→51.5 by linear interpolation; martensite(0.76%C)→65. Rule of mixtures: H = 0.5×51.5 + 0.5×65 = 58.25 HRC → 58 HRC (2 s.f.). Independent Avrami check: fitting 1% at 90 s and 50% at 750 s gives n=2.00 (correct for bainite) and t(99%)≈1936 s > 750 s, confirming the hold is mid-transformation, not complete.

（执行证据）
At 350°C the Callister TTT 50%-completion curve is at ≈750 s, so the 750 s hold gives 50% bainite + 50% martensite (remaining austenite → martensite on final quench). Bainite hardness at 350°C interpolated from the diagram = 51.5 HRC, martensite = 65 HRC; rule of mixtures gives 0.5(51.5)+0.5(65)=58.25 HRC. Avrami fit (n=2.00, t₉₉≈1936 s) independently confirms 750 s is mid-transformation, not past finish.

## 最终答案
58 \text{ HRC}
