## 题目
Magnesium (Mg) has an HCP crystal structure, a c/a ratio of 1.624, and a density of $1.74 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$. Compute the atomic radius for Mg .The unit of the answer is nm.

## 过程
The correct value is **0.1716 nm**.

### Step-by-step

**1. Density relation for HCP:**

$$\rho = \frac{nA}{V N_A}, \qquad n=6,\ A=24.31\ \text{g/mol},\ N_A=6.022\times10^{23}$$

**2. HCP unit-cell volume** (with $c/a = 1.624$):

$$V = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}a^2 c = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\frac{c}{a}\right)a^3 = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}(1.624)\,a^3$$

**3. Solve for $a$:**

$$V = \frac{6(24.31)}{1.74(6.022\times10^{23})} = 1.392\times10^{-22}\ \text{cm}^3$$

$$a^3 = \frac{1.392\times10^{-22}}{3.448} \Rightarrow a = 3.432\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm}$$

**4. Atomic radius** (HCP: $a = 2R$):

$$R = \frac{a}{2} = 1.716\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm} = 0.1716\ \text{nm}$$

$$\boxed{0.1716}$$

## 最终答案
0.1716
