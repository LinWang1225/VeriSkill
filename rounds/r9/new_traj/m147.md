## 过程
The required energy is sensible heat only: Q = m c_p ΔT, with m = 5 kg and ΔT = 150°C − 20°C = 130 K (a temperature difference, so °C-to-K cancellation is exact). The specific heats are read directly from the provided Table 17.1: aluminum 900, brass (70Cu-30Zn) 375, alumina (Al₂O₃) 775, polypropylene 1925 J/(kg·K). No phase change is involved: 150°C is below the melting points of aluminum (660°C), brass (~900°C), alumina (2072°C), and polypropylene (~165°C), so constant-c_p sensible heat is valid over the whole range (model-validity check passes; no extrapolation or non-physical value). Computing Q = 5 × c_p × 130 = 650·c_p:
  aluminum: 650×900 = 585,000 J
  brass:    650×375 = 243,750 J
  alumina:  650×775 = 503,750 J
  polypropylene: 650×1925 = 1,251,250 J
