## 题目
(a) Calculate the saturation magnetization ($\mu_{0} M_{\mathrm{s}}$) for $\mathrm{Fe}_{3} \mathrm{O}_{4}$ if the lattice constant ($a_{0}$) of the larger unit cell, which consists of eight smaller unit cells, is $8.37 \AA$.The unit of the answer is T.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Structure of Fe₃O₄ (magnetite):** Inverse spinel. The conventional cubic unit cell (the "larger unit cell" = 8 small cells) contains **8 formula units** of Fe₃O₄, giving:
- **8 Fe³⁺** on tetrahedral (A) sites
- **8 Fe²⁺ + 8 Fe³⁺** on octahedral (B) sites

**Magnetic moments (spin-only):**
- Fe³⁺ (3d⁵): 5 μ_B
- Fe²⁺ (3d⁶): 4 μ_B

**Net moment:** The A-site Fe³⁺ moments (antiparallel to B sites) cancel with the B-site Fe³⁺ moments. Only the 8 Fe²⁺ contribute:

$$M_{\text{net}} = 8 \times 4\,\mu_B = 32\,\mu_B$$

**Saturation magnetization:**

$$\mu_0 M_s = \mu_0 \frac{M_{\text{net}}}{a_0^3} = \mu_0 \frac{32\,\mu_B}{a_0^3}$$

With $a_0 = 8.37\times10^{-10}$ m, $\mu_B = 9.274\times10^{-24}$ A·m², $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ T·m/A:

$$\mu_0 M_s = \frac{(4\pi\times10^{-7})(32)(9.274\times10^{-24})}{(8.37\times10^{-10})^3} \approx 0.636 \text{ T}$$

$$\boxed{0.636}$$

## 最终答案
0.636
