## 题目
Determine the minimum radius of an atom that will just fit into(a) the tetrahedral interstitial site in FCC nickel; and(b) the octahedral interstitial site in BCC lithium. Give your answer as a tuple: (tetrahedral_site_radius_FCC_Ni, octahedral_site_radius_BCC_Li).The units of each required answer are (Å, Å), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Part (a): Tetrahedral interstitial site in FCC nickel**

In FCC, atoms touch along the face diagonal: $4R = a\sqrt{2}$, so $R = a\sqrt{2}/4$.

The tetrahedral site is at position $(\tfrac{1}{4}, \tfrac{1}{4}, \tfrac{1}{4})$. Its distance to the nearest corner atom (origin) is $d = a\sqrt{3}/4$.

$$r = d - R = \frac{a(\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2})}{4} \implies \frac{r}{R} = \frac{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.225$$

For FCC nickel, $R = 0.125\text{ nm} = 1.25\text{ Å}$:
$$r = 0.225 \times 1.25 \approx 0.281\text{ Å}$$

**Part (b): Octahedral interstitial site in BCC lithium**

In BCC, atoms touch along the body diagonal: $4R = a\sqrt{3}$, so $R = a\sqrt{3}/4$.

The octahedral sites in BCC are at face-center positions like $(\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}, 0)$. The nearest atoms are the two body-center atoms at distance $a/2$.

$$r = \frac{a}{2} - R = \frac{a(2-\sqrt{3})}{4} \implies \frac{r}{R} = \frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 0.155$$

For BCC lithium, $R = 0.152\text{ nm} = 1.52\text{ Å}$:
$$r = 0.155 \times 1.52 \approx 0.236\text{ Å}$$

$$\boxed{(0.281,\ 0.236)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.281,\ 0.236)
