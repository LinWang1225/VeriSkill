## 题目
A cantilever beam of length \(L = 1.0\,\text{m}\) (\(1000\,\text{mm}\)) has a section modulus  
\(Z = 2148\,\text{mm}^{3}\).  
The beam is made from heat-treated AISI 4340 steel with an ultimate tensile strength  
\(S_{\mathrm{u}} = 1800\,\text{MPa}\).  
Its fully corrected endurance limit may be taken as  
\(S_{e}=0.56\,S_{\mathrm{u}}\).

A mean load \(F_{\text{mean}} = 1000\,\text{N}\) is applied at the free end and fluctuates symmetrically about this value.  
Determine the largest alternating load amplitude \(F_{\text{alt}}\) that can be applied without causing fatigue failure, using

\[
n_{\text{s}} = 1.5 \quad\text{(static safety factor)}, \qquad
n_{\text{d}} = 3 \quad\text{(dynamic safety factor)},
\]

and the Soderberg fatigue criterion.

Give your name as a tuple (the Soderberg fatigue criterion, the largest alternating load amplitude \(F_{\text{alt}}\)).The units of each required answer are (MPa, N), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1 — Endurance limit (Soderberg criterion intercept on the alternating-stress axis):**

$$S_e = 0.56\,S_u = 0.56 \times 1800 = 1008 \text{ MPa}$$

**Step 2 — Stresses at the fixed end of the cantilever** (max bending moment $M = FL$):

$$\sigma = \frac{FL}{Z}, \qquad \sigma_{\text{mean}} = \frac{1000\times1000}{2148} = 465.55\text{ MPa}$$

**Step 3 — Soderberg criterion with separate safety factors.**  
The design line connects $\left(\frac{S_e}{n_d},\,0\right)$ and $\left(0,\,\frac{S_y}{n_s}\right)$, giving

$$\frac{n_d\,\sigma_{\text{alt}}}{S_e} + \frac{n_s\,\sigma_{\text{mean}}}{S_y} = 1.$$

Only $S_u$ is provided, so the mean-stress intercept uses $S_y = S_u = 1800$ MPa:

$$\frac{3\,\sigma_{\text{alt}}}{1008} + \frac{1.5\times 465.55}{1800} = 1
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{3\,\sigma_{\text{alt}}}{1008} = 1 - 0.38796 = 0.61204$$

$$\sigma_{\text{alt}} = \frac{0.61204 \times 1008}{3} = 205.65\text{ MPa}$$

**Step 4 — Convert back to load amplitude:**

$$F_{\text{alt}} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{alt}}\,Z}{L} = \frac{205.65 \times 2148}{1000} = 441.73\text{ N}$$

**Check:** $\sigma_{\max} = 671.2$ MPa $< S_y/n_s = 1200$ MPa ✓, and the criterion evaluates to exactly 1.0 ✓.

The requested tuple is $(S_e,\; F_{\text{alt}}) = (1008,\; 441.73)$.

$$\boxed{(1008,\; 441.73)}$$

## 最终答案
(1008,\; 441.73)
