## 题目
The difference between the specific heats at constant pressure and constant volume is described by the expression

$$
c{p}-c{v}=\frac{\alpha{v}^{2} v{0} T}{\beta}
$$

where $\alpha{v}$ is the volume coefficient of thermal expansion, $v{0}$ is the specific volume (i.e., volume per unit mass, or the reciprocal of density), $\beta$ is the compressibility, and $T$ is the absolute temperature. Compute the values of $c{v}$ at room temperature (293 K) for aluminum and iron using the data in  table 17.1 , assuming that $\alpha{v}=3 \alpha{l}$ and given that the values of $\beta$ for Al and Fe are $1.77 \times 10^{-11}$ and $2.65 \times 10^{-12}(\mathrm{~Pa})^{-1}$, respectively. Give your answer as a tuple: (value of $c_v$ for aluminum, value of $c_v$ for iron).

## 过程
## Step-by-step Solution

The relation is:

$$c_p - c_v = \frac{\alpha_v^2 v_0 T}{\beta}, \qquad \alpha_v = 3\alpha_l, \qquad v_0 = \frac{1}{\rho}$$

**Data from Table 17.1 and given values:**

| Quantity | Aluminum | Iron |
|---|---|---|
| $c_p$ (J/kg·K) | 900 | 448 |
| $\alpha_l$ (×10⁻⁶ /°C) | 23.6 | 11.8 |
| $\beta$ (Pa⁻¹) | 1.77×10⁻¹¹ | 2.65×10⁻¹² |
| $\rho$ (kg/m³) | 2.71×10³ | 7.87×10³ |

**Aluminum:**
- $\alpha_v = 3(23.6\times10^{-6}) = 70.8\times10^{-6}$ K⁻¹
- $v_0 = 1/(2.71\times10^3) = 3.69\times10^{-4}$ m³/kg
- $c_p - c_v = \frac{(70.8\times10^{-6})^2(3.69\times10^{-4})(293)}{1.77\times10^{-11}} \approx 30.6$ J/kg·K
- $c_v = 900 - 30.6 \approx 869.4$ J/kg·K

**Iron:**
- $\alpha_v = 3(11.8\times10^{-6}) = 35.4\times10^{-6}$ K⁻¹
- $v_0 = 1/(7.87\times10^3) = 1.27\times10^{-4}$ m³/kg
- $c_p - c_v = \frac{(35.4\times10^{-6})^2(1.27\times10^{-4})(293)}{2.65\times10^{-12}} \approx 17.6$ J/kg·K
- $c_v = 448 - 17.6 \approx 430.4$ J/kg·K

$$\boxed{(869.4,\ 430.4)}$$

## 最终答案
(869.4,\ 430.4)
