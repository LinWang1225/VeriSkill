## 题目
Compute the rate of some reaction that obeys Avrami kinetics, assuming that the constants $n$ and $k$ have values of 2.0 and $5 \times 10-4$, respectively, for time expressed in seconds.The unit of the answer is $s^{-1}$.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Avrami kinetics: y = 1 - exp(-k t^n). The reaction rate is dy/dt = d/dt[1 - exp(-k t^n)] = n k t^{n-1} exp(-k t^n). Substituting n = 2.0 and k = 5e-4 (time in seconds): dy/dt = 2*(5e-4)*t^(1)*exp(-(5e-4)*t^2) = (1.0e-3) t exp(-5.0e-4 t^2). Unit check: k carries units s^{-n} = s^{-2}, so n*k*t^{n-1} has units s^{-2}·s^{1} = s^{-1}; the exponential is dimensionless, giving s^{-1} as required. Dependency audit (per g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication): the target quantity dy/dt depends on time t; the problem statement provides neither t nor a conversion fraction y (which could be inverted via t = [(-ln(1-y))/k]^{1/n} to eliminate t). I exhausted the given constraints — only n and k are supplied — and no relation exists that fixes t. Therefore t cannot be eliminated and no single numerical value is determinable; the answer is retained as the explicit function of t. SymPy verified: diff(1-exp(-k*t**n),t) at n=2,k=5/10000 simplifies to t*exp(-t**2/2000)/1000, matching 1.0e-3*t*exp(-5.0e-4*t^2). Sigfig: k = 5e-4 has 1 sig fig, so the prefactor is reported as 1e-3 (1 sig fig) consistent with the weakest input.

（执行证据）
SymPy differentiation of y=1-exp(-k t^n) yields dy/dt = n k t^{n-1} exp(-k t^n); at n=2, k=5e-4 this is t·exp(-t²/2000)/1000 = 1.0e-3·t·exp(-5.0e-4·t²) s⁻¹. The problem supplies only n and k — no time t and no conversion fraction y to invert for t — so per the missing-input constraint the rate must be left as a function of t rather than fabricating an evaluation point.

## 最终答案
\dfrac{dy}{dt} = \left(1.0\times10^{-3}\right)\,t\,\exp\!\left[-\left(5.0\times10^{-4}\right)t^{2}\right]\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}\quad(t\ \text{in seconds; no single numerical value——time }t\text{ not specified})
