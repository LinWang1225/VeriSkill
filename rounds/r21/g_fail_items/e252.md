## 题目
After finding the electrical conductivity of cobalt at $0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, we decide we would like to double that conductivity. To what temperature must we cool the metal? Give your answer as the value of required_temperature_degC.The unit of the answer is °C.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-model-validity-range
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
For a metal, ρ(T)=ρ_20[1+α(T−20)] and σ=1/ρ. Doubling σ(0°C) means halving ρ(0°C): ρ(T)=ρ(0)/2. Since ρ(0)=ρ_20[1−20α], setting ρ_20[1+α(T−20)]=ρ_20[1−20α]/2 gives 1+α(T−20)=(1−20α)/2 → T=10−1/(2α). The resistivity ρ_20 cancels; only α matters. For cobalt, α=6.04×10⁻³ °C⁻¹ at 20°C (CRC Handbook). T=10−1/(2·0.00604)=10−82.78=−72.78°C. Validity check: cobalt Curie point T_C≈1121°C and Debye Θ_D≈112°C; both 0°C and −72.8°C are well below T_C (no ferromagnetic↔paramagnetic boundary crossed, single α valid). The target is below Θ_D, so the linear law is an extrapolation, but it yields a positive, finite, physically reasonable resistivity, so per the textbook-expected model it is retained (annotated as a low-temperature extrapolation). Code verified ρ(T)/ρ(0)=0.500 and σ(T)/σ(0)=2.00. Sig figs: α has 3 → report 3.

（执行证据）
Derived T=10−1/(2α) (ρ_20 cancels). With α=6.04×10⁻³ °C⁻¹ for cobalt (CRC, 20°C ref): T=−72.78°C. Python check confirmed ρ(T)/ρ(0°C)=0.500 exactly, i.e. σ(T)=2σ(0°C). Curie-point check passed (both temps <1121°C, single α valid).

## 最终答案
-72.8
