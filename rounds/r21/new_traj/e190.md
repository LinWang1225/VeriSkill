## 题目
A cantilever beam of length \(L = 1.0\,\text{m}\) (\(1000\,\text{mm}\)) has a section modulus  
\(Z = 2148\,\text{mm}^{3}\).  
The beam is made from heat-treated AISI 4340 steel with an ultimate tensile strength  
\(S_{\mathrm{u}} = 1800\,\text{MPa}\).  
Its fully corrected endurance limit may be taken as  
\(S_{e}=0.56\,S_{\mathrm{u}}\).

A mean load \(F_{\text{mean}} = 1000\,\text{N}\) is applied at the free end and fluctuates symmetrically about this value.  
Determine the largest alternating load amplitude \(F_{\text{alt}}\) that can be applied without causing fatigue failure, using

\[
n_{\text{s}} = 1.5 \quad\text{(static safety factor)}, \qquad
n_{\text{d}} = 3 \quad\text{(dynamic safety factor)},
\]

and the Soderberg fatigue criterion.

Give your name as a tuple (the Soderberg fatigue criterion, the largest alternating load amplitude \(F_{\text{alt}}\)).The units of each required answer are (MPa, N), respectively.

## 过程
Cantilever point load at free end: max bending moment at the wall M = F·L. With L = 1000 mm and section modulus Z = 2148 mm³, the mean stress is σ_m = M_mean/Z = (1000 N·1000 mm)/2148 mm³ = 465.55 MPa. Endurance limit S_e = 0.56·S_u = 0.56·1800 = 1008 MPa. The load fluctuates symmetrically about F_mean, so σ_alt = M_alt/Z = (F_alt·1000)/2148 MPa. The problem supplies only the ultimate strength S_u = 1800 MPa as the static strength; with no yield strength given, the static term of the Soderberg line uses S_u (equivalently S_y = S_u), which is the only available static strength. Soderberg with separate safety factors (static ns on the mean-stress term, dynamic nd on the alternating term): n_d·σ_a/S_e + n_s·σ_m/S_u = 1. Substituting: 3·σ_a/1008 + 1.5·465.55/1800 = 1 → 3·σ_a/1008 = 1 − 0.38796 = 0.61204 → σ_a = 205.65 MPa. Then F_alt = σ_a·Z/L = 205.65·2148/1000 = 441.7 N. Static check: σ_max = σ_m + σ_a = 671 MPa < S_u/n_s = 1200 MPa, so fatigue (Soderberg) governs, not static yielding. The Soderberg LHS evaluates to 1.000000 (verified in code). Inputs 0.56 (2 sf) and L=1.0 (2 sf) bound the result to ~2–3 sf; reported to 3 sf.

（执行证据）
S_e = 0.56·1800 = 1008 MPa; σ_m = 10⁶/2148 = 465.55 MPa. Soderberg design equation 3·σ_a/1008 + 1.5·465.55/1800 = 1 gives σ_a = 205.65 MPa, hence F_alt = σ_a·Z/L = 205.65·2148/1000 = 441.7 N. Static check σ_max = 671 MPa < 1200 MPa confirms fatigue governs; Soderberg LHS = 1.000000 (code-verified).

## 最终答案
(\sigma_a,\,F_{\mathrm{alt}}) = (206\ \text{MPa},\ 442\ \text{N})
