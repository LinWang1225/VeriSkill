## 题目
Calculate \%IC of the interatomic bonds for the intermetallic compound $A l_6 M n$. The electronegativities for Al and Mn are both 1.5.The unit of the answer is %.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
The percent ionic character (%IC) of a bond is given by Pauling's empirical relation: %IC = (1 - exp[-0.25·(Δχ)²]) × 100, where Δχ is the electronegativity difference between the two bonded atoms. For Al₆Mn the relevant bond is Al–Mn. The problem states χ(Al) = 1.5 and χ(Mn) = 1.5, so Δχ = |1.5 − 1.5| = 0. Substituting: %IC = (1 − exp[−0.25·0²]) × 100 = (1 − exp[0]) × 100 = (1 − 1) × 100 = 0%. No external standard values were introduced (electronegativities supplied by the problem), no approximation was made, and the answer is reported in the % unit requested by the problem. Python evaluation confirms IC = 0.0.

（执行证据）
