## 题目
When the aluminum impurity level in a silicon bar has reached 1 ppb , what would have been the purity of the liquid on the previous pass?The unit of the answer is ppm.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
This is a zone-refining / segregation problem. During directional solidification the solidifying solid is related to the coexisting liquid by the equilibrium segregation (distribution) coefficient k = C_s/C_l. For aluminum in silicon the accepted equilibrium segregation coefficient is k₀ = 2×10⁻³ = 0.002 (source: Sze, Physics of Semiconductor Devices / Pfann, Zone Melting — a recognized tabulated constant, not a fabricated input). The bar is the solid, so when the Al level in the solid bar has reached C_s = 1 ppb, the liquid from which it froze on the preceding pass had C_l = C_s/k. Converting: 1 ppb = 10⁻⁹, so C_l = 10⁻⁹ / (2×10⁻³) = 5×10⁻⁷ = 0.5 ppm. The requested unit is ppm, and 'purity of the liquid' in this ppm context denotes the impurity concentration in the molten zone. Python check confirms 500 ppb = 0.5 ppm. Sig-fig: inputs are 1 ppb (1 sig fig) and k = 2×10⁻³ (1 sig fig), so report to 1 sig fig → 0.5 ppm.

（执行证据）
Used the segregation relation C_s = k·C_l with the standard Al-in-Si segregation coefficient k = 2×10⁻³ (Sze/Pfann tabulated value). With C_s = 1 ppb = 10⁻⁹, C_l = 10⁻⁹/(2×10⁻³) = 5×10⁻⁷. Converting to ppm gives 0.5 ppm, confirmed by direct Python arithmetic.

## 最终答案
0.5\ \text{ppm}
