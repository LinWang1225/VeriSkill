## 题目
Above $882^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, titanium has a BCC crystal structure, with $a=0.332 \mathrm{~nm}$. Below this temperature, titanium has a HCP structure with $a=0.2978 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4735 \mathrm{~nm}$. Determine the percent volume change when BCC titanium transforms to HCP titanium. Is this a contraction or expansion? Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_volume_change, transformation_type).The unit of the answer is (%, ).

## 过程
The volume per atom is the correct basis for comparison since the two structures have different numbers of atoms per cell.

- **BCC** (2 atoms/cell): $V_{\text{BCC}}/\text{atom} = a^3/2$
- **HCP** (6 atoms/cell): $V_{\text{HCP}}/\text{atom} = \dfrac{(3\sqrt{3}/2)\,a^2 c}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 c$

$$\frac{\Delta V}{V} = \frac{V_{\text{HCP}} - V_{\text{BCC}}}{V_{\text{BCC}}}\times 100 \approx -0.623\%$$

The negative sign indicates a **contraction** (volume decreases on cooling from BCC to HCP).

$$\boxed{(-0.623,\ \text{contraction})}$$

## 最终答案
(-0.623,\ \text{contraction})
