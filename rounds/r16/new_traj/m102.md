## 题目
A gas mixture is found to contain two diatomic $A$ and $B$ species ( $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ ), the partial pressures of both of which are 0.1013 MPa ( 1 atm ). This mixture is to be enriched in the partial pressure of the $A$ species by passing both gases through a thin sheet of some metal at an elevated temperature. The resulting enriched mixture is to have a partial pressure of 0.051 MPa ( 0.5 atm ) for gas $A$ and 0.0203 MPa ( 0.2 atm ) for gas $B$. The concentrations of $A$ and $B\left(C_{A}\right.$ and $C_{B}$, in $\left.mol / m^{3}\right)$ are functions of gas partial pressures $\left(p_{A_{2}}\right.$ and $p_{B_{2}}$, in MPa) and absolute temperature according to the following expressions:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& C_{\mathrm{A}}=1.5 \times 10^{3} \sqrt{p_{\mathrm{A}_{2}}} \exp \left(-\frac{20,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right) \\
& C_{\mathrm{B}}=2.0 \times 10^{3} \sqrt{p_{\mathrm{B}_{2}}} \exp \left(-\frac{27,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

Furthermore, the diffusion coefficients for the diffusion of these gases in the metal are functions of the absolute temperature as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& D_{\mathrm{A}}\left(\mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}\right)=5.0 \times 10^{-7} \exp \left(-\frac{13,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right) \\
& D_{\mathrm{B}}\left(\mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}\right)=3.0 \times 10^{-6} \exp \left(-\frac{21,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

In addition that the permeate must be produced at a flux of 1.0×10⁻⁵ mol m⁻² s⁻¹ (total of A₂ + B₂) under a counter‐current sweep on the permeate side, determine the temperature at which the ratio of partial pressures 0.051 MPa∶0.0203 MPa can be maintained in steady state.The unit of the answer is K.

## 激活技能

- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-model-validity-range

## 过程
Dissolution is dissociative (Sieverts law, C∝√p_diatomic), so C_A,C_B are dissolved ATOM concentrations and D_A,D_B are atom diffusion coefficients. Steady-state flux through a sheet of thickness L: J_i = D_i(C_i,feed - C_i,perm)/L (any atom→molecule factor of 2 is common to both species and cancels in the ratio). Under a counter-current sweep the permeate stream composition equals the flux composition, so the required permeate partial-pressure ratio gives J_A2/J_B2 = p_A2/p_B2 = 0.5/0.2 = 2.5. Substituting the given Arrhenius/Sieverts forms, L cancels and\nJ_A2/J_B2 = [5.0e-7·1.5e3·(√0.1013−√0.051)]/[3.0e-6·2.0e3·(√0.1013−√0.0203)]·exp[(−13000−20000+21000+27000)/(RT)]\n= K·exp(15000/(RT)), with K = 0.0657321. Setting this equal to 2.5: exp(15000/RT)=2.5/K=38.0332, so T = 15000/(R·ln(38.0332)) = 495.87 K. The (D0,Q) pairs are used exactly as given in the problem statement (same species, same expressions), so no cross-table mixing. The total-flux condition (1.0e-5 mol m⁻² s⁻¹) then only fixes L (≈1.6–3.2 mm depending on the atom/molecule convention), not T. T≈496 K is a physically reasonable elevated temperature for a metal membrane and lies within the single-phase Arrhenius regime of the given expressions.

（执行证据）
The flux ratio reduces to K·exp(15000/RT) with K=0.0657321 because the sheet thickness and any atom/molecule factor cancel. Setting the ratio equal to the permeate pressure ratio 0.5/0.2=2.5 gives exp(15000/RT)=38.0332, hence T=15000/(8.314·ln 38.0332)=495.87 K. Substituting back reproduces J_A2/J_B2=2.500 exactly, confirming T≈496 K.

## 最终答案
T \approx 496\ \text{K}
