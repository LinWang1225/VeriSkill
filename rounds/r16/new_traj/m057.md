## 题目
What is the resistivity of an Au alloy containing 1.5 weight $\% \mathrm{Cu}$? Assume that Nordheim's coefficient (C) for Cu dissolved in Au is $450 \mathrm{n} \Omega \cdot \mathrm{m}$.

Reference: The resistivity of Au is $2.35 \mu \Omega \cdot \mathrm{~cm}$ or $23.5 \mathrm{n} \Omega \cdot \mathrm{m}$. $M_{\mathrm{Au}}=197 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}, M_{\mathrm{Cu}}=63.5 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.The unit of the answer is nΩ·m.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-model-validity-range

## 过程
Nordheim's rule for a binary solid-solution alloy: ρ = ρ_Au + C·x·(1−x), where x is the ATOM fraction (not weight fraction) of the solute Cu. The weight-% must be converted to atom fraction — the basis for x in Nordheim's rule is atomic fraction, by definition of the rule.

Take 100 g of alloy: m_Cu = 1.5 g, m_Au = 98.5 g.
  n_Cu = 1.5/63.5 = 0.023622 mol
  n_Au = 98.5/197 = 0.500000 mol
  x_Cu = n_Cu/(n_Cu+n_Au) = 0.04511

Model-validity check: Au–Cu is a complete-solid-solution system and 1.5 wt% (x≈0.045) is dilute, well inside Nordheim's applicable range; the parabolic rule gives a physical positive result, so no model switch is needed.

ρ = 23.5 + 450·(0.04511)·(0.95489)
  = 23.5 + 19.38
  = 42.88 nΩ·m

Sigfig: weakest input is 1.5 wt% (2 sig fig) and 450 (ambiguous, conservatively 2), so n_min = 2 → round to 43 nΩ·m.

Code output: x_Cu = 0.0451128, contribution = 19.3849, ρ = 42.8849 nΩ·m.

（执行证据）
Converted 1.5 wt% Cu to atom fraction using M_Au=197, M_Cu=63.5, giving x_Cu = 0.04511. Applied Nordheim's rule ρ = 23.5 + 450·x·(1−x) = 23.5 + 19.38 = 42.88 nΩ·m. Rounded to 2 significant figures (limited by 1.5 wt%) gives 43 nΩ·m.

## 最终答案
43 \, \mathrm{n}\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}
