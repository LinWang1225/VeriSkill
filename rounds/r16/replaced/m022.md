## 题目
Estimate the resistivity of a copper $-0.06 \mathrm{wt} \%$ phosphorus alloy at $200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as the value of resistivity.The unit of the answer is Ω·m.

## 过程
## Solution

**Approach: Matthiessen's Rule**

For a dilute alloy, the total resistivity separates into a temperature-dependent (thermal) part and a temperature-independent (impurity) part:

$$\rho_{\text{total}}(T) = \rho_{\text{thermal}}(T) + \rho_{\text{impurity}}$$

### Step 1 — Thermal contribution (pure Cu at 200°C)

Using pure copper properties (Callister Table 12.1):
- $\rho_{\text{Cu}}(20°\text{C}) = 1.7 \times 10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$
- $\alpha = 3.9 \times 10^{-3}\ (°\text{C})^{-1}$

$$\rho_{\text{thermal}}(200°\text{C}) = 1.7\times10^{-8}\big[1 + 0.0039\times(200-20)\big] = 1.7\times10^{-8}\times 1.702 = 2.89\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

### Step 2 — Impurity contribution (phosphorus, temperature-independent)

From the resistivity-increment table for solutes in Cu, phosphorus contributes $0.134\ \mu\Omega\cdot\text{m}$ per wt%:

$$\rho_{\text{impurity}} = 0.134\times10^{-6}\ \frac{\Omega\cdot\text{m}}{\text{wt\%}} \times 0.06\ \text{wt\%} = 8.04\times10^{-9}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

### Step 3 — Total resistivity

$$\rho_{\text{total}} = 2.89\times10^{-8} + 0.80\times10^{-8} \approx 3.7\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

**Sanity check:** At 20°C this alloy has $\rho \approx 2.5\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$, i.e., ~69% IACS — consistent with phosphorus-deoxidized copper at this P level.

$$\boxed{3.7 \times 10^{-8}}$$

## 最终答案
3.7 \times 10^{-8}
