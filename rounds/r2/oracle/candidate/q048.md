## 题目
What is the centered moving average of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty from FY1939 - 1941 inclusive rounded to the nearest thousandths place?

## 激活技能

- g-officeqa-extract-compute.md

## 过程
TASK: centered moving average (3-period, centered at FY1940) of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty, FY1939-FY1941 inclusive, rounded to thousandths.

SOURCE LOCATION (g-officeqa-locate-source): The customs table 'Customs Duties and Taxes Collected, and Values of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, Estimated by Tariff Schedules' (Table 10/11, later 50) reports, per fiscal year, 'Total duties and taxes', 'Total free list', and 'Total value of dutiable and taxable imports' plus 'Free list' value. Per the skill's fiscal-year mapping, the December bulletin of calendar year Y contains complete FY Y data. Used treasury_bulletin_1940_12.txt (final FY1939 & FY1940) and treasury_bulletin_1941_12.txt (final FY1941). Units: '[In thousands of dollars)' (1940_12 L534; 1941_12 L2361).

DEFINITION: 'goods subject to duty' = dutiable imports. The table's free list is explicitly duty-free: 1940_12 L594 footnote '3/ Duty-free commodities taxed under the Revenue Act of 1932 and subsequent acts.' Therefore free-list goods are NOT subject to duty and are excluded from both numerator and denominator. Rate_y = (Total duties&taxes_y − Total free list_y) / (Total value_y − Free list value_y).

EXTRACTION (with exact source lines and excerpts):
— 1940_12 L566: '| Total duties and taxes | 312095.0 | 340006.0 | ...' → FY1939=312095, FY1940=340006.
— 1940_12 L565: '| Total free list | 10213.0 | 11178.0 | ...' → free-list duties FY1939=10213, FY1940=11178.
— 1940_12 L589: '| Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 820157 | 919709 | ...' → value FY1939=820157, FY1940=919709.
— 1940_12 L588: '| Free list 3/ | 34667 | 52724 | ...' → free-list value FY1939=34667, FY1940=52724.
— 1941_12 L2394: '| Total duties and taxes | 340006 | 385306 | ...' → FY1941=385306 (FY1940=340006 cross-checks).
— 1941_12 L2393: '| Total free list | 11175 | 28141 | ...' → free-list duties FY1941=28141.
— 1941_12 L2415: '| Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 319709 | 1011262 | ...' → FY1941 value=1011262. (The 319709 shown for FY1940 is an OCR typo; 1940_12 L589 gives 919709, used for FY1940.)
— 1941_12 L2414: '| Free list 3/ | 52724 | 116390 | ...' → free-list value FY1941=116390.

CROSS-CHECK: FY1940 'Total duties and taxes' = 340006 in both 1940_12 (L566) and 1941_12 (L2394); free-list value FY1940 = 52724 in both (1940_12 L588, 1941_12 L2414). Free-list duties FY1940 revised 11178→11175 (diff 3, negligible); used 11178 from the FY1940 final bulletin.

COMPUTATION (centered_moving_average procedure, 3-period CMA = (r_1939+r_1940+r_1941)/3, verified in Python):
FY1939: dutiable duties = 312095 − 10213 = 301882; dutiable value = 820157 − 34667 = 785490; rate = 301882/785490 = 0.384323.
FY1940: dutiable duties = 340006 − 11178 = 328828; dutiable value = 919709 − 52724 = 866985; rate = 328828/866985 = 0.379278.
FY1941: dutiable duties = 385306 − 28141 = 357165; dutiable value = 1011262 − 116390 = 894872; rate = 357165/894872 = 0.399124.
CMA = (0.384323 + 0.379278 + 0.399124) / 3 = 1.162725 / 3 = 0.387575.
Rounded to nearest thousandths: 0.388.

（执行证据）
Extracted the four required rows from treasury_bulletin_1940_12.txt (lines 565, 566, 588, 589) and treasury_bulletin_1941_12.txt (lines 2393, 2394, 2414, 2415); footnote at 1940_12 L594 confirms the free list is duty-free, justifying its exclusion from 'goods subject to duty'. ## 最终答案
0.388 (dimensionless rate; equivalently 38.757%). The 3-period centered moving average of the customs duty rate on dutiable imports, centered at FY1940 over FY1939-FY1941, is 0.388 when rounded to the nearest thousandths place.
