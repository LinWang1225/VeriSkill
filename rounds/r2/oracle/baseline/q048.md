## 题目
What is the centered moving average of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty from FY1939 - 1941 inclusive rounded to the nearest thousandths place?

## 激活技能
（未使用技能）

## 过程
TASK: centered moving average (3-year window, centered on FY1940) of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty, FY1939-FY1941 inclusive. Rate = (customs duties on dutiable goods) / (value of dutiable goods). "Goods subject to duty" = dutiable goods, so the duty-free "Free list" column (duty-free commodities taxed only under Revenue Acts) is excluded from both numerator and denominator.

SOURCE 1 — FY1939: data/treasury_bulletins/treasury_bulletin_1939_09.txt, table "Customs Duties and Taxes Estimated, and Value of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, by Tariff Schedules: Fiscal Year 1939" (In thousands of dollars).
  - Line 515 (duties Total row): "| Total | 25692 | 3069 | 2793 | 2402 | 36056 | 10214 | 271 | 312102 | +6735 | 318837 |" → Total duties & taxes = 312102; Free list = 10214.
  - Line 528 (value Total row): "| Total | 37848 | 5767 | 8654 | 12030 | 129714 | 34690 | 230 | 820298 | - | - |" → Total value of dutiable & taxable imports = 820298; Free list = 34690.
  - Dutiable duties = 312102 − 10214 = 301888; Dutiable value = 820298 − 34690 = 785608.
  - Rate FY1939 = 301888 / 785608 = 0.38427307.

SOURCE 2 — FY1940 & FY1941: data/treasury_bulletins/treasury_bulletin_1941_09.txt.
  - Tariff table "Customs Duties and Taxes Collected, and Values of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, Estimated by Tariff Schedules" (cols = Fiscal year 1940, Fiscal year 1941).
    - Line 1998: "| Total free list | 11178 | 28141 | ..." → free-list duties FY1940=11178, FY1941=28141.
    - Line 1999: "| Total dutiee and taxes | 140006 | 385306 | ..." → FY1941 total = 385306 (verified); FY1940 "140006" is an OCR/extraction error (see cross-check below; correct value 340006).
    - Line 2019: "| Free list 3/ | 52724 | 116390 | ..." → free-list value FY1940=52724, FY1941=116390.
    - Line 2020: "| Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 919709 | 1011262 | ..." → total value FY1940=919709, FY1941=1011262 (FY1941 verified by summing the 12 monthly value columns = 1011262 exactly).
  - By-countries table "Custom Duties and Taxes Collected, Estimated by Countries from which Imports Originated" (line 2034 header: "Fiscal year 1940 | Fiscal year 1941 | 1940 June | ... | 1941 June").
    - Line 2084: "| Grand Total | 340006.0 | 385306.0 | 27903.0 | 23562.0 | 22687.0 | 22441.0 | 28796.0 | 27313.0 | 31048.0 | 31987.0 | 32642.0 | 38749 | 48905.0 | 40104 | 37072.0 | nan |" → FY1940 total duties = 340006 (this is the correct figure; the tariff table's 140006 was a digit error). FY1941 = 385306.
    - Verification of FY1941 = 385306: sum of monthly Jul1940–Jun1941 = 23562+22687+22441+28796+27313+31048+31987+32642+38749+48905+40104+37072 = 385306 ✓ (the tariff table's monthly June-1941 entry 77972 was an OCR error for 37072).
  - FY1940: dutiable duties = 340006 − 11178 = 328828; dutiable value = 919709 − 52724 = 866985; rate = 328828 / 866985 = 0.37927761.
  - FY1941: dutiable duties = 385306 − 28141 = 357165; dutiable value = 1011262 − 116390 = 894872; rate = 357165 / 894872 = 0.39912412.

CENTERED MOVING AVERAGE (3-point window centered on FY1940):
  CMA = (r_1939 + r_1940 + r_1941) / 3 = (0.38427307 + 0.37927761 + 0.39912412) / 3 = 1.16267480 / 3 = 0.38755827.
  Rounded to the nearest thousandths place = 0.388 (equivalently 38.756%).

（执行证据）
Read the FY1939 tariff table in treasury_bulletin_1939_09.txt (Total duties 312102, free list 10214; Total value 820298, free list 34690) and the FY1940/41 tables in treasury_bulletin_1941_09.txt (by-countries Grand Total duties 340006/385306; tariff-table total value 919709/1011262; free list 11178/28141 duties and 52724/116390 value). Verified FY1941 duties 385306 by summing its 12 monthly columns (with June-1941=37072) and identified the tariff table's 140006/77972 as extraction errors. Dutiable-only rates 0.384273/0.379278/0.399124 give CMA = 0.387558 → 0.388.

## 最终答案
0.388 (the centered moving average of the customs duty rate on goods subject to duty over FY1939–FY1941; equivalently 38.756%).
