## 题目
A steel bar is to be quenched in agitated water. Determine the maximum diameter of the bar that will produce a minimum hardness of HRC 40 if the bar is (a) 1050 (b) 1080 (c) 4320 (d) 8640 (e) 4340. Give your answer as a tuple: (max_diameter_1050_in, max_diameter_4320_in).The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
This is a Callister hardenability problem. Minimum hardness in a quenched bar occurs at the center (slowest cooling), so the center cooling-rate curve is used.

Step 1 — For each steel, read the Jominy hardenability curve (HRC vs distance-from-quenched-end J in 1/16 in, Callister Fig. 14.6) and find J where HRC = 40:
  • 1050: HRC 50@J1 → 28@J2 ⇒ J₄₀ = 1 + (50−40)/(50−28) = 1.45
  • 4320: curve passes through HRC 40 at J = 4 (48@J1, 45@J2, 40@J4, 35@J6)

Step 2 — Convert J to bar diameter using the agitated-water center curve (Callister Fig. 14.8), anchors D(in)→J: 0.5→1.5, 1.0→3.0, 1.5→5.0, 2.0→7.0, 2.5→9.0, 3.0→11.0, 3.5→13.5, 4.0→16.0.
  • 1050: J=1.45 ⇒ D ≈ 0.48 in (between D=0.5,J=1.5 and D→0)
  • 4320: J=4.0 ⇒ D = 1 + (4−3)/(7−3) = 1.25 in

Code (piecewise-linear chart inversion) gives (0.48, 1.25).

Independent verification (second path): fit the chart with a power law J = 3.17·D^1.15 (different model, no reuse of the linear segments) and invert ⇒ 1050 D=0.505, 4320 D=1.224, consistent with the first path. Limit check: J=3 → D=0.95 (≈1.0 anchor ✓), J=7 → D=2.00 (anchor ✓).

Sig-fig: chart readings are good to ~2 figures; report 1050 → 0.5 in, 4320 → 1.25 in.

（执行证据）
Jominy HRC-40 crossings: 1050 at J≈1.45 (1/16 in), 4320 at J=4 (1/16 in). Inverting the agitated-water center cooling-rate chart (anchors D=1 in→J=3, D=2 in→J=7) gives D₁₀₅₀≈0.48 in and D₄₃₂₀=1.25 in. An independent power-law fit of the same chart reproduces 0.505 and 1.224, confirming the result.

## 最终答案
(0.5, 1.25)
