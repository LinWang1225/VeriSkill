## 题目
$\alpha$-titanium at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ has an hcp crystal structure with $a=0.2950 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4683 \mathrm{~nm}$ at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. At $900^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, it transforms to $\beta$-titanium with a bcc crystal structure with $a=0.332$. The average coefficient of thermal expansion of $\alpha$-titanium is $8.4 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$. Calculate the $\%$ volume change as $\beta$-titanium transforms to $\alpha$-titanium on cooling.
The unit of the answer is %.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
The β→α transformation occurs at 900°C, so both phases must be compared at 900°C. β-Ti is bcc with a=0.332 nm given at 900°C; its unit-cell volume is V_β=a³ (2 atoms/cell). α-Ti is hcp with a=0.2950 nm, c=0.4683 nm given at 20°C, so each linear dimension is expanded to 900°C using the average linear CTE: L(900)=L(20)(1+α·ΔT) with ΔT=900−20=880 K, α=8.4×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹. The hcp cell volume is V_α=a²c·(√3/2) (also 2 atoms/cell), so comparing unit-cell volumes equals comparing volume-per-atom. Code: f=1+8.4e-6·880=1.007392; a₉=0.2950·f, c₉=0.4683·f; V_α(900)=a₉²·c₉·√3/2=0.0360823 nm³; V_β(900)=0.332³=0.0365944 nm³. % change=(V_α−V_β)/V_β×100=−1.3993%. Independent recheck: compute V_α(20)=a₀²c₀√3/2 then apply exact volume factor (1+αΔT)³ to get V_α(900)=0.0360823 nm³, same pct=−1.3993%. Sigfig: weakest input is α=8.4e-6 (2 sig figs), so round to 2 sig figs → −1.4%.

（执行证据）
V_β(900°C)=0.332³=0.0365944 nm³ (bcc, 2 atoms/cell). α expanded from 20°C with factor (1+8.4e-6·880)=1.007392 gives V_α(900°C)=0.0360823 nm³ (hcp, 2 atoms/cell). (V_α−V_β)/V_β×100=−1.3993%, which rounds to −1.4% (2 sig figs, limited by the 2-signature CTE). The negative sign means the volume contracts on cooling from β to α.

## 最终答案
-1.4%
