## 题目
For BCC iron, compute (a) the interplanar spacing and (b) the diffraction angle for the (220) set of planes. The lattice parameter for Fe is 0.2866 nm. Assume that monochromatic radiation having a wavelength of 0.1790 nm is used, and the order of reflection is 1. Give your answer as a tuple (answer to (a), answer to (b)).The units of each required answer are (nm, °), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
Cubic interplanar spacing: d_hkl = a/√(h²+k²+l²). For (220), a=0.2866 nm: d_220 = 0.2866/√(4+4+0) = 0.2866/√8 = 0.2866/2.828427 = 0.101328 nm. Bragg's law nλ = 2d sinθ with n=1, λ=0.1790 nm: sinθ = 0.1790/(2·0.101328) = 0.883267, θ = arcsin(0.883267) = 62.039°. The diffraction angle is 2θ = 124.078°. Sigfig: inputs a=0.2866 (4 sf) and λ=0.1790 (4 sf) → report 4 sf: d=0.1013 nm, 2θ=124.1°. Independent verification: (220) is the second-order equivalent of the (110) fundamental; Fe (110) gives 2θ≈52.4° with this λ, and doubling the reciprocal-space vector must give a larger 2θ near 124°, consistent. Also Bragg residual 2d·sinθ − λ = 2(0.101328)sin(62.039°) − 0.1790 = 0 to 1e-12, and sinθ<1 confirms the reflection is physically allowed.

（执行证据）
d_220 = 0.2866/√8 = 0.101328 nm → 0.1013 nm (4 sf). sinθ = 0.1790/(2·0.101328) = 0.883267, θ = 62.039°, so diffraction angle 2θ = 124.078° → 124.1° (4 sf). Independent check: 2d·sinθ reproduces λ=0.1790 nm exactly and sinθ<1, confirming the reflection is allowed and the angle is consistent with the (220) being a higher-order reflection of the (110) family.

## 最终答案
(0.1013 nm, 124.1°)
