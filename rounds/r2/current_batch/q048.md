## 题目
What is the centered moving average of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty from FY1939 - 1941 inclusive rounded to the nearest thousandths place?

## 过程
（模型自述的取数与计算过程）
The customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty is calculated for each fiscal year as (Total duties and taxes − Free list duties − Miscellaneous) / (Total value of dutiable and taxable imports − Free list value − Miscellaneous value). This excludes duty-free but taxable items from both numerator and denominator, since "goods subject to duty" means dutiable goods only.

FY1939: (312,095 − 10,213 − 271) / (820,157 − 34,667 − 257) = 301,611 / 785,233 = 0.384104
FY1940: (340,006 − 11,178 − 273) / (919,709 − 52,724 − 275) = 328,555 / 866,710 = 0.379083
FY1941: (385,306 − 28,141 − 305) / (1,011,262 − 116,390 − 367) = 356,860 / 894,505 = 0.398947

The 3-period centered moving average (centered at FY1940): (0.384104 + 0.379083 + 0.398947) / 3 = 0.387378, rounded to 0.387.

Data sources: Treasury Bulletin December 1940 (lines 566, 568-569, 589) for FY1939-1940; Treasury Bulletin December 1941 (lines 2394, 2413-2415) for FY1941.
