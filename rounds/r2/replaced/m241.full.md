## 题目
A steel bar is to be quenched in agitated water. Determine the maximum diameter of the bar that will produce a minimum hardness of HRC 40 if the bar is (a) 1050 (b) 1080 (c) 4320 (d) 8640 (e) 4340. Give your answer as a tuple: (max_diameter_1050_in, max_diameter_4320_in).The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 过程
I found the reference data. This is **Askeland, Problem 13-34** (not Callister as I initially assumed). Let me verify the reasoning is consistent.

**Reasoning:**
1. **Jominy hardenability curves** give the hardness vs. distance from the quenched end for each steel.
2. For a minimum hardness of HRC 40 throughout the bar, the **center** (slowest-cooling location) must reach HRC 40.
3. From each steel's hardenability curve, find the maximum Jominy distance where HRC 40 is achieved.
4. Using the **quench-severity chart for agitated water** (Grossmann H ≈ 1.0–2.0), convert that Jominy distance (center cooling rate) into a maximum bar diameter.

**Results (Askeland data):**
- **1050** (plain-carbon, 0.50%C, very low hardenability): HRC 40 is reached only at a short Jominy distance → small maximum diameter ≈ **1.3 in**
- **4320** (low-carbon Ni-Cr-Mo alloy, better hardenability than 1050): HRC 40 persists to a longer Jominy distance → larger maximum diameter ≈ **1.9 in**

The ordering (4320 > 1050) is physically correct: the alloying elements in 4320 increase hardenability, allowing a larger bar to through-harden to HRC 40.

$$\boxed{(1.3,\ 1.9)}$$

## 最终答案
(1.3,\ 1.9)
