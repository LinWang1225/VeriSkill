## 题目
What is the minimum accelerating voltage required to produce $K_\alpha$ x-rays in nickel?The unit of the answer is V.

## 过程
## Reasoning

To produce Kα x-rays, a K-shell (n=1) electron must first be ejected to create a vacancy. The minimum energy the bombarding electron must carry equals the **K-shell binding energy** (the K absorption edge) of the target element.

For nickel (Z = 28):
- The K-edge binding energy is the tabulated value **E_K ≈ 8.331 keV**

Setting the electron kinetic energy equal to this ionization energy:

$$e V_{\min} = E_K \;\Rightarrow\; V_{\min} = \frac{E_K}{e} = 8331\ \text{V}$$

(Any voltage below this cannot knock out a K-shell electron, so no Kα line appears.)

$$\boxed{8331}$$

## 最终答案
8331
