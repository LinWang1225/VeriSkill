## 题目
A eutectoid steel has been heated to $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 1 h , quenched to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and held for 750 s , and finally quenched to room temperature. What is the hardness?The unit of the answer is HRC.

## 过程

Step 1 — Austenitization: 800°C > A1=727°C, in the single-phase γ (austenite) region; 1 h is ample for full homogenization of a eutectoid (0.76 wt% C) steel. So the starting state entering the quench is 100% austenite.

Step 2 — Locate the isothermal hold on the TTT diagram (Callister, Fig. 10.22, isothermal transformation diagram for a eutectoid Fe–C alloy). The hold temperature T_h = 350°C lies below the nose (~550°C) and above Ms (~220°C), i.e. in the bainite region. Reading the start and finish (completion) curves at the 350°C horizontal: t_s ≈ 10^2 s (≈100 s), t_f ≈ 10^2.8 s (≈600–700 s). The hold time t_h = 750 s ⇒ log10(t_h) = 2.875, which lies just past the completion curve (t_h ≥ t_f). Per skill g-matsci-ttt-phase-from-hold-time: t_h ≥ t_f ⇒ the transformation goes to completion isothermally at 350°C → 100% bainite; the final quench to room temperature changes nothing (no retained austenite left to convert). No 50%-curve interpolation and no HRC linear mixing is invoked.

Step 3 — Read hardness from the diagram's hardness scale at 350°C for fully transformed bainite (Callister Fig. 10.22 hardness annotation, bainite region at 350°C): H ≈ 40 HRC. This is a single-phase diagram read; no rule-of-mixtures applied (HRC is nonlinear in phase fraction per the skill).

Step 4 — Sig figs: inputs 800°C, 350°C, 750 s, 1 h are given to ~2–3 sig figs; a diagram-read hardness is only reliable to the nearest HRC. Report 40 HRC.
