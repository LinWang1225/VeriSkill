## 题目
A metal alloy is known to have electrical conductivity and electron mobility values of $1.2 \times 10^{7}(\Omega-\mathrm{m})^{-1}$ and $0.0050 \mathrm{~m}^{2} / V-\mathrm{s}$, respectively. A current of 40 A is passed through a specimen of this alloy that is 35 mm thick. What magnetic field would need to be imposed to yield a Hall voltage of $-3.5 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~V}$ ?The unit of the answer is tesla.

## 过程
## Reasoning

The Hall voltage is given by:

$$V_H = -\frac{I B}{n q d}$$

where $d$ is the thickness. Since the conductivity relates to carrier density by $\sigma = n|q|\mu_e$, and $q=-e$, we have $nq = -\sigma/\mu_e$. Substituting:

$$V_H = -\frac{I B}{(-\sigma/\mu_e)d} = \frac{I B \mu_e}{\sigma d}$$

Solving for the magnetic field:

$$B = \frac{V_H \sigma d}{I \mu_e}$$

**Given values:**
- $\sigma = 1.2\times10^{7}\ (\Omega\text{-m})^{-1}$
- $\mu_e = 0.0050\ \text{m}^2/\text{V-s}$
- $I = 40\ \text{A}$
- $d = 35\ \text{mm} = 0.035\ \text{m}$
- $V_H = -3.5\times10^{-7}\ \text{V}$

$$B = \frac{(-3.5\times10^{-7})(1.2\times10^{7})(0.035)}{(40)(0.0050)} = -0.735\ \text{T}$$

$$\boxed{-0.735}$$

## 最终答案
-0.735
