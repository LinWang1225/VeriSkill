## 题目
A structural component in the form of a wide plate is to be fabricated from a steel alloy that has a planestrain fracture toughness of $98.9 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{~m}}(90 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\text { in. }})$ and a yield strength of 860 MPa (125,000 psi). The flaw size resolution limit of the flaw detection apparatus is 3.0 mm ( 0.12 in .). If the design stress is one-half the yield strength and the value of $Y$ is 1.0, determine whether a critical flaw for this plate is subject to detection.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Design stress σ = ½σ_y = ½(860 MPa) = 430 MPa. Plane-strain fracture condition K_Ic = Yσ√(πa_c) solved for the critical flaw length: a_c = (1/π)(K_Ic/(Yσ))². Substituting K_Ic = 98.9 MPa√m, Y = 1.0, σ = 430 MPa gives a_c = (1/π)(98.9/430)² = (1/π)(0.23000)² = 0.016839 m = 16.84 mm. Rounding to the precision of the inputs (98.9 has 3 sf; 860/430 treated at 3 sf per the textbook convention; the 3.0 mm limit is used only for the comparison, not in computing a_c) gives a_c ≈ 16.8 mm. Comparison with the flaw-detection resolution limit: 16.8 mm > 3.0 mm, so the critical flaw is larger than the smallest detectable flaw and is therefore subject to detection. Code output: a_c = 0.01683859 m = 16.8386 mm.

（执行证据）
a_c = (1/π)(98.9/(1.0·430))² = 0.016839 m = 16.84 mm → 16.8 mm. Since 16.8 mm exceeds the 3.0 mm flaw-size resolution limit, a critical flaw will be detected.

## 最终答案
16.8 \text{ mm} \;(> 3.0\text{ mm, so the critical flaw is subject to detection})
