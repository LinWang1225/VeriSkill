## 题目
What is the minimum accelerating voltage required to produce $K_\alpha$ x-rays in nickel?The unit of the answer is V.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
To produce K_α x-rays an incident electron must first create a K-shell vacancy, so the minimum accelerating voltage satisfies eV_min = E_K-edge (the K-shell binding energy), not E_Kα. With no tabulated edge supplied (closed book), use Moseley's law for the K series, √(ν̃/R)=Z−σ with σ≈1. The K-edge is the series limit (n=∞→n=1), so E_K-edge = hcR_∞(Z−1)² = 13.6 eV·(Z−1)². For nickel Z=28: V_min = 13.6×27² = 13.6×729 = 9914.4 V. (Using the more precise Rydberg 13.6057 eV gives 9918.6 V.) To 3 significant figures: 9.91×10³ V.

（执行证据）
K_α production requires a K-shell vacancy, so eV_min = E_K-edge (K binding energy), which exceeds E_Kα. Moseley's law (σ=1) gives the K series limit E_K-edge = 13.6 eV·(Z−1)². For Ni (Z=28): 13.6×27² = 9914.4 V ≈ 9.91×10³ V.

## 最终答案
9.91\times10^{3}\ \text{V}
