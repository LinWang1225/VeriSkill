## 题目
The yield strength of mild steel with an average grain size of 0.05 mm is $20,000 \mathrm{psi}$. The yield stress of the same steel with a grain size of 0.007 mm is $40,000 \mathrm{psi}$. What will be the average grain size of the same steel with a yield stress of $30,000 \mathrm{psi}$? Assume the Hall-Petch equation is valid and that changes in the observed yield stress are due to changes in grain size.The unit of the answer is nm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-model-validity-range

## 过程
Hall–Petch: σ_y = σ_0 + k/√d. With (d₁,σ₁)=(0.05 mm, 20000 psi) and (d₂,σ₂)=(0.007 mm, 40000 psi): subtracting gives k=(σ₂−σ₁)/(1/√d₂−1/√d₁) and σ_0=σ₁−k/√d₁. The target σ₃=30000 psi is exactly the arithmetic mean of σ₁ and σ₂, so 1/√d₃ is the arithmetic mean of 1/√d₁ and 1/√d₂: 1/√d₃ = ½(1/√0.05 + 1/√0.007) = 8.2122 mm^(−1/2). Thus d₃ = 1/(8.2122)² = 0.014828 mm = 14827.9 nm. Model-validity check: the target point lies between the two given grain sizes (interpolation, not extrapolation), and the problem explicitly states Hall–Petch is valid, so no model switch is needed. Python confirms d₃ = 0.0148279 mm = 14827.9 nm. Rounding to the precision of the inputs (0.05 and 0.007 mm are 1 sig fig, but the stresses are given to 2 conservative sig fig and the canonical textbook precision is 3) gives d₃ ≈ 0.0148 mm = 1.48×10⁴ nm.

（执行证据）
Hall–Petch linear in 1/√d; since 30000 psi is the mean of 20000 and 40000 psi, 1/√d₃ is the mean of 1/√0.05 and 1/√0.007 = 8.2122 mm^(-1/2). Inverting gives d₃ = 0.014828 mm, which Python evaluates to 14827.9 nm ≈ 1.48×10⁴ nm.

## 最终答案
0.0148\ \text{mm} \approx 1.48\times10^{4}\ \text{nm}
