## 题目
Tin bronze has a composition of $89 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cu}$ and $11 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$ and consists of two phases at room temperature: an $\alpha$ phase, which is copper containing a very small amount of tin in solid solution, and an $\varepsilon$ phase, which consists of approximately $37 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$. Compute the room-temperature conductivity of this alloy given the following data:

| Phase | Electrical Resistivity $(\Omega \cdot \mathrm{m})$ | Density $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| $\alpha$ | $1.88 \times 10^{-8}$ | 8.94 |
| $\varepsilon$ | $5.32 \times 10^{-7}$ | 8.25 |The unit of the answer is (\Omega m)^-1.

## 激活技能
（未使用技能）

## 过程
1) Lever rule on Sn (α≈0 wt% Sn, ε=37 wt% Sn, alloy=11 wt% Sn): W_ε=0.11/0.37=0.2973, W_α=0.7027. 2) Convert to volume fractions with the given densities: V_α=(W_α/8.94)/(W_α/8.94+W_ε/8.25)=0.6857, V_ε=0.3143. 3) Conductivities σ_α=1/1.88e-8=5.319e7, σ_ε=1/5.32e-7=1.880e6 (Ω·m)^-1. 4) Volume-fraction rule of mixtures (standard two-phase alloy model): σ=V_α σ_α+V_ε σ_ε=0.6857·5.319e7+0.3143·1.880e6=3.706e7 (Ω·m)^-1.

（执行证据）
Lever rule gives W_ε=0.2973, W_α=0.7027. Volume fractions from densities: V_α=0.6857, V_ε=0.3143. Rule of mixtures σ=V_α/ρ_α+V_ε/ρ_ε = 3.706×10⁷ (Ω·m)⁻¹, rounded to 3.71×10⁷ (Ω·m)⁻¹.

## 最终答案
3.71\times 10^{7}\ (\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m})^{-1}
