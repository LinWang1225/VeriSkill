## 题目
A thick steel sheet of area 100 in. ${ }^{2}$ is exposed to air near the ocean. After a one-year period it was found to experience a weight loss of 485 g due to corrosion. To what rate of corrosion, in both mpy and $\mathrm{mm} / \mathrm{yr}$, does this correspond? The density of steel is $7.9 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple (the rate of corrosion in mpy, the rate of corrosion in mm/yr).The units of each required answer are (mpy, mm/yr), respectively.

（未使用技能）

## 过程
Corrosion rate = penetration depth = (mass loss)/(density × area). Volume lost V = 485/7.9 = 61.39 cm³. Area A = 100 in² = 100×2.54² = 645.16 cm². Depth d = 61.39/645.16 = 0.09516 cm. In mm/yr: 0.09516×10 = 0.9516. In mpy: (0.09516/2.54)×1000 = 37.46. Code confirmed: mpy = 37.464, mm/yr = 0.95158.

（执行证据）
V = 485/7.9 = 61.39 cm³; A = 645.16 cm²; depth = 0.09516 cm. Converting gives 37.46 mpy and 0.9516 mm/yr, verified by direct Python computation.

## 最终答案
(37.5\ \text{mpy},\ 0.952\ \text{mm/yr})
