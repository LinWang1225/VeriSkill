## 题目
What is the relative difference in percentage points of saving note redemption rate out of the average amount outstanding for the 1980 and 1981 calendar years, rounded to the nearest hundredth places?

## 过程
（模型自述的取数与计算过程）
The question asks for the relative difference in percentage points of the saving note redemption rate out of the average amount outstanding for the 1980 and 1981 calendar years.

**Data from source documents:**

From the March 1982 Treasury Bulletin (Table SN-1, lines 3837-3838):
- Calendar year 1980: Redemptions Total = $71 million, Amount outstanding (end of year) = $300 million
- Calendar year 1981: Redemptions Total = $51 million, Amount outstanding (end of year) = $269 million

From the September 1980 Treasury Bulletin (line 5262) and March 1982 bulletin:
- Amount outstanding at end of Dec 1979 = $352 million (used as beginning balance for 1980)

**Calculation:**

For each year, the average amount outstanding is computed as (beginning balance + ending balance) / 2, and the redemption rate is redemptions divided by the average amount outstanding.

1980:
- Average outstanding = (352 + 300) / 2 = $326 million
- Redemption rate = 71 / 326 = 21.78%

1981:
- Average outstanding = (300 + 269) / 2 = $284.5 million
- Redemption rate = 51 / 284.5 = 17.93%

Relative difference = 21.78% - 17.93% = 3.85 percentage points (rounded to nearest hundredth).
