## 题目
What is the centered moving average of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty from FY1939 - 1941 inclusive rounded to the nearest thousandths place?

## 激活技能
（未使用技能）

## 过程
Task: centered moving average (3-yr, centered at FY1940) of the customs duty rate on goods subject to duty for FY1939-1941 inclusive. Rate = (customs duties & taxes on DUTIABLE goods) / (value of DUTIABLE goods); free-list goods are duty-free (not 'subject to duty'), so they are excluded from both numerator and denominator. Source table in each Sept bulletin: 'Customs Duties and Taxes Estimated, and Value of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, by Tariff Schedules' (in thousands of dollars).

FY1939 — file data/treasury_bulletins/treasury_bulletin_1939_09.txt, table titled 'Customs Duties and Taxes Estimated, and Value of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, by Tariff Schedules: Fiscal Year 1939'. Total rows (lines ~516/529): '| Total | 25692 | 3069 | 2793 | 2402 | 36056 | 10214 | 271 | 312102 | +6735 | 318837 |' (duties: Free list=10214, Total=312102) and '| Total | 37848 | 5767 | 8654 | 12030 | 129714 | 34690 | 230 | 820298 | - | - |' (value: Free list=34690, Total=820298). Dutiable duties = 312102-10214 = 301888; dutiable value = 820298-34690 = 785608; r39 = 301888/785608 = 0.384273 (38.4273%).

FY1940 & FY1941 — file data/treasury_bulletins/treasury_bulletin_1941_09.txt, table 'Customs Duties and Taxes Collected, and Values of Dutiable and Taxable Imports, Estimated by Tariff Schedules' with explicit 'Fiscal year > 1940' and 'Fiscal year > 1941' columns. Rows (lines 1998-2020): '| Total free list | 11178 | 28141 | ...' (free-list duties: FY1940=11178, FY1941=28141); '| Total dutiee and taxes | 140006 | 385306 | ...' (FY1941 total duties=385306; the FY1940 cell 140006 is a garbled/extraction-error total — summing the 16 individual tariff-schedule duty rows for FY1940 gives 16527+9921+27302+1842+57723+22634+53063+37122+9103+10431+42690+2616+2461+1720+33401+273+11178 = 340007, independently confirmed by summing the 12 monthly 'Total' duty cells in treasury_bulletin_1940_09.txt = 340005). '| Free list 3/ | 52724 | 116390 | ...' (free-list value: FY1940=52724, FY1941=116390); '| Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 919709 | 1011262 | ...' (total value: FY1940=919709, FY1941=1011262). FY1940: dutiable duties = 340007-11178 = 328829; dutiable value = 919709-52724 = 866985; r40 = 328829/866985 = 0.379279 (37.9279%). FY1941: dutiable duties = 385306-28141 = 357165; dutiable value = 1011262-116390 = 894872; r41 = 357165/894872 = 0.399124 (39.9124%).

Centered 3-yr moving average (centered at FY1940) = (r39+r40+r41)/3 = (0.384273+0.379279+0.399124)/3 = 1.162676/3 = 0.387559 (= 38.755865%). Rounded to the nearest thousandths place: as a decimal rate 0.388; as a percent 38.756%.

（执行证据）
Ran Python on figures extracted directly from the Sept bulletins: FY1939 rate=301888/785608=0.384273, FY1940 rate=328829/866985=0.379279, FY1941 rate=357165/894872=0.399124; centered moving average=(0.384273+0.379279+0.399124)/3=0.387559, which rounds to 0.388 (38.756%). All input figures were verified against the source-text Total/Free-list rows quoted above, and the FY1940 duty total (340007) was cross-checked by independently summing the 12 monthly cells in treasury_bulletin_1940_09.txt (340005).

## 最终答案
0.388 (the centered moving average customs duty rate as a decimal fraction; equivalently 38.756%).
