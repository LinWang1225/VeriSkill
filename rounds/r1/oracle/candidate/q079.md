## 题目
What is the geometric mean across each of the 4 U.S. reserve asset values at the end of calendar month July across 2010-2013 inclusive rounded to the nearest hundredths place (geometric mean across 16 total values returned as a single value)?

## 激活技能

## 过程
Task: geometric mean of the 4 U.S. reserve asset component values at end of July for 2010-2013 (4 years × 4 assets = 16 values), rounded to hundredths.

SOURCE LOCATION (g-officeqa-locate-source): Table IFS-1 'U.S. Reserve Assets' reports end-of-calendar-month reserve assets. Per the skill's M-2 publication-lag rule for the post-1945 era, a bulletin published in month M carries data through M-2; the September (09) quarterly bulletin therefore carries end-of-July values. So July 2010→treasury_bulletin_2010_09.txt, July 2011→2011_09, July 2012→2012_09, July 2013→2013_09. Verified each file's IFS-1 table contains a 'July' row.

TABLE STRUCTURE (g-officeqa-extract-verify step 2): IFS-1 header (e.g. 2010_09 line 2044): '| End of calendar year or month | Total reserve assets 1 (1) | Gold stock 2 (2) | Special drawing rights 1,3 (3) | Foreign currencies 4 (4) | Reserve position in International Monetary Fund 1,5 (5) |'. Unit line (2042): 'In millions of dollars.' The '4 U.S. reserve asset values' = the 4 component columns (Gold stock, SDR, Foreign currencies, Reserve position in IMF); Total is their sum, not a separate asset. 4 components × 4 years = 16 values, matching the question.

EXTRACTION (with original-line evidence):
• July 2010 — treasury_bulletin_2010_09.txt line 2062: '| July | 129127 | 11041 | 56013 | 49743 | 12330 |' → Gold=11041, SDR=56013, FC=49743, RP=12330.
• July 2011 — treasury_bulletin_2011_09.txt line 1990: '| July | 145882 | 11041 | 57182 | 54635 | 23024 |' → 11041, 57182, 54635, 23024.
• July 2012 — treasury_bulletin_2012_09.txt line 2015: '| July | 149384 | 11041 | 54011 | 50293 | 34039 |' → 11041, 54011, 50293, 34039.
• July 2013 — treasury_bulletin_2013_09.txt line 1981: '| July | 147427 | 11041 | 54214 | 47688 | 34483 |' → 11041, 54214, 47688, 34483.

CROSS-VERIFY (step 5): Back-figure check — 2011_09 line 1978 '| 2010 - July | 129127 | 11041 | 56013 | 49743 | 12330 |' matches 2010_09 exactly; 2013_09 line 1969 '| 2012 - July | 149384 | 11041 | 54011 | 50293 | 34039 |' matches 2012_09 exactly. (Note: the 2012_09 bulletin revises 2011-July reserve position to 26845 under a new footnote-6 definition including New Arrangements to Borrow; I use the contemporaneous September-bulletin vintage for every year, which is internally consistent and uniform across 2010-2013.)

SUBTOTAL/SUM CHECK (step 3): components vs Total — 2010: 11041+56013+49743+12330=129127=Total ✓; 2011: 11041+57182+54635+23024=145882=Total ✓; 2012: 11041+54011+50293+34039=149384=Total ✓; 2013: 11041+54214+47688+34483=147426 vs Total 147427 (off 1, consistent with footnote 'Detail may not add to totals due to rounding') ✓.

COMPUTATION (step 6, Python statistics.geometric_mean + log-method cross-check): 16 values = [11041,56013,49743,12330, 11041,57182,54635,23024, 11041,54011,50293,34039, 11041,54214,47688,34483]. geometric_mean = exp(mean(ln(values))) = 29347.00570935089 (both methods agree).

ROUNDING (step 7): nearest hundredths → 29347.01. Unit: millions of dollars (from table unit line).

（执行证据）
Grep located 'TABLE IFS-1.—U.S. Reserve Assets' in each September bulletin (2010_09 line 2040, 2011_09 line 1968, 2012_09 line 1993, 2013_09 line 1959). Read of the July rows extracted component values: 2010 line 2062 '129127 | 11041 | 56013 | 49743 | 12330'; 2011 line 1990 '145882 | 11041 | 57182 | 54635 | 23024'; 2012 line 2015 '149384 | 11041 | 54011 | 50293 | 34039'; 2013 line 1981 '147427 | 11041 | 54214 | 47688 | 34483'. Python statistics.geometric_mean of the 16 values returned 29347.00570935089, which rounds to 29347.01.

## 最终答案
29347.01 million dollars (the geometric mean of the 16 end-of-July U.S. reserve asset component values — Gold stock, Special drawing rights, Foreign currencies, and Reserve position in the IMF — across July 2010, 2011, 2012, and 2013, from Table IFS-1 of the September Treasury Bulletin of each year, rounded to the nearest hundredth).
