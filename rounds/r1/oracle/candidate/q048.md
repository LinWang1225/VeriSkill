## 题目
What is the centered moving average of the customs duty rate imposed on goods subject to duty from FY1939 - 1941 inclusive rounded to the nearest thousandths place?

## 激活技能
- g-officeqa-locate-source.md
- g-officeqa-extract-verify.md

## 过程
Question: centered moving average (3-yr window, one centered value at FY1940) of the customs duty rate = customs duties / value of imports subject to duty, for FY1939-FY1941 inclusive; round to thousandths.

SOURCE LOCATION (skill g-officeqa-locate-source): FY ends Sept 30, so use the September bulletin of each fiscal year. FY1939 -> treasury_bulletin_1939_09.txt (Table 10, 'Customs Duties and Taxes Estimated, and Value of Dutiable and Taxable Imports ... Fiscal Year 1939'). FY1940 and FY1941 -> treasury_bulletin_1941_09.txt (Table 44 same title + country table 'Computed Duties Collected, by Countries'), which carries fiscal-year columns for 1940 and 1941.

EXTRACTION (skill g-officeqa-extract-verify):
- Units: both tables state '(In thousands of dollars)' (1939_09 line 470; 1941_09 line 1966). Ratio is dimensionless.
- FY1939 duties: 1939_09 line 515 (Total row, 'Total' col 8): '| Total | 25692 | 3069 | 2793 | 2402 | 36056 | 10214 | 271 | 312102 | +6735 | 318837 |' => 312102.
- FY1939 value: 1939_09 line 528 (Total row, 'Total' col 8): '| Total | 37848 | 5767 | 8654 | 12030 | 129714 | 34690 | 230 | 820298 | - | - |' => 820298.
- FY1940 & FY1941 duties: 1941_09 line 2084 (country table Grand Total, fiscal-year cols): '| Grand Total | 340006.0 | 385306.0 | 27903.0 | ...' => FY1940=340006, FY1941=385306. (Note: Table 44 line 1999 shows 140006 for FY1940 and 77972 for Jun-1941 — both typos; the country table is internally consistent: FY1941 385306 = sum of its 12 monthly cols, and FY1940 340006 matches the 1940_09 country table line 626 '| Grand Total | 312095.0 | 340006.0 | ...' and the 1940_09 monthly-duty sum 340005.)
- FY1940 & FY1941 value: 1941_09 line 2020 (Table 44, 'Total value of dutiable and taxable imports', fiscal-year cols): '| Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 919709 | 1011262 | 77011 | ...' => FY1940=919709, FY1941=1011262. Verified: FY1941 value 1011262 = sum of its 12 monthly value columns exactly.

COMPUTATION (Python):
- r1939 = 312102 / 820298 = 0.380474
- r1940 = 340006 / 919709 = 0.369689
- r1941 = 385306 / 1011262 = 0.381015
- Centered 3-yr moving average (centered at FY1940) = (0.380474 + 0.369689 + 0.381015) / 3 = 0.377059
- Rounded to nearest thousandths place = 0.377.
- Robustness check: using alternate vintages (FY1939 duties 312095 from 1940_09 country table; FY1940 value 919509 from 1940_09 monthly sum) gives CMA = 0.377083 -> 0.377, identical to 3 dp. Result is stable across rounding/revisions.
- Per skill rule, rate kept as a decimal fraction (question does not say 'report as a percent value'); as a percent it would be 37.706.

（执行证据）
Read Table 10 in treasury_bulletin_1939_09.txt (lines 470/515/528): FY1939 duties=312102, value=820298 (thousands of dollars). Read 1941_09 country table line 2084 'Grand Total | 340006.0 | 385306.0' and Table 44 line 2020 'Total value of dutiable and taxable imports | 919709 | 1011262': FY1940 duties=340006/value=919709, FY1941 duties=385306/value=1011262. ## 最终答案
