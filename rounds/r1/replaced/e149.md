## 题目
Two previously undeformed cylindrical specimens of an alloy are to be strain hardened by reducing their cross-sectional areas (while maintaining their circular cross sections). For one specimen, the initial and deformed radii are 15 and 12 mm , respectively. The second specimen, with an initial radius of 11 mm , must have the same deformed hardness as the first specimen; compute the second specimen's radius after deformation.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 过程
Verified numerically: $r_d = 8.8$ mm.

$$\boxed{8.8}$$

## 最终答案
8.8
