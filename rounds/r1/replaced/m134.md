## 题目
Tin bronze has a composition of $89 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cu}$ and $11 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$ and consists of two phases at room temperature: an $\alpha$ phase, which is copper containing a very small amount of tin in solid solution, and an $\varepsilon$ phase, which consists of approximately $37 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$. Compute the room-temperature conductivity of this alloy given the following data:

| Phase | Electrical Resistivity $(\Omega \cdot \mathrm{m})$ | Density $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| $\alpha$ | $1.88 \times 10^{-8}$ | 8.94 |
| $\varepsilon$ | $5.32 \times 10^{-7}$ | 8.25 |The unit of the answer is (\Omega m)^-1.

## 过程
## Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Mass fractions via the lever rule**

With $C_0 = 11$ wt% Sn, $C_\alpha = 0$ wt% Sn, $C_\varepsilon = 37$ wt% Sn:

$$W_\alpha = \frac{C_\varepsilon - C_0}{C_\varepsilon - C_\alpha} = \frac{37-11}{37-0} = \frac{26}{37} = 0.7027$$

$$W_\varepsilon = \frac{C_0 - C_\alpha}{C_\varepsilon - C_\alpha} = \frac{11}{37} = 0.2973$$

**Step 2: Convert mass fractions to volume fractions**

$$V_\alpha = \frac{W_\alpha/\rho_\alpha}{W_\alpha/\rho_\alpha + W_\varepsilon/\rho_\varepsilon} = \frac{0.7027/8.94}{0.7027/8.94 + 0.2973/8.25} = 0.6857$$

$$V_\varepsilon = 1 - V_\alpha = 0.3143$$

**Step 3: Phase conductivities**

$$\sigma_\alpha = \frac{1}{1.88\times10^{-8}} = 5.319\times10^{7}\;(\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1}$$

$$\sigma_\varepsilon = \frac{1}{5.32\times10^{-7}} = 1.880\times10^{6}\;(\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1}$$

**Step 4: Rule of mixtures (volume-fraction weighted)**

$$\sigma_{\text{alloy}} = V_\alpha\sigma_\alpha + V_\varepsilon\sigma_\varepsilon$$

$$= (0.6857)(5.319\times10^{7}) + (0.3143)(1.880\times10^{6})$$

$$= 3.647\times10^{7} + 5.91\times10^{5} = 3.71\times10^{7}\;(\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1}$$

$$\boxed{3.71\times10^{7}}$$

## 最终答案
3.71\times10^{7}
