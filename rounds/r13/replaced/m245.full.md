## 题目
Yttria $\left(\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3\right)$ particles $750 \AA$ in diameter are introduced into tungsten by internal oxidation. Measurements using an electron microscope show that there are $5 \times 10^{14}$ oxide particles per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the $\mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Y}$ originally in the alloy. The density of $\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is $5.01 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of wt_percent_Y.The unit of the answer is %.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1 — Particle size in cm:**
$$d = 750\,\text{Å} = 750 \times 10^{-8}\,\text{cm} = 7.5\times10^{-6}\,\text{cm}$$

**Step 2 — Volume of one spherical particle:**
$$V_p = \7b\frac{\pi}{6}\d^3 = \frac{\pi}{6}(7.5\times10^{-6})^3 = 2.209\times10^{-16}\,\text{cm}^3$$

**Step 3 — Total volume of Y₂O₃ per cm³ of alloy:**
$$V_{\text{Y₂O₃}} = (5\times10^{14})(2.209\times10^{-16}) = 0.1104\,\text{cm}^3$$

**Step 4 — Mass of Y₂O₃ per cm³:**
$$m_{\text{Y₂O₃}} = 0.1104 \times 5.01 = 0.553\,\text{g}$$

**Step 5 — Mass of Y contained in that Y₂O₃:**

Using molar masses ($M_Y = 88.91$, $M_{\text{Y₂O₃}} = 225.81$):
$$m_Y = 0.553 \times \frac{2(88.91)}{225.81} = 0.436\,\text{g}$$

**Step 6 — Wt% Y in the alloy:**

The tungsten matrix occupies the remaining volume, with mass $m_W = 19.3\,\text{g}$ per cm³:

$$\text{wt\% Y} = \frac{m_Y}{m_W + m_Y}\times 100 = \frac{0.436}{19.3 + 0.436}\times 100 \approx 2.21\%$$

$$\boxed{2.21}$$

## 最终答案
2.21
