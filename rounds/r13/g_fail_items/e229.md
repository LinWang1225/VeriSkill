## 题目
Above $882^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, titanium has a BCC crystal structure, with $a=0.332 \mathrm{~nm}$. Below this temperature, titanium has a HCP structure with $a=0.2978 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4735 \mathrm{~nm}$. Determine the percent volume change when BCC titanium transforms to HCP titanium. Is this a contraction or expansion? Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_volume_change, transformation_type).The unit of the answer is (%, ).

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Compare volume per atom, not raw unit-cell volumes, because the cells contain different atom counts (BCC: Z=2; HCP conventional hexagonal cell: Z=6, primitive HCP: Z=2). V_BCC/atom = a_BCC^3/2 = (0.332)^3/2 = 0.0182972 nm^3. V_HCP/atom = (sqrt3/2 * a_HCP^2 * c_HCP)/2 = (sqrt3/2 * 0.2978^2 * 0.4735)/2 = 0.0181832 nm^3. Percent change = (V_HCP - V_BCC)/V_BCC * 100 = (0.0181832 - 0.0182972)/0.0182972 * 100 = -0.6230%. Negative => contraction. Sigfigs: weakest input a_BCC=0.332 has 3 sigfigs, so report 3: -0.623%.

（执行证据）
V_BCC/atom = 0.0182972 nm^3 and V_HCP/atom = 0.0181832 nm^3, both normalized per atom (BCC Z=2, HCP Z=2 primitive). The ratio gives (V_HCP-V_BCC)/V_BCC = -0.00623, i.e. -0.623%. Since V_HCP < V_BCC, the BCC→HCP transformation is a contraction.

## 最终答案
(-0.623, contraction)
