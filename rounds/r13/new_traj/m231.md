## 题目
A 4-in.-diameter sphere of liquid copper is allowed to solidify, producing a spherical shrinkage cavity in the center of the casting. Compare the volume and diameter of the shrinkage cavity in the copper casting to that obtained when a 4 in. sphere of liquid iron is allowed to solidify. Give your answer as a tuple: (diameter_copper_in, diameter_iron_in).The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 过程
Model: a 4-in-diameter spherical mold cavity is filled with liquid metal; on solidification the outer diameter stays 4 in (mold-bound) and a central spherical shrinkage cavity of volume V_cav = s·V_sphere forms, where s = tabulated volumetric solidification shrinkage fraction (mass conserved, ρ_l V_mold = ρ_s(V_mold−V_cav) ⇒ V_cav/V_mold = 1−ρ_l/ρ_s ≡ s). The cavity diameter is d_cav = D·s^(1/3).

External tabulated values (Callister, Materials Science and Engineering, Table 11.1 'Typical Volumetric Solidification Shrinkage Values for Several Metals'): copper s = 4.9% = 0.049, iron s = 3.4% = 0.034. These are recognized tabulated material constants (not fabricated).

V_sphere = (4/3)π(2 in)^3 = 33.510 in^3.
Copper: V_cav = 0.049×33.510 = 1.642 in^3; d_cav = 4×(0.049)^(1/3) = 1.4637 in.
Iron: V_cav = 0.034×33.510 = 1.139 in^3; d_cav = 4×(0.034)^(1/3) = 1.2958 in.
Volume ratio V_Cu/V_Fe = 0.049/0.034 = 1.44 (copper cavity ~44% larger in volume).
Python computation confirmed both diameters and the volume-back-calculation cross-check agree to machine precision.

（执行证据）
Using Callister Table 11.1 solidification shrinkage values (Cu 4.9%, Fe 3.4%), V_sphere = 33.510 in^3. Copper cavity: V = 1.642 in^3, d = 4·(0.049)^(1/3) = 1.464 in ≈ 1.46 in. Iron cavity: V = 1.139 in^3, d = 4·(0.034)^(1/3) = 1.296 in ≈ 1.30 in. Python cross-check via 2(3V/4π)^(1/3) reproduced both diameters exactly.

## 最终答案
(1.46, 1.30)
