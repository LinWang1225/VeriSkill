## 题目
After finding the electrical conductivity of cobalt at $0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, we decide we would like to double that conductivity. To what temperature must we cool the metal? Give your answer as the value of required_temperature_degC.The unit of the answer is °C.

## 过程
The sanity check confirms σ(T)/σ(0) = 2.0 exactly. 

## Solution Summary

Using the linear resistivity–temperature relation referenced at 20°C, $\rho(T) = \rho_{20}[1 + \alpha(T-20)]$, with cobalt's temperature coefficient $\alpha = 0.00604\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}^{-1}$:

- At 0°C: $\rho(0) = \rho_{20}(1 - 20\alpha)$, so $\sigma(0) = 1/\rho(0)$.
- Doubling conductivity means halving resistivity: $\rho(T) = \rho(0)/2$.

Setting $\rho_{20}[1+\alpha(T-20)] = \rho_{20}(1-20\alpha)/2$, the resistivity $\rho_{20}$ cancels, giving:

$$T = 10 - \frac{1}{2\alpha} = 10 - \frac{1}{2(0.00604)} = 10 - 82.78 = -72.78\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

$$\boxed{-72.78}$$

## 最终答案
-72.78
