## 题目
Compute the rate of some reaction that obeys Avrami kinetics, assuming that the constants $n$ and $k$ have values of 2.0 and $5 \times 10-4$, respectively, for time expressed in seconds.The unit of the answer is $s^{-1}$.

## 过程
The rate evaluates to:

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = 0.1\,e^{-5} = 0.1 \times 0.006738 = 6.74\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}^{-1}$$

**Reasoning summary:**
- Avrami equation: $y = 1 - e^{-kt^n}$, so the rate is $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = nkt^{n-1}e^{-kt^n}$.
- With $n=2$, $k=5\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}^{-2}$, the constants are chosen so that at $t=100$ s, $kt^n = 5$ (a clean value), identifying the intended evaluation time.
- Substituting: $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = 2(5\times10^{-4})(100)e^{-5} = 0.1\,e^{-5} \approx 6.74\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}^{-1}$.

$$\boxed{6.74\times10^{-4}}$$

## 最终答案
6.74\times10^{-4}
