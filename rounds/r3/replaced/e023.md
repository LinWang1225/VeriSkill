## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 过程
Looking at this problem, I need to analyze manganese ferrite, **MnFe₂O₄**, which has the spinel structure with tetrahedral (A) sites and octahedral (B) sites. In a ferrimagnetic spinel, the A and B sublattice moments align antiparallel, so the net moment per formula unit is **|M_B − M_A|**.

Given: Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB (both have the same spin-only moment).

**(a) Inverse spinel:** Fe³⁺ occupies the tetrahedral (A) site; Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ share the octahedral (B) sites.
- M_A = 5 (Fe³⁺)
- M_B = 5 + 5 = 10 (Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺)
- Net = |10 − 5| = **5 μB**

**(b) Normal spinel:** Mn²⁺ occupies the tetrahedral (A) site; two Fe³⁺ occupy the octahedral (B) sites.
- M_A = 5 (Mn²⁺)
- M_B = 5 + 5 = 10 (2 × Fe³⁺)
- Net = |10 − 5| = **5 μB**

**(c) 50:50 mixture:** Since both the pure inverse and pure normal structures give the same moment (5 μB), any linear combination also gives:
- Net = **5 μB**

The key insight is that because Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ have **identical** magnetic moments (5 μB each), interchanging them between A and B sites does not change the net moment. The structure (inverse, normal, or any mixture) has no effect on the result.

$$\boxed{(5,\ 5,\ 5)}$$

## 最终答案
(5,\ 5,\ 5)
