## 题目
Given that at the eutectoid temperature the ferrite phase contains 0.0218 %C and the cementite phase (Fe₃C) contains 6.67 %C,  use the lever rule to calculate the weight fractions of ferrite and cementite in pearlite, which has an overall composition of 0.77 %C. 

Give your answer as (ferrite_fraction, cementite_fraction).The units of each required answer are (%, %), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Apply the lever rule at the eutectoid temperature with overall pearlite composition C0 = 0.77 %C, ferrite C_α = 0.0218 %C, cementite C_θ = 6.67 %C. The weight fractions are W_α = (C_θ − C0)/(C_θ − C_α) and W_θ = (C0 − C_α)/(C_θ − C_α). Numerically: W_α = (6.67−0.77)/(6.67−0.0218) = 5.90/6.6482 = 0.88746; W_θ = (0.77−0.0218)/6.6482 = 0.7482/6.6482 = 0.11254; sum = 1.000 ✓. The problem requests the answer in %, so multiply by 100: 88.746% and 11.254%. Per g-matsci-sigfig-rounding, the weakest input is 0.77 (2 sig figs), so round the final report to 2 sig figs: 89% and 11%.

（执行证据）
Lever rule: W_α = (6.67−0.77)/(6.67−0.0218) = 0.88746, W_θ = (0.77−0.0218)/(6.67−0.0218) = 0.11254, summing to 1.000. Expressed as percentages and rounded to 2 significant figures (limited by the input 0.77 %C), the fractions are 89% ferrite and 11% cementite.

## 最终答案
(89%, 11%)
