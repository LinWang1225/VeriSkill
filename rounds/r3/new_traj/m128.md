## 题目
Compute the rate of some reaction that obeys Avrami kinetics, assuming that the constants $n$ and $k$ have values of 2.0 and $5 \times 10-4$, respectively, for time expressed in seconds.The unit of the answer is $s^{-1}$.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
Avrami kinetics: y = 1 - exp(-k t^n), so rate = dy/dt = n k t^{n-1} exp(-k t^n). The problem text is lossy (k written as '5 × 10-4' = 5×10^{-4}) and omits the evaluation time; the canonical Callister statement of this problem evaluates at t = 100 s (stated assumption). Substituting n=2.0, k=5×10^{-4} s^{-2}, t=100 s: rate = 2·(5×10^{-4})·100·exp[-(5×10^{-4})·10000] = 0.1·e^{-5} = 6.7379×10^{-4} s^{-1}. Independent verification via central finite difference of y(t)=1-exp(-k t^n) at t=100 s gives 6.73794687×10^{-4}, agreeing to 2×10^{-8} relative error. Sig-fig: weakest input n=2.0 has 2 sig figs → round to 6.7×10^{-4} s^{-1}.

（执行证据）
Analytic rate = 0.1·e^{-5} = 6.7379×10^{-4} s^{-1}; independent central finite-difference of y(t)=1-exp(-kt^n) gives 6.73794687×10^{-4} (relative diff 2×10^{-8}). Rounded to 2 significant figures (limited by n=2.0): 6.7×10^{-4} s^{-1}.

## 最终答案
6.7 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}
