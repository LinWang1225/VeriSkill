## 题目
The following data are obtained for an armco iron alloy during the generation of steady-state ferromagnetic hysteresis loop: \begin{tabular}{cc} \hline $\boldsymbol{H}($ amperes $/ \mathrm{m})$ & $\boldsymbol{B}\left(\right.$ weber $\left./ \mathrm{m}^{\mathbf{2}}\right)$ \\ \hline 56 & 0.50 \\ 30 & 0.46 \\ 10 & 0.40 \\ 0 & 0.36 \\ -10 & 0.28 \\ -20 & 0.12 \\ -25 & 0 \\ -40 & -0.28 \\ -56 & -0.50 \\ \hline \end{tabular} (a) Plot the data. (b) What is the remanent induction? (c) What is the coercive field? Give your answer as a tuple: (remanent_induction, coercive_field).The units of each required answer are (Wb/m^2, A/m), respectively.

## 过程
The plot confirms the two intersection points:
- **Remanent induction**: where the loop crosses the B-axis (H = 0) → B = 0.36 Wb/m²
- **Coercive field**: where the loop crosses the H-axis (B = 0) → H = −25 A/m, magnitude = 25 A/m

## Final Answer

The remanent induction is the flux density remaining when H = 0, which is **0.36 Wb/m²**. The coercive field is the demagnetizing field needed to bring B back to zero, which occurs at H = −25 A/m, reported as a magnitude of **25 A/m**.

$$\boxed{(0.36,\ 25)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.36,\ 25)
