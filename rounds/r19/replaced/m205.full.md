## 题目
The density of aluminum is $2.7 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is about $4 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Describe the characteristics of the aluminum-oxide film. Compare these with the oxide film that forms on tungsten. The density of tungsten is $19.254 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $W O_3$ is $7.3 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple: (P-B_ratio_aluminum, P-B_ratio_tungsten).The units of each required answer are (, ), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

The **Pilling–Bedworth (P-B) ratio** determines whether an oxide film is protective:

$$R_{PB} = \frac{M_{\text{oxide}}\,\rho_{\text{metal}}}{n\,M_{\text{metal}}\,\rho_{\text{oxide}}}$$

where $n$ is the number of metal atoms per formula unit of oxide.

### Aluminum → Al₂O₃
- $M_{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3}=101.96$, $M_{\text{Al}}=26.98$, $n=2$
- $R_{PB}=\dfrac{101.96\times 2.7}{2\times 26.98\times 4}\approx 1.28$

Since $1 < R_{PB} < 2$, the Al₂O₃ film is **compact, adherent, and protective** — it fully covers the metal surface without large compressive stress. This is why aluminum resists further oxidation (passivation).

### Tungsten → WO₃
- $M_{\text{WO}_3}=231.84$, $M_{\text{W}}=183.84$, $n=1$
- $R_{PB}=\dfrac{231.84\times 19.254}{1\times 183.84\times 7.3}\approx 3.33$

Since $R_{PB} > 2$, the WO₃ film is **too voluminous**: it builds up large compressive stresses, cracks and spalls, exposing fresh metal — hence **non-protective**. Tungsten therefore continues to oxidize (and WO₃ is volatile at high temperature, worsening the loss).

### Comparison
| | Al / Al₂O₃ | W / WO₃ |
|---|---|---|
| P-B ratio | 1.28 | 3.33 |
| Film character | dense, adherent, protective | porous/cracking, non-protective |

$$\boxed{(1.28,\ 3.33)}$$

## 最终答案
(1.28,\ 3.33)
