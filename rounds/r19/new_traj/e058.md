## 题目
The following data are obtained for an armco iron alloy during the generation of steady-state ferromagnetic hysteresis loop: \begin{tabular}{cc} \hline $\boldsymbol{H}($ amperes $/ \mathrm{m})$ & $\boldsymbol{B}\left(\right.$ weber $\left./ \mathrm{m}^{\mathbf{2}}\right)$ \\ \hline 56 & 0.50 \\ 30 & 0.46 \\ 10 & 0.40 \\ 0 & 0.36 \\ -10 & 0.28 \\ -20 & 0.12 \\ -25 & 0 \\ -40 & -0.28 \\ -56 & -0.50 \\ \hline \end{tabular} (a) Plot the data. (b) What is the remanent induction? (c) What is the coercive field? Give your answer as a tuple: (remanent_induction, coercive_field).The units of each required answer are (Wb/m^2, A/m), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
This is a ferromagnetic hysteresis-loop read-off (Callister-style). (a) Plotting H vs B gives the descending branch of the loop. (b) Remanent induction = B at H=0, read directly from the table: H=0 → B=0.36 Wb/m². (c) Coercive field = H at B=0, read directly from the table: B=0 → H=-25 A/m. By the standard Callister convention the coercive field Hc is the magnitude of the applied field required to reduce B to zero (the negative intercept is labeled -Hc on the loop figure), so Hc = 25 A/m. Sigfig check: inputs are 2 sig figs (0.36, 25), so both reported values are within the n_min=2 limit; no rounding needed.

（执行证据）
From the table, the row H=0 gives B=0.36 Wb/m², which is the remanent induction. The row B=0 gives H=-25 A/m, so the coercive field magnitude is 25 A/m. Python plot/read-off confirms Br=0.36 and |Hc|=25 with no interpolation required since both axis crossings are exact data points.

## 最终答案
(0.36, 25)
