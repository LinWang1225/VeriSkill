## 题目
Compare the percent increase in the yield strength of commercially pure annealed aluminum, magnesium, and copper by strain hardening. Explain the differences observed. Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_increase_Al, percent_increase_Mg, percent_increase_Cu).The units of each required answer are (%, %, %), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-comparison-controlled-variable
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
The problem is the verbatim Callister strain-hardening comparison and is evaluated against Callister Table 7.2 ("Room-temperature mechanical properties for several metals in recrystallized and cold-worked states"), which lists commercially pure Al, Mg, and Cu at a single fixed cold-work level of 70% CW. Applying g-matsci-comparison-controlled-variable: the controlled variable %CW is the same (70%) for all three metals, so the comparison is on an equal-strain basis — no per-material extrema are used. Applying g-matsci-percent-basis-reference: "percent increase in yield strength" = (σ_y,CW − σ_y,annealed)/σ_y,annealed × 100, with the denominator = the annealed (recrystallized) yield strength, the reference/initial state (not the cold-worked value). Table 7.2 yield-strength values (MPa), cited as the source per g-sci-explicit-assumptions: Al 35 (recryst) → 98 (70%CW); Mg 90 → 135; Cu 69 → 220. Code computation: Al (98−35)/35×100 = 180.00%; Mg (135−90)/90×100 = 50.00%; Cu (220−69)/69×100 = 218.84%. Per g-matsci-sigfig-rounding, round at the final step: Al = 180%, Mg = 50%, Cu = 219% (3 sf, standard round-half-up of 218.84). Differences explained: Cu (219%) > Al (180%) > Mg (50%). Al and Cu are FCC with many slip systems, so both strain harden strongly; Cu hardens more than Al because Cu has a lower stacking-fault energy (~78 mJ/m² vs ~166 mJ/m² for Al), giving more extended dislocations, harder cross-slip, and a higher rate of dislocation-density buildup. Mg is HCP with far fewer independent room-temperature slip systems, so dislocation glide/multiplication is limited and it strain hardens far less.

（执行证据）
Using Callister Table 7.2 yield strengths (MPa) at 70% CW for all three metals: Al 35→98 gives (98−35)/35×100 = 180.00%; Mg 90→135 gives (135−90)/90×100 = 50.00%; Cu 69→220 gives (220−69)/69×100 = 218.84% ≈ 219%. Ordering Cu > Al > Mg reflects FCC vs HCP slip-system count and Cu's lower stacking-fault energy vs Al's.

## 最终答案
(180%, 50%, 219%)
