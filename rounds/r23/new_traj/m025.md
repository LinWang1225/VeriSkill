## 题目
A copper penny has a mass of 2.60 g . Assuming pure copper, how much of this mass is contributed by (a) the neutrons in the copper nuclei and (b) electrons? Give your answer as a tuple: (neutron_mass, electron_mass).The units of each required answer are (g, g), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
Copper: Z = 29, standard atomic weight M_Cu = 63.546 g/mol (CRC Handbook). For natural copper the average neutron number per atom is A_avg - Z = 63.546 - 29 = 34.55 (cross-checked with isotopic abundances 69.15% 63Cu/30.85% 65Cu → 34.62, agreeing to 3 s.f.). Moles of Cu = 2.60/63.546 = 0.04091 mol; atoms = 0.04091 × 6.022×10^23 = 2.463×10^22. (a) N_neutrons = 2.463×10^22 × 34.55 = 8.51×10^23; m_neutron = 1.6749×10^-24 g → mass = 1.426 g → 1.43 g (3 s.f.). (b) N_electrons = 2.463×10^22 × 29 = 7.14×10^23; m_electron = 9.109×10^-31 g → mass = 6.51×10^-7 g. Input 2.60 g has 3 s.f., so report 3 s.f.

（执行证据）
Atoms of Cu = (2.60/63.546)×6.022e23 = 2.463e22. Neutron mass = 2.463e22 × 34.55 × 1.6749e-24 = 1.43 g. Electron mass = 2.463e22 × 29 × 9.109e-31 = 6.51×10^-7 g. Both rounded to 3 significant figures matching the 2.60 g input.

## 最终答案
(1.43, 6.51e-7)
