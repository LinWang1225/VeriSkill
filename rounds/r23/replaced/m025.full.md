## 题目
A copper penny has a mass of 2.60 g . Assuming pure copper, how much of this mass is contributed by (a) the neutrons in the copper nuclei and (b) electrons? Give your answer as a tuple: (neutron_mass, electron_mass).The units of each required answer are (g, g), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
Copper: Z=29, standard atomic weight M_Cu=63.546 g/mol (source: standard periodic table). Natural Cu is ⁶³Cu (69.15%) and ⁶⁵Cu (30.85%), giving 34 and 36 neutrons respectively, so the isotope-weighted average neutron count per atom is 0.6915·34 + 0.3085·36 = 34.617. Number of atoms in 2.60 g: N = (2.60/63.546)·N_A = 2.464×10²². (a) Neutron mass = N·34.617·m_n with m_n=1.67493×10⁻²⁴ g → 1.4286 g. (b) Electron mass = N·29·m_e with m_e=9.1094×10⁻²⁸ g → 6.509×10⁻⁴ g. Independent verification via a per-mole path using atomic mass units (m_n=1.008665 u, m_e=5.4858×10⁻⁴ u, 1 u=1.66054×10⁻²⁴ g) reproduced 1.4286 g and 6.509×10⁻⁴ g exactly. Input 2.60 g has 3 sig figs, so results are rounded to 3 sig figs (standard half-up): 1.43 g and 6.51×10⁻⁴ g.

（执行证据）
N_atoms = (2.60/63.546)·6.022×10²³ = 2.464×10²². Neutron mass = 2.464×10²² × 34.617 × 1.67493×10⁻²⁴ g = 1.4286 g → 1.43 g. Electron mass = 2.464×10²² × 29 × 9.1094×10⁻²⁸ g = 6.509×10⁻⁴ g → 6.51×10⁻⁴ g. An independent per-mole calculation using atomic mass units reproduced both values to 5 significant figures.

## 最终答案
(1.43, 6.51\times10^{-4})
