## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 激活技能

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄, a spinel AB₂O₄ with Z = 8 formula units per unit cell (per the skill's spinel Z table). Per formula unit: 1 tetrahedral (A) site and 2 octahedral (B) sites; the A and B sublattice moments are antiparallel, so net = |M_B − M_A|. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB and Fe³⁺ = 5 μB.

(a) Inverse spinel [Fe³⁺]_tet[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]_octO₄: M_A = 5 (Fe³⁺), M_B = 5+5 = 10 (Mn²⁺+Fe³⁺); net per f.u. = |10−5| = 5 μB.
(b) Normal spinel [Mn²⁺]_tet[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]_octO₄: M_A = 5 (Mn²⁺), M_B = 5+5 = 10 (2 Fe³⁺); net per f.u. = |10−5| = 5 μB.
(c) 50:50 mixture: net per f.u. = 0.5·5 + 0.5·5 = 5 μB.

The problem asks for the moment of a unit cell, and the binding skill requires scaling the per-formula-unit value by Z = 8 for a spinel. Code confirms: per f.u. (5, 5, 5) → per unit cell (40, 40, 40) μB.

（执行证据）
Inverse: |(Mn²⁺+Fe³⁺) − Fe³⁺| = |10−5| = 5 μB/f.u.; Normal: |2Fe³⁺ − Mn²⁺| = |10−5| = 5 μB/f.u.; both equal because Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ carry the same 5 μB. Spinel Z = 8, so unit-cell moment = 8 × 5 = 40 μB for all three cases (Python: 8×5 = 40).

## 最终答案
(40, 40, 40)\ \mu_B
