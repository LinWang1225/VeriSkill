## 题目
Consider a cylindrical specimen of some hypothetical metal alloy that has a diameter of 10.0 mm (0.39 in.). A tensile force of $1500 \mathrm{~N}(340 \mathrm{lb})$ produces an elastic reduction in diameter of $6.7 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~mm}(2.64 \times$ $10^{-5}$ in.). Compute the elastic modulus of this alloy, given that Poisson's ratio is 0.35 .The unit of the answer is GPa.

## 过程
## Step-by-Step Solution

**Given:**
- Diameter $d_0 = 10.0 \text{ mm}$
- Tensile force $F = 1500 \text{ N}$
- Elastic reduction in diameter $\Delta d = 6.7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ mm}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu = 0.35$

**Step 1 — Lateral (transverse) strain:**
$$\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = \frac{\Delta d}{d_0} = \frac{6.7 \times 10^{-4}}{10.0} = 6.7 \times 10^{-5}$$

**Step 2 — Axial strain via Poisson's ratio** ($\nu = -\varepsilon_{\text{lat}}/\varepsilon_{\text{ax}}$):
$$\varepsilon_{\text{ax}} = \frac{\varepsilon_{\text{lat}}}{\nu} = \frac{6.7 \times 10^{-5}}{0.35} = 1.914 \times 10^{-4}$$

**Step 3 — Engineering stress:**
$$A_0 = \pi\left(\frac{d_0}{2}\right)^2 = \pi(5.0)^2 = 78.54 \text{ mm}^2 = 7.854 \times 10^{-5} \text{ m}^2$$
$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A_0} = \frac{1500}{7.854 \times 10^{-5}} = 1.910 \times 10^{7} \text{ Pa}$$

**Step 4 — Elastic modulus** ($E = \sigma / \varepsilon_{\text{ax}}$):
$$E = \frac{1.910 \times 10^{7}}{1.914 \times 10^{-4}} \approx 9.98 \times 10^{10} \text{ Pa} \approx 100 \text{ GPa}$$

$$\boxed{100}$$

## 最终答案
100
