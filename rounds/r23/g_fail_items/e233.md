## 题目
A 0.001 in . BCC iron foil is used to separate a high hydrogen content gas from a low hydrogen content gas at $650^{\circ} \mathrm{C} .5 \times 10^8 \mathrm{H}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{cm}^3$ are in equilibrium on one side of the foil, and $2 \times 10^3 \mathrm{H}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{cm}^3$ are in equilibrium with the other side. Determine (a) the concentration gradient of hydrogen and (b) the flux of hydrogen through the foil. Give your answer as a tuple: (concentration_gradient, flux).The units of each required answer are (H atoms/(cm³·cm), H atoms/(cm²·s)), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**(a) Concentration gradient**

Converting thickness: Δx = 0.001 in × 2.54 cm/in = 0.00254 cm

Taking x from the high-concentration side to the low-concentration side:

$$\frac{dC}{dx} = \frac{C_2 - C_1}{\Delta x} = \frac{2\times10^3 - 5\times10^8}{0.00254} = -1.97\times10^{11}\ \frac{\text{H atoms}}{\text{cm}^3\cdot\text{cm}}$$

**(b) Hydrogen flux** — Fick's first law: $J = -D\frac{dC}{dx}$

Diffusion coefficient for H in BCC iron at 923 K, using $D_0 = 1.4\times10^{-3}$ m²/s and $Q_d = 13{,}400$ J/mol:

$$D = D_0\,e^{-Q_d/RT} = 1.4\times10^{-3}\,e^{-13400/(8.31\times923)} = 2.44\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s} = 2.44\ \text{cm}^2/\text{s}$$

$$J = -D\frac{dC}{dx} = -(2.44)(-1.97\times10^{11}) = 4.80\times10^{11}\ \frac{\text{H atoms}}{\text{cm}^2\cdot\text{s}}$$

$$\boxed{(-1.97\times10^{11},\ 4.80\times10^{11})}$$

## 最终答案
(-1.97\times10^{11},\ 4.80\times10^{11})
