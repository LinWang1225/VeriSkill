## 题目
Calculate the steady-state heat transfer rate through a $10-\mathrm{mm}$-thick sheet of copper for a $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ temperature drop from $550^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as the value of heat_transfer_rate.The unit of the answer is J/m^2*s.

## 过程
## Reasoning

For steady-state conduction through a plane wall, Fourier's law applies. Because the problem specifies the **absolute temperatures** (550°C → 500°C) rather than just the 50°C drop, copper's temperature-dependent conductivity $k(T)$ must be used. The heat flux is

$$q'' = \frac{1}{L}\int_{T_{\text{cold}}}^{T_{\text{hot}}} k(T)\,dT$$

**Given:**
- Thickness $L = 10\ \text{mm} = 0.010\ \text{m}$
- $T_{\text{hot}} = 550^{\circ}\text{C} = 823\ \text{K}$, $T_{\text{cold}} = 500^{\circ}\text{C} = 773\ \text{K}$
- $\Delta T = 50\ \text{K}$

**Copper $k(T)$** (standard data, W/m·K): at 773 K → 368.6, at 798 K → 367.1, at 823 K → 365.9. Over this range $k$ drops noticeably from the room-temperature value of 401, so using a constant 401 would overestimate the flux by ~9%.

**Integration** (linear interpolation between standard data points):

$$\int_{773}^{823} k(T)\,dT \approx 18\,358.6\ \text{W/m}$$

$$q'' = \frac{18\,358.6}{0.010} \approx 1.84\times 10^{6}\ \text{W/m}^2$$

This agrees with the quick check $q'' \approx k(525^{\circ}\text{C})\,\Delta T/L = 367.1\times 50/0.01 \approx 1.836\times10^{6}$.

$$\boxed{1.84\times10^{6}}$$

## 最终答案
1.84\times10^{6}
