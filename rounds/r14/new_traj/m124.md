## 题目
The fatigue data for a steel alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude [MPa (ksi)] | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| $470(68.0)$ | $10^{4}$ |
| $440(63.4)$ | $3 \times 10^{4}$ |
| $390(56.2)$ | $10^{5}$ |
| $350(51.0)$ | $3 \times 10^{5}$ |
| $310(45.3)$ | $10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $3 \times 10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{7}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{8}$ |

(a) What is the fatigue limit for this alloy?
(b) Determine fatigue lifetimes at stress amplitudes of 415 MPa (60,000 psi).
(c) Estimate fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ and $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles.\

Give your answer as a tuple (fatigue limit, fatigue lifetimes of 415 MPa, fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ cycles, fatigue strengths at $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles).The units of each required answer are (MPa, cycles, MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sn-fatigue-loglog-interpolation
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
(a) Fatigue limit: the three low-stress points (290 MPa at 3e6, 1e7, 1e8 cycles) form a horizontal plateau, so S_e = 290 MPa. This plateau is excluded from the Basquin fit. (b,c) The five finite-life points (470,1e4),(440,3e4),(390,1e5),(350,3e5),(310,1e6) are all treated as lying on one Basquin law S = A·N^b. A single log-log least-squares fit over ALL five points gives log10(A)=3.04880, b=-0.092246, i.e. A=1118.9, b=-0.09225. Residuals in logS are [-0.0077,+0.0077,+0.0035,+0.0005,-0.0040] (max ~1.8% in S, no systematic bias) → unified fit justified. Re-solving all sub-questions with this ONE curve: (b) N=(415/A)^(1/b)=4.672e4 cycles; (c) S=A·N^b at N=2e4 → 448.8 MPa, at N=6e5 → 327.9 MPa. All queried N lie within the fitted range [1e4,1e6], so no extrapolation. Code run; results above.

（执行证据）
Unified log-log least squares over the 5 finite-life points gave A=1118.9, b=-0.09225 with max residual 1.8% in S, confirming a single Basquin law. Fatigue limit = 290 MPa from the plateau. Inverting the one fitted curve: N(415 MPa)=4.67e4 cycles, S(2e4)=449 MPa, S(6e5)=328 MPa.

## 最终答案
(290\ \text{MPa},\ 4.67\times10^{4}\ \text{cycles},\ 449\ \text{MPa},\ 328\ \text{MPa})
