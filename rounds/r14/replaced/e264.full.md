## 题目
The atomic radii of \(\mathrm{K}^{+}\) and \(\mathrm{Br}^{-}\) ions are 0.138 and 0.196 nm , respectively.
(a) Using Equations 2.9 and 2.10, calculate the force of attraction between these two ions at their equilibrium interionic separation (i.e., when the ions just touch one another).
(b) What is the force of repulsion at this same separation distance?
Give your answer as a tuple (force of attraction, force of repulsion).The units of each required answer are (N, N), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
This is the Callister ionic-bonding problem. Equilibrium interionic separation is the sum of ionic radii: r0 = r(K+) + r(Br-) = 0.138 + 0.196 = 0.334 nm = 3.34e-10 m. Eq 2.9 (attractive force, Callister convention with Z1,Z2 as valence magnitudes and a leading minus sign): F_A = -Z1*Z2*e^2/(4*pi*eps0*r^2). For K+ and Br-, |Z1|=|Z2|=1. Using e=1.602176634e-19 C and 1/(4*pi*eps0)=8.987551787e9 N·m^2/C^2: |F_A| = 8.987551787e9*(1.602176634e-19)^2/(3.34e-10)^2 = 2.068e-9 N, so F_A = -2.07e-9 N (attractive, negative). (b) At the equilibrium separation the net force is zero (F_A + F_R = 0), so the repulsive force exactly balances the attractive force: F_R = -F_A = +2.07e-9 N. Independent recheck using eps0=8.854187817e-12 directly (different formula path) gives 2.068089167e-9 N, matching to 3 sf. Inputs 0.138 and 0.196 each have 3 significant figures, so the sum r0=0.334 nm carries 3 sf and the final answers are reported to 3 sf.

（执行证据）
r0 = 0.138+0.196 = 0.334 nm = 3.34e-10 m. Eq 2.9 gives F_A = -e^2/(4*pi*eps0*r0^2) = -2.068e-9 N, rounded to 3 sf -> -2.07e-9 N. At equilibrium F_A+F_R=0, so F_R = +2.07e-9 N. Two independent code paths (k_e form and eps0 form) both yield 2.068089167e-9 N.

## 最终答案
(-2.07\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{N},\ +2.07\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{N})
