## 题目
A structural component in the form of a wide plate is to be fabricated from a steel alloy that has a planestrain fracture toughness of $98.9 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{~m}}(90 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\text { in. }})$ and a yield strength of 860 MPa (125,000 psi). The flaw size resolution limit of the flaw detection apparatus is 3.0 mm ( 0.12 in .). If the design stress is one-half the yield strength and the value of $Y$ is 1.0, determine whether a critical flaw for this plate is subject to detection.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1: Design stress**
$$\sigma = \frac{1}{2}\sigma_y = \frac{1}{2}(860) = 430 \text{ MPa}$$

**Step 2: Critical flaw size** from $K_{Ic} = Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a_c}$:

$$a_c = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{K_{Ic}}{Y\sigma}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{98.9}{1.0 \times 430}\right)^2$$

$$a_c = \frac{1}{\pi}(0.22977)^2 = \frac{0.05279}{\pi} = 0.01684 \text{ m} = 16.84 \text{ mm}$$

**Step 3: Compare with detection limit**

The critical flaw size $a_c = 16.84$ mm is **much larger** than the flaw detection resolution limit of 3.0 mm. Therefore, a critical flaw for this plate **is subject to detection** — the apparatus can detect flaws well below the critical size, so the component is safe from undetected critical flaws.

$$\boxed{16.84}$$

## 最终答案
16.84
