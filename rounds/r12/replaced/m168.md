## 题目
An AISI 1020 steel angle of dimensions $150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 12 \mathrm{~mm}(6 \mathrm{in} . \times 4 \mathrm{in} . \times$ $1 / 2 \mathrm{in}$.) is to be welded to a steel plate by fillet welds along the edges of the 150 mm leg. The angle should support a load of $270 \mathrm{kN}(60,000 \mathrm{lb})$ acting along its length. Determine the lengths of the welds to be specified. The welding electrode used is AWS-AISI E6012 with a tensile strength of $414 \mathrm{MPa}(60 \mathrm{ksi})$. Give your answer as the value of weld_length.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 过程

Geometry: equal-leg angle 150×150×12 mm (metric as written); the 150 mm leg against the plate is welded along its two long edges (two parallel fillet welds, length l each, separated by 150 mm). Load F=270 kN acts along the length through the angle centroid. Angle centroid from heel: x_c=(a²+at−t²)/(2(2a−t)) = 41.94 mm. Weld-group centroid at 75 mm → eccentricity e=75−41.94=33.06 mm → torque T=F·e=8.93×10⁶ N·mm about the weld centroid (no bending, since the load is parallel to the welds).

Declared assumptions (g-sci-explicit-assumptions): (1) weld leg size h = angle thickness = 12 mm — the only thickness given; standard full-strength convention (fillet leg = member thickness), not a fabricated value. (2) Allowable shear on the throat τ_all = 0.30·S_u = 0.30×414 = 124.2 MPa (AISC ASD for E6012 electrode). Throat = 0.707·h = 8.485 mm → capacity per unit weld length = 8.485×124.2 = 1053.9 N/mm.

Line-weld torsion (unit throat): A=2l, I_x=l³/6, I_y=a²l/2, J=I_x+I_y. Critical point is the heel-weld end (x=0, |z−z̄|=l/2) where torsional shear ADDS to direct shear: τ_x=T(l/2)/J, τ_z=T·a/(2J)+F/(2l). Set √(τ_x²+τ_z²)=1053.9 N/mm and solve for l. The toe-weld end (torsion subtracts) gives only 607 N/mm, confirming the heel governs.

Numerical solve: l = 173.18 mm.
