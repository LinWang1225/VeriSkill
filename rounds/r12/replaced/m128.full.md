## 题目
Compute the rate of some reaction that obeys Avrami kinetics, assuming that the constants $n$ and $k$ have values of 2.0 and $5 \times 10-4$, respectively, for time expressed in seconds.The unit of the answer is $s^{-1}$.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Avrami kinetics: y = 1 - exp(-k t^n). Rate = dy/dt = n k t^{n-1} exp(-k t^n). Substituting n=2.0, k=5e-4: dy/dt = 2*(5e-4)*t*exp(-(5e-4)*t^2) = 1e-3 * t * exp(-5e-4 * t^2) s^{-1}. Verified with sympy: diff(1-exp(-k*t**n),t).subs(n=2,k=5/10000) = t*exp(-t**2/2000)/1000. The target quantity (rate) depends on time t, but the problem statement gives no value of t and no transformation fraction y from which t could be eliminated via y=1-exp(-k t^n). Per the missing-input constraint, I do not invent a 'clean' time (e.g. t=1 s or t=100 s) to force a single number; the answer is retained as the explicit function of t. Units check: k has units s^{-n}=s^{-2}, so n k t^{n-1} ~ s^{-2}*s = s^{-1}, matching the stated s^{-1}. Sig figs: k=5e-4 has 1 sf, n=2.0 has 2 sf, so the prefactor is reported to 1 sf (1e-3).

（执行证据）
sympy confirmed dy/dt = t*exp(-t**2/2000)/1000 for n=2, k=5e-4, i.e. 1e-3*t*exp(-5e-4*t^2) s^{-1}. The rate depends on time t, which the problem does not specify (and gives no transformation fraction to eliminate it), so the result is reported as the explicit function of t rather than a fabricated single number.

## 最终答案
\dot{y}(t)=\dfrac{dy}{dt}=n k\,t^{n-1}e^{-k t^{n}}=1\times10^{-3}\,t\,e^{-5\times10^{-4}\,t^{2}}\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}\quad(t\text{ in seconds; no single numerical value is determined because }t\text{ is not given})
