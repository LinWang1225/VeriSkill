## 题目
In an ionic-concentration corrosion cell involving chromium, which forms a trivalent ion $\left(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\right)$, an electrical current of 10 mA is measured. How many atoms per second are oxidized at the anode? Give your answer as the value of atoms_per_second.The unit of the answer is s^-1.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-missing-input-no-fabrication

## 过程
Chromium is trivalent, so the anode half-reaction is Cr → Cr³⁺ + 3e⁻: each Cr atom oxidized releases 3 electrons. The measured current is the charge per second flowing through the external circuit, I = 10 mA = 1.0×10⁻² C s⁻¹. The electron rate is I/e, and the atom-oxidation rate is that divided by 3:

atoms_per_second = I / (3 e),  with e = 1.602176634×10⁻¹⁹ C.

Numerically: 0.010 / (3 × 1.602176634×10⁻¹⁹) = 2.0805×10¹⁶ s⁻¹.

Sigfig check: the only input, 10 mA, carries 2 significant figures, so the reported value is rounded to 2 sig figs: 2.1×10¹⁶ s⁻¹.

（执行证据）
Half-reaction Cr → Cr³⁺ + 3e⁻ gives 3 electrons per atom oxidized. Using I = 10 mA and e = 1.602176634×10⁻¹⁹ C, atoms_per_second = I/(3e) = 2.0805×10¹⁶ s⁻¹. Rounded to the 2 significant figures of the input (10 mA): 2.1×10¹⁶ s⁻¹.

## 最终答案
2.1\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}
