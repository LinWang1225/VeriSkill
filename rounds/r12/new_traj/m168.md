## 题目
An AISI 1020 steel angle of dimensions $150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 12 \mathrm{~mm}(6 \mathrm{in} . \times 4 \mathrm{in} . \times$ $1 / 2 \mathrm{in}$.) is to be welded to a steel plate by fillet welds along the edges of the 150 mm leg. The angle should support a load of $270 \mathrm{kN}(60,000 \mathrm{lb})$ acting along its length. Determine the lengths of the welds to be specified. The welding electrode used is AWS-AISI E6012 with a tensile strength of $414 \mathrm{MPa}(60 \mathrm{ksi})$. Give your answer as the value of weld_length.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-weld-group-control-point
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Equal-leg angle 150×150×12; centroid from heel x̄=ȳ=(a²+at−t²)/[2(2a−t)]=41.94 mm. Balanced design (weld centroid = load line → pure shear) needs l=4·x̄=167.75 mm, which EXCEEDS the 150 mm leg → infeasible, so full leg-length welds b=d=150 mm are used and the fillet leg h is sized. Weld-group centroid=(37.5,37.5); eccentricity e=4.44 mm; M_x=M_y=F·e=1.198×10⁶ N·mm. Weld-group second moments (line model) I_x=I_y=703125 mm³. Load is axial (along member, ⊥ to weld plane), so f_z=F/L+M_x·y'/I_x+M_y·x'/I_y. Evaluated at the three corners: heel f=772.2 N/mm (direct and bending partially cancel — non-conservative end), both toes f=1027.8 N/mm (direct and bending ADD — conservative control point, per g-matsci-weld-group-control-point). Allowable throat shear τ_all=0.30·S_u=0.30·414=124.2 MPa (AWS/Shigley, E6012 electrode). Throat stress=f/(0.707 h)=τ_all → h=1027.8/(0.707·124.2)=11.70 mm. Code confirms f_max=1027.800 N/mm, h=11.705 mm.

（执行证据）
Angle centroid x̄=41.9375 mm; balanced length 167.75 mm > 150 mm leg so infeasible → full 150 mm welds. Control point at the toes (direct shear 900 N/mm + bending 127.8 N/mm add to 1027.8 N/mm; heel only 772.2 N/mm). Required fillet leg h = 1027.8/(0.707×124.2) = 11.70 mm (specify 12 mm standard).

## 最终答案
11.7 \text{ mm}
