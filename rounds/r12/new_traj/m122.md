## 题目
A structural component in the form of a wide plate is to be fabricated from a steel alloy that has a planestrain fracture toughness of $98.9 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{~m}}(90 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\text { in. }})$ and a yield strength of 860 MPa (125,000 psi). The flaw size resolution limit of the flaw detection apparatus is 3.0 mm ( 0.12 in .). If the design stress is one-half the yield strength and the value of $Y$ is 1.0, determine whether a critical flaw for this plate is subject to detection.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Fracture condition for a wide plate with through crack: K_Ic = Y·σ·√(π·a_c). Design stress σ = ½σ_y = ½(860 MPa) = 430 MPa. Solving for the critical flaw size: a_c = (1/π)(K_Ic/(Yσ))² = (1/π)(98.9/(1.0·430))² = 1.6839×10⁻² m = 16.84 mm. Inputs carry 3 sig figs (98.9, 860, 1.0, 3.0), so report 3 sig figs: 16.8 mm. Compare with flaw detection resolution limit 3.0 mm: 16.8 mm > 3.0 mm, so a critical flaw is larger than the detection limit and is therefore subject to detection. Code output: a_c = 0.0168386 m = 16.84 mm; detectable = True.

（执行证据）
Using K_Ic = Yσ√(πa_c) with σ = 430 MPa, Y = 1.0, K_Ic = 98.9 MPa√m gives a_c = 16.84 mm. Rounded to 3 sig figs (matching the least-precise input) → 16.8 mm. Because 16.8 mm exceeds the 3.0 mm detection resolution limit, the critical flaw is detectable.

## 最终答案
16.8 mm (critical flaw size; since 16.8 mm > 3.0 mm, a critical flaw is subject to detection)
