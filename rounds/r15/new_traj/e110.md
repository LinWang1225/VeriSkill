## 题目
What is the composition, in atom percent, of an alloy that consists of $4.5 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{~Pb}$ and $95.5 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$ ? The atomic weights for Pb and Sn are $207.19 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ and $118.71 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$, respectively.

Give your answer as a tuple (the atom percent of Pb, the atom precent of Sn).The units of each required answer are (at%, at%), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Atom percent is defined as at%_i = (n_i / Σ n_j) × 100, where n_i = m_i / A_i are moles of each element. 分母 = total moles (依据：atom percent 的定义为某元素摩尔数占合金总摩尔数的百分比，分母是总摩尔数，不是质量). Take a 100 g basis: m_Pb = 4.5 g, m_Sn = 95.5 g. n_Pb = 4.5/207.19 = 0.021719 mol; n_Sn = 95.5/118.71 = 0.804482 mol; total = 0.826201 mol. at%_Pb = 0.021719/0.826201 × 100 = 2.6288 at%; at%_Sn = 0.804482/0.826201 × 100 = 97.3712 at%. The two sum to 100, confirming the basis choice. Sigfig: the limiting input is 4.5 (2 sf), so the strict 2-sf report would be (2.6, 97); I retain one guard digit and report (2.63, 97.37) as the standard textbook precision.

（执行证据）
With a 100 g basis: n_Pb = 4.5/207.19 = 0.02172 mol and n_Sn = 95.5/118.71 = 0.80448 mol, total = 0.82620 mol. Dividing each by the total and multiplying by 100 gives at%_Pb = 2.6288 and at%_Sn = 97.3712, which sum to 100.

## 最终答案
(2.63\ \text{at%},\ 97.37\ \text{at%})
