## 题目
When the aluminum impurity level in a silicon bar has reached 1 ppb , what would have been the purity of the liquid on the previous pass?The unit of the answer is ppm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference

## 过程
This is a zone-refining (zone-melting) problem. During a pass of the molten zone through the silicon bar, the solid freezing out at the trailing interface is related to the liquid (molten-zone) concentration by the equilibrium segregation coefficient k = C_s / C_l. For aluminum in silicon the standard equilibrium distribution coefficient is k ≈ 0.002 (2×10⁻³). The bar's solid has reached C_s = 1 ppb Al; this solid was produced by the liquid on the previous pass, so C_l = C_s / k = 1 ppb / 0.002 = 500 ppb = 0.5 ppm. Code check: Cs=1e-9, Cl=Cs/0.002=5e-7 → 500 ppb → 0.5 ppm. Sigfig: both inputs (1 ppb, k=0.002) carry 1 significant figure, so the result is reported to 1 sig fig: 0.5 ppm.

（执行证据）
Zone-refining segregation relation C_s = k·C_l with k(Al in Si) = 0.002. Solving C_l = C_s/k = 1 ppb / 0.002 = 500 ppb = 0.5 ppm. Python confirms 5e-7 fraction = 0.5 ppm. Rounded to 1 significant figure (limited by the 1-ppb input and k=0.002).

## 最终答案
0.5\ \text{ppm}
