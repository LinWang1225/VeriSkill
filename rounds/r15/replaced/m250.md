## 题目
Calculate the electrical conductivity of nickel at $-50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and at $+500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as a tuple: (electrical_conductivity_at_minus_50C, electrical_conductivity_at_plus_500C).The units of each required answer are (ohm⁻¹·cm⁻¹, ohm⁻¹·cm⁻¹), respectively.

## 过程
Using the linear resistivity–temperature relation for nickel:

**At −50°C:** $\rho = 6.99\times10^{-8}[1+0.00641(-70)] = 3.85\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$
$$\sigma = \frac{1}{\rho} = 2.60\times10^{7}\ (\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1} = 2.60\times10^{5}\ \Omega^{-1}\text{cm}^{-1}$$

**At +500°C:** $\rho = 6.99\times10^{-8}[1+0.00641(480)] = 2.85\times10^{-7}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$
$$\sigma = \frac{1}{\rho} = 3.51\times10^{6}\ (\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1} = 3.51\times10^{4}\ \Omega^{-1}\text{cm}^{-1}$$

$$\boxed{(2.60\times10^{5},\ 3.51\times10^{4})}$$

## 最终答案
(2.60\times10^{5},\ 3.51\times10^{4})
