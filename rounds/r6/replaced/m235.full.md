## 题目
A eutectoid steel has been heated to $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 1 h , quenched to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and held for 750 s , and finally quenched to room temperature. What is the hardness?The unit of the answer is HRC.

## 过程
## Step-by-Step Analysis

### Step 1: Austenitizing (800°C, 1 h)
Since 800°C > A₁ (727°C) for eutectoid steel, the steel becomes **fully austenitized** (100% γ-austenite).

### Step 2: Isothermal Transformation (350°C, 750 s)
On the TTT diagram for eutectoid steel, 350°C lies in the **bainite region** (below the nose at ~550°C, above M_s ~220°C):

| TTT Curve at 350°C | Time |
|---|---|
| Start of transformation | ~100 s (10²) |
| 50% transformation | ~1000 s (10³) |
| 100% complete | ~10,000 s (10⁴) |

At **750 s**, we are between the **start** (~100 s) and the **50% line** (~1000 s):
- log₁₀(750) = 2.875 → 87.5% of the way from start to 50% line
- **~50% of austenite transforms to lower bainite**

### Step 3: Final Quench to Room Temperature
The remaining **~50% untransformed austenite** converts to **martensite** during the final quench.

### Step 4: Hardness Calculation

**Microstructure**: ~50% bainite + ~50% martensite

| Phase | Hardness |
|---|---|
| Bainite (at 350°C, lower bainite) | ~40 HRC |
| Martensite (eutectoid steel) | ~65 HRC |

Using the **rule of mixtures**:

$$H = 0.5 \times 40 + 0.5 \times 65 = 52.5 \approx 50 \text{ HRC}$$

$$\boxed{50}$$

## 最终答案
50
