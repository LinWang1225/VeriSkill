## 题目
Determine the planar density and packing fraction for FCC nickel in the (100), (110), and (111) planes. Which, if any, of these planes are close packed? Give your answer as a tuple: (planar_density_100, packing_fraction_100, planar_density_110, packing_fraction_110, planar_density_111, packing_fraction_111, close_packed_planes).The units of each required answer are (atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , ), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

For **FCC nickel**, the atomic radius is R = 0.125 nm, giving lattice parameter **a = 2R√2 = 0.354 nm = 3.54 × 10⁻⁸ cm**.

### (100) plane
- Square of side a; **2 atoms** (4 corners × ¼ + 1 face-center × 1).
- Area = a² → PD = 2/a²
- Packing fraction = 2πR² / a² = 2πR²/8R² = **π/4 ≈ 0.785**

### (110) plane
- Rectangle a × a√2; **2 atoms** (4 corners × ¼ + 2 edge face-centers × ½).
- Area = a²√2 → PD = 2/(a²√2)
- Packing fraction = 2πR²/(8R²√2) = **π/(4√2) ≈ 0.555**

### (111) plane
- Equilateral triangle, side a√2; **2 atoms** (3 corners × ⅙ + 3 edge-centers × ½).
- Area = (√3/2)a² → PD = 4/(√3 a²)
- Packing fraction = 2πR²/(4√3 R²) = **π/(2√3) ≈ 0.907**

The **(111)** plane has the highest packing fraction (0.907), so it is the **close-packed** plane. (100) and (110) are not close-packed.

### Numerical results
- PD₁₀₀ = 1.60 × 10¹⁵ atoms/cm², PF₁₀₀ = 0.785
- PD₁₁₀ = 1.13 × 10¹⁵ atoms/cm², PF₁₁₀ = 0.555
- PD₁₁₁ = 1.85 × 10¹⁵ atoms/cm², PF₁₁₁ = 0.907

$$\boxed{(1.60\times10^{15},\ 0.785,\ 1.13\times10^{15},\ 0.555,\ 1.85\times10^{15},\ 0.907,\ (111))}$$

## 最终答案
(1.60\times10^{15},\ 0.785,\ 1.13\times10^{15},\ 0.555,\ 1.85\times10^{15},\ 0.907,\ (111))
