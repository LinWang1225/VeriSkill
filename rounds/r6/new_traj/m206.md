## 题目
Determine the planar density and packing fraction for FCC nickel in the (100), (110), and (111) planes. Which, if any, of these planes are close packed? Give your answer as a tuple: (planar_density_100, packing_fraction_100, planar_density_110, packing_fraction_110, planar_density_111, packing_fraction_111, close_packed_planes).The units of each required answer are (atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , ), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
FCC Ni, a = 0.3524 nm (standard crystallographic value; R = a/(2√2) = 0.1246 nm). Atoms per plane: (100)→2 in area a²; (110)→2 in area a²√2; (111)→2 in area a²√3/2. Planar densities: PD100=2/a²=1.610e15, PD110=2/(a²√2)=1.139e15, PD111=4/(a²√3)=1.860e15 atoms/cm². Packing fractions (NπR²/A, R=a/(2√2)): PF100=π/4=0.7854, PF110=π/(4√2)=0.5554, PF111=π/(2√3)=0.9069. (111) reaches the close-packing maximum 0.907, so only (111) is close packed. Independent check: PFs are pure geometry (no a dependence) and match the closed forms π/4, π/(4√2), π/(2√3) exactly, confirming the atom counts/areas; (111) PF=0.9069 is the known 2D close-packed limit.

（执行证据）
With a=0.3524 nm: PD100=2/a²=1.610e15, PD110=2/(a²√2)=1.139e15, PD111=4/(a²√3)=1.860e15 atoms/cm². Packing fractions reduce to pure geometry π/4=0.7854, π/(4√2)=0.5554, π/(2√3)=0.9069, cross-checked against closed forms. Only (111) attains the close-packed limit 0.907.

## 最终答案
(1.610×10^15 atoms/cm², 0.7854, 1.139×10^15 atoms/cm², 0.5554, 1.860×10^15 atoms/cm², 0.9069, (111))
