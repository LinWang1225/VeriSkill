## 题目
Tin bronze has a composition of $89 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cu}$ and $11 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$ and consists of two phases at room temperature: an $\alpha$ phase, which is copper containing a very small amount of tin in solid solution, and an $\varepsilon$ phase, which consists of approximately $37 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$. Compute the room-temperature conductivity of this alloy given the following data:

| Phase | Electrical Resistivity $(\Omega \cdot \mathrm{m})$ | Density $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| $\alpha$ | $1.88 \times 10^{-8}$ | 8.94 |
| $\varepsilon$ | $5.32 \times 10^{-7}$ | 8.25 |The unit of the answer is (\Omega m)^-1.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-mixing-rule-select
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Lever rule with C_α≈0, C_ε=37, C₀=11 wt% Sn gives W_α=26/37=0.7027, W_ε=11/37=0.2973. Converting to volume fractions via densities: V_α=(W_α/8.94)/(W_α/8.94+W_ε/8.25)=0.6857, V_ε=0.3143. Problem specifies no phase geometry relative to current; per Callister-class convention (g-matsci-mixing-rule-select) the default for transverse conductivity is the Reuss/series model — weight resistivity by volume fraction then invert. (Data given as resistivities, not conductivities, consistent with this.) ρ_eff = V_α·ρ_α + V_ε·ρ_ε = 0.6857(1.88e-8)+0.3143(5.32e-7) = 1.801e-7 Ω·m. σ = 1/ρ_eff = 5.5518e6 (Ω·m)^-1. Self-check: series result is pulled toward the high-resistivity ε phase (below pure-Cu σ=5.32e7), correct 'series偏向高阻相' direction. Sigfig: inputs 89/11/37 wt% are 2 sig figs (n_min=2), so round final to 2 sig figs → 5.6e6 (Ω·m)^-1.

（执行证据）
Lever rule gives mass fractions W_α=0.7027, W_ε=0.2973; density conversion gives volume fractions V_α=0.6857, V_ε=0.3143. Series/Reuss mixing (resistivity-weighted, the Callister default for unspecified-geometry transverse conductivity): ρ_eff=1.801×10⁻⁷ Ω·m, so σ=5.55×10⁶ (Ω·m)⁻¹, rounded to 2 sig figs → 5.6×10⁶ (Ω·m)⁻¹.

## 最终答案
5.6 \times 10^{6}\ (\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m})^{-1}
