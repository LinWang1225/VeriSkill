## 题目
When the aluminum impurity level in a silicon bar has reached 1 ppb , what would have been the purity of the liquid on the previous pass?The unit of the answer is ppm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
This is a zone-refining/segregation problem. At the solid–liquid interface during a pass, the solidifying silicon rejects Al into the melt because Al's equilibrium segregation (distribution) coefficient in Si is k0 = C_s/C_l ≈ 0.002 (standard textbook value, k0 < 1). The solid bar concentration is related to the liquid zone concentration by C_s = k0·C_l. The bar has reached C_s = 1 ppb; the liquid on the pass that produced it was C_l = C_s/k0 = 1 ppb / 0.002 = 500 ppb. Converting: 500 ppb = 500×10⁻⁹ = 5×10⁻⁷ = 0.5×10⁻⁶ = 0.5 ppm. Sigfig: inputs '1 ppb' and k0=0.002 each have 1 significant figure, so the answer is reported to 1 sigfig → 0.5 ppm. Independent verification via absolute-fraction path (Cs=1e-9, Cl=Cs/k, Cl/1e-6) gives 0.5 ppm; limit check k→1 gives Cl=Cs as required.

（执行证据）
Using C_s = k0·C_l with k0(Al in Si) = 0.002 and C_s = 1 ppb gives C_l = 500 ppb = 0.5 ppm. Independent recomputation in absolute fractions yields 5×10⁻⁷ → 0.5 ppm, and the k→1 limit correctly reduces to C_l = C_s.

## 最终答案
0.5 ppm
