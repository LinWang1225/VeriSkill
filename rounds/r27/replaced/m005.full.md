## 题目
Given r silicon = 0.117 nm, calculate the planar density of atoms in the (111) plane of silicon.The unit of the answer is $\frac{\text{ atoms }}{\mathrm{nm}^{2}}$.

## 过程
With $r = 0.117$ nm, $a = 0.5404$ nm, giving:

$$\text{PD}_{(111)} = \frac{3}{16\sqrt{3}\,(0.117)^2} \approx 7.908 \ \frac{\text{atoms}}{\text{nm}^2}$$

$\boxed{7.908}$

## 最终答案
7.908
