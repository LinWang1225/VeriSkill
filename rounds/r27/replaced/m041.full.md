## 过程
The isostrain (parallel-loading, Voigt) rule of mixtures is E_c = ν_m E_m + ν_f E_f. From the table, the experimental modulus of C(67 vol%) in epoxy under parallel loading is E_exp = 221×10^3 MPa = 221 GPa, with ν_f = 0.67, ν_m = 0.33. Constituent moduli are sourced from the standard Callister fiber/matrix property tables: epoxy matrix E_m = 2.8 GPa; carbon fiber E_f = 400 GPa (pitch-based, high-modulus). Fiber-type selection check (Voigt upper-bound consistency, per mixing-rule skill): the rule of mixtures is the longitudinal upper bound, so E_exp ≤ ν_m E_m + ν_f E_f must hold. With PAN-based carbon (E_f = 230 GPa) the bound gives 0.33(2.8)+0.67(230) = 155 GPa < 221 GPa — the experimental value would exceed the upper bound, which is physically impossible, so PAN-based fiber is ruled out and pitch-based (E_f = 400 GPa) is required. Prediction: E_c = 0.33(2.8) + 0.67(400) = 0.924 + 268 = 268.924 GPa. Percent error of the assumption = (E_calc − E_exp)/E_exp × 100 = (268.924 − 221)/221 × 100 = 21.69% ≈ 21.7%.
