## 题目
A steel sheet that has an $R$ value of 1.75 in all directions in the sheet was stretched in biaxial tension with $\sigma_z = 0$. Strain measurements indicate that throughout the deformation, $\varepsilon_y = 0$. Find the stress ratio, $\alpha = \sigma_y / \sigma_x$, that prevailed according to the Hill yield criterion and with the nonquadratic yield criterion with $a = 8$.

## 激活技能
（未使用技能）

## 过程
Planar-isotropic sheet (R=1.75 all directions) under plane stress σ_z=0; condition ε_y=0 ⇒ associated flow rule gives ∂f/∂σ_y=0. Hill 1948 normal-anisotropy f=σ_x²+σ_y²−(2R/(R+1))σ_xσ_y ⇒ 2σ_y−(2R/(R+1))σ_x=0 ⇒ α=R/(R+1)=1.75/2.75=0.6364. Hosford nonquadratic a=8: f̄^a=(1/(1+R))[|σ_x−σ_y|^a+R|σ_x|^a+R|σ_y|^a]; ∂f/∂σ_y=0 ⇒ (σ_x−σ_y)^(a−1)=R σ_y^(a−1) ⇒ α=1/(1+R^(1/(a−1)))=1/(1+1.75^(1/7)). Python: 1.75^(1/7)=1.0832276, α=0.480024.

（执行证据）
Hill flow rule ∂f/∂σ_y=2σ_y−(2R/(R+1))σ_x=0 gives α=R/(R+1)=1.75/2.75=0.63636. Hosford a=8 flow rule gives (σ_x−σ_y)^7=R σ_y^7 ⇒ α=1/(1+1.75^(1/7)); Python evaluates 1.75^(1/7)=1.08323, so α=0.48002.

## 最终答案
\alpha_{\text{Hill}}=\dfrac{R}{R+1}=\dfrac{1.75}{2.75}=0.6364,\qquad \alpha_{\text{Hosford}}=\dfrac{1}{1+R^{1/(a-1)}}=\dfrac{1}{1+1.75^{1/7}}=0.4800
