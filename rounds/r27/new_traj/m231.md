## 题目
A 4-in.-diameter sphere of liquid copper is allowed to solidify, producing a spherical shrinkage cavity in the center of the casting. Compare the volume and diameter of the shrinkage cavity in the copper casting to that obtained when a 4 in. sphere of liquid iron is allowed to solidify. Give your answer as a tuple: (diameter_copper_in, diameter_iron_in).The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-param-lookup-consistent-source
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-final-answer-unit-dimension-check

## 过程
A central shrinkage cavity is produced by the volumetric solidification shrinkage ε; the cavity is spherical, so d_cav = d_sphere·ε^(1/3) and V_cav = ε·V_sphere. Using one consistent standard source (Callister solidification-shrinkage table): copper ε = 5.1%, iron ε = 3.0%. V_sphere = (4/3)π(2 in)^3 = 33.51 in^3. Copper: V_cav = 0.051×33.51 = 1.71 in^3, d_cav = 4·(0.051)^(1/3) = 1.483 in. Iron: V_cav = 0.030×33.51 = 1.01 in^3, d_cav = 4·(0.030)^(1/3) = 1.243 in. Sig-fig check: shrinkage values carry 2 sf; reporting 3 sf is within the tolerated textbook convention. Units: diameter in inches, matching the requested (in, in).

（执行证据）
V_sphere = 33.51 in^3. With ε_Cu = 5.1%: d_cav = 4·(0.051)^(1/3) = 1.483 in. With ε_Fe = 3.0%: d_cav = 4·(0.030)^(1/3) = 1.243 in. Cavity volumes are 1.71 in^3 (Cu) and 1.01 in^3 (Fe).

## 最终答案
(1.48, 1.24)
