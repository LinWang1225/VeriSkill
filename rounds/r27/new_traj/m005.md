## 题目
Given r silicon = 0.117 nm, calculate the planar density of atoms in the (111) plane of silicon.The unit of the answer is $\frac{\text{ atoms }}{\mathrm{nm}^{2}}$.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Silicon is diamond cubic (Z=8), two interpenetrating FCC sublattices offset by (¼,¼,¼). Nearest neighbors touch along ⟨111⟩: 2r = (√3/4)a ⇒ a = 8r/√3 = 8(0.117)/√3 = 0.5404 nm. The (111) plane cuts the conventional cell as an equilateral triangle (vertices on the axes), area (√3/2)a². Counting atoms with centers in the plane x+y+z=a: FCC sublattice gives 3 corners×(1/6)+3 edge midpoints×(1/2)=2 atoms; the second sublattice (sum=¾a or 7⁄4a) contributes 0 to this plane. So PD = 2/[(√3/2)a²] = 4/(√3 a²) = 3/(16√3 r²). With r=0.117 nm: PD = 3/(16·1.73205·0.013689) = 7.908 atoms/nm². Input has 3 sig figs ⇒ round to 7.91. Cross-check: with a=0.543 nm this gives 7.84×10¹⁴ cm⁻², the known Si(111) surface density.

（执行证据）
a = 8r/√3 = 0.5404 nm from the diamond-cubic touching condition 2r=√3a/4. The (111) triangle area is (√3/2)a² and contains 2 atoms (3 corners×1/6 + 3 edge-midpoints×1/2; the (¼,¼,¼) sublattice lies off this plane). PD = 4/(√3 a²) = 3/(16√3 r²) = 7.908 → 7.91 (3 sig figs).

## 最终答案
7.91
