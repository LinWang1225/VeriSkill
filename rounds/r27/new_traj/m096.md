## 题目
Carbon is allowed to diffuse through a steel plate 10 mm thick.  The concentrations of carbon at the two faces are 0.85 and 0.40 kg C/m³ Fe (maintained constant).  If the diffusion pre-exponential factor and activation energy are D₀ = 5.0×10⁻⁷ m²/s and Q = 77 000 J/mol, respectively, compute the temperature at which the diffusion flux is J = 6.3×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m²·s).The unit of the answer is K.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-arrhenius-paired-d0-q
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Steady-state Fick's first law: J = D·ΔC/Δx → D = J·Δx/ΔC = (6.3×10⁻¹⁰)(0.01)/0.45 = 1.40×10⁻¹¹ m²/s. Arrhenius D = D₀·exp(−Q/RT) inverted: T = Q/[R·ln(D₀/D)]. D₀/D = 5.0×10⁻⁷/1.40×10⁻¹¹ = 3.5714×10⁴, ln = 10.4833. T = 77000/(8.314×10.4833) = 883.45 K. Skill checks: D₀ and Q both given in-problem (paired by construction); D₀=5.0×10⁻⁷ within interstitial-diffusion range; T≈610 °C lies in BCC ferrite stability range, consistent with Q≈77 kJ/mol for C in BCC Fe. Sigfigs: weakest inputs 2 sig figs, +1 textbook tolerance → report 883 K.

（执行证据）
D = J·Δx/ΔC = 1.40×10⁻¹¹ m²/s; ln(D₀/D) = ln(3.5714×10⁴) = 10.4833; T = 77000/(8.314×10.4833) = 883.45 K → 883 K. Phase check: 610 °C is in ferrite range, consistent with the given Q.

## 最终答案
883
