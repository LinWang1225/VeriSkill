## 题目
(a) Calculate the saturation magnetization ($\mu_{0} M_{\mathrm{s}}$) for $\mathrm{Fe}_{3} \mathrm{O}_{4}$ if the lattice constant ($a_{0}$) of the larger unit cell, which consists of eight smaller unit cells, is $8.37 \AA$.The unit of the answer is T.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Fe3O4 (magnetite) is an inverse spinel: [Fe3+]_tet[Fe2+Fe3+]_oct O4. The Fe3+ moments on tetrahedral (A) and octahedral (B) sublattices are antiparallel and cancel, leaving only the Fe2+ moment = 4 μ_B per formula unit. The 'larger unit cell consisting of eight smaller unit cells' is the conventional spinel cell with Z = 8 formula units (spinel AB2O4: Z=8). Net moment per cell = 8 × 4 μ_B = 32 μ_B. Saturation magnetization M_s = (net moment)/V with V = a0^3, and μ0 M_s = μ0·(32 μ_B)/a0^3. Using μ0 = 4π×10^-7 T·m/A, μ_B = 9.274×10^-24 A·m^2, a0 = 8.37×10^-10 m: μ0 M_s = 0.63599 T. Input a0 = 8.37 Å has 3 sig figs, so round to 3 sig figs → 0.636.

（执行证据）
Inverse-spinel sublattice cancellation leaves 4 μ_B per Fe3O4 formula unit (Fe2+ only). Conventional spinel cell has Z=8 formula units, so net cell moment = 32 μ_B. Computing μ0·(32 μ_B)/a0^3 with a0=8.37 Å gives 0.63599 T, rounded to 3 sig figs (matching the 3 sig figs of a0) = 0.636.

## 最终答案
0.636
