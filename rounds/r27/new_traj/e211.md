## 题目
The yield strength of mild steel with an average grain size of 0.05 mm is $20,000 \mathrm{psi}$. The yield stress of the same steel with a grain size of 0.007 mm is $40,000 \mathrm{psi}$. What will be the average grain size of the same steel with a yield stress of $30,000 \mathrm{psi}$? Assume the Hall-Petch equation is valid and that changes in the observed yield stress are due to changes in grain size.The unit of the answer is nm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
Hall-Petch: σ_y = σ_0 + k·d^(-1/2), linear in x = d^(-1/2). Given (d1=0.05 mm, σ1=20000 psi) and (d2=0.007 mm, σ2=40000 psi). Since σ3=30000 psi is exactly the midpoint of σ1 and σ2, by linearity x3 = (x1+x2)/2. x1 = 1/√0.05 = 4.4721, x2 = 1/√0.007 = 11.9523, x3 = 8.2122. d3 = 1/x3² = 0.014828 mm = 14828 nm. Verified by solving k=(σ2-σ1)/(x2-x1)=2673.7, σ0=8042.7, giving σ0+k·x3=30000.0 psi exactly.

（执行证据）
Computed x1=4.4721, x2=11.9523, midpoint x3=8.2122, giving d3=1/x3²=0.014828 mm = 14828 nm. Back-substitution through the fitted Hall-Petch constants (σ0≈8042.7 psi, k≈2673.7 psi·mm^0.5) reproduces σ3=30000 psi exactly, confirming the linearity-in-d^(-1/2) interpolation.

## 最终答案
14828
