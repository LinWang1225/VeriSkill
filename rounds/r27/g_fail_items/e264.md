## 题目
The atomic radii of \(\mathrm{K}^{+}\) and \(\mathrm{Br}^{-}\) ions are 0.138 and 0.196 nm , respectively.
(a) Using Equations 2.9 and 2.10, calculate the force of attraction between these two ions at their equilibrium interionic separation (i.e., when the ions just touch one another).
(b) What is the force of repulsion at this same separation distance?
Give your answer as a tuple (force of attraction, force of repulsion).The units of each required answer are (N, N), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-named-force-magnitude-report
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
r0 = 0.138 nm + 0.196 nm = 0.334 nm = 3.34e-10 m. Attractive force magnitude from Eq. 2.9 (Coulomb): |F_A| = Z1*Z2*e^2/(4*pi*eps0*r0^2) with Z1=Z2=1, e=1.602176634e-19 C, eps0=8.854187817e-12 F/m. Python: |F| = 2.0681e-9 N. At equilibrium separation net force = 0, so Eq. 2.10 repulsive force has equal magnitude: |F_R| = |F_A|. Inputs 0.138 and 0.196 each have 3 significant figures, so round to 3 sf -> 2.07e-9 N. Named-force skill: report magnitudes (non-negative) for 'force of attraction' and 'force of repulsion'; both equal.

（执行证据）
r0 = 3.34e-10 m from sum of ionic radii. Coulomb magnitude |F| = e^2/(4*pi*eps0*r0^2) = 2.0681e-9 N, rounded to 3 sf = 2.07e-9 N. At equilibrium F_A + F_R = 0, so attraction and repulsion have equal magnitude; named-force convention reports both as non-negative magnitudes.

## 最终答案
(2.07\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{N},\ 2.07\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{N})
