## 题目
Yttria $\left(\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3\right)$ particles $750 \AA$ in diameter are introduced into tungsten by internal oxidation. Measurements using an electron microscope show that there are $5 \times 10^{14}$ oxide particles per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the $\mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Y}$ originally in the alloy. The density of $\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is $5.01 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of wt_percent_Y.The unit of the answer is %.

## 激活技能

## 过程
Consider 1 cm³ of the final internally-oxidized material. Particle diameter d = 750 Å = 750×10⁻⁸ cm = 7.5×10⁻⁶ cm, so r = 3.75×10⁻⁶ cm. Volume of one Y₂O₃ particle: V_p = (4/3)πr³ = 2.209×10⁻¹⁶ cm³. With N = 5×10¹⁴ particles/cm³, the total particle volume fraction is f_v = N·V_p = 0.1104 cm³ per cm³ of material. The Y₂O₃ mass in this 1 cm³ is m_Y2O3 = f_v·ρ_Y2O3 = 0.1104×5.01 = 0.5533 g. The mass fraction of Y in Y₂O₃ is 2M_Y/(2M_Y+3M_O) = 2(88.906)/225.81 = 0.7874, so m_Y = 0.5533×0.7874 = 0.4357 g. The W matrix occupies the remaining volume (1 − f_v) = 0.8896 cm³ (particles displace W; using the full 1 cm³ for W would double-count volume). Using the standard density of tungsten ρ_W = 19.3 g/cm³ (recognized constant, since the problem does not state it), m_W = (1−f_v)·ρ_W = 0.8896×19.3 = 17.168 g. The original alloy was W + Y only (oxygen entered during oxidation), so the percent basis for 'wt% Y originally' is the original alloy mass m_W + m_Y (not the oxidized mass m_W + m_Y2O3). Thus wt% Y = m_Y/(m_W + m_Y)×100 = 0.4357/(17.168+0.4357)×100 = 2.475%.

（执行证据）
Particle volume fraction f_v = 5×10¹⁴·(4/3)π(3.75×10⁻⁶)³ = 0.1104. Y mass per cm³ = 0.1104×5.01×(2·88.906/225.81) = 0.4357 g; W mass per cm³ = (1−0.1104)×19.3 = 17.168 g. wt% Y originally = 0.4357/(17.168+0.4357)×100 = 2.475% ≈ 2.48%.

## 最终答案
2.48
