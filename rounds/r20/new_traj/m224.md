## 题目
(a) A 0.4-in.-diameter, 12-in.-long titanium bar has a yield strength of $50,000 \mathrm{psi}$, a modulus of elasticity of $16 \times 10^6 \mathrm{psi}$, and a Poisson's ratio of 0.30. Determine the length and diameter of the bar when a $500-\mathrm{lb}$ load is applied. (b) When a tensile load is applied to a 1.5-cm-diameter copper bar, the diameter is reduced to $1.498-\mathrm{cm}$ diameter. Determine the applied load, using the data in Table. Give your answer as a tuple: (final_length_in, final_diameter_in).

Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline TABLE 6-3 & \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{Elastic properties and melting temperature ( $T_m$ ) of selected materials} \\
\hline Material & $T_m\left({ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$ & $E$ (psi) & Poisson's ratio ( $v$ ) \\
\hline Pb & 327 & $2.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.45 \\
\hline Mg & 650 & $6.5 \times 10^6$ & 0.29 \\
\hline Al & 660 & $10.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.33 \\
\hline Cu & 1085 & $18.1 \times 10^6$ & 0.36 \\
\hline Fe & 1538 & $30.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.27 \\
\hline W & 3410 & $59.2 \times 10^6$ & 0.28 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ & 2020 & $55.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.26 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Si}_3 \mathrm{~N}_4$ & & $44.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.24 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 过程
Part (a): A = π(0.4/2)² = 0.12566 in²; σ = F/A = 500/0.12566 = 3978.9 psi. Check elastic range: σ = 3979 psi ≪ σ_y = 50,000 psi → Hooke's law valid (g-sci-model-validity-range: inside elastic limit, no model switch). Axial strain ε_z = σ/E = 3978.9/(16×10⁶) = 2.4868×10⁻⁴. Lateral strain ε_x = −ν ε_z = −0.30×2.4868×10⁻⁴ = −7.460×10⁻⁵. Final length L_f = L₀(1+ε_z) = 12(1+2.4868×10⁻⁴) = 12.002984 in. Final diameter d_f = d₀(1+ε_x) = 0.4(1−7.460×10⁻⁵) = 0.399970 in. Part (b) (consistency check, not requested in tuple): Cu lateral strain = (1.498−1.5)/1.5 = −1.3333×10⁻³; ε_z = 1.3333×10⁻³/0.36 = 3.704×10⁻³; σ = 18.1×10⁶×3.704×10⁻³ = 67,037 psi; A = π(1.5/2.54/2)² = 0.27391 in²; F = 18,362 lb (consistent with elastic Cu). Units kept in psi/in throughout (g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion). Code output: Lf=12.002984, df=0.399970.

（执行证据）
σ = 3979 psi < 50,000 psi yield ⇒ elastic; ε_z = 2.4868×10⁻⁴, ε_x = −7.460×10⁻⁵. Computed L_f = 12.002984 in and d_f = 0.399970 in, rounded to (12.003, 0.39997) in.

## 最终答案
