## 题目
Suppose a 24 -in.-long round bar is to support a load of 400 lb without any permanent deformation. Calculate the minimum diameter of the bar if it is made from (a) AZ80A-T5 magnesium alloy and (b) 6061-T6 aluminum alloy. Calculate the weight of the bar and the approximate cost (based on pure Mg and $\mathrm{Al})$ in each case. Give your answer as a tuple: (min_diameter_AZ80A_in, weight_AZ80A_lb, cost_AZ80A_USD, min_diameter_6061_in, weight_6061_lb, cost_6061_USD).The units of each required answer are (in, lb, USD, in, lb, USD), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-grade-designation-not-source
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
No-permanent-deformation criterion => design stress = yield strength. d_min = sqrt(4F/(pi*sigma_y)); W = (F/sigma_y)*L*rho; cost = W * pure-metal price. Property lookups from Callister Appendix B (US customary): AZ80A-T5 Mg sigma_y = 275 MPa = 39,885 psi -> 40,000 psi (40 ksi), rho = 0.066 lb/in^3; 6061-T6 Al sigma_y = 276 MPa = 40,030 psi -> 40,000 psi (40 ksi), rho = 0.0975 lb/in^3. Pure-metal prices from Callister price table: Mg $3.00/lb, Al $1.30/lb. Grade numbers used only to select the table row, not as answer sources. Code: A=400/40000=0.0100 in^2; d=sqrt(4*0.01/pi)=0.112838 in; W_Mg=0.01*24*0.066=0.01584 lb, cost=0.04752; W_Al=0.01*24*0.0975=0.02340 lb, cost=0.03042. Reported to 3 s.f.

（执行证据）
Both alloys tabulate to 40,000 psi yield strength (AZ80A-T5: 275 MPa=39,885 psi; 6061-T6: 276 MPa=40,030 psi), giving identical minimum diameter d=sqrt(4*400/(pi*40000))=0.1128 in and area 0.0100 in^2. Mg bar weight 0.01*24*0.066=0.01584 lb costs 0.01584*3.00=$0.0475; Al bar weight 0.01*24*0.0975=0.02340 lb costs 0.02340*1.30=$0.0304. Mg is lighter but more costly per pound, so the Al bar is cheaper overall.

## 最终答案
(0.113\ \text{in},\ 0.0158\ \text{lb},\ 0.0475\ \text{USD},\ 0.113\ \text{in},\ 0.0234\ \text{lb},\ 0.0304\ \text{USD})
