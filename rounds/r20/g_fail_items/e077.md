## 题目
Magnesium (Mg) has an HCP crystal structure and a density of $1.74 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$. For HCP, $n=6$ \frac{atoms}{unit} cell, and the atomic weight for $\mathrm{Mg}, A{\mathrm{Mg}}=24.31 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.
(a) What is the volume of its unit cell in cubic centimeters?
(b) If the c/a ratio is 1.624 , compute the values of $c$.

Give your answer as a tuple (volume, the values of $c$).The units of each required answer are (cm^3/unit cell, nm), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

### (a) Volume of the unit cell

Using the density relation:

$$\rho = \frac{n \cdot A}{V \cdot N_A} \implies V = \frac{n \cdot A}{\rho \cdot N_A}$$

$$V = \frac{6 \times 24.31}{1.74 \times 6.022\times10^{23}} \approx 1.392 \times 10^{-22} \ \text{cm}^3/\text{unit cell}$$

### (b) Value of $c$

For HCP, the unit cell volume is:

$$V = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2} a^2 c$$

With $c/a = 1.624$, so $c = 1.624\,a$:

$$V = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}(1.624)\,a^3 \implies a = \left[\frac{V}{\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}(1.624)}\right]^{1/3}$$

This gives $a \approx 3.208 \times 10^{-8}$ cm $= 0.3209$ nm, and:

$$c = 1.624\,a \approx 0.5573 \ \text{nm}$$

$$\boxed{(1.392 \times 10^{-22},\ 0.5573)}$$

## 最终答案
(1.392 \times 10^{-22},\ 0.5573)
