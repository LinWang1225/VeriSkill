## 题目
Yttria $\left(\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3\right)$ particles $750 \AA$ in diameter are introduced into tungsten by internal oxidation. Measurements using an electron microscope show that there are $5 \times 10^{14}$ oxide particles per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the $\mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Y}$ originally in the alloy. The density of $\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is $5.01 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of wt_percent_Y.The unit of the answer is %.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Consider 1 cm^3 of the internally oxidized sample. Each Y2O3 particle is a sphere of diameter d = 750 Å = 7.5e-6 cm, radius r = 3.75e-6 cm, volume V_p = (4/3)πr^3 = 2.209e-16 cm^3. With N = 5e14 particles/cm^3, the total oxide volume is V_ox = N·V_p = 0.1104 cm^3 per cm^3 of sample (an 11% volume fraction — NOT negligible, so the W matrix volume is 1 − V_ox, not 1). Mass of oxide = V_ox·ρ_oxide = 0.1104×5.01 = 0.5533 g. Yttrium is conserved during oxidation, so mass of Y = mass of oxide × (2M_Y/M_Y2O3) with M_Y = 88.906, M_O = 15.999, M_Y2O3 = 225.82, giving m_Y = 0.5533×(177.81/225.82) = 0.4357 g. The tungsten matrix occupies the remaining volume (1 − 0.1104) cm^3; using ρ_W = 19.3 g/cm^3 (CRC Handbook standard room-temperature value, looked up since not given), m_W = 0.8896×19.3 = 17.17 g. The original alloy (before oxidation) contained only W + Y (no oxygen), so by the wt% definition the denominator is the original alloy mass = m_W + m_Y = 17.17 + 0.436 = 17.61 g, not the full 1 cm^3×ρ_W (which would double-count the particle volume as tungsten). Thus wt% Y = m_Y/(m_W + m_Y)×100 = 0.4357/17.604×100 = 2.475%. Code output confirms 2.4751.

（执行证据）
Particle volume fraction N·(π/6)d^3 = 0.1104 (11%, so W volume is 1−V_ox, not 1). Mass Y conserved from oxide = 0.4357 g; mass W = (1−0.1104)×19.3 = 17.17 g. wt% Y = 0.4357/(17.17+0.4357)×100 = 2.475 ≈ 2.48%.

## 最终答案
2.48
