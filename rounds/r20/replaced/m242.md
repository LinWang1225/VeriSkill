## 题目
Suppose a 24 -in.-long round bar is to support a load of 400 lb without any permanent deformation. Calculate the minimum diameter of the bar if it is made from (a) AZ80A-T5 magnesium alloy and (b) 6061-T6 aluminum alloy. Calculate the weight of the bar and the approximate cost (based on pure Mg and $\mathrm{Al})$ in each case. Give your answer as a tuple: (min_diameter_AZ80A_in, weight_AZ80A_lb, cost_AZ80A_USD, min_diameter_6061_in, weight_6061_lb, cost_6061_USD).The units of each required answer are (in, lb, USD, in, lb, USD), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Design criterion:** To avoid permanent deformation, the stress must not exceed the yield strength:

$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{F}{\pi d^2/4} \le \sigma_y \;\Rightarrow\; d_{\min} = \sqrt{\frac{4F}{\pi\,\sigma_y}}$$

**Material properties (standard textbook values):**

| Alloy | Yield strength | Density |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| AZ80A-T5 Mg | 275 MPa ≈ 39,885 psi | 1.74 g/cm³ (0.0629 lb/in³) |
| 6061-T6 Al | 276 MPa ≈ 40,030 psi | 2.70 g/cm³ (0.0975 lb/in³) |

**Minimum diameters:**
- AZ80A-T5: $d = \sqrt{4(400)/(\pi\cdot39885)} \approx 0.1130$ in
- 6061-T6: $d = \sqrt{4(400)/(\pi\cdot40030)} \approx 0.1128$ in

Both alloys have nearly the same yield strength, so the diameters are nearly identical — the real comparison is weight and cost.

**Weight** ($W = \rho\, \pi d^2/4 \cdot L$, using pure-metal densities):
- Mg: $0.0629 \times \pi(0.1130)^2/4 \times 24 \approx 0.0151$ lb
- Al: $0.0975 \times \pi(0.1128)^2/4 \times 24 \approx 0.0234$ lb

**Cost** (approximate pure-metal prices: Mg ≈ $3.00/lb, Al ≈ $1.00/lb):
- Mg: $0.0151 \times 3.00 \approx \$0.0454$
- Al: $0.0234 \times 1.00 \approx \$0.0234$

**Design takeaway:** The magnesium bar is ~35% lighter than the aluminum bar but costs roughly twice as much — the classic weight-vs.-cost trade-off.

$$\boxed{(0.113,\;0.0151,\;0.0454,\;0.1128,\;0.0234,\;0.0234)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.113,\;0.0151,\;0.0454,\;0.1128,\;0.0234,\;0.0234)
