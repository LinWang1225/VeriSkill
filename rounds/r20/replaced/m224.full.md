## 题目
(a) A 0.4-in.-diameter, 12-in.-long titanium bar has a yield strength of $50,000 \mathrm{psi}$, a modulus of elasticity of $16 \times 10^6 \mathrm{psi}$, and a Poisson's ratio of 0.30. Determine the length and diameter of the bar when a $500-\mathrm{lb}$ load is applied. (b) When a tensile load is applied to a 1.5-cm-diameter copper bar, the diameter is reduced to $1.498-\mathrm{cm}$ diameter. Determine the applied load, using the data in Table. Give your answer as a tuple: (final_length_in, final_diameter_in).

Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline TABLE 6-3 & \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{Elastic properties and melting temperature ( $T_m$ ) of selected materials} \\
\hline Material & $T_m\left({ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$ & $E$ (psi) & Poisson's ratio ( $v$ ) \\
\hline Pb & 327 & $2.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.45 \\
\hline Mg & 650 & $6.5 \times 10^6$ & 0.29 \\
\hline Al & 660 & $10.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.33 \\
\hline Cu & 1085 & $18.1 \times 10^6$ & 0.36 \\
\hline Fe & 1538 & $30.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.27 \\
\hline W & 3410 & $59.2 \times 10^6$ & 0.28 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ & 2020 & $55.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.26 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Si}_3 \mathrm{~N}_4$ & & $44.0 \times 10^6$ & 0.24 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (in, in), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

### Part (a) — Titanium bar under 500 lb load

**Geometry & stress:**
- Cross-sectional area: $A_0 = \pi(0.2)^2 = 0.1257\ \text{in}^2$
- Applied stress: $\sigma = P/A_0 = 500/0.1257 = 3978.9\ \text{psi}$

Since $\sigma = 3978.9\ \text{psi} \ll \sigma_y = 50{,}000\ \text{psi}$, the deformation is **elastic**.

**Strains:**
- Axial: $\varepsilon = \sigma/E = 3978.9/(16\times10^6) = 2.487\times10^{-4}$
- Lateral: $\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = -\nu\varepsilon = -0.30(2.487\times10^{-4}) = -7.46\times10^{-5}$

**Final dimensions:**
- $L_f = L_0(1+\varepsilon) = 12(1+2.487\times10^{-4}) = 12.0030\ \text{in}$
- $d_f = d_0(1+\varepsilon_{\text{lat}}) = 0.4(1-7.46\times10^{-5}) = 0.39997\ \text{in}$

### Part (b) — Copper bar, load determination

From Table 6-3 for Cu: $E = 18.1\times10^6\ \text{psi}$, $\nu = 0.36$.

- Lateral strain: $\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = (1.498-1.500)/1.500 = -1.333\times10^{-3}$
- Axial strain: $\varepsilon = -\varepsilon_{\text{lat}}/\nu = 1.333\times10^{-3}/0.36 = 3.704\times10^{-3}$
- Stress: $\sigma = E\varepsilon = (18.1\times10^6)(3.704\times10^{-3}) = 67{,}037\ \text{psi}$
- Area in in²: $A = \pi(0.75\ \text{cm})^2(0.3937\ \text{in/cm})^2 = 0.2739\ \text{in}^2$
- Load: $P = \sigma A = 67{,}037 \times 0.2739 \approx 18{,}360\ \text{lb}$

The requested tuple is the final length and diameter from part (a):

$$\boxed{(12.0030,\ 0.39997)}$$

## 最终答案
(12.0030,\ 0.39997)
