## 题目
If the atomic radius of lead is 0.175 nm, calculate the volume of its unit cell in cubic meters.The unit of the answer is m^3.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Lead is FCC (face-centered cubic) at ambient conditions. In FCC the atoms touch along the face diagonal: face diagonal = 4R = a√2, so a = 2√2·R. With R = 0.175 nm = 1.75e-10 m: a = 2√2·1.75e-10 = 4.9497e-10 m. Unit-cell volume V = a^3 = 1.2127e-28 m^3. The unit-cell-formula-unit-scaling skill was checked: the question asks for the unit-cell volume directly (a geometric quantity of the whole cell), not a per-formula-unit quantity, so no Z scaling applies. Sigfig skill: input 0.175 has 3 sig figs, so report 3 sig figs → 1.21e-28 m^3. Python check: a=4.949747468e-10, V=1.2126881297e-28.

（执行证据）
Lead is FCC, so a = 2√2·R. With R = 0.175 nm, a = 4.9497e-10 m and V = a^3 = 1.2127e-28 m^3 (Python-verified). Rounded to the 3 sig figs of the input: 1.21×10⁻²⁸ m³.

## 最终答案
1.21 \times 10^{-28}\ \text{m}^3
