## 题目
What temperature is required to obtain $0.50 \% \mathrm{C}$ at a distance of 0.5 mm beneath the surface of a $0.20 \%$ C steel in 2 h , when $1.10 \% \mathrm{C}$ is present at the surface? Assume that the iron is FCC.The unit of the answer is °C.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-chart-lookup-read
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-model-validity-range
- g-sci-independent-verify
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Carburization of a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration obeys Fick's second law: (C_x - C_0)/(C_s - C_0) = 1 - erf(x/(2√(Dt))). With C_x=0.50%, C_0=0.20%, C_s=1.10%, x=0.5 mm=5e-4 m, t=2 h=7200 s: LHS=(0.50-0.20)/(1.10-0.20)=0.3333, so erf(z)=0.6667. Inverting gives z=0.6841, hence D = x²/(4 z² t) = 1.855e-11 m²/s. For C in FCC iron (Callister, 9th ed.): D0=2.3e-5 m²/s, Q=148000 J/mol, R=8.31 J/(mol·K). From D=D0·exp(-Q/RT): T = Q/[R·ln(D0/D)] = 1269 K = 996 °C. Validity: 1269 K is within the austenite (FCC) range where these Arrhenius parameters apply. Independent recheck using the published erf table (erf(0.68)=0.6638, erf(0.69)=0.6702) with linear interpolation gives z=0.6845 → T=996 °C, confirming the result.

（执行证据）
LHS=(0.50−0.20)/(1.10−0.20)=0.3333 → erf(z)=0.6667 → z=0.6841 → D=1.86e-11 m²/s. Using D0=2.3e-5 m²/s and Q=148000 J/mol for C in FCC iron: T=Q/[R·ln(D0/D)]=1269 K=996 °C. An independent path via the tabulated erf table (interpolated z=0.6845) gives 996 °C, agreeing to the nearest degree.

## 最终答案
996\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}
