## 题目
An AISI 1020 steel angle of dimensions $150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 150 \mathrm{~mm} \times 12 \mathrm{~mm}(6 \mathrm{in} . \times 4 \mathrm{in} . \times$ $1 / 2 \mathrm{in}$.) is to be welded to a steel plate by fillet welds along the edges of the 150 mm leg. The angle should support a load of $270 \mathrm{kN}(60,000 \mathrm{lb})$ acting along its length. Determine the lengths of the welds to be specified. The welding electrode used is AWS-AISI E6012 with a tensile strength of $414 \mathrm{MPa}(60 \mathrm{ksi})$. Give your answer as the value of weld_length.The unit of the answer is mm.

## 过程
## Solution

**Geometry & centroid.** For the equal-leg angle L150×150×12, placing the root (heel) corner at the origin, the centroid is at
$$\bar{x}_c=\frac{(150)(12)(75)+(138)(12)(6)}{3456}=41.94\text{ mm from the heel}$$

The 270 kN load acts axially through this centroid. The two fillet welds run along the length at the heel (x=0) and toe (x=150).

**Centroid alignment (no torsion).** To keep the welds in pure direct shear, the weld-group centroid must coincide with the load line:
$$\frac{150\,l_1}{l_1+l_2}=41.94\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{l_1}{l_2}=\frac{41.94}{108.06}=0.388$$

**Strength.** With weld leg size = thickness = 12 mm and allowable throat shear for E6012 (AISC ASD):
$$\tau_{\text{allow}}=0.3\,S_u=0.3(414)=124.2\text{ MPa}$$
$$F/\ell = 0.707\,h\,\tau_{\text{allow}}=0.707(12)(124.2)=1053.7\text{ N/mm}$$

$$L_{\text{total}}=\frac{270\,000}{1053.7}=256.2\text{ mm}$$

**Distribution:**
- $l_1$ (toe) = 71.6 mm
- $l_2$ (heel) = 184.6 mm

The total weld length to be specified is 256.2 mm.

$$\boxed{256.2}$$

## 最终答案
256.2
