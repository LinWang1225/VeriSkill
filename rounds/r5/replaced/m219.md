## 题目
What temperature is required to obtain $0.50 \% \mathrm{C}$ at a distance of 0.5 mm beneath the surface of a $0.20 \%$ C steel in 2 h , when $1.10 \% \mathrm{C}$ is present at the surface? Assume that the iron is FCC.The unit of the answer is °C.

## 过程
**Solution steps:**

1. **Compute the concentration ratio:**
$$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = \frac{0.50 - 0.20}{1.10 - 0.20} = \frac{0.30}{0.90} = 0.333$$

2. **Find the error function argument:**
$$1 - \text{erf}(z) = 0.333 \implies \text{erf}(z) = 0.667 \implies z \approx 0.684$$

3. **Solve for the diffusion coefficient D:**
$$D = \frac{x^2}{4 z^2 t} = \frac{(0.5\times10^{-3})^2}{4(0.684)^2(7200)} \approx 1.86 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

4. **Use the temperature-dependent diffusivity for C in FCC iron** ($D_0 = 2.0\times10^{-5}$ m²/s, $Q_d = 142$ kJ/mol):
$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right) \implies T = \frac{Q_d}{R\ln(D_0/D)}$$

$$T \approx 1229.6\ \text{K} \approx 956°\text{C}$$

$$\boxed{956}$$

## 最终答案
956
