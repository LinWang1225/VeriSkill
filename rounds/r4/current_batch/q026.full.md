## 题目
Between the third Thursday and fourth Wednesday in Jan 1939, what was the net total capital inflow or outflow in thousands of dollars between the US and Latin America?

## 激活技能

## 过程
STEP 1 — Determine date range
Third Thursday of January 1939: Jan 19, 1939
Fourth Wednesday of January 1939: Jan 25, 1939
Between = Jan 19 (Thu) to Jan 25 (Wed).
Verified via Python datetime: Jan 1, 1939 = Sunday → Thursdays: [5,12,19,26]; Wednesdays: [4,11,18,25].

STEP 2 — Locate correct Treasury Bulletin edition
Per skill (g-officeqa-locate-source): bulletin issued in month M contains data up to M-1.
Data for January 1939 → look at Feb 1939, Mar 1939, or Apr 1939 bulletins.
Feb 1939 bulletin (line 133): "statistics for November 1938" → too early.
Mar 1939 bulletin: tables are "for December 1938" → close but not Jan.
Apr 1939 bulletin (grep): "Security Transactions ... for January 1939" (line 1381), "Short-Term Foreign Liabilities ... for January 1939" (line 1249) → correct edition.

STEP 3 — Locate the Net Capital Movement table
In Apr 1939 bulletin, page 26 (line 1172):
"Net Capital Movement between the United States and Foreign Countries 1935 through January 1939"
Table header (line 1178): "Classification and period by reporting dates | United Kingdom | France | ... | Latin America | ..."
Column definition: "Latin America (including Mexico, Central and South America, and the West Indies)" (Feb 1939 bulletin, line 137).
Unit: "In thousands of dollars. Capital inflow or capital outflow (-)" (line 1176).

STEP 4 — Extract weekly data for Latin America
The table has four components; NET CAPITAL MOVEMENT = their sum.
