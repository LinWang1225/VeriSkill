## 题目
What was the amount spent in millions of nominal dollars by the highest spending U.S Federal Department in the fiscal year of 1955?

## 过程
（模型自述的取数与计算过程）
The highest spending U.S. Federal Department in fiscal year 1955 was the Department of Defense. Its total spending is the sum of all military functions from Table 3 of the December 1955 Treasury Bulletin: Central defense activities ($490M) + Air Force military functions ($16,647M) + Army military functions ($8,879M) + Navy military functions ($9,714M) = $35,730 million. Footnote 27 confirms that the military functions cover "all military functions of the Department of Defense." The broader "National security" category ($40,989M) includes non-departmental items such as the Atomic Energy Commission ($1,856M), mutual security military assistance ($1,895M), strategic materials ($802M), and other items ($706M) that are not part of the Department of Defense.
