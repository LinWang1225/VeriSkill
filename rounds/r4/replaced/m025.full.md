## 题目
A copper penny has a mass of 2.60 g . Assuming pure copper, how much of this mass is contributed by (a) the neutrons in the copper nuclei and (b) electrons? Give your answer as a tuple: (neutron_mass, electron_mass).The units of each required answer are (g, g), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Copper properties:** atomic number $Z = 29$, atomic weight $\approx 63.55\ \text{g/mol}$ (natural copper).

**Step 1 — Number of atoms in the penny:**
$$n = \frac{2.60}{63.55} = 0.0409\ \text{mol}$$
$$N = 0.0409 \times 6.022\times10^{23} \approx 2.46\times10^{22}\ \text{atoms}$$

**Step 2 — Neutrons:** Average neutrons per atom $= 63.55 - 29 = 34.55$.
$$N_n = 2.46\times10^{22} \times 34.55 \approx 8.51\times10^{23}\ \text{neutrons}$$
$$m_n = 8.51\times10^{23} \times 1.675\times10^{-24}\ \text{g} \approx 1.43\ \text{g}$$

**Step 3 — Electrons:** 29 electrons per atom.
$$N_e = 2.46\times10^{22} \times 29 \approx 7.14\times10^{23}\ \text{electrons}$$
$$m_e = 7.14\times10^{23} \times 9.11\times10^{-31}\ \text{g} \approx 6.5\times10^{-7}\ \text{g}$$

The neutron contribution dominates (≈1.43 g of the 2.60 g), while the electron contribution is negligible (~$10^{-7}$ g), consistent with electrons being ~1/1836 the mass of a nucleon.

$$\boxed{(1.43,\ 6.5\times10^{-7})}$$

## 最终答案
(1.43,\ 6.5\times10^{-7})
