## 题目
A rotating steel shaft is supported by a sleeve bearing. Compare the rate of wear when the sleeve is (a) made of the same steel and (b) made of copper. Give your answer as the value of relative_volume_loss_ratio.The unit of the answer is 20.

## 过程
The relative volume loss ratio is:

$$\frac{\dot{V}_b}{\dot{V}_a} = 2 \times \frac{H_{\text{steel}}}{H_{\text{cu}}} = 2 \times 10 = 20$$

The copper sleeve wears **20 times faster** than the steel sleeve — both because copper is ~10× softer (wear ∝ 1/H) **and** because in the steel-on-steel case the wear is shared equally between two surfaces (factor of 2), whereas with steel-on-copper the softer copper takes essentially all the wear.

$$\boxed{20}$$

## 最终答案
20
