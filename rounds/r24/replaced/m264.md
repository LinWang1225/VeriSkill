## 题目
In a plot of the logarithm (to the base 10) of the diffusion coefficient versus reciprocal of absolute temperature for the diffusion of copper in gold, two points are  $1 / T_{1}=0.8 \times$ $10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $1 / T_{2}=1.1 \times 10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $ \log D_{1}=-12.40$ and $ \log D_{2}=-15.45$. Determine values for the activation energy and the preexponential. Give your answer as a tuple (activation energy, preexponential).The units of each required answer are (kJ/mol, m^2/s), respectively.

## 过程
Using the Arrhenius form \(D = D_0\exp(-Q/RT)\), taking \(\log_{10}\) gives:

\[
\log D = \log D_0 - \frac{Q}{2.303\,R}\cdot\frac{1}{T}
\]

**Slope** from the two points:

\[
m = \frac{\log D_2 - \log D_1}{(1/T_2)-(1/T_1)}=\frac{-15.45-(-12.40)}{1.1\times10^{-3}-0.8\times10^{-3}}=\frac{-3.05}{0.3\times10^{-3}}\approx -1.0167\times10^{4}
\]

**Activation energy:**

\[
Q = -m\cdot 2.303\,R = (1.0167\times10^{4})(2.303)(8.314)\approx 1.9466\times10^{5}\,\text{J/mol}\approx 194.66\,\text{kJ/mol}
\]

**Preexponential** (intercept):

\[
\log D_0 = \log D_1 - m(1/T_1) = -12.40 -(-1.0167\times10^{4})(0.8\times10^{-3})\approx -4.267
\]

\[
D_0 = 10^{-4.267}\approx 5.41\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}
\]

\[
\boxed{(194.66,\ 5.41\times10^{-5})}
\]

## 最终答案
(194.66,\ 5.41\times10^{-5})
