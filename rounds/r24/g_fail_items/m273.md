## 题目
Zinc undergoes corrosion in an acidic solution according to the reaction:
\[
\mathrm{Zn} + 2\mathrm{H}^{+} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Zn}^{2+} + \mathrm{H}_{2}
\]

The rates of both oxidation and reduction half-reactions are controlled by activation polarization.

(a) Compute the rate of oxidation of Zn (in $\mathrm{mol/cm^{2}\cdot s}$), given the following activation polarization data:

\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|}
    \hline
    \textbf{For Zn} & \textbf{For Hydrogen} \\
    \hline
    V_{\left(\mathrm{Zn}/\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\right)} = -0.763\,\mathrm{V} & V_{\left(\mathrm{H}^{+}/\mathrm{H}_{2}\right)} = 0\,\mathrm{V} \\
    \hline
    i_{0} = 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} & i_{0} = 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} \\
    \hline
    \beta = +0.09 & \beta = -0.08 \\
    \hline
\end{array}
\]


(b) Compute the value of the corrosion potential.

Give your answer as a tuple (rate of oxidation in (a), the value of the corrosion potential in (b)).
The units of each required answer are (mol/(cm${}^{2}$s), V), respectively.

- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
Applied mixed-potential theory with Tafel activation polarization eta = beta*log10(i/i0), beta carrying the given sign. Equating the two half-cell potentials at the common corrosion potential gives a linear equation in x=log10(i_c): -0.763 + 0.09(x+7) = -0.08(x+10) => 0.17x = -0.667 => x = -3.9235, so i_c = 1.19e-4 A/cm^2. (a) Oxidation rate r = i_c/(nF) with n=2, F=96485 C/mol (Faraday constant, standard value) => r = 6.18e-10 mol/(cm^2 s). (b) V_corr = 0 + (-0.08)*log10(i_c/1e-10) = -0.486 V, cross-checked with the Zn side giving the same -0.486 V. Units aligned to the requested (mol/(cm^2 s), V). Sigfig: weakest inputs (beta 0.09/0.08, i0 powers) ~2 sf, so report 2 sf.

（执行证据）
Solving 0.17x = -0.667 gives log10(i_c) = -3.9235, hence i_c = 1.19e-4 A/cm^2. Rate = i_c/(2*96485) = 6.18e-10 mol/(cm^2 s). V_corr = -0.08*log10(i_c/1e-10) = -0.486 V, identical to the Zn-side value -0.486 V, confirming the mixed potential.

## 最终答案
(6.2\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{mol/(cm^2\cdot s)},\ -0.49\ \mathrm{V})
