## 题目
Nickel containing $2 \mathrm{wt} \%$ thorium is produced in powder form, consolidated into a part, and sintered in the presence of oxygen, causing all of the thorium to produce $\mathrm{ThO}_2$ spheres 80 nm in diameter. Calculate the number of spheres per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. The density of $\mathrm{ThO}_2$ is $9.69 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of number_of_spheres_per_cm3.The unit of the answer is per cm³.

## 过程
## Step-by-Step Solution

**Basis:** 100 g of the Ni–Th alloy → 2 g Th and 98 g Ni

**Step 1 — Mass of ThO₂ formed**

All Th oxidizes to ThO₂ (1:1 mole ratio):
$$M_{\text{ThO}_2} = 232.04 + 2(16.00) = 264.04 \text{ g/mol}$$
$$m_{\text{ThO}_2} = 2.0 \times \frac{264.04}{232.04} = 2.276 \text{ g}$$

**Step 2 — Volumes**

$$V_{\text{ThO}_2} = \frac{2.276}{9.69} = 0.2349 \text{ cm}^3, \qquad V_{\text{Ni}} = \frac{98}{8.90} = 11.011 \text{ cm}^3$$
$$V_{\text{total}} = 11.011 + 0.2349 = 11.246 \text{ cm}^3$$

**Step 3 — Volume of one sphere** (d = 80 nm → r = 40 nm = 4×10⁻⁶ cm)

$$V_{\text{sphere}} = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi (4\times10^{-6})^3 = 2.681\times10^{-16} \text{ cm}^3$$

**Step 4 — Number of spheres per cm³**

$$N = \frac{V_{\text{ThO}_2}}{V_{\text{sphere}}} = \frac{0.2349}{2.681\times10^{-16}} = 8.76\times10^{14} \text{ spheres}$$

$$\frac{N}{V_{\text{total}}} = \frac{8.76\times10^{14}}{11.246} = 7.79\times10^{13} \text{ spheres/cm}^3$$

$$\boxed{7.79\times10^{13}}$$

## 最终答案
7.79\times10^{13}
