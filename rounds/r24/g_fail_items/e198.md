## 题目
Slip was found to first occur in some aluminum crystals when the resolved shear stress reached 1.0 MPa. Assuming that this is the stress necessary to operate the largest Frank-Read sources, calculate the size, $d$, of these sources.The unit of the answer is $\mu$m.

## 过程
With $G = 26$ GPa and $b = 0.286$ nm:

$$d = \frac{(26\times10^9)(0.286\times10^{-9})}{1.0\times10^6} = 7.45\times10^{-6}\,\text{m} \approx 7.4\,\mu\text{m}$$

The shear modulus and Burgers vector of aluminum combine with the 1.0 MPa critical stress to give a source size on the order of a few micrometers, consistent with the largest Frank-Read sources being the easiest to activate (longest segments require the least stress).

$$\boxed{7.4}$$

## 最终答案
7.4
