## 题目
Suppose we just dip a 1 -cm-diameter, 10cm -long rod of aluminum into one liter of water at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The other end of the rod is in contact with a heat source operating at $400^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Determine the length of time required to heat the water to $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ if $75 \%$ of the heat is lost by radiation from the bar.The unit of the answer is min.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1: Heat required to raise the water temperature**

$$Q = mc\Delta T = (1000\,\text{g})(4.186\,\text{J/g·°C})(25-20)\,\text{°C} = 20{,}930\,\text{J}$$

**Step 2: Steady-state heat conduction through the aluminum rod (Fourier's Law)**

$$\dot{Q} = \frac{kA\Delta T}{L}$$

- Thermal conductivity of aluminum: $k = 205\,\text{W/(m·K)}$
- Cross-sectional area: $A = \pi(0.005)^2 = 7.854\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2$
- Temperature gradient: $\Delta T = 400 - 20 = 380\,\text{K}$
- Length: $L = 0.10\,\text{m}$

$$\dot{Q} = \frac{205 \times 7.854\times10^{-5} \times 380}{0.10} = 61.18\,\text{W}$$

**Step 3: Account for radiation loss**

Since 75% of heat is lost by radiation, only 25% reaches the water:

$$\dot{Q}_{\text{water}} = 0.25 \times 61.18 = 15.30\,\text{J/s}$$

**Step 4: Time required**

$$t = \frac{Q}{\dot{Q}_{\text{water}}} = \frac{20{,}930}{15.30} = 1368\,\text{s} \approx 22.8\,\text{min}$$

$$\boxed{22.8}$$

## 最终答案
22.8
