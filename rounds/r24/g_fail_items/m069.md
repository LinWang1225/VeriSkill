## 题目
Magnesium (Mg) has an HCP crystal structure, a c/a ratio of 1.624, and a density of $1.74 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$. Compute the atomic radius for Mg .The unit of the answer is nm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
For HCP Mg use the conventional hexagonal cell with Z=6 atoms and volume V=(√3/2)a²c (配套表硬检查: HCP, V=√3/2·a²c, Z=6 — 合法配对). Density ρ = Z·A/(N_A·V), with A_Mg=24.305 g/mol. Given c/a=1.624, V=(√3/2)·(c/a)·a³. Solve V=Z·A/(ρ·N_A)=6·24.305/(1.74·6.022e23)=1.3917e-22 cm³. Then a³=V/[(√3/2)·1.624], giving a=0.4625 nm. In HCP the close-packed basal-plane neighbor distance equals a, and atoms touch so a=2R, hence R=a/2=0.2313 nm. Sig fig: inputs 1.74 (3 sf) and 1.624 (4 sf) → n_min=3, report 3 sf → 0.231 nm.

（执行证据）
V = 6·24.305/(1.74·6.022e23) = 1.3917e-22 cm³; a = [V/((√3/2)·1.624)]^(1/3) = 0.4625 nm; R = a/2 = 0.2313 nm → 0.231 nm (3 sig fig).

## 最终答案
0.231 \text{ nm}
