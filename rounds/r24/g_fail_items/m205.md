## 题目
The density of aluminum is $2.7 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is about $4 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Describe the characteristics of the aluminum-oxide film. Compare these with the oxide film that forms on tungsten. The density of tungsten is $19.254 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $W O_3$ is $7.3 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple: (P-B_ratio_aluminum, P-B_ratio_tungsten).The units of each required answer are (, ), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Pilling–Bedworth ratio R_PB = M_oxide·ρ_metal / (n·M_metal·ρ_oxide). External standard atomic weights (IUPAC): Al=26.98, W=183.84, O=16.00 g/mol (declared, not from problem). Aluminum: M(Al2O3)=101.96, n=2, R = (101.96·2.7)/(2·26.98·4.0) = 1.2754. Tungsten: M(WO3)=231.84, n=1, R = (231.84·19.254)/(183.84·7.3) = 3.3261. Python confirms. Rounding to 3 sig figs (consistent with canonical textbook P-B values and the precision of given densities): Al→1.28, W→3.33. Al value >1 and near unity → protective adherent film; W value ≫1 → non-protective, cracking/spalling oxide.

（执行证据）
Computed R_PB(Al/Al2O3) = (101.96·2.7)/(2·26.98·4.0) = 1.2754 ≈ 1.28; R_PB(W/WO3) = (231.84·19.254)/(183.84·7.3) = 3.3261 ≈ 3.33. Aluminum's ratio near 1 gives a protective film; tungsten's ratio ≫1 gives a non-protective, cracking oxide.

## 最终答案
(1.28, 3.33)
