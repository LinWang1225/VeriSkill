## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄ (A=Mn²⁺, B=Fe³⁺), a spinel AB₂O₄ with Z=8 formula units per unit cell. In spinels the tetrahedral (A) and octahedral (B) sublattice moments are antiparallel, so the net moment per formula unit is |M_oct − M_tet|.

Normal spinel [Mn²⁺]_tet[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]_oct: M_tet=5, M_oct=2·5=10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB/f.u.
Inverse spinel [Fe³⁺]_tet[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]_oct: M_tet=5, M_oct=5+5=10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB/f.u. (Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ both =5 μB, so the distribution is irrelevant — all configurations give 5 μB/f.u.).
50:50 mixture: 0.5·5 + 0.5·5 = 5 μB/f.u.

The problem asks for the moment of a UNIT CELL. Per g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling, spinel has Z=8, so unit-cell moment = per-formula-unit × 8 = 5×8 = 40 μB for all three cases. Code confirms (5,5,5) per f.u. → (40,40,40) per unit cell.

（执行证据）
Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ both carry 5 μB, so for MnFe₂O₄ every cation arrangement (normal, inverse, or any mixture) yields a net 5 μB per formula unit: |2·5−5|=5. The wording 'magnetic moment of a unit cell' with spinel Z=8 (per the unitcell-formula-unit-scaling skill) scales this to 5×8 = 40 μB per unit cell for all three cases. Python check output: per unit cell (×8) = (40, 40, 40.0).

## 最终答案
(40, 40, 40) \,\mu_B
