## 题目
Calculate the length of the Burgers vector in copper. The lattice parameter of copper $(\mathrm{Cu})$ is 0.36151 nm . The close-packed directions.The unit of the answer is nm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Copper is FCC. In an FCC metal the shortest lattice translation (the perfect-dislocation Burgers vector) lies along the close-packed ⟨110⟩ direction and has magnitude |b| = a/√2. With a = 0.36151 nm: |b| = 0.36151/√2 = 0.25562617… nm. The input has 5 significant figures, so round the result to 5 sig figs → 0.25563.

（执行证据）
Cu is FCC, so the Burgers vector is a/√2 along ⟨110⟩. Computing 0.36151/√2 gives 0.25562617… nm, which rounds to 0.25563 (5 sig figs, matching the lattice-parameter precision).

## 最终答案
0.25563
