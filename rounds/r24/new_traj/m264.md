## 题目
In a plot of the logarithm (to the base 10) of the diffusion coefficient versus reciprocal of absolute temperature for the diffusion of copper in gold, two points are  $1 / T_{1}=0.8 \times$ $10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $1 / T_{2}=1.1 \times 10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $ \log D_{1}=-12.40$ and $ \log D_{2}=-15.45$. Determine values for the activation energy and the preexponential. Give your answer as a tuple (activation energy, preexponential).The units of each required answer are (kJ/mol, m^2/s), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Arrhenius in base-10 form: log10 D = log10 D0 - Q/(2.303 R T), so on a log10 D vs 1/T plot the slope = -Q/(2.303 R) and intercept = log10 D0. slope = (-15.45+12.40)/(1.1e-3-0.8e-3) = -3.05/3.0e-4 = -10166.7 K. Q = 10166.7 * ln(10) * 8.314 = 194627 J/mol ≈ 194.6 kJ/mol. log10 D0 = y1 - slope*x1 = -12.40 - (-10166.7)(0.8e-3) = -4.2667, so D0 = 10^-4.2667 = 5.41e-5 m^2/s. Inputs carry ~3 sig figs (log values to 2 decimals, 1/T to 2), so report Q to 4 sig figs and D0 to 3.

（执行证据）
Slope of log10 D vs 1/T = -10166.7 K from the two given points. Q = -slope·ln(10)·R = 194.6 kJ/mol. Intercept gives log10 D0 = -4.267, hence D0 = 5.41×10⁻⁵ m²/s.

## 最终答案
(194.6,\;5.41\times10^{-5})
