## 题目
An isothermally transformed eutectoid steel is found to have a yield strength of 410 MPa. Estimate (a) the transformation temperature and (b) the interlamellar spacing in the pearlite. Give your answer as a tuple: (transformation_temperature_C, interlamellar_spacing_cm).The units of each required answer are (°C, cm), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

This is a classic Callister problem using two empirical relationships for pearlite formed by isothermal transformation of eutectoid steel:

**Equation 1 — Yield strength vs. interlamellar spacing:**
$$\sigma_y \text{ (MPa)} = 139 + \frac{46.0}{S}$$
where $S$ is in μm. This 1/S (Orowan-type) dependence arises because dislocations must bow between cementite lamellae — finer spacing means stronger barriers.

**Equation 2 — Interlamellar spacing vs. undercooling:**
$$S \text{ (μm)} = \frac{8.02}{\Delta T} = \frac{8.02}{T_{\text{eutectoid}} - T}$$
The eutectoid temperature for the Fe–Fe₃C system is $T_{\text{eutectoid}} = 727°\text{C}$.

### (b) Interlamellar spacing
$$410 = 139 + \frac{46.0}{S} \implies S = \frac{46.0}{410 - 139} = \frac{46.0}{271} = 0.1697 \;\mu\text{m}$$

Converting to cm ($1\;\mu\text{m} = 10^{-4}$ cm):
$$S = 1.697 \times 10^{-5} \text{ cm}$$

### (a) Transformation temperature
$$\Delta T = \frac{8.02}{S} = \frac{8.02}{0.1697} = 47.25 \text{ K}$$
$$T = 727 - 47.25 \approx 680°\text{C}$$

This is physically sensible: a moderate undercooling (~47°C below the eutectoid) produces moderately fine pearlite with moderate strength (410 MPa).

$$\boxed{(680,\ 1.70\times10^{-5})}$$

## 最终答案
(680,\ 1.70\times10^{-5})
