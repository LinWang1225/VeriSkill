## 过程
The skills/actor/ directory is empty, so no skills were applied. Geometry: A_0 = π(12.8/2)² = 128.68 mm². Engineering stress σ = F/A_0 and strain ε = ΔL/L_0 were tabulated from every load–elongation pair. The elastic (linear) region runs through the first six points (0 to 76,200 N); a least-squares fit forced through the origin gives E = Σ(σε)/Σ(ε²) = 197.55 GPa (the seventh point, 89,100 N, drops to a secant modulus of 173 GPa, marking the onset of plasticity). The 0.002-strain-offset construction σ = E(ε − 0.002) was intersected with the piecewise-linear stress–strain curve, giving the yield point ε_y = 0.005764, σ_y = 743.68 MPa. The modulus of resilience is the elastic strain energy per unit volume up to yielding, U_r = ½σ_yε_y = σ_y²/(2E) = 743.68²/(2·197550) = 1.3998 MPa = 1.40×10⁶ J/m³.

（执行证据）
E = 197.55 GPa from the linear elastic region (points 0–76,200 N). The 0.002-offset yield construction gives σ_y = 743.68 MPa at ε_y = 0.005764. Hence U_r = σ_y²/(2E) = 1.3998×10⁶ J/m³ ≈ 1.40×10⁶ J/m³ (≈ 203 psi).
