## 题目
Question: One-half of an electrochemical cell consists of a pure nickel electrode in a solution of \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) ions; the other half is a cadmium electrode immersed in a \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) solution.
(a) If the cell is a standard one, write the spontaneous overall reaction and calculate the voltage that is generated.
(b) Compute the cell potential at \(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\) if the \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) and \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) concentrations are 0.5 and \(10^{-3} M\), respectively.
Give your answer as a tuple (voltage for part a, cell potential for part b).The units of each required answer are (V, V), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Standard reduction potentials:**
- Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni,  E° = −0.25 V
- Cd²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cd,  E° = −0.40 V

### Part (a) — Standard cell

Cd has the more negative reduction potential, so Cd is oxidized (anode) and Ni²⁺ is reduced (cathode):

- Cathode: Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni
- Anode: Cd → Cd²⁺ + 2e⁻
- **Overall: Ni²⁺ + Cd → Ni + Cd²⁺**

$$E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{Ni}} - E^\circ_{\text{Cd}} = -0.25 - (-0.40) = 0.15\ \text{V}$$

### Part (b) — Nernst equation

For the reaction Ni²⁺ + Cd → Ni + Cd²⁺, the reaction quotient is

$$Q = \frac{[\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}]}{[\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}]} = \frac{0.5}{10^{-3}} = 500$$

$$E = E^\circ - \frac{0.0592}{n}\log Q = 0.15 - \frac{0.0592}{2}\log(500)$$

$$E = 0.15 - 0.0296(2.699) \approx 0.070\ \text{V}$$

$$\boxed{(0.15,\ 0.0701)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.15,\ 0.0701)
