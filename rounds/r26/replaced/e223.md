## 题目
Design a filter to transmit at least $95 \%$ of the energy of a beam composed of zinc $K_{\alpha} \mathrm{x}$-rays, using aluminum as the shielding material. (The aluminum has a linear absorption coefficient of $108 \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}$.) Assume no loss to reflection. Give your answer as the value of required_thickness.The unit of the answer is cm.

## 过程
## Reasoning

The intensity transmission through an absorbing material follows the **Beer–Lambert law**:

$$\frac{I}{I_0} = e^{-\mu x}$$

where:
- $\mu = 108\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ (linear absorption coefficient of aluminum for zinc $K_\alpha$ x-rays),
- $x$ is the thickness,
- We require at least 95% transmission: $I/I_0 = 0.95$.

Solving for $x$:

$$0.95 = e^{-\mu x}$$
$$\ln(0.95) = -\mu x$$
$$x = -\frac{\ln(0.95)}{\mu} = -\frac{\ln(0.95)}{108}$$

Computing:

$$x = -\frac{-0.051293}{108} \approx 4.75\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{cm}$$

This is the **maximum** thickness that still transmits *at least* 95%; any thinner filter transmits more. The required thickness is:

$$\boxed{4.75\times10^{-4}}$$

## 最终答案
4.75\times10^{-4}
