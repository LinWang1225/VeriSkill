## 题目
Both sides of an alumina plate are coated with a metal electrode. The surface area of the electrode is $5 \mathrm{~mm}^{2}$ and the relative dielectric constant and dielectric strength of the alumina are 9.1 and 17 volt $/ \mathrm{mm}$, respectively. What is the maximum amount of electric charge that can be stored on the alumina plate's surface?The unit of the answer is F.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
The alumina plate with electrodes on both faces is a parallel-plate capacitor. Capacitance C = ε₀ε_r A / d and breakdown voltage V_max = E_bd · d, where E_bd is the dielectric strength and d the plate thickness. The maximum stored charge is Q_max = C·V_max = (ε₀ε_r A / d)·(E_bd·d) = ε₀ε_r A E_bd — the unknown thickness cancels, which is why the problem need not give it. Faithful unit conversion: A = 5 mm² = 5×10⁻⁶ m²; E_bd = 17 V/mm = 1.7×10⁴ V/m; ε₀ = 8.854187817×10⁻¹² F/m; ε_r = 9.1. Q_max = 8.854187817e-12 × 9.1 × 5e-6 × 1.7e4 = 6.8487×10⁻¹² C. (The stated answer unit "F" is dimensionally the unit of capacitance, but capacitance cannot be determined without thickness; the only thickness-independent, well-defined quantity is the charge, whose numerical value is what is reported.) Sig figs: inputs 9.1 (2 sf) and 17 (2 sf) dominate; area 5 mm² (1 sf) — reporting 2 sf (6.8×10⁻¹²) is within the one-extra-digit tolerance, while 3 sf would overstate precision.

（执行证据）
Thickness cancels in Q_max = C·V_max = ε₀ε_r A E_bd. Substituting ε₀=8.854e-12 F/m, ε_r=9.1, A=5×10⁻⁶ m², E_bd=1.7×10⁴ V/m gives Q_max = 6.8487×10⁻¹², rounded to 2 significant figures as 6.8×10⁻¹².

## 最终答案
6.8\times 10^{-12}
