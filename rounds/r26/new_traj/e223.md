## 题目
Design a filter to transmit at least $95 \%$ of the energy of a beam composed of zinc $K_{\alpha} \mathrm{x}$-rays, using aluminum as the shielding material. (The aluminum has a linear absorption coefficient of $108 \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}$.) Assume no loss to reflection. Give your answer as the value of required_thickness.The unit of the answer is cm.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
Beer–Lambert law (no reflection loss): transmitted energy fraction I/I₀ = exp(−μx). To transmit at least 95%, set exp(−μx) = 0.95 at the limiting thickness: x = −ln(0.95)/μ. With μ = 108 cm⁻¹: x = −ln(0.95)/108 = 0.0004749379… cm. Input 108 has 3 sig figs; 95% is the design threshold. Rounding to 3 sig figs gives 4.75×10⁻⁴ cm.

（执行证据）
Applied I/I₀ = e^(−μx) with μ = 108 cm⁻¹ and I/I₀ = 0.95. Solved x = −ln(0.95)/108 = 4.749×10⁻⁴ cm, rounded to 4.75×10⁻⁴ cm (3 sig figs).

## 最终答案
4.75\times10^{-4}
