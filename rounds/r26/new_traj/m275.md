## 题目
One-half of an electrochemical cell consists of a pure nickel electrode in a solution of \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) ions; the other half is a cadmium electrode immersed in a \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) solution.
(a) If the cell is a standard one, write the spontaneous overall reaction and calculate the voltage that is generated.
(b) Compute the cell potential at \(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\) if the \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) and \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) concentrations are 0.5 and \(10^{-3} M\), respectively.
Give your answer as a tuple (voltage for part a, cell potential for part b).The units of each required answer are (V, V), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
Standard reduction potentials (Callister standard EMF series): E°(Ni2+/Ni)=−0.25 V, E°(Cd2+/Cd)=−0.40 V. (a) Cd is more easily oxidized → anode; Ni2+ reduced → cathode. Spontaneous reaction: Cd + Ni2+ → Cd2+ + Ni. E°_cell = E°_cathode − E°_anode = (−0.25)−(−0.40) = +0.15 V. (b) Nernst at 25°C, n=2, Q=[Cd2+]/[Ni2+]=0.5/1e-3=500. E = E° − (RT/nF)ln Q = 0.15 − (8.314·298.15/(2·96485))·ln(500) = 0.15 − 0.07983 = 0.07017 V. Independent recheck with base-10 form 0.05916/n·log10(Q): 0.15 − (0.05916/2)·2.6990 = 0.07016 V, consistent. Sigfig: standard potentials given to 2 sig figs → report 0.15 and 0.070.

（执行证据）
E°_cell = (−0.25)−(−0.40) = +0.15 V for Cd+Ni2+→Cd2++Ni. Nernst with Q=500, n=2 gives E = 0.15 − (RT/2F)ln(500) = 0.07017 V; base-10 form (0.05916/2)·log10(500) gives 0.07016 V, matching. Final tuple (0.15, 0.070) V.

## 最终答案
(0.15, 0.070)
