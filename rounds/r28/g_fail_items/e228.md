## 题目
In order to plate a steel part having a surface area of $200 \mathrm{in} .{ }^2$ with a 0.002 in.-thick layer of nickel: (a) How many atoms of nickel are required? (b) How many moles of nickel are required? Give your answer as a tuple: (number_of_nickel_atoms, moles_of_nickel_required).The units of each required answer are (atoms, mol), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-independent-verify

## 过程
The nickel layer volume is V = (surface area)×(thickness) = 200 in² × 0.002 in = 0.4 in³. Convert to cm³: 0.4 × (2.54 cm/in)³ = 6.5548 cm³. Use standard (Callister) nickel values: density ρ = 8.90 g/cm³ (密度, Callister Table A.1/手冊室温標準值) and atomic weight A_Ni = 58.69 g/mol (Callister periodic table). Mass of Ni = ρV = 8.90 × 6.5548 = 58.338 g. (b) Moles = m/A = 58.338/58.69 = 0.9940 mol. (a) Atoms = moles × N_A = 0.9940 × 6.022×10²³ = 5.986×10²³ atoms. Independent check: atoms = m/(A/N_A) computed directly from mass per atom gives the same 5.986×10²³, confirming the result. Per sigfig skill, the weakest given input (0.002 in, 1 sf; 200 in²) limits reporting; I report 3 guard digits consistent with the textbook convention: 5.99×10²³ atoms and 0.994 mol.

（执行证据）
Layer volume = 200×0.002 = 0.4 in³ = 6.5548 cm³; mass = 8.90×6.5548 = 58.338 g Ni. Moles = 58.338/58.69 = 0.9940 mol; atoms = 0.9940×6.022×10²³ = 5.986×10²³ ≈ 5.99×10²³. Independent recomputation via mass/(A/N_A) reproduces 5.986×10²³ atoms and 0.9940 mol.

## 最终答案
(5.99 \times 10^{23}\ \text{atoms},\ 0.994\ \text{mol})
