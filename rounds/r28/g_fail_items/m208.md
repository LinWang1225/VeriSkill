## 题目
Would you expect CsBr to have the sodium chloride, zinc blende, fluorite, or cesium chloride structure? Based on your answer, determine (a) the lattice parameter; (b) the density; and (c) the packing factor. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (attice_parameter_Å, density_g_cm3, packing_factor).The units of each required answer are (Å, g/cm³, ), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**Structure determination:**

Using the radius-ratio rule with ionic radii r(Cs⁺) = 1.70 Å and r(Br⁻) = 1.96 Å:

$$\frac{r_{Cs^+}}{r_{Br^-}} = \frac{1.70}{1.96} = 0.867$$

Since **0.732 < 0.867 < 1.0**, the coordination number is 8, which corresponds to the **cesium chloride (CsCl) structure**. This agrees with the known crystal chemistry: CsCl, CsBr, and CsI all adopt the CsCl structure.

**(a) Lattice parameter:** In the CsCl structure, ions touch along the body diagonal:
$$a\sqrt{3} = 2(r_{Cs^+} + r_{Br^-}) \;\Rightarrow\; a = \frac{2(1.70+1.96)}{\sqrt{3}} = 4.23\;\text{Å}$$

**(b) Density:** The CsCl structure has n = 1 formula unit per unit cell, with M(CsBr) = 132.905 + 79.904 = 212.809 g/mol:
$$\rho = \frac{n \cdot M}{V_c \cdot N_A} = \frac{1 \times 212.809}{(4.226\times10^{-8})^3 \times 6.022\times10^{23}} = 4.68\;\text{g/cm}^3$$

**(c) Packing factor:** With one Cs⁺ and one Br⁻ per cell:
$$APF = \frac{\frac{4}{3}\pi(r_{Cs^+}^3 + r_{Br^-}^3)}{a^3} = 0.690$$

$$\boxed{(4.23,\ 4.68,\ 0.690)}$$

## 最终答案
(4.23,\ 4.68,\ 0.690)
