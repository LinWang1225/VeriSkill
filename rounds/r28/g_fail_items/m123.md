## 题目
The fatigue data for a brass alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude (MPa) | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| 170 | $3.7 \times 10^{4}$ |
| 148 | $1.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 130 | $3.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 114 | $1.0 \times 10^{6}$ |
| 92 | $1.0 \times 10^{7}$ |
| 80 | $1.0 \times 10^{8}$ |
| 74 | $1.0 \times 10^{9}$ |

Suppose that the fatigue data for the brass alloy were taken from bending-rotating tests and that a rod of this alloy is to be used for an automobile axle that rotates at an average rotational velocity of 1800 revolutions per minute. Give the maximum bending stress amplitude possible for each of the following lifetimes of the rod: (a) 1 year, (b) 1 month, (c) 1 day, and (d) 1 hour.

Give your answer as a tuple (maximum bending stress amplitude for 1 year, 1 month, 1 day, 1 hour).The units of each required answer are (MPa, MPa, MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-sci-model-validity-range
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Rotating bending: 1 revolution = 1 full stress cycle, so N = (1800 rev/min)×(lifetime in min). Lifetimes: 1 h=60 min, 1 d=1440 min, 1 month=30 d=43200 min, 1 yr=365 d=525600 min → N = 1.08e5, 2.592e6, 7.776e7, 9.4608e8 cycles. Brass shows no fatigue limit, so fit the S-N data to the Basquin form σ_a = a·N^b via log-log linear regression of all 7 points: log10(σ/MPa) = -0.08226·log10(N) + 2.5733 (a=374.35, b=-0.08226). All four N lie inside the data range [3.7e4, 1e9], so the fit is used within its validity range (no extrapolation; all outputs physical). Substituting: σ_a = 68.4, 84.0, 111, 144 MPa for 1 yr, 1 month, 1 day, 1 h. Rounding to the precision of the data (2–3 sig figs) gives (68, 84, 110, 144) MPa.

（执行证据）
Log-log regression of the 7 S-N points yields log10(σ/MPa) = -0.08226·log10(N) + 2.5733. Cycle counts at 1800 rpm: 1.08e5 (1 h), 2.592e6 (1 d), 7.776e7 (1 month), 9.461e8 (1 yr) — all within the data range. Substituting gives σ_a = 144.3, 111.1, 84.0, 68.4 MPa, i.e. (68, 84, 110, 144) MPa for (1 yr, 1 month, 1 day, 1 h).

## 最终答案
(68, 84, 110, 144) \text{ MPa}
