## 题目
Consider a cylindrical specimen of some hypothetical metal alloy that has a diameter of 10.0 mm (0.39 in.). A tensile force of $1500 \mathrm{~N}(340 \mathrm{lb})$ produces an elastic reduction in diameter of $6.7 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~mm}(2.64 \times$ $10^{-5}$ in.). Compute the elastic modulus of this alloy, given that Poisson's ratio is 0.35 .The unit of the answer is GPa.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
Lateral (transverse) strain: ε_t = Δd/d = (6.7×10⁻⁴ mm)/(10.0 mm) = 6.7×10⁻⁵. Poisson's ratio ν = −ε_t/ε_axial ⇒ axial tensile strain ε_a = ε_t/ν = 6.7×10⁻⁵/0.35 = 1.9143×10⁻⁴. Cross-sectional area A = πd²/4 = π(10.0×10⁻³)²/4 = 7.854×10⁻⁵ m². Engineering stress σ = F/A = 1500/7.854×10⁻⁵ = 1.9099×10⁷ Pa. Elastic modulus E = σ/ε_a = 1.9099×10⁷/1.9143×10⁻⁴ = 9.977×10¹⁰ Pa = 99.77 GPa. Code confirms E = 99.7688 GPa. Sig-fig: weakest inputs (6.7×10⁻⁴, 0.35) each have 2 sf ⇒ round to 2 sf ⇒ 1.0×10² GPa = 100 GPa.

（执行证据）
ε_t = 6.7×10⁻⁵, ε_a = 1.9143×10⁻⁴, A = 7.854×10⁻⁵ m², σ = 1.9099×10⁷ Pa, giving E = 9.977×10¹⁰ Pa = 99.77 GPa from Python. Rounded to 2 significant figures (limited by 6.7×10⁻⁴ and 0.35) yields 100 GPa.

## 最终答案
100 GPa
