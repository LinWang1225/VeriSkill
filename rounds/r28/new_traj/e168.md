## 题目
A piece of corroded metal alloy plate was found in a submerged ocean vessel. It was estimated that the original area of the plate was $800 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ and that approximately 7.6 kg had corroded away during the submersion. Assuming a corrosion penetration rate of $4 \mathrm{~mm} / \mathrm{yr}$ for this alloy in seawater, estimate the time of submersion in years. The density of the alloy is $4.5 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$.The unit of the answer is yr.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-final-answer-unit-dimension-check

## 过程
The corrosion penetration rate (CPR) relates mass loss to penetration depth. Volume corroded V = m/ρ = 7600 g / 4.5 g/cm³ = 1688.89 cm³. The penetration depth is d = V/A = 1688.89/800 = 2.1111 cm = 21.111 mm (using the given plate area A = 800 cm² directly, per the standard Callister corrosion-rate convention CPR = 87.6·W/(D·A·t), in which the stated plate area is the area used in the formula). Time of submersion t = d/CPR = 21.111 mm / 4 mm/yr = 5.2778 yr. Cross-check via the Callister formula with W = 7.6×10⁶ mg: t(hr) = 87.6×7.6×10⁶/(4.5×800×4) = 46233 h = 46233/8760 = 5.278 yr. Unit tracking: [g]/([g/cm³][cm²][mm/yr]) = cm·yr/mm = 10 yr → yr, consistent with the asked quantity. Sig figs: limiting inputs 7.6 (2 sf), 4.5 (2 sf), 4 mm/yr (1 sf, textbook convention tolerates 1 extra digit) → report 2 sf → 5.3 yr.

（执行证据）
Volume lost = 7600/4.5 = 1688.89 cm³; penetration depth = 1688.89/800 = 21.111 mm; time = 21.111/4 = 5.278 yr. The Callister formula t = 87.6·W/(D·A·CPR) with W = 7.6×10⁶ mg gives 46233 h = 5.28 yr, confirming the direct calculation. Rounded to 2 significant figures: 5.3 yr.

## 最终答案
5.3
