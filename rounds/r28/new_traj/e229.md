## 题目
Above $882^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, titanium has a BCC crystal structure, with $a=0.332 \mathrm{~nm}$. Below this temperature, titanium has a HCP structure with $a=0.2978 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4735 \mathrm{~nm}$. Determine the percent volume change when BCC titanium transforms to HCP titanium. Is this a contraction or expansion? Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_volume_change, transformation_type).The unit of the answer is (%, ).

## 过程
The BCC and HCP unit cells contain different numbers of atoms (Z_BCC=2, Z_HCP=6), so the volume change must be compared on a per-atom basis: v = V_cell/Z. Pairing table (hard-checked legal): BCC (V=a^3, Z=2); HCP conventional (V=(3√3/2)a^2 c, Z=6). Cross-verified with HCP primitive (V=(√3/2)a^2 c, Z=2) — identical v_hcp. v_BCC = (0.332)^3/2 = 0.01829718 nm^3; v_HCP = (3√3/2)(0.2978)^2(0.4735)/6 = 0.01818319 nm^3. Percent change = (v_HCP - v_BCC)/v_BCC × 100 = -0.6230% (reference = initial BCC, per the definition of 'change when BCC transforms to HCP'). ΔV<0 → contraction. The answer is a tuple with an independent type field, so the numeric field reports the positive magnitude |ΔV|/V_BCC×100 = 0.623 and the label carries direction. Sigfigs: weakest input a=0.332 has 3 sf → report 0.623.

（执行证据）
Per-atom volumes: v_BCC = a^3/2 = 0.01829718 nm^3 (Z=2); v_HCP = (3√3/2)a^2 c/6 = 0.01818319 nm^3 (Z=6), cross-checked equal to primitive-cell value (√3/2)a^2 c/2. Percent change (v_HCP−v_BCC)/v_BCC×100 = −0.6230%, negative ⇒ contraction. Tuple reports magnitude 0.623 with 'contraction' label (3 sig figs, limited by a=0.332 nm).

## 最终答案
(0.623, \text{contraction})
