## 题目
The relative dielectric constant of 1 mole of Ar gas was measured at $0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and 1 atm pressure to be 1.00056. (a) Calculate the polarizability of Ar. (b) Calculate the relative dielectric constant if the pressure is increased to 2 atm. (c) Estimate the radius of an Ar atom and compare to that of tabulated data. 

Give your answer as a tuple (polarizability of Ar, relative dielectric constant at 2 atm, radius of an Ar atom).The units of each required answer are (F·m², , m), respectively.

## 激活技能

## 过程
Use Clausius–Mossotti (εr−1)/(εr+2)=Nα/(3ε0). At 0°C,1 atm: Vm=RT/P=0.022414 m³, N=NA/Vm=2.687e25 m⁻³. (a) (0.00056/3.00056)=1.8663e-4 → α=3ε0·lhs/N=1.845e-40 F·m². (b) 2 atm → N doubles → x=3.733e-4 → εr=(1+2x)/(1−x)=1.00112. (c) α=4πε0r³ → r=(α/4πε0)^(1/3)=1.184e-10 m. Cross-check with dilute approx εr−1=Nα/ε0 gives 0.0005599 ≈ given 0.00056, confirming consistency.

（执行证据）
Clausius–Mossotti with N=2.687e25 m⁻³ yields α=1.845e-40 F·m². Doubling N at 2 atm gives εr=1.00112. Conducting-sphere radius r=(α/4πε0)^(1/3)=1.18e-10 m (1.18 Å), between Ar covalent (0.71 Å) and van der Waals (1.88 Å) tabulated radii.

## 最终答案
(1.85\times10^{-40},\ 1.00112,\ 1.18\times10^{-10})
