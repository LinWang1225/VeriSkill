## 题目
The density of BCC iron is $7.882 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$, and the lattice parameter is 0.2866 nm when hydrogen atoms are introduced at interstitial positions. Calculate (a) the atomic fraction of hydrogen atoms; and (b) the number of unit cells on average that contain hydrogen atoms. Give your answer as a tuple: (atomic_fraction_of_hydrogen, number_of_unit_cells_on_average_that_contain_hydrogen_atoms.).The units of each required answer are (, ), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

For BCC iron, there are 2 Fe atoms per unit cell. When hydrogen occupies interstitial sites, the extra mass comes only from the H atoms. The density equation is:

$$\rho = \frac{n_{\text{Fe}}M_{\text{Fe}} + n_{\text{H}}M_{\text{H}}}{N_A a^3}$$

**Solving for hydrogen atoms per unit cell:**

$$n_{\text{H}} = \frac{\rho N_A a^3 - n_{\text{Fe}}M_{\text{Fe}}}{M_{\text{H}}}$$

With $\rho = 7.882\;\text{g/cm}^3$, $a = 0.2866\times10^{-7}\;\text{cm}$, $n_{\text{Fe}}=2$, $M_{\text{Fe}}=55.85$, $M_{\text{H}}=1.008$, $N_A=6.023\times10^{23}$:

$$n_{\text{H}} = \frac{7.882(6.023\times10^{23})(2.866\times10^{-8})^3 - 2(55.85)}{1.008} \approx 0.0573$$

**(a) Atomic fraction of hydrogen:**

$$x_{\text{H}} = \frac{n_{\text{H}}}{n_{\text{Fe}}+n_{\text{H}}} = \frac{0.0573}{2.0573} \approx 0.0279$$

**(b) Number of unit cells on average that contain hydrogen atoms:**

Since $n_{\text{H}} \approx 0.0573$ H atoms per unit cell (dilute, so multiple occupancy is negligible), the average number of unit cells per hydrogen atom is:

$$\frac{1}{n_{\text{H}}} \approx \frac{1}{0.0573} \approx 17.4$$

i.e., roughly 1 in every 17.4 unit cells contains a hydrogen atom.

$$\boxed{(0.028,\ 17.4)}$$

## 最终答案
(0.028,\ 17.4)
