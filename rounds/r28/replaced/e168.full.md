## 题目
A piece of corroded metal alloy plate was found in a submerged ocean vessel. It was estimated that the original area of the plate was $800 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ and that approximately 7.6 kg had corroded away during the submersion. Assuming a corrosion penetration rate of $4 \mathrm{~mm} / \mathrm{yr}$ for this alloy in seawater, estimate the time of submersion in years. The density of the alloy is $4.5 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$.The unit of the answer is yr.

## 过程
The calculation gives:

$$t = \frac{7600}{4.5 \times 800 \times 2 \times 0.4} = \frac{7600}{2880} \approx 2.64\text{ yr}$$

The factor of 2 accounts for corrosion attacking **both faces** of the submerged plate. Each side penetrates at 4 mm/yr, so the total thickness lost per year is 8 mm/yr across the plate's cross-section.

$$\boxed{2.64}$$

## 最终答案
2.64
