## 题目
The relative dielectric constant of 1 mole of Ar gas was measured at $0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and 1 atm pressure to be 1.00056. (a) Calculate the polarizability of Ar. (b) Calculate the relative dielectric constant if the pressure is increased to 2 atm. (c) Estimate the radius of an Ar atom and compare to that of tabulated data. 

Give your answer as a tuple (polarizability of Ar, relative dielectric constant at 2 atm, radius of an Ar atom).The units of each required answer are (F·m², , m), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**(a) Polarizability of Ar**

Using the Clausius-Mossotti relation for a non-polar gas, which simplifies (since ε_r ≈ 1) to:

$$\varepsilon_r - 1 = \frac{N\alpha}{\varepsilon_0}$$

The number density from the ideal gas law: $N = \frac{N_A P}{RT} = 2.687 \times 10^{25}\ \text{m}^{-3}$

$$\alpha = \frac{(\varepsilon_r - 1)\varepsilon_0}{N} = \frac{0.00056 \times 8.854\times10^{-12}}{2.687\times10^{25}} \approx 1.85 \times 10^{-40}\ \text{F·m}^2$$

**(b) Relative dielectric constant at 2 atm**

At constant temperature, doubling the pressure doubles N, so (ε_r − 1) doubles:

$$\varepsilon_r' = 1 + 2(0.00056) = 1.00112$$

**(c) Estimated radius of Ar atom**

Treating the atom as a conducting sphere: $\alpha = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^3$

$$r = \left(\frac{\alpha}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\right)^{1/3} = \left(\frac{1.85\times10^{-40}}{4\pi \times 8.854\times10^{-12}}\right)^{1/3} \approx 1.18 \times 10^{-10}\ \text{m} = 1.18\ \text{Å}$$

**Comparison:** The estimated radius (1.18 Å) falls between the tabulated covalent radius (~0.71 Å) and the van der Waals radius (~1.88 Å) of Ar. The conducting-sphere model gives a reasonable order-of-magnitude estimate, somewhat smaller than the van der Waals radius but larger than the covalent radius — sensible for a noble-gas atom interacting via induced dipoles.

$$\boxed{\left(1.85 \times 10^{-40},\ 1.00112,\ 1.18 \times 10^{-10}\right)}$$

## 最终答案
\left(1.85 \times 10^{-40},\ 1.00112,\ 1.18 \times 10^{-10}\right)
