## 题目
Above $882^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, titanium has a BCC crystal structure, with $a=0.332 \mathrm{~nm}$. Below this temperature, titanium has a HCP structure with $a=0.2978 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4735 \mathrm{~nm}$. Determine the percent volume change when BCC titanium transforms to HCP titanium. Is this a contraction or expansion? Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_volume_change, transformation_type).The unit of the answer is (%, ).

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
BCC: V_cell = a³ = (0.332 nm)³ = 0.036594 nm³, Z=2 → v_BCC = 0.018297 nm³/atom. HCP conventional hex cell: V_cell = (3√3/2)·a²c = (3√3/2)(0.2978)²(0.4735) = 0.109099 nm³, Z=6 → v_HCP = 0.018183 nm³/atom. 配套表 check: BCC (a³, Z=2) and HCP ((3√3/2)a²c, Z=6) are both legal pairings, so per-atom volumes are comparable on an equal-atom basis. Percent basis = initial (BCC) state per definition of 'volume change when BCC→HCP': %ΔV = (v_HCP − v_BCC)/v_BCC × 100 = (0.018183 − 0.018297)/0.018297 × 100 = −0.6230%. Negative sign ⇒ contraction. Sig figs: least-precise input a_BCC=0.332 has 3 sf → round to 3 sf → −0.623. Code output: pct = -0.6230300535334798.

（执行证据）
Per-atom volumes: v_BCC = (0.332)³/2 = 0.018297 nm³/atom; v_HCP = (3√3/2)(0.2978)²(0.4735)/6 = 0.018183 nm³/atom. Percent change relative to initial BCC = (v_HCP−v_BCC)/v_BCC×100 = −0.623%. The negative sign indicates a contraction.

## 最终答案
(-0.623, \text{contraction})
