## 题目
“Samarium iron garnet has the formula Sm₃Fe₅O₁₂ which can be written Sm₃ᶜ Fe₂ᵃ Fe₃ᵈ O₁₂, where the subscripts ᵃ, ᶜ, and ᵈ denote the crystallographic a, c, and d sites.  The 2 Fe³⁺ ions on the a‐sites have their spins aligned parallel to those of the 3 Sm³⁺ ions on the c‐sites, and these are antiparallel to the 3 Fe³⁺ ions on the d‐sites.  Given that (1) each cubic unit cell (edge 1.2529 nm) contains eight Sm₃Fe₅O₁₂ formula units, (2) the saturation magnetization Mₛ=1.35×10⁵ A/m, and (3) each Fe³⁺ carries 5 μ_B, compute the number of Bohr magnetons μ_Sm carried by each Sm³⁺ ion.”The unit of the answer is BM.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-final-answer-unit-dimension-check

## 过程
Per formula unit: a-sites 2 Fe³⁺ (10 μ_B) ∥ c-sites 3 Sm³⁺ (3 μ_Sm), antiparallel to d-sites 3 Fe³⁺ (15 μ_B). Net moment per f.u. = (10 + 3 μ_Sm) − 15 = 3 μ_Sm − 5 (μ_B). Unit cell contains Z = 8 formula units (given), V_cell = a³ with a = 1.2529 nm. Ms = Z(3 μ_Sm − 5) μ_B / a³. Solving: 3 μ_Sm − 5 = Ms·a³/(Z·μ_B) = (1.35e5)(1.2529e-9)³/(8·9.274e-24) = 3.5787. Thus μ_Sm = (3.5787 + 5)/3 = 2.8596 ≈ 2.86 (3 sig figs, limited by Ms = 1.35×10⁵).

（执行证据）
Net moment per formula unit = 3·μ_Sm − 5 (μ_B), with a-site (10 μ_B) and c-site (3 μ_Sm) spins opposing d-site (15 μ_B). Using Ms·a³/(Z·μ_B) = 3.5787 with Z = 8 gives μ_Sm = (3.5787+5)/3 = 2.86.

## 最终答案
2.86
