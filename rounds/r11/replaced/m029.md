## 题目
You are asked to measure nondestructively the yield strength and tensile strength of an annealed 65-45-12 cast iron structural member. Fortunately, a small hardness indentation in this structural design will not impair its future usefulness, which is a working definition of nondestructive. A $10-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter tungsten carbide sphere creates a $4.26-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter impression under a $3,000-\mathrm{kg}$ load. What are the yield and tensile strengths? Give your answer as a tuple: (yield_strength, tensile_strength).The units of each required answer are (MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

### Step 1: Calculate the Brinell Hardness Number (BHN)

The Brinell hardness is given by:

$$HB = \frac{2P}{\pi D\left(D - \sqrt{D^2 - d^2}\right)}$$

With $P = 3000$ kg, $D = 10$ mm, $d = 4.26$ mm:

$$HB = \frac{2(3000)}{\pi(10)\left(10 - \sqrt{100 - 18.15}\right)} = \frac{6000}{\pi(10)(10 - 9.047)} \approx 200.5$$

### Step 2: Apply ductile cast iron hardness–strength correlations

For **ductile cast iron**, the empirical correlations relating Brinell hardness to strength (as presented in Callister) are:

- **Tensile strength:** $\sigma_{UTS} \text{ (MPa)} \approx 2.5 \times HB$
- **Yield strength:** $\sigma_y \text{ (MPa)} \approx 1.6 \times HB$

These differ from the steel correlation ($3.45 \times HB$) because graphite nodules in ductile iron reduce the strength-to-hardness ratio.

### Step 3: Compute the strengths

$$\sigma_y = 1.6 \times 200.45 = 320.7 \text{ MPa} \approx 321 \text{ MPa}$$

$$\sigma_{UTS} = 2.5 \times 200.45 = 501.1 \text{ MPa} \approx 501 \text{ MPa}$$

### Verification
The 65-45-12 grade specifies minimum $YS = 310$ MPa and $TS = 448$ MPa with a hardness range of 156–217 HB. Since the measured BHN ≈ 200 falls in the **upper portion** of this range, estimated strengths somewhat above the minimum specified values are expected — consistent with our results.

$$\boxed{(321,\ 501)}$$

## 最终答案
(321,\ 501)
