## 题目
A 4 -cm-diameter, $0.5-\mathrm{mm}$-thick spherical container made of BCC iron holds nitrogen at $700^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The concentration at the inner surface is 0.05 atomic percent and at the outer surface is 0.002 atomic percent. Calculate the number of grams of nitrogen that are lost from the container per hour. Give your answer as the value of grams_lost_per_hour.The unit of the answer is g/h.

## 过程
## Solution

This is a steady-state diffusion problem through a spherical shell. For a sphere, the total mass flow rate $W$ (constant in steady state) is found by integrating Fick's first law over the spherical shell:

$$W = \frac{4\pi D\,(C_1 - C_2)}{\dfrac{1}{r_1} - \dfrac{1}{r_2}}$$

**1. Diffusivity of N in BCC iron at 700 °C** (Callister data: $D_0 = 4.3\times10^{-7}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 144{,}000\,\text{J/mol}$):

$$D = D_0\,e^{-Q_d/RT} = 4.3\times10^{-7}\,e^{-144000/(8.31\cdot 973)} \approx 7.92\times10^{-15}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**2. Convert atomic % to mass concentration** (dilute N in Fe, using $\rho_{\text{Fe}}=7.87\,\text{g/cm}^3$, $M_{\text{Fe}}=55.85$, $M_{\text{N}}=14.01$):

$$C = \frac{\text{at\%}}{100}\cdot\frac{\rho_{\text{Fe}}}{M_{\text{Fe}}}\cdot M_{\text{N}}$$

- $C_1 = 9.87\times10^{-4}\,\text{g/cm}^3$, $C_2 = 3.95\times10^{-5}\,\text{g/cm}^3$
- $\Delta C = 9.48\times10^{-4}\,\text{g/cm}^3 = 947.6\,\text{g/m}^3$

**3. Geometry:** inner radius $r_1 = 2.00\,\text{cm}$, outer radius $r_2 = 2.05\,\text{cm}$ (4 cm diameter, 0.5 mm wall).

**4. Mass loss rate:**

$$W = \frac{4\pi(7.92\times10^{-15})(947.6)}{1/0.0200 - 1/0.0205} \approx 7.74\times10^{-11}\,\text{g/s}$$

$$\boxed{2.79\times10^{-7}}$$

grams of nitrogen lost per hour (≈ $2.8\times10^{-7}\,\text{g/h}$).

## 最终答案
2.79\times10^{-7}
