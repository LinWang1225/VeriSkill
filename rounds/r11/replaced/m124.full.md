## 题目
The fatigue data for a steel alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude [MPa (ksi)] | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| $470(68.0)$ | $10^{4}$ |
| $440(63.4)$ | $3 \times 10^{4}$ |
| $390(56.2)$ | $10^{5}$ |
| $350(51.0)$ | $3 \times 10^{5}$ |
| $310(45.3)$ | $10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $3 \times 10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{7}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{8}$ |

(a) What is the fatigue limit for this alloy?
(b) Determine fatigue lifetimes at stress amplitudes of 415 MPa (60,000 psi).
(c) Estimate fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ and $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles.\

Give your answer as a tuple (fatigue limit, fatigue lifetimes of 415 MPa, fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ cycles, fatigue strengths at $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles).The units of each required answer are (MPa, cycles, MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

**(a) Fatigue limit:** The S-N curve becomes horizontal at **290 MPa** — the alloy survives to 3×10⁶, 10⁷, and 10⁸ cycles without failing at this stress. This is the fatigue limit.

**(b) Fatigue lifetime at 415 MPa:** Using linear interpolation on the S–log N plot, 415 MPa lies between the data points (440 MPa, 3×10⁴) and (390 MPa, 10⁵):

$$\log N = 4.477 + \frac{415-440}{390-440}(5.0-4.477) \approx 4.739$$

$$N \approx 10^{4.739} \approx 5.5\times10^{4}\ \text{cycles}$$

**(c) Fatigue strengths:**

- At 2×10⁴ cycles (between 10⁴ and 3×10⁴): interpolating between (470, 10⁴) and (440, 3×10⁴) gives **≈ 451 MPa**.
- At 6×10⁵ cycles (between 3×10⁵ and 10⁶): interpolating between (350, 3×10⁵) and (310, 10⁶) gives **≈ 327 MPa**.

## Final Answer

$$\boxed{(290,\ 5.5\times10^{4},\ 451,\ 327)}$$

## 最终答案
(290,\ 5.5\times10^{4},\ 451,\ 327)
