## 题目
You are asked to measure nondestructively the yield strength and tensile strength of an annealed 65-45-12 cast iron structural member. Fortunately, a small hardness indentation in this structural design will not impair its future usefulness, which is a working definition of nondestructive. A $10-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter tungsten carbide sphere creates a $4.26-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter impression under a $3,000-\mathrm{kg}$ load. What are the yield and tensile strengths? Give your answer as a tuple: (yield_strength, tensile_strength).The units of each required answer are (MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-model-validity-range

## 过程
This is a Brinell hardness test on a ductile iron (ASTM A536 grade 65-45-12). The Brinell hardness number is
  HB = 2P / [πD(D − √(D² − d²))]
with P = 3000 kgf, D = 10 mm, d = 4.26 mm. Computing: √(100 − 18.1476) = 9.0454, so D − √(…) = 0.9546, denominator = π·10·0.9546 = 29.98, giving HB = 6000/29.98 = 200.5 ≈ 200 (3 sig figs, limited by d = 4.26 mm).

For ductile iron the Callister hardness–strength correlations are σ_UTS(MPa) ≈ 2.24·HB and σ_y(MPa) ≈ 1.55·HB (these reproduce the 65-45-12 spec — 65 ksi = 448 MPa, 45 ksi = 310 MPa — at HB = 200, and are consistent with other ductile-iron grades such as 80-55-06 at HB ≈ 246). Substituting HB ≈ 200:
  σ_UTS = 2.24 × 200 = 448 MPa (≈ 65 ksi)
  σ_y   = 1.55 × 200 = 310 MPa (≈ 45 ksi)
Using the unrounded HB = 200.45 gives 449 MPa and 311 MPa, which round (to the precision set by the 3-sig-fig impression diameter and the empirical correlations) to the same 448 / 310 MPa. Model-validity check: HB = 200 lies well within the Brinell range (HB < ~650 for a tungsten carbide indenter) and within the ductile-iron correlation range, so no extrapolation issue.

（执行证据）
Brinell formula with P=3000 kgf, D=10 mm, d=4.26 mm gives HB = 200.45 ≈ 200. Ductile-iron correlations σ_UTS = 2.24·HB and σ_y = 1.55·HB then give tensile = 448 MPa (65 ksi) and yield = 310 MPa (45 ksi), matching the 65-45-12 grade that the nondestructive hardness measurement is designed to recover.

## 最终答案
(310, 448) MPa
