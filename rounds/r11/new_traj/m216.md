## 题目
A 4 -cm-diameter, $0.5-\mathrm{mm}$-thick spherical container made of BCC iron holds nitrogen at $700^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The concentration at the inner surface is 0.05 atomic percent and at the outer surface is 0.002 atomic percent. Calculate the number of grams of nitrogen that are lost from the container per hour. Give your answer as the value of grams_lost_per_hour.The unit of the answer is g/h.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-arrhenius-paired-d0-q
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Steady-state Fick diffusion through a spherical shell: c(r)=A+B/r, giving ṁ = 4πD(c_i−c_o)/(1/r_i−1/r_o). Diffusivity for N in BCC α-Fe taken from Callister Table 5.2 as a paired same-table/same-phase value (D0=3.0e-7 m²/s, Q=76150 J/mol); at 973 K, D=2.45e-11 m²/s (physically consistent with C-in-BCC-Fe ≈3.1e-11; the alternative D0=4.6e-4 pairing was rejected because it gives D≈4e-8, ~1000× too fast for an interstitial in BCC iron). Geometry: r_i=0.020 m, r_o=0.0205 m. Concentrations converted from at% to kg/m³ via dilute-interstitial relation C_N = (at%/100)·ρ_Fe·(M_N/M_Fe) with ρ_Fe=7870 kg/m³, M_Fe=55.845, M_N=14.007 → c_i=0.987, c_o=0.0395 kg/m³. Code: ṁ=2.39e-10 kg/s → 8.61e-4 g/h. Inputs are ~2 sig fig → report 8.6e-4 g/h.

（执行证据）
D(N in BCC Fe, 973 K)=2.45e-11 m²/s from Callister Table 5.2 paired values (D0=3.0e-7, Q=76150). Concentration difference c_i−c_o=0.947 kg/m³. Spherical-shell steady-state rate ṁ=4πDΔc/(1/r_i−1/r_o)=2.39e-10 kg/s, giving 8.6e-4 g/h.

## 最终答案
8.6\times10^{-4}
