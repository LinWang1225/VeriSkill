## 题目
A $2 \mathrm{~cm} \times 4 \mathrm{~cm} \times 6 \mathrm{~cm}$ magnesium casting is produced. After cooling to room temperature, the casting is found to weigh 80 g. Determine (a) the volume of the shrinkage cavity at the center of the casting and (b) the percent shrinkage that must have occurred during solidification. Give your answer as a tuple: (shrinkage_cavity_volume_cm3, percent_shrinkage).The units of each required answer are (cm³, %), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
Nominal casting volume V = 2×4×6 = 48 cm³. Density of solid Mg at room temperature taken as ρ = 1.74 g/cm³ (standard handbook value, explicitly declared since not given in problem). Actual volume of solid magnesium in the 80 g casting: V_solid = m/ρ = 80/1.74 = 45.977 cm³. (a) Shrinkage cavity volume = V − V_solid = 48 − 45.977 = 2.023 cm³. (b) Percent shrinkage = V_cavity/V × 100 = 2.023/48 × 100 = 4.21%. Sig-fig check: weakest input is 80 g (2 sig figs), so round final to 2 sig figs: 2.0 cm³ and 4.2%. Python confirmed: V_cavity = 2.02299, pct = 4.21456.

（执行证据）
V_nominal = 48 cm³; V_solid = 80/1.74 = 45.977 cm³; cavity = 2.023 cm³ → 2.0 cm³ (2 sf). Percent shrinkage = 2.023/48×100 = 4.21% → 4.2% (2 sf). Density ρ = 1.74 g/cm³ is the standard room-temperature value for magnesium, declared as an external lookup.

## 最终答案
(2.0\ \text{cm}^3,\ 4.2\%)
