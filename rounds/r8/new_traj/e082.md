## 题目
Iron titanate, $\mathrm{FeTiO}{3}$, forms in the ilmenite crystal structure that consists of an HCP arrangement of $\mathrm{O}^{2-}$ ions.
(a) What fraction of the total tetrahedral sites will be occupied?
(b) What fraction of the total octahedral sites will be occupied?

Give your answer as a tuple (fraction of the total tetrahedral sites be occupied,  fraction of the total octahedral sites be occupied).

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions

## 过程
FeTiO3 (ilmenite) has an HCP sublattice of O²⁻ with cations in interstitial sites. Ilmenite is the ordered version of the corundum (Al2O3) structure: Fe²⁺ and Ti⁴⁺ alternate on the octahedral cation sites, and no cation sits in a tetrahedral site. For an HCP anion sublattice there is 1 octahedral site per anion and 2 tetrahedral sites per anion. Per formula unit FeTiO3: 3 O²⁻, 2 cations (1 Fe + 1 Ti). Octahedral sites = 3, occupied = 2 → 2/3. Tetrahedral sites = 6, occupied = 0 → 0. The question asks a fraction (ratio), so Z cancels and no unit-cell/formula-unit scaling is needed; the per-formula-unit ratio is valid directly. Code confirms: tet = 0.0, oct = 0.6667.

（执行证据）
Ilmenite = ordered corundum: cations occupy octahedral holes only, filling 2 of 3 octahedral sites per FeTiO3 formula unit (2 cations / 3 O), and none of the 6 tetrahedral sites. Octahedral fraction = 2/3, tetrahedral fraction = 0. Python check returns 0.0 and 0.6667.

## 最终答案
(0,\ \tfrac{2}{3})
