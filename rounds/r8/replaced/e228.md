## 题目
In order to plate a steel part having a surface area of $200 \mathrm{in} .{ }^2$ with a 0.002 in.-thick layer of nickel: (a) How many atoms of nickel are required? (b) How many moles of nickel are required? Give your answer as a tuple: (number_of_nickel_atoms, moles_of_nickel_required).The units of each required answer are (atoms, mol), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Step 1: Volume of nickel required**

$$V = \text{area} \times \text{thickness} = 200 \text{ in}^2 \times 0.002 \text{ in} = 0.4 \text{ in}^3$$

**Step 2: Convert volume to cm³**

$$V = 0.4 \text{ in}^3 \times (2.54 \text{ cm/in})^3 = 6.555 \text{ cm}^3$$

**Step 3: Mass of nickel** (using density of Ni = 8.902 g/cm³)

$$m = \rho V = 8.902 \text{ g/cm}^3 \times 6.555 \text{ cm}^3 = 58.35 \text{ g}$$

**Step 4: Moles of nickel** (using atomic weight of Ni = 58.69 g/mol)

$$n = \frac{m}{M} = \frac{58.35 \text{ g}}{58.69 \text{ g/mol}} = 0.994 \text{ mol}$$

**Step 5: Atoms of nickel** (using Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol)

$$N = n \times N_A = 0.994 \text{ mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms/mol} = 5.99 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms}$$

$$\boxed{(5.99 \times 10^{23},\ 0.994)}$$

## 最终答案
(5.99 \times 10^{23},\ 0.994)
