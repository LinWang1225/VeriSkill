## 题目
Calculate the length of the Burgers vector in copper. The lattice parameter of copper $(\mathrm{Cu})$ is 0.36151 nm . The close-packed directions.The unit of the answer is nm.

## 过程
Copper has an **FCC** crystal structure. In FCC, the Burgers vector lies along the close-packed direction $\langle 110 \rangle$, with magnitude

$$b = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$$

With $a = 0.36151\ \text{nm}$:

$$b = \frac{0.36151}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.25563\ \text{nm}$$

$$\boxed{0.25563}$$

## 最终答案
0.25563
