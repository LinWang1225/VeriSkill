## 题目
The fraction recrystallized-time data for the recrystallization at $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ of a previously deformed aluminum are tabulated here. Assuming that the kinetics of this process obey the Avrami relationship, determine the fraction recrystallized after a total time of 116.8 min .

| Fraction Recrystallized | Time $(\mathbf{m i n})$ |
| :--: | :--: |
| 0.30 | 95.2 |
| 0.80 | 126.6 |

## 过程
Using the Avrami relationship, $y = 1 - \exp(-k t^n)$, which rearranges to:

$$\ln[-\ln(1-y)] = \ln k + n \ln t$$

With the two data points $(y_1,t_1)=(0.30, 95.2)$ and $(y_2,t_2)=(0.80, 126.6)$, I solve the two simultaneous linear equations for $n$ and $k$:

- $n = \dfrac{\ln[-\ln(1-y_2)] - \ln[-\ln(1-y_1)]}{\ln t_2 - \ln t_1} \approx 5.29$
- $k \approx 1.24\times10^{-11}$

Then at $t = 116.8$ min:

$$y = 1 - \exp\!\big(-k\,t^{\,n}\big) \approx 0.65$$

$$\boxed{0.66}$$

## 最终答案
0.66
