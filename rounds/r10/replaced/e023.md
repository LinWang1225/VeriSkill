## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 激活技能

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄, a spinel AB₂O₄ with A = Mn²⁺ (5 μB) and B = Fe³⁺ (5 μB). Per formula unit there is 1 occupied tetrahedral (A) site and 2 occupied octahedral (B) sites. In spinels the two sublattices couple antiparallel (ferrimagnetism), so the net moment per formula unit is |M_oct − M_tet|.

(a) Inverse spinel: [Fe³⁺]_tet[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]_octO₄ → M_tet = 5, M_oct = 5+5 = 10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB.
(b) Normal spinel: [Mn²⁺]_tet[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]_octO₄ → M_tet = 5, M_oct = 5+5 = 10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB.
(c) 50:50 mixture: (5+5)/2 = 5 μB.

Because every cation (Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺) carries the same spin-only moment of 5 μB, the octahedral sublattice always holds two cations (10 μB) and the tetrahedral sublattice always holds one cation (5 μB), regardless of which cation sits where. Hence the net moment is arrangement-independent.

Independent verification (g-sci-independent-verify): instead of evaluating each cation arrangement, count ions per sublattice — octahedral always = 2 ions × 5 μB = 10, tetrahedral always = 1 ion × 5 μB = 5, net = 5 for all three cases. Code confirms: inverse=5, normal=5, mixed=5.0.

（执行证据）
MnFe₂O₄ inverse: M_oct=Mn²⁺+Fe³⁺=10, M_tet=Fe³⁺=5 → net 5 μB. Normal: M_oct=2·Fe³⁺=10, M_tet=Mn²⁺=5 → net 5 μB. 50:50 mixture → 5 μB. All cations share the same 5 μB spin-only moment, so the net moment is independent of cation distribution; Python confirms (5, 5, 5).

## 最终答案
(5,\ 5,\ 5)\ \mu_B
