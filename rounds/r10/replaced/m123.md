## 题目
The fatigue data for a brass alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude (MPa) | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| 170 | $3.7 \times 10^{4}$ |
| 148 | $1.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 130 | $3.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 114 | $1.0 \times 10^{6}$ |
| 92 | $1.0 \times 10^{7}$ |
| 80 | $1.0 \times 10^{8}$ |
| 74 | $1.0 \times 10^{9}$ |

Suppose that the fatigue data for the brass alloy were taken from bending-rotating tests and that a rod of this alloy is to be used for an automobile axle that rotates at an average rotational velocity of 1800 revolutions per minute. Give the maximum bending stress amplitude possible for each of the following lifetimes of the rod: (a) 1 year, (b) 1 month, (c) 1 day, and (d) 1 hour.

Give your answer as a tuple (maximum bending stress amplitude for 1 year, 1 month, 1 day, 1 hour).The units of each required answer are (MPa, MPa, MPa, MPa), respectively.

## 过程
## Reasoning

In rotating-bending fatigue, **one revolution = one stress cycle**. So the total cycles to failure for a given lifetime is:

$$N = (\text{rpm}) \times (\text{time in minutes})$$

with rpm = 1800.

| Lifetime | Minutes | N (cycles) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | 365×24×60 | 9.46×10⁸ |
| 1 month | 30×24×60 | 7.78×10⁷ |
| 1 day | 24×60 | 2.59×10⁶ |
| 1 hour | 60 | 1.08×10⁵ |

To get stress amplitude from N, I fit the S–N data on a **log σ – log N** basis (standard Basquin/log-linear treatment) and linearly interpolate between the tabulated points. Each computed N falls between two data points:

- 1 hour (N=1.08×10⁵): between 1.0×10⁵ (148 MPa) and 3.0×10⁵ (130 MPa) → **146.7 MPa**
- 1 day (N=2.59×10⁶): between 1.0×10⁶ (114 MPa) and 1.0×10⁷ (92 MPa) → **104.3 MPa**
- 1 month (N=7.78×10⁷): between 1.0×10⁷ (92 MPa) and 1.0×10⁸ (80 MPa) → **81.2 MPa**
- 1 year (N=9.46×10⁸): between 1.0×10⁸ (80 MPa) and 1.0×10⁹ (74 MPa) → **74.1 MPa**

$$\boxed{(74.1,\ 81.2,\ 104.3,\ 146.7)}$$

## 最终答案
(74.1,\ 81.2,\ 104.3,\ 146.7)
