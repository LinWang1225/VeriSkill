## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄, a spinel AB₂O₄. Per formula unit there is 1 tetrahedral (A) site and 2 octahedral (B) sites; the A and B sublattice moments order antiparallel (Néel ferrimagnetism), so net = |M_B − M_A|. Cation moments (table, 题面给定): Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB.

(a) Inverse spinel [Fe³⁺]_A[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]_B: M_A = 5, M_B = 5+5 = 10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB per f.u.
(b) Normal spinel [Mn²⁺]_A[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]_B: M_A = 5, M_B = 5+5 = 10 → net = |10−5| = 5 μB per f.u.
(c) 50:50 mixture: 0.5·5 + 0.5·5 = 5 μB per f.u. (the case is degenerate because Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ carry identical 5 μB moments, so every cation arrangement gives the same net per f.u.).

The problem asks for the moment 'of a unit cell'. Per skill g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling, a spinel conventional cubic unit cell contains Z = 8 formula units (standard crystallographic value, Callister Table for spinel structures); the per-formula-unit net moment must be scaled by Z to obtain the unit-cell moment. Unit-cell moment = 8 × 5 = 40 μB in all three cases. Python confirms: per f.u. (5, 5, 5); per unit cell (40, 40, 40). Inputs are exact integers (table values), so no sigfig rounding loss.

（执行证据）
Per formula unit, inverse = |10−5| = 5, normal = |10−5| = 5, mixed = 5 μB (Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ both 5 μB makes the arrangement degenerate). Problem asks 'of a unit cell'; spinel Z = 8 formula units per unit cell, so multiply by 8. Final: 8×5 = 40 μB for inverse, normal, and mixed alike → (40, 40, 40) μB.

## 最终答案
(40, 40, 40) \,\mu_B
