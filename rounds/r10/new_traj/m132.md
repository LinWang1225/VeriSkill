## 题目
The fraction recrystallized-time data for the recrystallization at $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ of a previously deformed aluminum are tabulated here. Assuming that the kinetics of this process obey the Avrami relationship, determine the fraction recrystallized after a total time of 116.8 min .

| Fraction Recrystallized | Time $(\mathbf{m i n})$ |
| :--: | :--: |
| 0.30 | 95.2 |
| 0.80 | 126.6 |

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-model-validity-range

## 过程
The Avrami relationship is y = 1 - exp(-k t^n), where y is the fraction recrystallized. Rearranging gives -ln(1-y) = k t^n. With the two data points (y1,t1)=(0.30,95.2) and (y2,t2)=(0.80,126.6): f1=-ln(0.70), f2=-ln(0.20). Dividing eliminates k: f2/f1 = (t2/t1)^n, so n = ln(f2/f1)/ln(t2/t1) = 5.286. Then k = f1/t1^n = 1.239e-11. Evaluating at t=116.8 min: y = 1 - exp(-k·t^n) = 0.6505. The interpolation point (116.8 min) lies between the two fitted times, so no extrapolation concern (model-validity check passes). The least-precise inputs (0.30, 0.80) have 2 significant figures, so the result is rounded to 2 sig figs: 0.65.

（执行证据）
Avrami fit gives n=5.286 and k=1.239e-11 from the two tabulated points. Substituting t=116.8 min yields y=1-exp(-k t^n)=0.6505. Rounded to 2 significant figures (limited by the 0.30/0.80 inputs), the fraction recrystallized is 0.65.

## 最终答案
0.65
