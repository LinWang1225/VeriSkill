## 题目
The planar density of the (112) plane in BCC iron is $9.94 \times 10^{14}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{cm}^{2}$. Calculate (a) the planar density of the (110) plane and (b) the interplanar spacings for both the (112) and (110) planes. The lattice parameter of BCC iron is 0.2866 nm or $2.866 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~cm}$. Give your answer as a tuple (planar density of (110) plane, interplanar spacing of (112) plane, interplanar spacing of (110) plane).The units of each required answer are (atoms/cm^2, cm, cm), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
BCC iron, a = 2.866e-8 cm. (110) plane: rectangle a × a√2, effective atoms = 4 corners×¼ + 1 body-center (lies on x+y=a) = 2. PD(110)=2/(a²√2)=1.722e15 atoms/cm². Verified convention against given PD(112): the (112) triangle (vertices (a,0,0),(0,a,0),(0,0,a/2)) has area a²√6/4=5.030e-16 cm²; 0.5 atom/area = 9.94e14, matching the given value exactly, confirming the effective-atom/area convention used for (110). Interplanar spacings via d_hkl=a/√(h²+k²+l²): d112=a/√6=1.1700e-8 cm; d110=a/√2=2.0266e-8 cm. Inputs have 4 sig fig (a=0.2866 nm); reported to 3 sig fig matching the given PD(112) precision: 1.72e15, 1.17e-8, 2.03e-8.

（执行证据）
PD(110)=2/(a²√2) with a=2.866e-8 cm gives 1.722e15 atoms/cm². Convention check: 0.5 atom over the (112) triangle area a²√6/4=5.030e-16 cm² reproduces the given 9.94e14 atoms/cm² exactly. d112=a/√6=1.170e-8 cm and d110=a/√2=2.027e-8 cm.

## 最终答案
(1.72\times10^{15},\ 1.17\times10^{-8},\ 2.03\times10^{-8})
