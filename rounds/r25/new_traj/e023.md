## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:

\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄, a spinel AB₂O₄ with Z = 8 formula units per cubic unit cell (standard crystallographic value listed in the skill). Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB and Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, and the tetrahedral (A) and octahedral (B) sublattice moments are antiparallel.

Per formula unit:
• Normal spinel [Mn²⁺]_tet[Fe³⁺₂]_oct: M_tet = 5, M_oct = 2×5 = 10 → net = |10 − 5| = 5 μB.
• Inverse spinel [Fe³⁺]_tet[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]_oct: M_tet = 5, M_oct = 5 + 5 = 10 → net = |10 − 5| = 5 μB.
• 50:50 mixture: 0.5×5 + 0.5×5 = 5 μB.

The problem asks for the moment 'of a unit cell'. The binding skill (g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling) flags exactly this case: a spinel unit cell contains Z = 8 formula units, so the unit-cell moment = per-formula-unit moment × Z. Multiplying each by 8 gives 40 μB. (Because Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ carry identical moments, the result is structure-independent.)

（执行证据）
Per formula unit, normal = |2·5 − 5| = 5 μB, inverse = |(5+5) − 5| = 5 μB, mixed = 5 μB. Spinel AB₂O₄ has Z = 8 formula units per unit cell, and the problem asks 'of a unit cell', so each is scaled by 8. Final: 8×5 = 40 μB for inverse, normal, and mixed alike.

## 最终答案
(40,\ 40,\ 40)
