## 题目
Yttria $\left(\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3\right)$ particles $750 \AA$ in diameter are introduced into tungsten by internal oxidation. Measurements using an electron microscope show that there are $5 \times 10^{14}$ oxide particles per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the $\mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Y}$ originally in the alloy. The density of $\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is $5.01 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of wt_percent_Y.The unit of the answer is %.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form
- g-sci-given-unit-faithful-conversion

## 过程
Particle radius r = 3.75e-6 cm; V_p = (4/3)πr³ = 2.209e-16 cm³. Oxide volume fraction f = N·V_p = 5e14 × 2.209e-16 = 0.1105 cm³ per cm³ alloy. Mass Y₂O₃ per cm³ = 0.1105×5.01 = 0.5536 g. Y mass fraction in Y₂O₃ = 2(88.91)/[2(88.91)+3(16)] = 177.82/225.82 = 0.7874. Mass Y per cm³ = 0.5536×0.7874 = 0.436 g. Tungsten matrix mass per cm³ = ρ_W = 19.3 g (standard density of W, the matrix named in the problem). wt% Y = m_Y/(m_Y+m_W)×100 = 0.436/(0.436+19.3)×100 = 2.21% → 2.2 (2 sig figs, limited by 5×10¹⁴ and 750).

（执行证据）
V_p = 2.209e-16 cm³; oxide volume fraction = 0.1105; m_Y₂O₃ = 0.5536 g per cm³; m_Y = 0.436 g per cm³ using Y mass fraction 0.7874 in Y₂O₃. With tungsten matrix mass 19.3 g per cm³, wt% Y = 0.436/(0.436+19.3)×100 = 2.21%, reported as 2.2.

## 最终答案
2.2
