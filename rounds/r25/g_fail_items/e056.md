## 过程
## Reasoning

For **parallel loading of continuous filaments**, the conductivity follows the **rule of mixtures** (Voigt bound):

$$\sigma_{\parallel} = V_{W}\,\sigma_{W} + V_{Cu}\,\sigma_{Cu}$$

**Volume fractions** (from the table: W 50 vol% in Cu):
- $V_W = 0.50$
- $V_{Cu} = 0.50$

**Conductivities at 20 °C** (standard handbook values):
- Copper: $\rho_{Cu} = 1.68\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m} \;\Rightarrow\; \sigma_{Cu} = 5.96\times10^{7}\ \Omega^{-1}\text{m}^{-1}$
- Tungsten: $\rho_{W} = 5.6\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m} \;\Rightarrow\; \sigma_{W} = 1.79\times10^{7}\ \Omega^{-1}\text{m}^{-1}$

**Calculation:**

$$\sigma_{\parallel} = 0.50(1.79\times10^{7}) + 0.50(5.96\times10^{7}) = 0.50(7.75\times10^{7}) \approx 3.87\times10^{7}\ \Omega^{-1}\text{m}^{-1}$$

$$\boxed{3.87\times10^{7}}$$

## 最终答案
3.87\times10^{7}
