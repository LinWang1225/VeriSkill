## 题目
A 4-in.-diameter aluminum bar solidifies to a depth of 0.5 in. beneath the surface in 5 minutes. After 20 minutes, the bar has solidified to a depth of 1.5 in. How much time is required for the bar to solidify completely?The unit of the answer is min.

## 过程
## Solving

From the two data points:
$$0.25 = 5a + b,\qquad 2.25 = 20a + b$$

Subtracting: $2.0 = 15a \Rightarrow a = \tfrac{2}{15}$, and $b = \tfrac{1}{4} - 5\!\cdot\!\tfrac{2}{15} = -\tfrac{5}{12}$.

So the solidification law is:
$$d^2 = \tfrac{2}{15}t - \tfrac{5}{12}$$

## Complete solidification

The 4-in.-diameter bar is fully solid when the solidification fronts meet at the center, i.e., when the depth reaches the radius, $d = 2$ in:

$$4 = \tfrac{2}{15}t - \tfrac{5}{12}$$

$$\tfrac{2}{15}t = 4 + \tfrac{5}{12} = \tfrac{53}{12}$$

$$t = \tfrac{53}{12}\cdot\tfrac{15}{2} = \tfrac{795}{24} = 33.125 \text{ min}$$

$$\boxed{33.125}$$

## 最终答案
33.125
