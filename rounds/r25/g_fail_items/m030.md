## 题目
An alloy is evaluated for potential creep deformation in a short-term laboratory experiment. The creep rate ( $\dot{\epsilon}$ ) is found to be $1 \%$ per hour at $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and $5.5 \times 10^{-2} \%$ per hour at $700^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. (a) Calculate the activation energy for creep in this temperature range. (b) Estimate the creep rate to be expected at a service temperature of $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. (c) What important assumption underlies the validity of your answer in part (b)? 

Give your answer as a tuple: (activation_energy, creep_rate, assumption).The units of each required answer are (kJ/mol, % per hour), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

Using the Arrhenius creep law $\dot{\varepsilon}=A\exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q}{RT}\right)$, the ratio of two measured rates eliminates $A$:

$$\ln\!\frac{\dot\varepsilon_1}{\dot\varepsilon_2}=\frac{Q}{R}\!\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)$$

with $T_1=1073\,\text{K}$ ($800°\text{C}$), $T_2=973\,\text{K}$ ($700°\text{C}$), $\dot\varepsilon_1=1$, $\dot\varepsilon_2=5.5\times10^{-2}$ %/hr.

**(a)** $Q = R\ln(\dot\varepsilon_1/\dot\varepsilon_2)\big/\bigl(1/T_2-1/T_1\bigr) \approx 251.8\ \text{kJ/mol}$.

**(b)** Extrapolating to $T_3=773\,\text{K}$ ($500°\text{C}$):

$$\dot\varepsilon_3=\dot\varepsilon_1\exp\!\left[-\frac{Q}{R}\!\left(\frac{1}{T_3}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)\right]\approx 1.75\times10^{-5}\ \%\!/\text{hr}.$$

**(c)** The extrapolation assumes the **same creep mechanism (and therefore the same activation energy $Q$) remains rate-controlling** over the entire temperature range from the laboratory data down to the service temperature of $500°\text{C}$ — i.e., no change in deformation mechanism (e.g., no transition from dislocation creep to diffusional/diffusion-controlled creep) and the pre-exponential $A$ stays constant.

$$\boxed{(251.76,\ 1.75\times10^{-5},\ \text{the same creep mechanism with the same activation energy remains rate-controlling over the entire temperature range down to }500^{\circ}\mathrm{C})}$$

## 最终答案
(251.76,\ 1.75\times10^{-5},\ \text{the same creep mechanism with the same activation energy remains rate-controlling over the entire temperature range down to }500^{\circ}\mathrm{C})
