## 题目
The planar density of the (112) plane in BCC iron is $9.94 \times 10^{14}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{cm}^{2}$. Calculate (a) the planar density of the (110) plane and (b) the interplanar spacings for both the (112) and (110) planes. The lattice parameter of BCC iron is 0.2866 nm or $2.866 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~cm}$. Give your answer as a tuple (planar density of (110) plane, interplanar spacing of (112) plane, interplanar spacing of (110) plane).The units of each required answer are (atoms/cm^2, cm, cm), respectively.

## 过程
## Solution

**Interplanar spacings** (cubic, BCC):
$$d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}, \quad a = 2.866\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$$

- $d_{112} = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{6}} = 1.17\times10^{-8}$ cm
- $d_{110} = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = 2.03\times10^{-8}$ cm

**Planar density of (110):** The (110) plane in BCC is a rectangle of sides $a$ and $a\sqrt{2}$ (area $= a^2\sqrt{2}$) containing 4 corner atoms × ¼ + 1 body-center atom = 2 atoms:

$$\text{PD}_{110} = \frac{2}{a^2\sqrt{2}} = 1.72\times10^{15}\ \text{atoms/cm}^2$$

**Check:** $\text{PD}_{112} = \dfrac{2}{a^2\sqrt{6}} = 9.94\times10^{14}$ ✓ (matches the given value).

$$\boxed{\left(1.72\times10^{15},\ 1.17\times10^{-8},\ 2.03\times10^{-8}\right)}$$

## 最终答案
\left(1.72\times10^{15},\ 1.17\times10^{-8},\ 2.03\times10^{-8}\right)
