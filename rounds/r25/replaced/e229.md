## 题目
Above $882^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, titanium has a BCC crystal structure, with $a=0.332 \mathrm{~nm}$. Below this temperature, titanium has a HCP structure with $a=0.2978 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4735 \mathrm{~nm}$. Determine the percent volume change when BCC titanium transforms to HCP titanium. Is this a contraction or expansion? Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_volume_change, transformation_type).The unit of the answer is (%, ).

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-matsci-percent-basis-reference
- g-matsci-sigfig-rounding

## 过程
Volume change on BCC→HCP must be compared per atom (same amount of substance). BCC: conventional cell V=a³ with Z=2, so v_BCC=a³/2=(0.332)³/2=0.0182972 nm³. HCP: the (V_cell,Z) pair must be consistent. Density check with Ti (ρ≈4.5 g/cm³, M=47.867): pairing (V=√3/2·a²c, Z=6) gives ρ=13.11 g/cm³ (WRONG); pairings (V=√3/2·a²c, Z=2) and (V=3√3/2·a²c, Z=6) both give ρ=4.37 g/cm³ (correct). Using the consistent per-atom volume v_HCP=(√3/2·a²c)/2=(√3/2·0.2978²·0.4735)/2=0.0181832 nm³. Percent basis: transformation is BCC→HCP, so the reference (denominator) is the INITIAL state = BCC: ΔV%=(v_HCP−v_BCC)/v_BCC×100=(0.0181832−0.0182972)/0.0182972×100=−0.6230%. Negative ⇒ contraction. Sig figs: least-precise input a_BCC=0.332 has 3 sf, so report 3 sf → −0.623%.

（执行证据）
Per-atom volumes: v_BCC=(0.332)³/2=0.0182972 nm³; v_HCP=(√3/2·0.2978²·0.4735)/2=0.0181832 nm³ (using consistent Z=2 with V=√3/2·a²c, verified by ρ=4.37 g/cm³ matching Ti). Percent change relative to initial BCC: (v_HCP−v_BCC)/v_BCC×100 = −0.623%, a contraction.

## 最终答案
(-0.623, contraction)
