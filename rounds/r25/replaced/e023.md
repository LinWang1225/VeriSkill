## 题目
Many commercial ferrites can be prepared in a combination of both normal and inverse spinel structures. Given Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB, calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite if it occurs in (a) an inverse spinel, (b) a normal spinel, or (c) a 50:50 mixture of inverse and normal spinel structures. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (inverse_spinel_moment, normal_spinel_moment, mixed_spinel_moment).

Refer to Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{2}{|r|}{Magnetic Moment of Various Transition Metal Ions} \\
\hline Ion & Moment ( $\left.\mu_B\right)^{\text {a }}$ \\
\hline $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ & 4 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ & 5 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Co}^{2+}$ & 3 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ & 2 \\
\hline $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}The units of each required answer are (μB, μB, μB), respectively.

## 激活技能
- g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling
- g-sci-explicit-assumptions
- g-sci-answer-final-numeric-form

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is MnFe₂O₄, a spinel AB₂O₄ with Z = 8 formula units per unit cell (spinel, per g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling table). Given moments: Mn²⁺ = 5 μB, Fe³⁺ = 5 μB (题面/table). In Néel ordering the tetrahedral (A) and octahedral (B) sublattice moments are antiparallel, so net per formula unit = |M_B − M_A|.

(a) Inverse spinel Fe³⁺[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]O₄: A = Fe³⁺ = 5; B = Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺ = 5 + 5 = 10; net per f.u. = |10 − 5| = 5 μB → unit cell = 5 × 8 = 40 μB.
(b) Normal spinel Mn²⁺[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]O₄: A = Mn²⁺ = 5; B = 2·Fe³⁺ = 10; net per f.u. = |10 − 5| = 5 μB → unit cell = 5 × 8 = 40 μB.
(c) 50:50 mixture = 0.5·40 + 0.5·40 = 40 μB.

All three coincide because every ion carries 5 μB, so any A/B partition leaves one ion on A and two on B per formula unit, giving net 5 μB × Z=8 = 40 μB. Code output: 40, 40, 40.0.

（执行证据）
Spinel Z = 8 formula units/unit cell (g-matsci-unitcell-formula-unit-scaling). Inverse Fe³⁺[Mn²⁺Fe³⁺]O₄ gives |(5+5)−5|×8 = 40 μB; normal Mn²⁺[Fe³⁺Fe³⁺]O₄ gives |10−5|×8 = 40 μB; 50:50 mixture averages to 40 μB. All three equal 40 because Mn²⁺ and Fe³⁺ both carry 5 μB, so the A/B partition is immaterial.

## 最终答案
(40, 40, 40)
