## 题目
Estimate the plasma frequency \(\left(v_{p}\right)\) of lithium metal using the atomic density of lithium metal.

Give your final answer in units of Hz.
Note: *   Atomic density of lithium metal

## 过程
The plasma frequency (angular) is

$$\omega_p = \sqrt{\frac{n e^2}{\varepsilon_0 m_e}}$$

where \(n\) is the free-electron density. Lithium has one valence electron per atom (Z = 1), so \(n\) equals the atomic density.

Lithium metal is BCC with lattice parameter \(a = 3.51\ \text{Å} = 3.51\times10^{-10}\ \text{m}\), giving 2 atoms per unit cell:

$$n = \frac{2}{a^3} = \frac{2}{(3.51\times10^{-10})^3} \approx 4.625\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$

Substituting \(e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\ \text{C}\), \(\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}\ \text{F/m}\), \(m_e = 9.109\times10^{-31}\ \text{kg}\):

$$\omega_p = \sqrt{\frac{(4.625\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})^2}{(8.854\times10^{-12})(9.109\times10^{-31})}} \approx 1.213\times10^{16}\ \text{rad/s}$$

Converting to ordinary frequency:

$$\nu_p = \frac{\omega_p}{2\pi} \approx 1.93\times10^{15}\ \text{Hz}$$

## 最终答案
1.93e15
