## 题目
$\alpha$-titanium at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ has an hcp crystal structure with $a=0.2950 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $c=0.4683 \mathrm{~nm}$ at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. At $900^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, it transforms to $\beta$-titanium with a bcc crystal structure with $a=0.332$. The average coefficient of thermal expansion of $\alpha$-titanium is $8.4 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$. Calculate the $\%$ volume change as $\beta$-titanium transforms to $\alpha$-titanium on cooling.

Give your final answer in units of %.

## 过程
To compare volumes, we use **volume per atom** since the unit cells contain different numbers of atoms (hcp: 6 atoms; bcc: 2 atoms).

**1. α-Ti (hcp) volume at 20°C:**
$$V_{\alpha,20} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^2 c = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}(0.2950)^2(0.4683)$$
Per atom: $V_{\alpha,20}^{\text{atom}} = V_{\alpha,20}/6$.

**2. Thermally expand α-Ti to 900°C:**
Volumetric expansion coefficient $= 3\alpha_L = 3(8.4\times10^{-6})$, $\Delta T = 880\,\text{K}$:
$$V_{\alpha,900}^{\text{atom}} = V_{\alpha,20}^{\text{atom}}\bigl(1 + 3\alpha_L\Delta T\bigr)$$

**3. β-Ti (bcc) volume at 900°C:**
$$V_{\beta,900} = a^3 = (0.332)^3,\quad V_{\beta,900}^{\text{atom}} = V_{\beta,900}/2$$

**4. % volume change (β → α on cooling):**
$$\%\Delta V = \frac{V_{\alpha,900}^{\text{atom}} - V_{\beta,900}^{\text{atom}}}{V_{\beta,900}^{\text{atom}}}\times 100$$

Numeric evaluation: $V_{\alpha,900}^{\text{atom}} = 0.018038\,\text{nm}^3$, $V_{\beta,900}^{\text{atom}} = 0.018297\,\text{nm}^3$, giving $\%\Delta V = -1.415\%$.

## 最终答案
-1.415
