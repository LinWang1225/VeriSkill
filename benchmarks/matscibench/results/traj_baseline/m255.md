## 题目
An aluminum casting is made using the permanent mold process. In this process, the liquid aluminum is poured into a gray cast iron mold that is heated to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. We wish to produce an aluminum casting that is 15 in . long at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Calculate the length of the cavity that must be machined into the gray cast iron mold.
Give your final answer in units of m.

## 过程
The cavity is machined into the gray cast iron mold at room temperature (25°C). When the mold is heated to 350°C, the cavity expands. Liquid aluminum fills this expanded cavity and solidifies, so the solid aluminum at 350°C has the same length as the cavity at 350°C. The aluminum then contracts thermally as it cools from 350°C to 25°C, where we want it to be 15 in.

Let $L_c$ = cavity length machined at 25°C, $\Delta T = 350 - 25 = 325°C$.

Mold cavity expands on heating:
$$L_{c}(350) = L_c\,(1 + \alpha_{\text{Fe}}\Delta T)$$

Solid aluminum contracts on cooling to 25°C:
$$L_{\text{Al}}(25) = L_c(350)\,(1 - \alpha_{\text{Al}}\Delta T)$$

Setting $L_{\text{Al}}(25) = 15\text{ in}$:
$$L_c = \frac{15}{(1+\alpha_{\text{Fe}}\Delta T)(1-\alpha_{\text{Al}}\Delta T)}$$

Using $\alpha_{\text{Al}} = 23.6\times10^{-6}/°C$, $\alpha_{\text{Fe}} = 11.0\times10^{-6}/°C$:

- $\alpha_{\text{Fe}}\Delta T = 0.003575$, $\alpha_{\text{Al}}\Delta T = 0.00767$
- $L_c = \dfrac{15}{1.003575 \times 0.99233} = 15.062\text{ in}$

Converting to meters: $15.062 \times 0.0254 = 0.3826\text{ m}$.

## 最终答案
0.3826
