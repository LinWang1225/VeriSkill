## 题目
The fatigue data for a brass alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude (MPa) | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| 170 | $3.7 \times 10^{4}$ |
| 148 | $1.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 130 | $3.0 \times 10^{5}$ |
| 114 | $1.0 \times 10^{6}$ |
| 92 | $1.0 \times 10^{7}$ |
| 80 | $1.0 \times 10^{8}$ |
| 74 | $1.0 \times 10^{9}$ |

Suppose that the fatigue data for the brass alloy were taken from bending-rotating tests and that a rod of this alloy is to be used for an automobile axle that rotates at an average rotational velocity of 1800 revolutions per minute. Give the maximum bending stress amplitude possible for each of the following lifetimes of the rod: (a) 1 year, (b) 1 month, (c) 1 day, and (d) 1 hour.

Give your answer as a tuple (maximum bending stress amplitude for 1 year, 1 month, 1 day, 1 hour).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa, MPa, MPa).

## 过程
The rod rotates at 1800 rev/min, so the number of cycles to failure for a given lifetime is

$$N_f = 1800\;\text{(cycles/min)} \times t\;\text{(min)}.$$

Lifetimes in minutes and corresponding $N_f$:

| Lifetime | $t$ (min) | $N_f$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | 525,600 | $9.46\times10^{8}$ |
| 1 month | 43,200 | $7.78\times10^{7}$ |
| 1 day | 1,440 | $2.59\times10^{6}$ |
| 1 hour | 60 | $1.08\times10^{5}$ |

To find the stress amplitude for each $N_f$, perform a **log–log linear interpolation** between the two bracketing data points on the S–N curve:

$$\log S = \log S_1 + \frac{\log N_f - \log N_1}{\log N_2 - \log N_1}(\log S_2 - \log S_1).$$

Brackets and results:

- **1 year**: $N_f=9.46\times10^{8}$ between $(80\text{ MPa},10^{8})$ and $(74\text{ MPa},10^{9})$ → $S=74.14$ MPa
- **1 month**: $N_f=7.78\times10^{7}$ between $(92\text{ MPa},10^{7})$ and $(80\text{ MPa},10^{8})$ → $S=81.23$ MPa
- **1 day**: $N_f=2.59\times10^{6}$ between $(114\text{ MPa},10^{6})$ and $(92\text{ MPa},10^{7})$ → $S=104.33$ MPa
- **1 hour**: $N_f=1.08\times10^{5}$ between $(148\text{ MPa},10^{5})$ and $(130\text{ MPa},3\times10^{5})$ → $S=146.66$ MPa

## 最终答案
(74.14, 81.23, 104.33, 146.66)
