## 题目
ZnS has the zinc blende structure. If the density is $3.02 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and the lattice parameter is 0.59583 nm , determine the number of Schottky defects (a) per unit cell; and (b) per cubic centimeter. (d) $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ ions replace sodium ions in NaCl . Give your answer as a tuple: (defects_per_unit_cell, defects_per_cubic_centimeter).
Give your final answer in units of (defects/unit cell, defects/cm³).

## 过程
ZnS zinc blende: 4 formula units per unit cell (4 Zn²⁺ + 4 S²⁻). Molar mass M(ZnS) = 65.38 + 32.06 = 97.44 g/mol.

Theoretical (defect-free) density:

$$\rho_0 = \frac{n M}{N_A a^3},\quad n=4,\ a = 0.59583\,\text{nm}=5.9583\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$$

A Schottky defect in ZnS removes one Zn²⁺ and one S²⁻ (one formula unit's mass) while keeping stoichiometry. If $n_s$ = Schottky defects per unit cell:

$$\frac{\rho}{\rho_0} = \frac{4 - n_s}{4}\ \Rightarrow\ n_s = 4\!\left(1 - \frac{\rho}{\rho_0}\right)$$

Numerically: $\rho_0 = 3.0598$ g/cm³, so

$$n_s = 4\left(1 - \frac{3.02}{3.0598}\right) \approx 0.0520\ \text{defects/unit cell}$$

Defects per cm³:

$$\frac{n_s}{a^3} = \frac{0.0520}{(5.9583\times10^{-8})^3} \approx 2.46\times10^{20}\ \text{defects/cm}^3$$

(The Fe²⁺/NaCl clause is extraneous to the requested tuple.)

## 最终答案
(0.0520, 2.46e20)
