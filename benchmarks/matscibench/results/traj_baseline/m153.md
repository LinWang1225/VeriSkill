## 题目
The two ends of a cylindrical rod of nickel 120.00 mm long and 12.000 mm in diameter are maintained rigid. If the rod is initially at $70^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, to what temperature must it be cooled in order to have a $0.023-\mathrm{mm}$ reduction in diameter? The values of $v$ and $\alpha{l}$ for nickel are 0.31 and $13.3 \times 10^{-6}\left({ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)^{-1}$, respectively.
Give your final answer in units of ${}^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$.

## 过程
$$\Delta T = \frac{-0.023}{(12.000)(0.31)(13.3\times10^{-6})} \approx -464.87\;^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$$

$$T_f = 70 + (-464.87) \approx -394.87\;^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$$

## 最终答案
-394.87
