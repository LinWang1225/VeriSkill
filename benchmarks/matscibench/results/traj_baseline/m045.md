## 题目
In designing a solid-state device using B-doped Si, it is important that the conductivity not increase more than $10 \%$ (relative to the value at room temperature) during the operating lifetime. For this factor alone, what is the maximum operating temperature to be specified for this design? Give your answer as the value of maximum_operating_temperature.
Give your final answer in units of K.

## 过程
For a p-type (B-doped) Si in the extrinsic regime, the room-temperature conductivity is dominated by holes:

$$\sigma_{300} \approx q\,\mu_p\,N_A$$

As temperature rises, the intrinsic carrier concentration $n_i(T)$ grows exponentially and eventually the material begins to go intrinsic. With full acceptor ionization, charge neutrality gives

$$p=\frac{N_A+\sqrt{N_A^2+4n_i^2}}{2},\qquad n=\frac{-N_A+\sqrt{N_A^2+4n_i^2}}{2}$$

and

$$\sigma(T)=q(\mu_n n+\mu_p p).$$

The intrinsic carrier concentration follows

$$n_i(T)=n_i(300)\left(\frac{T}{300}\right)^{3/2}\exp\!\left[-\frac{E_g}{2k}\left(\frac1T-\frac1{300}\right)\right],$$

with $E_g=1.11\ \text{eV}$, $k=8.62\times10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}$, $n_i(300)=1.5\times10^{16}\ \text{m}^{-3}$, $\mu_n=0.1350$, $\mu_p=0.0480\ \text{m}^2/\text{V·s}$.

Using the standard Callister B-doped Si acceptor concentration $N_A=10^{21}\ \text{m}^{-3}$ (giving $\sigma_{300}=q\mu_pN_A\approx 7.69\ (\Omega·\text{m})^{-1}$), the design limit is

$$\frac{\sigma(T_{\max})}{\sigma_{300}}=1.10.$$

Solving numerically (brentq on $[300,1000]$ K):

- $\sigma(300)/\sigma_{300}=1.0000$
- $\sigma(T_{\max})/\sigma_{300}=1.1000$ at $T_{\max}=498.23\ \text{K}$.

The conductivity reaches exactly 110% of its room-temperature value at this temperature; above it the exponential growth of $n_i$ would violate the 10% lifetime budget.

## 最终答案
498.23
