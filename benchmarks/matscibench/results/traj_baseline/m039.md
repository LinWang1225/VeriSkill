## 题目
Estimate the density of a cobalt-chrome alloy used for artificial-hip joints as a weighted average of the densities of constituent elements: $50 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Co}$, $20 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cr}, 15 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{~W}$, and $15 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Ni}$. Give your answer as the value of density.
Give your final answer in units of Mg/m^3.

## 过程
The density is estimated as a weight-fraction-weighted average of the elemental densities (in g/cm³ ≡ Mg/m³):

- Co: ρ = 8.90, w = 0.50
- Cr: ρ = 7.19, w = 0.20
- W: ρ = 19.30, w = 0.15
- Ni: ρ = 8.90, w = 0.15

$$\bar{\rho} = \sum w_i \rho_i = 0.50(8.90) + 0.20(7.19) + 0.15(19.30) + 0.15(8.90)$$

$$= 4.45 + 1.438 + 2.895 + 1.335 = 10.118$$

## 最终答案
10.12
