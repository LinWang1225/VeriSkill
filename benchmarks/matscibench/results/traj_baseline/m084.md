## 题目
Iron (Fe) and vanadium (V) both crystallize in the body-centered cubic (BCC) structure. Vanadium forms a substitutional solid solution with iron up to approximately 20 weight percent at room temperature. Given a $90~\mathrm{wt}\%~\mathrm{Fe} - 10~\mathrm{wt}\%~\mathrm{V}$ alloy, compute the unit cell edge length (in nanometers) of this alloy.

Atomic radius of Fe: $0.124~\mathrm{nm}$
Atomic radius of V: $0.132~\mathrm{nm}$
Density of Fe: $7.87~\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
Density of V: $6.10~\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
Atomic weight of Fe: $55.85~\mathrm{g/mol}$
Atomic weight of V: $50.94~\mathrm{g/mol}$
Avogadro’s number: $6.022 \times 10^{23}~\mathrm{atoms/mol}$
Give your final answer in units of nm.

## 过程
For a substitutional solid solution of two BCC metals, the alloy's lattice parameter is found from the composition-weighted average atomic radius, using **atom fractions** (not weight fractions).

**1. Convert wt% → at%**

$$C_{\text{Fe}}=\frac{w_{\text{Fe}}/A_{\text{Fe}}}{w_{\text{Fe}}/A_{\text{Fe}}+w_{\text{V}}/A_{\text{V}}},\qquad C_{\text{V}}=\frac{w_{\text{V}}/A_{\text{V}}}{w_{\text{Fe}}/A_{\text{Fe}}+w_{\text{V}}/A_{\text{V}}}$$

With $w_{\text{Fe}}=0.90,\;w_{\text{V}}=0.10,\;A_{\text{Fe}}=55.85,\;A_{\text{V}}=50.94$:

$$C_{\text{Fe}}=0.8914,\qquad C_{\text{V}}=0.1086$$

**2. Average atomic radius (Vegard-type rule on radii)**

$$\bar R = C_{\text{Fe}}R_{\text{Fe}}+C_{\text{V}}R_{\text{V}} = 0.8914(0.124)+0.1086(0.132)=0.12487~\text{nm}$$

**3. BCC lattice parameter**

In BCC the atoms touch along the body diagonal: $\sqrt{3}\,a=4R$, so

$$a=\frac{4\bar R}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{4(0.12487)}{1.73205}=0.28837~\text{nm}$$

## 最终答案
0.2884
