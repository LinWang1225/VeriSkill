## 题目
Assuming that a turbine blade made of Nimonic 105 alloy had a life of $10,000 \mathrm{~h}$ at 150 MPa with a service temperature of $810^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, what is the expected life at the same stress but with a service temperature of $750^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ?  Give your answer as the value of expected_life.
Give your final answer in units of h.

## 过程
For creep rupture of Nimonic 105, use the Larson–Miller parameter at constant stress:

$$P = T(C + \log_{10} t_r) = \text{constant}$$

with the standard Larson–Miller constant for superalloys $C = 20$, $T$ in kelvin, $t_r$ in hours.

Given $t_1 = 10{,}000\ \text{h}$ at $T_1 = 810 + 273 = 1083\ \text{K}$, and $T_2 = 750 + 273 = 1023\ \text{K}$:

$$T_1(C + \log_{10} t_1) = T_2(C + \log_{10} t_2)$$

$$1083(20 + 4) = 1023(20 + \log_{10} t_2)$$

$$\log_{10} t_2 = \frac{1083 \times 24}{1023} - 20 = 5.4076$$

$$t_2 = 10^{5.4076} \approx 2.56 \times 10^{5}\ \text{h}$$

## 最终答案
255637.54
