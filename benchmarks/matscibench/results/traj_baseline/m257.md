## 题目
A 2-in.-inside-diameter, 12-ft-long copper distribution pipe in a plumbing system is accidentally connected to the power system of a manufacturing plant, causing a current of 0.05 A to flow through the pipe. If the wall thickness of the pipe is 0.125 in ., estimate the time required before the pipe begins to leak, assuming a uniform rate of corrosion.
Give your final answer in units of years.

## 过程
The pipe leaks when the entire wall thickness of copper has been uniformly dissolved by the stray current.

**Geometry of the wall to be consumed:**
- Inside diameter $D_i = 2$ in, wall thickness $= 0.125$ in → outside diameter $D_o = 2.25$ in.
- Length $L = 12$ ft.
- Volume of copper in the wall:
$$V = \frac{\pi}{4}(D_o^2 - D_i^2)L$$
Converting to cm: $D_i = 5.08$ cm, $D_o = 5.715$ cm, $L = 365.76$ cm.
$$V = \frac{\pi}{4}(5.715^2 - 5.08^2)(365.76) = 1969.2\ \text{cm}^3$$

**Mass of copper to dissolve** (using $\rho_{\text{Cu}} = 8.96\ \text{g/cm}^3$):
$$m = \rho V = 8.96 \times 1969.2 = 17643.7\ \text{g}$$

**Faraday's law of electrolysis** (copper dissolves as $\text{Cu}^{2+}$, so $n=2$):
$$m = \frac{I\,t\,M}{nF} \;\Rightarrow\; t = \frac{m\,n\,F}{I\,M}$$
with $M = 63.55$ g/mol, $F = 96485$ C/mol, $I = 0.05$ A:
$$t = \frac{17643.7 \times 2 \times 96485}{0.05 \times 63.55} = 1.0715\times10^{9}\ \text{s}$$

**Convert to years:**
$$t = \frac{1.0715\times10^{9}}{365.25\times24\times3600} \approx 33.95\ \text{years}$$

## 最终答案
33.95
