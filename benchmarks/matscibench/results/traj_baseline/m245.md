## 题目
Yttria $\left(\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3\right)$ particles $750 \AA$ in diameter are introduced into tungsten by internal oxidation. Measurements using an electron microscope show that there are $5 \times 10^{14}$ oxide particles per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the $\mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Y}$ originally in the alloy. The density of $\mathrm{Y}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is $5.01 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of wt_percent_Y.
Give your final answer in units of %.

## 过程
1. **Particle volume:** diameter $d = 750\,\text{Å} = 7.5\times10^{-6}\,\text{cm}$, so $r = 3.75\times10^{-6}\,\text{cm}$.
$$V_p = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 2.209\times10^{-16}\,\text{cm}^3$$

2. **Total oxide volume per cm³ of alloy:** $N = 5\times10^{14}\,\text{cm}^{-3}$
$$V_{\text{tot}} = N V_p = 0.1104\,\text{cm}^3/\text{cm}^3$$

3. **Mass of Y₂O₃ per cm³:** $\rho_{\text{Y}_2\text{O}_3}=5.01\,\text{g/cm}^3$
$$m_{\text{Y}_2\text{O}_3} = V_{\text{tot}}\cdot\rho = 0.5533\,\text{g}$$

4. **Mass of Y in the oxide:** $M_{\text{Y}_2\text{O}_3}=2(88.906)+3(16.00)=225.81$, mass fraction of Y $= 177.81/225.81 = 0.7874$
$$m_{\text{Y}} = 0.5533\times0.7874 = 0.4357\,\text{g}$$

5. **Mass of W per cm³:** $\rho_{\text{W}} = 19.3\,\text{g/cm}^3$ (tungsten density).

6. **wt% Y:**
$$\text{wt\% Y} = \frac{m_{\text{Y}}}{m_{\text{Y}}+m_{\text{W}}}\times100 = \frac{0.4357}{0.4357+19.3}\times100 = 2.208\%$$

## 最终答案
2.21
