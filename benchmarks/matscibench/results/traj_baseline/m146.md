## 题目
For each of the metals listed in the following table, compute the Pilling-Bedworth ratio. Also, on the basis of this value, specify whether you would expect the oxide scale that forms on the surface to be protective, and then justify your decision. Density data for both the metal and its oxide are also tabulated. Give your answer as a tuple: (Pilling-Bedworth ratios for Mg,Pilling-Bedworth ratios for V,Pilling-Bedworth ratios for Zn).

| Metal | Metal Density <br> $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ | Metal Oxide | Oxide Density <br> $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| $M g$ | 1.74 | $M g O$ | 3.58 |
| $V$ | 6.11 | $V{2} O{3}$ | 3.36 |
| Zn | 7.13 | ZnO | 5.61 |

## 过程
The Pilling–Bedworth ratio is

$$\mathrm{PBR}=\frac{M_{\text{oxide}}\,\rho_{\text{metal}}}{n\,M_{\text{metal}}\,\rho_{\text{oxide}}}$$

where $n$ is the number of metal atoms per oxide formula unit.

- **Mg → MgO** ($n=1$): $M_{\text{Mg}}=24.305$, $M_{\text{MgO}}=40.305$
  $\mathrm{PBR}=\dfrac{40.305\times1.74}{1\times24.305\times3.58}=0.806$
  PBR < 1 → oxide volume is smaller than the metal consumed, so the film is porous/non-continuous → **not protective**.

- **V → V₂O₃** ($n=2$): $M_{\text{V}}=50.94$, $M_{\text{V}_2\text{O}_3}=149.88$
  $\mathrm{PBR}=\dfrac{149.88\times6.11}{2\times50.94\times3.36}=2.68$
  PBR > 2 → oxide is too voluminous, builds up large compressive stresses that crack/spall → **not protective**.

- **Zn → ZnO** ($n=1$): $M_{\text{Zn}}=65.38$, $M_{\text{ZnO}}=81.38$
  $\mathrm{PBR}=\dfrac{81.38\times7.13}{1\times65.38\times5.61}=1.58$
  1 < PBR < 2 → oxide volume slightly exceeds metal consumed, giving a continuous, adherent, crack-free film → **protective**.

## 最终答案
(0.81, 2.68, 1.58)
