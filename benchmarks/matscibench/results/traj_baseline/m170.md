## 题目
Aluminum alloy 7075-T6 (yield strength $Y_{\text{Al}} = 511$ MPa, density $\rho_{\text{Al}} = 2.7$ g/cm$^3$) and Ti-6Al-4V (yield strength $Y_{\text{Ti}} = 939$ MPa, density $\rho_{\text{Ti}} = 4.5$ g/cm$^3$) are being considered for a 200 mm long tensile member carrying a load of 50 kN. Assume a factor of safety of 1.5.

Which material results in a lighter tensile member, and which material results in a lower embodied-energy impact?

Use the following specific embodied energies:  
Aluminum: 193.5 MJ/kg  
Titanium: 915 MJ/kg

Give your answer as a tuple $(m_{\text{Al}},\ m_{\text{Ti}},\ E_{\text{Al}},\ E_{\text{Ti}})$  

Give your final answer in units of (g, g, MJ, MJ).

## 过程
For a tensile member, the allowable stress is $\sigma_{\text{allow}} = Y/\text{FS}$, and the required cross-sectional area is

$$A = \frac{P\cdot \text{FS}}{Y}$$

The mass is $m = \rho A L$, and the embodied energy is $E = m \cdot e$ (with $m$ in kg).

**Aluminum 7075-T6** ($Y=511$ MPa, $\rho=2.7$ g/cm³):
- $A_{\text{Al}} = \dfrac{50000 \times 1.5}{511\times10^{6}} = 1.468\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$
- $m_{\text{Al}} = 2700 \times 1.468\times10^{-4} \times 0.2 = 0.07926\ \text{kg} = 79.26\ \text{g}$
- $E_{\text{Al}} = 0.07926 \times 193.5 = 15.34\ \text{MJ}$

**Ti-6Al-4V** ($Y=939$ MPa, $\rho=4.5$ g/cm³):
- $A_{\text{Ti}} = \dfrac{50000 \times 1.5}{939\times10^{6}} = 7.987\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2$
- $m_{\text{Ti}} = 4500 \times 7.987\times10^{-5} \times 0.2 = 0.07188\ \text{kg} = 71.88\ \text{g}$
- $E_{\text{Ti}} = 0.07188 \times 915 = 65.77\ \text{MJ}$

Comparison: Titanium gives the **lighter** member (71.88 g < 79.26 g), while Aluminum has the **lower embodied-energy impact** (15.34 MJ < 65.77 MJ).

## 最终答案
(79.2564, 71.8850, 15.3361, 65.7748)
