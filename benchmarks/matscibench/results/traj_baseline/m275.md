## 题目
Question: One-half of an electrochemical cell consists of a pure nickel electrode in a solution of \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) ions; the other half is a cadmium electrode immersed in a \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) solution.
(a) If the cell is a standard one, write the spontaneous overall reaction and calculate the voltage that is generated.
(b) Compute the cell potential at \(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\) if the \(\mathrm{Cd}^{2+}\) and \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) concentrations are 0.5 and \(10^{-3} M\), respectively.
Give your answer as a tuple (voltage for part a, cell potential for part b).
Give your final answer in units of (V, V).

## 过程
Standard reduction potentials: Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni, E° = −0.25 V; Cd²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cd, E° = −0.40 V.

For spontaneity the half-cell with the more positive potential reduces (cathode), the more negative oxidizes (anode). Thus Ni²⁺/Ni is the cathode and Cd/Cd²⁺ is the anode.

(a) Cathode: Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni; Anode: Cd → Cd²⁺ + 2e⁻. Overall spontaneous reaction:

$$\mathrm{Ni^{2+}} + \mathrm{Cd} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ni} + \mathrm{Cd^{2+}}$$

$$E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cathode}} - E^\circ_{\text{anode}} = (-0.25) - (-0.40) = 0.15\ \text{V}$$

(b) Nernst equation at 25°C with n = 2 and reaction quotient Q = [Cd²⁺]/[Ni²⁺] = 0.5/10⁻³ = 500:

$$E = E^\circ - \frac{RT}{nF}\ln Q = 0.15 - \frac{8.314\times298.15}{2\times96485}\ln(500)$$

Numerically: (RT/nF)ln Q = 0.012847 × 6.2146 = 0.07983 V, so E = 0.15 − 0.07983 = 0.0702 V.

## 最终答案
(0.15, 0.0702)
