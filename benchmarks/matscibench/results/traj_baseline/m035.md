## 题目
For an aluminum alloy, the activation energy for crystal growth is $120 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$. By what factor would the rate of crystal growth change by dropping the alloy temperature from $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to room temperature $\left(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$ ? Give your answer as the value of growth_rate_factor.

## 过程
The rate of crystal growth follows an Arrhenius relationship:

$$\text{rate} \propto \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT}\right)$$

The factor by which the rate changes when dropping from $T_1=500^{\circ}\mathrm{C}=773\,\mathrm{K}$ to $T_2=25^{\circ}\mathrm{C}=298\,\mathrm{K}$ is

$$\text{factor}=\frac{\exp(-Q/RT_2)}{\exp(-Q/RT_1)}=\exp\!\left[-\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)\right]$$

with $Q=120\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}=120000\,\mathrm{J/mol}$ and $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$.

Evaluating:

$$-\frac{120000}{8.314}\left(\frac{1}{298}-\frac{1}{773}\right)\approx -29.77$$

$$\text{factor}=e^{-29.77}\approx 1.19\times10^{-13}$$

## 最终答案
1.186644e-13
