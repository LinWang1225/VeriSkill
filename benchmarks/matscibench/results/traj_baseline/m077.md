## 题目
A hypothetical metal has the BCC crystal structure, a density of $7.24 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, and an atomic weight of 48.9 $\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{mol}$. What is the atomic radius of this metal?
Give your final answer in units of nm.

## 过程
For a BCC crystal structure, there are $n=2$ atoms per unit cell. The density relation is

$$\rho = \frac{n A}{V_c N_A}, \qquad V_c = a^3$$

Solving for the lattice parameter:

$$a^3 = \frac{n A}{\rho N_A} = \frac{2(48.9)}{(7.24)(6.022\times10^{23})}$$

$$a = 2.820\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm}$$

For BCC, the body diagonal relates to the atomic radius by $4R = a\sqrt{3}$, so

$$R = \frac{a\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{(2.820\times10^{-8})(1.732)}{4} = 1.221\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm}$$

Converting to nm ($1\ \text{cm}=10^{7}\ \text{nm}$):

$$R = 0.1221\ \text{nm}$$

## 最终答案
0.1221
