## 题目
Calculate the number of Frenkel defects per cubic meter in silver chloride at $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The energy for defect formation is 1.1 eV , whereas the density for AgCl is $5.50 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$ at $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$.
Give your final answer in units of defects/m^3.

## 过程
For Frenkel defects, the equilibrium concentration is

$$N_{Fr} = N \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_{Fr}}{2kT}\right)$$

where $N$ is the number of lattice sites (Ag sites) per unit volume, $Q_{Fr}=1.1\ \text{eV}$, $k=8.617\times10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}$, and $T=350+273=623\ \text{K}$.

**1. Number of lattice sites per m³:**

Molar mass of AgCl $=107.87+35.45=143.32\ \text{g/mol}$.

$$N = \frac{\rho}{M_{\text{AgCl}}}N_A \times 10^6
= \frac{5.50}{143.32}(6.022\times10^{23})\times10^6
\approx 2.311\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$

**2. Exponential factor:**

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{1.1}{2(8.617\times10^{-5})(623)}\right)
= \exp(-10.24) \approx 3.553\times10^{-5}$$

**3. Frenkel defect concentration:**

$$N_{Fr} = (2.311\times10^{28})(3.553\times10^{-5})
\approx 8.21\times10^{23}\ \text{defects/m}^3$$

## 最终答案
8.21e23
