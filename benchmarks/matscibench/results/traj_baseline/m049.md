## 题目
A Boeing 777 aircraft has the following breakdown of materials usage: Aluminum alloys - $70 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Composites - $11 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Steel - 11 wt \% Titanium alloys - $7 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Other - $1 \mathrm{wt} \%$. Take the average density of an aluminum alloy to be $2.80 \mathrm{Mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$, the average density for a titanium alloy to be $4.40 \mathrm{Mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$, the average density of steel to be $7.80 \mathrm{Mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$, and the average composite density to be $1.50 \mathrm{Mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$. Ignoring the "other," calculate the average density for the overall structural materials in this aircraft. Give your answer as the value of average_density.
Give your final answer in units of Mg/m^3.

## 过程
The average density of a mixture given in weight percentages is found from the inverse rule of mixtures (mass-weighted harmonic mean):

$$\frac{1}{\rho_{\text{avg}}} = \sum_i \frac{w_i}{\rho_i}$$

Ignoring "Other" (1 wt%), the remaining fractions sum to 99 wt%, so we renormalize:

- Al: 70/99, ρ = 2.80
- Composites: 11/99, ρ = 1.50
- Steel: 11/99, ρ = 7.80
- Ti: 7/99, ρ = 4.40

Evaluating:

$$\frac{1}{\rho_{\text{avg}}} = \frac{70/99}{2.80} + \frac{11/99}{1.50} + \frac{11/99}{7.80} + \frac{7/99}{4.40}$$

$$\rho_{\text{avg}} = 2.8018\ \mathrm{Mg/m^3}$$

## 最终答案
2.8018
