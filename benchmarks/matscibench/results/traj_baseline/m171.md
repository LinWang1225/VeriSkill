## 题目
An aluminum 2014 T 6 tube of 75 mm ( 3 in .) outer diameter and 1 mm ( 0.04 in .) thickness is subjected to internal pressure of $8.4 \mathrm{MPa}\left(1200 \mathrm{lb} / \mathrm{in}^2\right)$. What is the factor of safety that was taken against failure by yielding when the pipe was designed?

## 过程
For a thin-walled closed-end cylinder under internal pressure, the principal stresses are

$$\sigma_1 = \sigma_h = \frac{pD}{2t}\;(\text{hoop}),\qquad \sigma_2 = \sigma_l = \frac{pD}{4t}\;(\text{longitudinal}),\qquad \sigma_3 \approx 0\;(\text{radial}).$$

With $D=75\;\text{mm}$, $t=1\;\text{mm}$, $p=8.4\;\text{MPa}$:

$$\sigma_h = \frac{8.4\times 75}{2\times 1}=315\;\text{MPa},\qquad \sigma_l = \frac{315}{2}=157.5\;\text{MPa}.$$

For Aluminum 2014-T6, the yield strength is $\sigma_y = 414\;\text{MPa}$ (60 ksi).

Since $\sigma_3=0$ and $\sigma_1>\sigma_2>0$, both the maximum-shear-stress (Tresca) and maximum-normal-stress criteria are governed by the hoop stress:

$$n = \frac{\sigma_y}{\sigma_h} = \frac{414}{315} = 1.314.$$

## 最终答案
1.314
