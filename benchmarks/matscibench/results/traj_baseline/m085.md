## 题目
Copper (Cu) and platinum (Pt) both have the FCC crystal structure, and Cu forms a substitutional solid solution for concentrations up to approximately $6 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cu}$ at room temperature. Determine the concentration in weight percent of Cu that must be added to Pt to yield a unit cell edge length of 0.390 nm . The atomic weights for Cu and Pt are, respectively, 63.55 and $195.08 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$
Give your final answer in units of wt%.

## 过程
For an FCC alloy, 4 atoms per unit cell. The alloy density from the unit cell (with edge $a = 0.390\,\text{nm}=3.90\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$) is

$$\rho_{\text{alloy}}=\frac{n\,\bar A}{a^{3}N_{A}},\qquad 
\bar A=\frac{100}{C_{\text{Cu}}/A_{\text{Cu}}+(100-C_{\text{Cu}})/A_{\text{Pt}}}$$

with $n=4$, $A_{\text{Cu}}=63.55$, $A_{\text{Pt}}=195.08\,\text{g/mol}$, $N_A=6.022\times10^{23}$.

The alloy density also follows the rule of mixtures (volume-additive) using the pure-component densities $\rho_{\text{Cu}}=8.94$ and $\rho_{\text{Pt}}=21.45\,\text{g/cm^3}$:

$$\rho_{\text{alloy}}=\frac{1}{w_{\text{Cu}}/\rho_{\text{Cu}}+w_{\text{Pt}}/\rho_{\text{Pt}}},\quad w_i=C_i/100$$

Equating the two expressions and solving for $C_{\text{Cu}}$ numerically:

- $\rho$ from lattice at $C=2.85$: $20.627\,\text{g/cm}^3$
- $\rho$ from mixture at $C=2.85$: $20.627\,\text{g/cm}^3$

The two curves intersect at $C_{\text{Cu}}\approx 2.85\,\text{wt\%}$, which is well within the $\sim6\,\text{wt\%}$ solubility limit, so the substitutional-solid-solution assumption is valid.

## 最终答案
2.85
