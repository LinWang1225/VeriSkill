## 题目
A structural member is made of steel of mean yield strength of 200 MPa ( 28.6 ksi ) and a standard deviation on strength of $30 \mathrm{MPa}(4.3 \mathrm{ksi})$. The applied stress has a mean value of $150 \mathrm{MPa}(14.3 \mathrm{ksi})$ and a standard deviation of $5 \mathrm{MPa}\left(700 \mathrm{lb} / \mathrm{in}^2{ }^2\right)$. (a) What is the probability of failure? (b) What factor of safety is required if the allowable failure rate is $1 \%$ ? Give your answer as a tuple: (probability_of_failure, factor_of_safety).
Give your final answer in units of (%, ).

## 过程
This is a stress–strength interference (reliability) problem. Failure occurs when applied stress σ exceeds yield strength S. Define the safety margin M = S − σ. Assuming S and σ are independent normal random variables:

- μ_M = μ_S − μ_σ = 200 − 150 = 50 MPa
- σ_M = √(σ_S² + σ_σ²) = √(30² + 5²) = √925 = 30.414 MPa

**(a) Probability of failure**

P_f = P(M < 0) = Φ(−μ_M/σ_M) = Φ(−50/30.414) = Φ(−1.6440)

Evaluating: P_f ≈ 0.05009 ≈ **5.01%**.

**(b) Required factor of safety for 1% failure**

For P_f = 0.01, the required reliability index is β = Φ⁻¹(0.99) = 2.326.

The central factor of safety is n = μ_S/μ_σ. Treating the coefficients of variation as fixed material/load properties (COV_S = 30/200 = 0.15, COV_σ = 5/150 = 0.0333), the standard deviations scale with the means, giving

β = (n−1)/√[(0.15 n)² + 0.0333²] = 2.326

→ (n−1)² = 2.326²·(0.0225 n² + 0.001111)
→ 0.8783 n² − 2 n + 0.9940 = 0

Solving the quadratic and taking the physically meaningful root (n > 1): n ≈ **1.544**.

## 最终答案
(5.01, 1.544)
