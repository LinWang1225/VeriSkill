## 题目
Calculate the amounts and compositions of phases and microconstituents in a $\mathrm{Fe}-0.60 \% \mathrm{C}$ alloy at $726^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as a tuple (amount of proeutectoid alpha, amount of pearlite, composition of proeutectoid alpha, composition of pearlite).

## 过程
For a Fe-0.60%C alloy at 726°C (just below the eutectoid temperature 727°C), the alloy is hypoeutectoid (0.60 < 0.76). The two microconstituents are proeutectoid α (ferrite) and pearlite.

Using the lever rule with the eutectoid isotherm:
- Eutectoid composition: $C_e = 0.76\%$ C
- Max carbon in ferrite at eutectoid temp: $C_\alpha = 0.022\%$ C
- Alloy composition: $C_0 = 0.60\%$ C

$$W_{\alpha} = \frac{C_e - C_0}{C_e - C_\alpha} = \frac{0.76-0.60}{0.76-0.022} = \frac{0.16}{0.738} \approx 0.2168$$

$$W_{\text{pearlite}} = \frac{C_0 - C_\alpha}{C_e - C_\alpha} = \frac{0.60-0.022}{0.76-0.022} = \frac{0.578}{0.738} \approx 0.7832$$

Compositions (wt% C): proeutectoid α = 0.022, pearlite = 0.76.

## 最终答案
(0.2168, 0.7832, 0.022, 0.76)
