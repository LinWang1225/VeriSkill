## 题目
Find the solubility of b in a at $860^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, if $\Omega=-30,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$. Given universal gas constant is $R=8.134$.

## 过程
For a regular solution, the dilute-solution solubility of B in A is obtained from the chemical-potential (common-tangent) condition. In the dilute limit ($x_B \ll 1$), the regular-solution solvus equation

$$RT\ln x_B = \Omega(1-x_B)^2$$

reduces to $RT\ln x_B \approx \Omega$, giving

$$x_B = \exp\!\left(\frac{\Omega}{RT}\right).$$

With $\Omega = -30000\ \mathrm{J/mol}$, $R = 8.134$, and $T = 860 + 273.15 = 1133.15\ \mathrm{K}$:

$$RT = 8.134 \times 1133.15 = 9217.04,$$

$$\frac{\Omega}{RT} = \frac{-30000}{9217.04} = -3.2547,$$

$$x_B = e^{-3.2547} = 0.0386.$$

## 最终答案
0.0386
