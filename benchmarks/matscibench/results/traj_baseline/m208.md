## 题目
Would you expect CsBr to have the sodium chloride, zinc blende, fluorite, or cesium chloride structure? Based on your answer, determine (a) the lattice parameter; (b) the density; and (c) the packing factor. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (attice_parameter_Å, density_g_cm3, packing_factor).
Give your final answer in units of (Å, g/cm³, ).

## 过程
**Structure selection.** CsBr consists of Cs⁺ and Br⁻. Using ionic radii r(Cs⁺) = 0.170 nm and r(Br⁻) = 0.196 nm, the radius ratio is

r⁺/r⁻ = 0.170/0.196 = 0.867.

Since 0.867 > 0.732, the coordination number is 8, which corresponds to the **cesium chloride (CsCl) structure** (the other options—NaCl needs 0.414–0.732, zinc blende needs 0.225–0.414, fluorite is for MX₂). This is consistent with the known fact that all cesium halides except CsF adopt the CsCl structure.

In the CsCl structure the unit cell is simple cubic with 1 Cs⁺ at the corners (net 1) and 1 Br⁻ at the body center (net 1): **n = 1 formula unit per cell**. The ions touch along the body diagonal:

a√3 = 2(r⁺ + r⁻) → a = 2(r⁺ + r⁻)/√3.

**(a) Lattice parameter**

a = 2(0.170 + 0.196)/√3 nm = 0.4226 nm = **4.23 Å**.

**(b) Density** — ρ = nA/(V_c N_A), with A = 132.91 + 79.90 = 212.81 g/mol:

ρ = (1)(212.81) / [(4.226×10⁻⁸ cm)³ (6.022×10²³)] = **4.68 g/cm³**.

**(c) Packing factor** — APF = [(4/3)π(r_Cs³ + r_Br³)] / a³:

APF = (4/3)π(0.170³ + 0.196³) / (0.4226)³ = **0.69**.

## 最终答案
(4.23, 4.68, 0.69)
