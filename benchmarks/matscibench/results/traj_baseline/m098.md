## 题目
Consider the diffusion of some hypothetical metal $Y$ into another hypothetical metal $Z$ at $950^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$; after 10 h the concentration at the 0.5 mm position (in metal $Z$ ) is $2.0 \mathrm{wt} \% Y$. At what position will the concentration also be $2.0 \mathrm{wt} \% Y$ after a 17.5 h heat treatment again at $950^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ? Assume preexponential and activation energy values of 4.3 $\times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$ and $180,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$, respectively, for this diffusion system.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
For diffusion into a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration, Fick's second law gives:

$$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = 1 - \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

The same concentration $C_x = 2.0\,\text{wt\%}$ is reached in both treatments, so the left-hand side is identical. Since both treatments are at the **same temperature** ($950^{\circ}\text{C}$), the diffusion coefficient $D$ is the same in both cases (the given $D_0$ and $Q_d$ would only be needed if the temperatures differed). Thus the error-function arguments must be equal:

$$\frac{x_1}{2\sqrt{Dt_1}} = \frac{x_2}{2\sqrt{Dt_2}} \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{x_1}{\sqrt{t_1}} = \frac{x_2}{\sqrt{t_2}}$$

Solving for $x_2$:

$$x_2 = x_1\sqrt{\frac{t_2}{t_1}} = 0.5\;\text{mm}\times\sqrt{\frac{17.5}{10}} = 0.5\times 1.32288 = 0.6614\;\text{mm}$$

## 最终答案
0.6614
