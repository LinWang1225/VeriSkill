## 题目
Tin bronze has a composition of $89 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Cu}$ and $11 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$ and consists of two phases at room temperature: an $\alpha$ phase, which is copper containing a very small amount of tin in solid solution, and an $\varepsilon$ phase, which consists of approximately $37 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{Sn}$. Compute the room-temperature conductivity of this alloy given the following data:

| Phase | Electrical Resistivity $(\Omega \cdot \mathrm{m})$ | Density $\left(\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}\right)$ |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| $\alpha$ | $1.88 \times 10^{-8}$ | 8.94 |
| $\varepsilon$ | $5.32 \times 10^{-7}$ | 8.25 |
Give your final answer in units of (\Omega m)^-1.

## 过程
**1. Phase compositions (wt% Sn):** Overall $C_0=11$, $\alpha$ phase $C_\alpha\approx 0$ (very small tin), $\varepsilon$ phase $C_\varepsilon=37$.

**2. Lever rule — mass fractions:**
$$W_\alpha=\frac{C_\varepsilon-C_0}{C_\varepsilon-C_\alpha}=\frac{37-11}{37-0}=\frac{26}{37}=0.7027$$
$$W_\varepsilon=\frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_\varepsilon-C_\alpha}=\frac{11}{37}=0.2973$$

**3. Convert mass fractions to volume fractions** using densities $\rho_\alpha=8.94$, $\rho_\varepsilon=8.25$ g/cm³:
$$V_\alpha=\frac{W_\alpha/\rho_\alpha}{W_\alpha/\rho_\alpha+W_\varepsilon/\rho_\varepsilon}=0.6857,\qquad V_\varepsilon=0.3143$$

**4. Phase conductivities** ($\sigma=1/\rho_e$):
$$\sigma_\alpha=\frac{1}{1.88\times10^{-8}}=5.319\times10^{7}\;(\Omega\text{m})^{-1}$$
$$\sigma_\varepsilon=\frac{1}{5.32\times10^{-7}}=1.880\times10^{6}\;(\Omega\text{m})^{-1}$$

**5. Rule of mixtures (volume-fraction weighted conductivity):**
$$\sigma=V_\alpha\sigma_\alpha+V_\varepsilon\sigma_\varepsilon$$
$$=0.6857(5.319\times10^{7})+0.3143(1.880\times10^{6})=3.706\times10^{7}\;(\Omega\text{m})^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
3.706e7
