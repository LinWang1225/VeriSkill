## 题目
If a cylindrical rod of brass 150.00 mm long and 10.000 mm in diameter is heated from $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $160^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ while its ends are maintained rigid, determine its change in diameter. Hint: You may want to consult  table 7.1 . For brass, $v=0.34$; the value of $\alpha_l$ for brass is $20.0 \times 10^{-6}\left({ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)^{-1}$.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
The rod is heated from 20°C to 160°C (ΔT = 140°C) but its ends are held rigid, so the longitudinal thermal expansion is suppressed. This generates a compressive thermal stress.

For a fully constrained longitudinal direction, the total longitudinal strain is zero:
$$\varepsilon_z = \alpha_l \Delta T + \frac{\sigma}{E} = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \sigma = -E\alpha_l\Delta T$$

The diameter changes due to (1) free thermal expansion in the lateral direction and (2) the Poisson lateral strain from the longitudinal compressive stress:
$$\varepsilon_d = \alpha_l\Delta T - \nu\frac{\sigma}{E} = \alpha_l\Delta T + \nu\alpha_l\Delta T = \alpha_l\Delta T(1+\nu)$$

Thus
$$\Delta d = d_0\,\alpha_l\Delta T(1+\nu)$$

Substituting d₀ = 10.000 mm, α_l = 20.0×10⁻⁶ °C⁻¹, ΔT = 140°C, ν = 0.34:
$$\Delta d = 10.000 \times (20.0\times10^{-6}) \times 140 \times (1+0.34) = 0.03752\ \text{mm}$$

## 最终答案
0.03752
