## 过程
Specific strength is the breaking length $L = \dfrac{\sigma}{\rho g}$.

From the table, for W (50 vol%) in Cu (parallel loading of continuous filaments):
- T.S. $= 1100\ \text{MPa}$

Composite density by the rule of mixtures (50 vol% each):
$$\rho_c = V_W\rho_W + V_{Cu}\rho_{Cu} = 0.50(19.3) + 0.50(8.96) = 14.13\ \text{g/cm}^3 = 14130\ \text{kg/m}^3$$

Breaking length:
$$L = \frac{1100\times10^6\ \text{Pa}}{(14130\ \text{kg/m}^3)(9.81\ \text{m/s}^2)} = 7935.6\ \text{m} = 7.94\times10^6\ \text{mm}$$

## 最终答案
7.94e6
