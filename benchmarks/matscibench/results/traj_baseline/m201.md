## 题目
A ceramic enamel is to be applied to a 1020 steel plate. The ceramic has a fracture strength of 4000 psi , a modulus of elasticity of $15 \times 10^6 \mathrm{psi}$, and a coefficient of thermal expansion of $10 \times 10^{-6 \circ} \mathrm{C}^{-1}$. Design the maximum temperature change that can be allowed without cracking the ceramic.
Steel (modulus of elasticity) Eₛ = 30×10⁶ psi  
Steel (coefficient of thermal expansion) αₛ = 12×10⁻⁶ /°C  

Give your answer as a tuple: (max_temperature_change_restrained, max_temperature_change_net).
Give your final answer in units of (°C, °C).

## 过程
The ceramic is bonded to the steel, so both must share the same thermal strain. A temperature change ΔT creates a stress in the ceramic due to the mismatch in coefficients of thermal expansion, (αₛ − α_c) = 2×10⁻⁶ /°C. Cracking initiates when this stress reaches the fracture strength σ_f = 4000 psi.

**Case 1 — Restrained (steel rigid, ceramic forced to follow steel):**
$$\sigma = E_c(\alpha_s-\alpha_c)\Delta T = \sigma_f$$
$$\Delta T = \frac{4000}{15\times10^6 \times 2\times10^{-6}} = \frac{4000}{30} = 133.33\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

**Case 2 — Net (free composite, force balance + strain compatibility, equal areas):**
$$\sigma_c = (\alpha_s-\alpha_c)\Delta T\,\frac{E_c E_s}{E_c+E_s} = \sigma_f$$
$$\Delta T = \frac{\sigma_f(E_c+E_s)}{(\alpha_s-\alpha_c)E_c E_s} = \frac{4000\times45\times10^6}{2\times10^{-6}\times450\times10^{12}} = 200\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

The net case gives a larger allowable ΔT because the steel also deforms, reducing the effective strain mismatch.

## 最终答案
(133.33, 200.00)
