## 题目
Two points on a true stress-strain curve are $\sigma = 222$ MPa at $\varepsilon = 0.05$ and $\sigma = 303$ MPa at $\varepsilon = 0.15$.
A. Find the values of $K$ and $n$ in equation $\sigma=K\varepsilon^n$ that best fit the data. Then using these values of $K$ and $n$, predict the true stress at a strain of $\varepsilon = 0.30$.
B. Find the values of $\sigma_0$ and $A$ in equation $\sigma = \sigma_0[1-\exp(-A\varepsilon)]$ that best fit the data. Then using these values of $\sigma_0$ and $A$, predict the true stress at a strain of $\varepsilon = 0.30$.
Give your answer as an order tuple (true stress  in question A, true stress in question B)
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa).

## 过程
**Part A — power law $\sigma = K\varepsilon^n$**

Two data points give:
$$222 = K(0.05)^n,\qquad 303 = K(0.15)^n$$

Dividing eliminates $K$:
$$\frac{303}{222}=\left(\frac{0.15}{0.05}\right)^n = 3^n \;\Rightarrow\; n=\frac{\ln(303/222)}{\ln 3}=0.2831$$

Back-substitute:
$$K=\frac{222}{(0.05)^{0.2831}}=518.47\ \text{MPa}$$

Predict at $\varepsilon=0.30$:
$$\sigma_A = K(0.30)^n = 518.47(0.30)^{0.2831}=368.70\ \text{MPa}$$

(Equivalently $\sigma_A = 222\cdot(0.30/0.05)^n = 222\cdot 6^{0.2831}$.)

**Part B — saturation form $\sigma=\sigma_0[1-\exp(-A\varepsilon)]$**

The two equations
$$222=\sigma_0[1-e^{-0.05A}],\qquad 303=\sigma_0[1-e^{-0.15A}]$$
are transcendental; solving numerically (fsolve) gives
$$A=25.166,\qquad \sigma_0=310.11\ \text{MPa}$$

Check: $310.11(1-e^{-1.258})=222.0$ ✓ and $310.11(1-e^{-3.775})=303.0$ ✓.

Predict at $\varepsilon=0.30$:
$$\sigma_B = 310.11\,[1-\exp(-25.166\cdot 0.30)]=309.95\ \text{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
(368.70, 309.95)
