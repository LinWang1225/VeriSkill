## 题目
Suppose that solid nickel was able to nucleate homogeneously with an undercooling of only $22^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. How many atoms would have to group together spontaneously for this occur? Assume that the lattice parameter of the solid FCC nickel is 0.356 nm. Give your answer as the value of number_of_atoms.

## 过程
For homogeneous nucleation the critical radius is

$$r^*=\frac{2\gamma T_m}{\Delta H_f\,\Delta T}$$

Using the standard nickel data (Callister): solid–liquid interfacial energy $\gamma=0.255\ \mathrm{J/m^2}$, melting point $T_m=1455+273=1728\ \mathrm{K}$, latent heat of fusion per unit volume $\Delta H_f=2.76\times10^{9}\ \mathrm{J/m^3}$, and undercooling $\Delta T=22\ \mathrm{K}$:

$$r^*=\frac{2(0.255)(1728)}{(2.76\times10^{9})(22)}=1.451\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{m}=14.51\ \mathrm{nm}$$

Treating the critical nucleus as a sphere of radius $r^*$:

$$V^*=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{*3}=1.281\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{m^3}$$

FCC nickel has 4 atoms per unit cell, so the volume per atom is

$$V_{\text{atom}}=\frac{a^3}{4}=\frac{(0.356\times10^{-9})^3}{4}=1.128\times10^{-29}\ \mathrm{m^3}$$

Thus the number of atoms that must spontaneously group together is

$$n=\frac{V^*}{V_{\text{atom}}}=\frac{1.281\times10^{-23}}{1.128\times10^{-29}}\approx 1.135\times10^{6}$$
