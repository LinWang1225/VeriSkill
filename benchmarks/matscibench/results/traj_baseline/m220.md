## 题目
A $0.02 \%$ C steel is to be carburized at $1200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in 4 h , with the carbon content 0.6 mm beneath the surface reaching $0.45 \%$ C. Calculate the carbon content required at the surface of the steel.
Give your final answer in units of % C.

## 过程
Carburization is governed by Fick's second law for a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration:

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given:** $C_0 = 0.02\%$, $C_x = 0.45\%$ at $x = 0.6\ \text{mm}=6\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}$, $t = 4\ \text{h}=14400\ \text{s}$, $T = 1200^{\circ}\text{C}=1473.15\ \text{K}$.

**Diffusivity of C in austenite** (Callister): $D_0 = 2.0\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q = 142\ \text{kJ/mol}$.

$$D = D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT}\right) = 2.0\times10^{-5}\exp\!\left(-\frac{142000}{8.314\times1473.15}\right) \approx 1.84\times10^{-10}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Argument of erf:**

$$\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}} = \frac{6\times10^{-4}}{2\sqrt{(1.84\times10^{-10})(14400)}} \approx 0.1841$$

$$\operatorname{erf}(0.1841) \approx 0.2054$$

**Solve for $C_s$:**

$$C_s - C_x = 0.2054\,(C_s - C_0)$$
$$C_s(1-0.2054) = C_x - 0.2054\,C_0 = 0.45 - 0.2054(0.02) = 0.4459$$
$$C_s = \frac{0.4459}{0.7946} \approx 0.561\%$$

## 最终答案
0.561
