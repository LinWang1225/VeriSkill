## 题目
Calculate the radius of a tantalum (Ta) atom, given that Ta has a BCC crystal structure, a density of 16.6 $\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, and an atomic weight of $180.9 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.
Give your final answer in units of nm.

## 过程
For a BCC crystal structure, there are 2 atoms per unit cell (n = 2). The density relation is

$$\rho = \frac{n A}{V_c N_A} = \frac{n A}{a^3 N_A}$$

Solving for the lattice parameter:

$$a^3 = \frac{n A}{\rho N_A} = \frac{2 \times 180.9}{16.6 \times 6.022\times10^{23}}$$

$$a = 3.308\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm}$$

For BCC, atoms touch along the body diagonal, so $4R = a\sqrt{3}$:

$$R = \frac{a\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{(3.308\times10^{-8})\sqrt{3}}{4} = 1.432\times10^{-8}\ \text{cm}$$

Converting to nm (1 cm = 10⁷ nm):

$$R = 1.432\times10^{-8}\times10^{7} = 0.1432\ \text{nm}$$

## 最终答案
0.1432
