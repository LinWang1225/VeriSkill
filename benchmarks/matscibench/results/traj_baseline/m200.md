## 题目
An aluminum casting must measure 25.00 cm × 25.00 cm × 3.00 cm at 25 °C.  Pure aluminum melts (and solidifies) at 660 °C, and its linear coefficient of thermal expansion is α=25×10⁻⁶ °C⁻¹.  Design a pattern (made at 25 °C) whose dimensions will yield the correct final casting dimensions after the metal cools and contracts.  

Give your answer as a tuple (pattern_length_cm, pattern_width_cm, pattern_height_cm).
Give your final answer in units of (cm, cm, cm).

## 过程
The casting must end up at 25.00 cm × 25.00 cm × 3.00 cm at 25 °C. Molten aluminum is poured at 660 °C and fills the mold cavity (whose dimensions equal the pattern, made at 25 °C). As the metal cools from 660 °C to 25 °C it contracts, so the pattern must be oversized.

Using linear thermal expansion, a dimension measured as $L_0$ at 25 °C becomes $L_0(1+\alpha\Delta T)$ at the higher temperature. Setting the hot (cavity-filling) dimension equal to the pattern dimension:

$$L_{\text{pattern}} = L_{\text{final}}\,(1+\alpha\,\Delta T),\qquad \Delta T = 660-25 = 635\ ^\circ\text{C}$$

$$\alpha\,\Delta T = (25\times10^{-6})(635)=0.015875 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{factor}=1.015875$$

- Length/width: $25.00\times1.015875 = 25.396875$ cm
- Height: $3.00\times1.015875 = 3.047625$ cm

## 最终答案
(25.396875, 25.396875, 3.047625)
