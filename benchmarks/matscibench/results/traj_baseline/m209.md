## 题目
Calculate the number of vacancies per $\mathrm{cm}^3$ expected in copper at $1080^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ (just below the melting temperature). The energy for vacancy formation is $20,000 \mathrm{cal} / \mathrm{mol}$. Give your answer as as the value of number_of_vacancies_per_cm3.
Give your final answer in units of vacancies/cm³.

## 过程
The equilibrium vacancy concentration is given by

$$N_v = N \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{RT}\right)$$

where $N$ is the number of atomic sites per cm³, $Q_v = 20{,}000\ \text{cal/mol}$, $R = 1.987\ \text{cal/(mol·K)}$, and $T = 1080 + 273 = 1353\ \text{K}$.

For copper (density $\rho = 8.96\ \text{g/cm}^3$, atomic weight $A = 63.5\ \text{g/mol}$, $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}$):

$$N = \frac{\rho N_A}{A} = \frac{8.96 \times 6.022\times10^{23}}{63.5} \approx 8.50\times10^{22}\ \text{atoms/cm}^3$$

Exponential factor:

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{20000}{1.987 \times 1353}\right) \approx 5.88\times10^{-4}$$

Thus:

$$N_v \approx (8.50\times10^{22})(5.88\times10^{-4}) \approx 4.99\times10^{19}\ \text{vacancies/cm}^3$$

## 最终答案
4.99e19
