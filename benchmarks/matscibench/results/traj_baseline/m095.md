## 题目
The diffusion coefficients for carbon in nickel are given at two temperatures are as follows:
\begin{tabular}{cc}
\hline$T\left({ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$ & $D\left(\mathrm{~m}^2 / \mathrm{s}\right)$ \\
\hline 600 & $5.5 \times 10^{-14}$ \\
700 & $3.9 \times 10^{-13}$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
(a) Determine the values of $D_0$ and $Q_d$.
(b) What is the magnitude of D at $850^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ?

Give your answer as a tuple ($D_{0}$, $Q_{d}$, $D$ at $850^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$)
Give your final answer in units of (m^2/s, J/mol, m^2/s).

## 过程
Using the Arrhenius form $D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$, taking logs gives $\ln D = \ln D_0 - \dfrac{Q_d}{R}\dfrac{1}{T}$.

With $T_1=873.15\,\text{K}, D_1=5.5\times10^{-14}$ and $T_2=973.15\,\text{K}, D_2=3.9\times10^{-13}$:

$$\ln\frac{D_2}{D_1} = -\frac{Q_d}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)$$

Solving yields $Q_d \approx 1.384\times10^{5}\,\text{J/mol}$ and $D_0 \approx 1.04\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$.

Then at $850^{\circ}\text{C}$ ($T_3=1123.15\,\text{K}$):
$$D = D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT_3}\right) \approx 3.83\times10^{-12}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

## 最终答案
(1.04e-5, 138379.5, 3.83e-12)
