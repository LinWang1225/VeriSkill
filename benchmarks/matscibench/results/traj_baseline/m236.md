## 题目
A eutectoid steel has been heated to $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, quenched to $300^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and held for 10 s , and finally quenched to room temperature. What is the hardness?
Give your final answer in units of HRC.

## 过程
Since the 10 s hold at 300°C occurs before the bainite start curve (~100 s), no transformation happens during the isothermal hold. The retained austenite converts entirely to martensite on the final quench. For eutectoid steel (0.76 wt% C), 100% martensite has a hardness of approximately 65 HRC.

## 最终答案
65
