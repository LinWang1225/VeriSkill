## 题目
Suppose that liquid nickel is undercooled until homogeneous nucleation occurs. Calculate (a) the critical radius of the nucleus required and (b) the number of nickel atoms in the nucleus. Assume that the lattice parameter of the solid FCC nickel is 0.356 nm. Give your answer as a tuple: (critical_radius_Å, number_of_atoms).
Give your final answer in units of (Å, ).

## 过程
For homogeneous nucleation, the critical radius is:

$$r^* = \frac{2\gamma}{\Delta G_v}, \qquad \Delta G_v = \frac{\Delta H_f \,\Delta T}{T_m}$$

so

$$r^* = \frac{2\,\gamma\, T_m}{\Delta H_f\,\Delta T}$$

**Standard nickel parameters** (Callister):
- $T_m = 1728\ \text{K}$ (1455 °C)
- $\gamma = 0.255\ \text{J/m}^2$ (solid–liquid surface energy)
- $\Delta H_f = 2.65\times10^{9}\ \text{J/m}^3$ (heat of fusion per unit volume)
- $\Delta T = 319\ \text{K}$ (maximum undercooling for homogeneous nucleation of Ni)
- $a = 0.356\ \text{nm}$ (FCC lattice parameter, given)

**(a) Critical radius:**

$$r^* = \frac{2(0.255)(1728)}{(2.65\times10^{9})(319)} = 1.043\times10^{-9}\ \text{m} = 10.43\ \text{Å}$$

**(b) Number of atoms in the nucleus:**

The nucleus is a sphere of radius $r^*$; the FCC unit cell (volume $a^3$) contains 4 atoms.

$$V_{\text{nuc}} = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{*3} = 4.75\times10^{-27}\ \text{m}^3$$

$$V_{\text{cell}} = a^3 = (0.356\times10^{-9})^3 = 4.51\times10^{-29}\ \text{m}^3$$

$$N = \frac{V_{\text{nuc}}}{V_{\text{cell}}}\times 4 = \frac{4.75\times10^{-27}}{4.51\times10^{-29}}\times 4 \approx 105.2 \times 4 \approx 421$$

## 最终答案
(10.43, 421)
