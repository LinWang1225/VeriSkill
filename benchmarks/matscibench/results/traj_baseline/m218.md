## 题目
A carburizing process is carried out on a $0.10 \% \mathrm{C}$ steel by introducing $1.0 \% \mathrm{C}$ at the surface at $980^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, where the iron is FCC. Calculate the carbon content at 0.01 cm, 0.05 cm, and 0.10 cm beneath the surface after 1 h. Give your answer as a tuple: (carbon_content_0.01cm, carbon_content_0.05cm, carbon_content_0.10cm).
Give your final answer in units of (% C, % C, % C).

## 过程
Carburizing is governed by the solution to Fick's second law for a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration:

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

so

$$C_x = C_s - (C_s - C_0)\,\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given:**
- $C_s = 1.0\%$ C (surface), $C_0 = 0.10\%$ C (initial)
- $T = 980^\circ\mathrm{C} = 1253\ \mathrm{K}$, FCC iron
- $t = 1\ \mathrm{h} = 3600\ \mathrm{s}$

**Diffusion coefficient** for carbon in FCC iron (Callister Table 5.2):
- $D_0 = 2.3\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$, $Q_d = 148\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$

$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right) = 2.3\times10^{-5}\exp\!\left(-\frac{148000}{8.31\times1253}\right) \approx 1.54\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$$

**Evaluate at each depth** ($x$ converted to m, $x/(2\sqrt{Dt})$ computed, then $\operatorname{erf}$):

| $x$ (cm) | $x/(2\sqrt{Dt})$ | $\operatorname{erf}$ | $C_x$ (% C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 0.2120 | 0.2357 | 0.788 |
| 0.05 | 1.0602 | 0.8662 | 0.220 |
| 0.10 | 2.1205 | 0.9973 | 0.102 |

## 最终答案
(0.7878, 0.2204, 0.1024)
