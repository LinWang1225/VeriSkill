## 题目
Would you expect $\mathrm{UO}_2$ to have the sodium chloride, zinc blende, or fluorite structure? Based on your answer, determine (a) the lattice parameter, (b) the theoretical density, and (c) the packing factor. Give your answer as a tuple: ( lattice_parameter_angstrom, theoretical_density_g_per_cm3, packing_factor).
Give your final answer in units of (Å, g/cm³, ).

## 过程
Using cation–anion contact in the fluorite cell:

- a = (4/√3)(1.00 + 1.40) = **5.54 Å**
- ρ = 4(238.03 + 2·16.00)/(6.022×10²³ · (5.54×10⁻⁸)³) = **10.53 g/cm³**
- PF = [4·(4π/3)(1.00)³ + 8·(4π/3)(1.40)³]/(5.54)³ = **0.638**

## 最终答案
(5.54, 10.53, 0.638)
