## 题目
Consider a diffusion couple between pure tungsten and a tungsten alloy containing 1 at $\%$ thorium. After several minutes of exposure at $2000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, a transition zone of 0.01 cm thickness is established. What is the flux of thorium atoms at this time if diffusion is due to (a) volume diffusion, (b) grain boundary diffusion, and (c) surface diffusion? Refer to Table for the diffusion coefficients and activation energies for thorium in tungsten. The lattice parameter of BCC tungsten is $3.165 \AA$. Give your answer as a tuple (volume diffusion flux, grain boundary diffusion flux, surface diffusion flux).

Table:
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|l|}
\hline \multicolumn{5}{|l|}{TABLE 5-2 $\quad$ The effect of the type of diffusion for thorium in tungsten and for self-diffusion in silver} \\
\hline \multirow[b]{2}{*}{Diffusion Type} & \multicolumn{4}{|c|}{Diffusion Coefficient ( $D$ )} \\
\hline & \multicolumn{2}{|c|}{Thorium in Tungsten} & \multicolumn{2}{|c|}{Silver in Silver} \\
\hline & $D_0\left(\mathrm{~cm}^2 / \mathrm{s}\right)$ & $Q$ ( $\mathrm{cal} / \mathrm{mol}$ ) & $D_0\left(\mathrm{~cm}^2 / \mathrm{s}\right)$ & $Q(\mathrm{cal} / \mathrm{mol})$ \\
\hline Surface & 0.47 & 66,400 & 0.068 & 8,900 \\
\hline Grain boundary & 0.74 & 90,000 & 0.24 & 22,750 \\
\hline Volume & 1.00 & 120,000 & 0.99 & 45,700 \\
\hline \multicolumn{5}{|l|}{*Given by parameters for Equation 5-4.} \\
\hline

Give your final answer in units of (atoms/cm²·s, atoms/cm²·s, atoms/cm²·s).

## 过程
**Setup.** A diffusion couple between pure W (0 at% Th) and W–1 at% Th gives a concentration drop ΔC across the transition zone of thickness Δx = 0.01 cm at T = 2000 °C = 2273 K. Using Fick's first law in finite-difference form:

$$J = -D\frac{dC}{dx} \approx D\frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x}$$

**Atomic density of BCC W.** BCC has 2 atoms/unit cell, lattice parameter a = 3.165 Å = 3.165×10⁻⁸ cm:

$$N = \frac{2}{a^3} = \frac{2}{(3.165\times10^{-8})^3} = 6.308\times10^{22}\ \text{atoms/cm}^3$$

**Concentration difference.** For dilute 1 at% Th, ΔC ≈ 0.01·N = 6.308×10²⁰ atoms/cm³.

**Concentration gradient.**

$$\frac{dC}{dx} = \frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x} = \frac{6.308\times10^{20}}{0.01} = 6.308\times10^{22}\ \text{atoms/cm}^4$$

**Diffusion coefficients** from D = D₀ exp(−Q/RT) with R = 1.987 cal/(mol·K):

| Type | D₀ (cm²/s) | Q (cal/mol) | D (cm²/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume | 1.00 | 120,000 | 2.891×10⁻¹² |
| Grain boundary | 0.74 | 90,000 | 1.641×10⁻⁹ |
| Surface | 0.47 | 66,400 | 1.937×10⁻⁷ |

**Fluxes** J = D·(dC/dx):

- Volume: 2.891×10⁻¹² × 6.308×10²² = **1.82×10¹¹ atoms/cm²·s**
- Grain boundary: 1.641×10⁻⁹ × 6.308×10²² = **1.03×10¹⁴ atoms/cm²·s**
- Surface: 1.937×10⁻⁷ × 6.308×10²² = **1.22×10¹⁶ atoms/cm²·s**

## 最终答案
(1.82e11, 1.03e14, 1.22e16)
