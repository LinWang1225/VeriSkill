## 题目
In an ionic-concentration corrosion cell involving nickel (forming $\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}$ ), an electrical current of 5 mA is measured. How many Ni atoms per second are oxidized at the anode? Give your answer as the value of Ni_atoms_per_second.
Give your final answer in units of s^-1.

## 过程
The oxidation reaction at the anode is $\mathrm{Ni} \to \mathrm{Ni}^{2+} + 2e^-$, so each Ni atom releases $z = 2$ electrons.

The electrical current $I = 5\,\text{mA} = 5\times10^{-3}\,\text{A}$ equals the charge flow per second. The number of electrons passing per second is $I/e$, where $e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$. Dividing by 2 gives the Ni atoms oxidized per second:

$$\dot{N}_{\mathrm{Ni}} = \frac{I}{z e} = \frac{5\times10^{-3}}{2 \times 1.602\times10^{-19}} \approx 1.56\times10^{16}\,\text{s}^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
1.560377e16
