## 题目
Calculate the displacement of the electrons or ions for the following conditions: (a) electronic polarization in nickel of $2 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{C} / \mathrm{m}^2$; (b) electronic polarization in aluminum of $2 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{C} / \mathrm{m}^2$ (c) ionic polarization in NaCl of $4.3 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{C} / \mathrm{m}^2$; and (d) ionic polarization in ZnS of $5 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{C} / \mathrm{m}^2$. Give your answer as a tuple: (displacement_nickel_m, displacement_NaCl_m).
Give your final answer in units of (m, m).

## 过程
Polarization relates to displacement by $P = nqd$, so $d = \dfrac{P}{nq}$.

**(a) Nickel — electronic polarization (FCC)**
- Atomic radius $R = 0.125\,\text{nm}$; FCC lattice parameter $a = 2R\sqrt{2} = 3.536\times10^{-10}\,\text{m}$
- Atom number density $n = 4/a^3 = 9.05\times10^{28}\,\text{atoms/m}^3$
- Charge per atom $q = e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$, $P = 2\times10^{-7}\,\text{C/m}^2$
- $d_{\text{Ni}} = \dfrac{2\times10^{-7}}{(9.05\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})} = 1.38\times10^{-17}\,\text{m}$

**(c) NaCl — ionic polarization (NaCl structure)**
- $a = 2(r_{\text{Na}^+}+r_{\text{Cl}^-}) = 2(0.102+0.181)\,\text{nm} = 5.66\times10^{-10}\,\text{m}$
- Ion-pair density $n = 4/a^3 = 2.21\times10^{28}\,\text{pairs/m}^3$
- $q = e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$, $P = 4.3\times10^{-8}\,\text{C/m}^2$
- $d_{\text{NaCl}} = \dfrac{4.3\times10^{-8}}{(2.21\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})} = 1.22\times10^{-17}\,\text{m}$

## 最终答案
(1.38e-17, 1.22e-17)
