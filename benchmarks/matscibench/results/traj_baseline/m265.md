## 题目
Steady-state creep rate data are given in the following table for aluminum at 260℃(533K):

\begin{tabular}{cc}
\hline$\dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}_{\boldsymbol{s}}\left(\boldsymbol{h}^{\mathbf{- 1}}\right)$ & $\boldsymbol{\sigma} \mathbf{( M P a})$ \\
\hline $2.0 \times 10^{-4}$ & 3 \\
3.65 & 25 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

Compute the steady-state creep rate at a stress of 10 MPa and 260℃.
Give your final answer in units of $h^{-1}$.

## 过程
Steady-state creep follows the power law $\dot{\varepsilon}_s = K\sigma^n$. Using the two data points to eliminate $K$:

$$\frac{\dot{\varepsilon}_2}{\dot{\varepsilon}_1}=\left(\frac{\sigma_2}{\sigma_1}\right)^n \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{3.65}{2.0\times10^{-4}}=\left(\frac{25}{3}\right)^n$$

$$n=\frac{\ln(18250)}{\ln(25/3)}\approx 4.6277$$

Then $K=\dot{\varepsilon}_1/\sigma_1^n = 2.0\times10^{-4}/3^{4.6277}\approx 1.239\times10^{-6}$.

At $\sigma=10$ MPa:

$$\dot{\varepsilon}_s = K(10)^n = 1.239\times10^{-6}\cdot 10^{4.6277}\approx 5.257\times10^{-2}\ \text{h}^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
5.26e-2
