## 题目
For a superconducting material at a temperature $T$ below the critical temperature $T{C}$, the critical field $H{C}(T)$ depends on temperature according to the relationship

$$
H{C}(T)=H{C}(0)\left(1-\frac{T^{2}}{T{C}^{2}}\right)
$$

where $H{C}(0)$ is the critical field at 0 K .
(a) Using the data in  table 18.7 , calculate the critical magnetic fields for lead at 2.5 and 5.0 K .
(b) To what temperature must lead be cooled in a magnetic field of $15,000 \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{m}$ for it to be superconductive?

$$H_C(0) = \frac{B_C(0)}{\mu_0} = \frac{0.0803}{4\pi\times10^{-7}} \approx 63900.7\ \text{A/m}$$

**(a)** Using $H_C(T)=H_C(0)\left(1-\dfrac{T^{2}}{T_{C}^{2}}\right)$ with $T_C=7.19$ K:

- At $T=2.5$ K: $H_C = 63900.7\left(1-\dfrac{2.5^2}{7.19^2}\right) \approx 56175\ \text{A/m}$
- At $T=5.0$ K: $H_C = 63900.7\left(1-\dfrac{5.0^2}{7.19^2}\right) \approx 32999\ \text{A/m}$

**(b)** Set $H_C(T)=15000$ A/m and solve for $T$:

$$T = T_C\sqrt{1-\dfrac{H_C(T)}{H_C(0)}} = 7.19\sqrt{1-\dfrac{15000}{63900.7}} \approx 6.29\ \text{K}$$
