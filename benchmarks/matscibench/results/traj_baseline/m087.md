## 题目
A sheet of steel 5.0-mm thick has nitrogen atmospheres on both sides at $900^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and is permitted to achieve a steady-state diffusion condition. The diffusion coefficient for nitrogen in steel at this temperature is $1.85 \times 10^{-10}$ $m^{2} / s$, and the diffusion flux is found to be $1.0 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{2} \cdot \mathrm{~s}$. Also, it is known that the concentration of nitrogen in the steel at the high-pressure surface is $2 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$. How far into the sheet from this high-pressure side will the concentration be $0.5 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ ? Assume a linear concentration profile.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
Fick 第一定律（稳态）：$J = -D \dfrac{dC}{dx}$。浓度剖面线性，故 $\dfrac{dC}{dx}$ 为常数。

$$\frac{dC}{dx} = -\frac{J}{D} = -\frac{1.0\times10^{-7}}{1.85\times10^{-10}}$$

以高压侧为 $x=0$，$C(0)=2$，则

$$C(x) = 2 - \frac{J}{D}x$$

令 $C(x)=0.5$：

$$0.5 = 2 - \frac{J}{D}x \;\Rightarrow\; x = \frac{(2-0.5)D}{J} = \frac{1.5 \times 1.85\times10^{-10}}{1.0\times10^{-7}} = 2.775\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$$

换算为 mm：$x = 2.775$ mm（小于板厚 5.0 mm，在板内，合理）。

## 最终答案
2.775
