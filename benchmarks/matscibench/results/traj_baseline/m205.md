## 题目
The density of aluminum is $2.7 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ is about $4 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Describe the characteristics of the aluminum-oxide film. Compare these with the oxide film that forms on tungsten. The density of tungsten is $19.254 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$ and that of $W O_3$ is $7.3 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple: (P-B_ratio_aluminum, P-B_ratio_tungsten).
Give your final answer in units of (, ).

## 过程
The Pilling–Bedworth (P-B) ratio compares the volume of oxide formed to the volume of metal consumed:

$$r_{PB}=\frac{V_{\text{oxide}}}{V_{\text{metal}}}=\frac{M_{\text{oxide}}\,\rho_{\text{metal}}}{n\,M_{\text{metal}}\,\rho_{\text{oxide}}}$$

where $n$ is the number of metal atoms in the oxide formula.

**Aluminum → Al₂O₃** ($n=2$):
- $M_{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3}=2(26.98)+3(16)=101.96$ g/mol, $M_{\text{Al}}=26.98$ g/mol
- $r_{PB}=\dfrac{101.96\times 2.7}{2\times 26.98\times 4.0}\approx 1.28$

Since $1<r_{PB}<2$, the alumina film is compact, continuous, and adherent → **protective** (passivating).

**Tungsten → WO₃** ($n=1$):
- $M_{\text{WO}_3}=183.84+3(16)=231.84$ g/mol, $M_{\text{W}}=183.84$ g/mol
- $r_{PB}=\dfrac{231.84\times 19.254}{1\times 183.84\times 7.3}\approx 3.33$

Since $r_{PB}>2$, the oxide volume is far larger than the metal consumed, generating large compressive stresses that crack and spall → **non-protective**.

## 最终答案
(1.28, 3.33)
