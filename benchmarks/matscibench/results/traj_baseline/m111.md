## 题目
We have the following engineering stress–strain data for a tensile test on a ductile metal:

| Point | σ (MPa) | ε (in-drawn) |
|–|–:|–:|
| 1 | 100 | 0.000 |
| 2 | 250 | 0.005 |
| 3 | 400 | 0.010 |
| … | … | … |
| 8 |  797 | 0.0075 |
| 9 |  838 | 0.0100 |
| 10|  928 | 0.0150 |
| 11|  997 | 0.0200 |
| 12| 1163 | 0.0400 |
| 13| 1236 | 0.0600 |

For true strain, determine n and K in σₜ = K εₜⁿ.

Give your answer as a tuple (n, k).
Give your final answer in units of (,MPa).

## 过程
For the Hollomon relation σₜ = K εₜⁿ, convert engineering (σ, ε) to true quantities:

- σₜ = σ(1+ε)
- εₜ = ln(1+ε)

Use the plastic-region points (8–13). Taking logs gives ln σₜ = ln K + n ln εₜ, a linear fit in (ln εₜ, ln σₜ).

| Pt | σₜ (MPa) | εₜ | ln εₜ | ln σₜ |
|–|–:|–:|–:|–:|
| 8 | 802.98 | 0.007472 | −4.8966 | 6.6883 |
| 9 | 846.38 | 0.009950 | −4.6101 | 6.7410 |
|10 | 941.92 | 0.014889 | −4.2072 | 6.8479 |
|11 |1016.94 | 0.019803 | −3.9219 | 6.9246 |
|12 |1209.52 | 0.039221 | −3.2386 | 7.0980 |
|13 |1310.16 | 0.058269 | −2.8427 | 7.1779 |

Least-squares regression of ln σₜ on ln εₜ yields slope n = 0.2454 and intercept ln K = 7.8827, so K = e^{7.8827} = 2651.4 MPa. Fit quality R² = 0.9982.

## 最终答案
(0.245, 2651.4)
