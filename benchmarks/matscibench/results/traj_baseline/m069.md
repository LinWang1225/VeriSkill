## 题目
Magnesium (Mg) has an HCP crystal structure, a c/a ratio of 1.624, and a density of $1.74 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$. Compute the atomic radius for Mg .
Give your final answer in units of nm.

## 过程
For an HCP unit cell:

- Atoms per cell: $n = 6$
- Atomic weight of Mg: $A = 24.305\ \mathrm{g/mol}$
- Avogadro's number: $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}$
- Density: $\rho = 1.74\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $c/a = 1.624$

The HCP unit cell volume is
$$V = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^2 c = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\left(\frac{c}{a}\right)a^3$$

From $\rho = \dfrac{nA}{V N_A}$:
$$V = \frac{nA}{\rho N_A} = \frac{6 \times 24.305}{1.74 \times 6.022\times10^{23}} = 1.3917\times10^{-22}\ \mathrm{cm^3}$$

Solving for $a$:
$$a^3 = \frac{V}{(3\sqrt{3}/2)(c/a)} \Rightarrow a = 3.207\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{cm}$$

For HCP, $a = 2R$, so
$$R = \frac{a}{2} = 1.6035\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{cm} = 0.1604\ \mathrm{nm}$$

## 最终答案
0.1604
