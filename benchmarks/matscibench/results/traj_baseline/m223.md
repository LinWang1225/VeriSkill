## 题目
During freezing of a $\mathrm{Cu}-\mathrm{Zn}$ alloy, we find that the composition is nonuniform. By heating the alloy to $600^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 3 h , diffusion of zinc helps to make the composition more uniform. What temperature would be required if we wished to perform this homogenization treatment in 30 minutes?
Give your final answer in units of °C.

## 过程
For the same degree of homogenization, the diffusion parameter $Dt$ must be the same:

$$D_1 t_1 = D_2 t_2$$

With $D = D_0\exp(-Q/RT)$, the pre-exponential $D_0$ cancels:

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT_1}\right)t_1 = \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT_2}\right)t_2$$

Taking logs:

$$\ln\!\left(\frac{t_1}{t_2}\right) = \frac{Q}{R}\!\left(\frac{1}{T_1}-\frac{1}{T_2}\right)$$

For diffusion of Zn in Cu (Callister data): $Q = 189{,}000\ \text{J/mol}$, $R = 8.31\ \text{J/(mol·K)}$.

- $T_1 = 600 + 273 = 873\ \text{K}$, $t_1 = 3\ \text{h} = 10{,}800\ \text{s}$
- $t_2 = 30\ \text{min} = 1{,}800\ \text{s}$, so $t_1/t_2 = 6$

Solving for $T_2$:

$$\frac{1}{T_2} = \frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{R\ln 6}{Q}$$

$$T_2 \approx 937.5\ \text{K} \;\Rightarrow\; T_2 \approx 664.5^{\circ}\text{C}$$

## 最终答案
664.5
