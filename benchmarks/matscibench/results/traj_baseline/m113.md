## 题目
A three-point transverse bending test is conducted on a cylindrical specimen of aluminum oxide having a reported flexural strength of 300 MPa (43,500 psi). If the specimen radius is 5.0 mm ( 0.20 in .) and the support point separation distance is 15.0 mm ( 0.61 in .), would you expect the specimen to fracture when a load of 7500 N (1690 lb) is applied? Justify your answer.
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
For a cylindrical specimen in three-point bending, the maximum flexural stress at the midspan is

$$\sigma = \frac{Mc}{I}$$

with max moment $M = FL/4$, outer-fiber distance $c = R$, and circular cross-section second moment $I = \pi R^{4}/4$. Substituting:

$$\sigma = \frac{(FL/4)\,R}{\pi R^{4}/4} = \frac{FL}{\pi R^{3}}$$

Using $F = 7500\ \text{N}$, $L = 15.0\ \text{mm}$, $R = 5.0\ \text{mm}$ (and $1\ \text{N/mm}^2 = 1\ \text{MPa}$):

$$\sigma = \frac{7500 \times 15.0}{\pi \times (5.0)^3} = \frac{112500}{\pi \times 125} \approx 286.5\ \text{MPa}$$

The applied stress (≈ 286.5 MPa) is **less than** the reported flexural strength of 300 MPa, so the specimen is **not** expected to fracture.

## 最终答案
286.5
