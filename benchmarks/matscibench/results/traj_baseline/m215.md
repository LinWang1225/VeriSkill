## 题目
Compare the diffusion coefficients for hydrogen and nitrogen in FCC iron at $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and explain the difference. Give your answer as a tuple: (diffusion_coefficient_H, diffusion_coefficient_N).
Give your final answer in units of (cm²/s, cm²/s).

## 过程
Using the Arrhenius form $D = D_0 \exp(-Q_d/RT)$ at $T = 1000+273 = 1273\ \text{K}$, with Callister Table 5.2 data for FCC iron (austenite):

- Hydrogen: $D_0 = 1.7\times10^{-7}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 15{,}000\ \text{J/mol}$
- Nitrogen: $D_0 = 4.7\times10^{-7}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 147{,}000\ \text{J/mol}$

Evaluating (converting m²/s → cm²/s by ×10⁴):

$$D_H = 1.7\times10^{-7}e^{-15000/(8.314\cdot1273)} = 4.12\times10^{-8}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s} = 4.12\times10^{-4}\ \text{cm}^2/\text{s}$$

$$D_N = 4.7\times10^{-7}e^{-147000/(8.314\cdot1273)} = 4.37\times10^{-13}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s} = 4.37\times10^{-9}\ \text{cm}^2/\text{s}$$

The ratio $D_H/D_N \approx 9.4\times10^{4}$. Hydrogen diffuses ~10⁵ times faster because it is a far smaller atom that migrates through interstitial sites with a much lower activation energy ($15\ \text{kJ/mol}$ vs $147\ \text{kJ/mol}$); nitrogen, being larger, requires substantially more thermal energy to hop between interstitial positions in the close-packed FCC lattice.

## 最终答案
(4.12e-4, 4.37e-9)
