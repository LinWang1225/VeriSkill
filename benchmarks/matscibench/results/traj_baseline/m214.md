## 题目
The strength of titanium is found to be 65,000 psi when the grain size is $17 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}$ and 82,000 psi when the grain size is $0.8 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}$. Determine (a) the constants in the Hall-Petch equation; and (b) the strength of the titanium when the grain size is reduced to $0.2 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}$. Give your answer as a tuple: (K_psi_sqrt_m, sigma_0_psi, strength_psi).
Give your final answer in units of (psi sqrt(m), psi, psi).

## 过程
The Hall-Petch equation is $\sigma = \sigma_0 + K d^{-1/2}$.

Using the two data points:
- $\sigma_1 = 65000$ psi at $d_1 = 17\times10^{-6}$ m
- $\sigma_2 = 82000$ psi at $d_2 = 0.8\times10^{-6}$ m

Subtracting the two equations eliminates $\sigma_0$:

$$K = \frac{\sigma_2 - \sigma_1}{d_2^{-1/2} - d_1^{-1/2}}$$

Then $\sigma_0 = \sigma_1 - K d_1^{-1/2}$, and the strength at $d_3 = 0.2\times10^{-6}$ m is $\sigma_3 = \sigma_0 + K d_3^{-1/2}$.

Numeric evaluation gives:
- $K = 19.42$ psi·√m
- $\sigma_0 = 60290.56$ psi
- $\sigma_3 = 103709.44$ psi

## 最终答案
(19.42, 60290.56, 103709.44)
