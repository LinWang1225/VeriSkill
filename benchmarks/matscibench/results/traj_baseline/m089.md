## 过程
The carburization solution is

$$\frac{C_x-C_0}{C_s-C_0}=1-\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**1. Evaluate the concentration ratio:**

$$\frac{0.30-0.10}{0.90-0.10}=\frac{0.20}{0.80}=0.25$$

So $\operatorname{erf}(z)=1-0.25=0.75$, where $z=\dfrac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}$.

**2. Invert the error function (Table 6.1):**
$\operatorname{erf}(0.80)=0.7421$, $\operatorname{erf}(0.85)=0.7707$. Linear interpolation:

$$z=0.80+\frac{0.75-0.7421}{0.7707-0.7421}(0.05)\approx 0.8138$$

**3. Diffusivity of C in γ-Fe at 1100 °C (Table 6.2):**
$D_0=2.3\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d=148{,}000\,\text{J/mol}$, $T=1373\,\text{K}$:

$$D=D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)=2.3\times10^{-5}\exp\!\left(-\frac{148000}{8.31\times1373}\right)\approx 5.35\times10^{-11}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**4. Solve for time** with $x=4\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$:

$$t=\frac{x^2}{4Dz^2}=\frac{(4\times10^{-3})^2}{4(5.35\times10^{-11})(0.8138)^2}\approx 1.129\times10^{5}\,\text{s}$$

$$t\approx \frac{1.129\times10^{5}}{3600}\approx 31.36\,\text{h}$$

## 最终答案
31.36
