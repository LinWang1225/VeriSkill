## 题目
Calculate the electrical conductivity of nickel at $-50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and at $+500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as a tuple: (electrical_conductivity_at_minus_50C, electrical_conductivity_at_plus_500C).
Give your final answer in units of (ohm⁻¹·cm⁻¹, ohm⁻¹·cm⁻¹).

## 过程
For a metal, resistivity varies linearly with temperature:

$$\rho(T) = \rho_{20}\,[1 + \alpha(T - 20^\circ\mathrm{C})]$$

and conductivity is $\sigma = 1/\rho$.

**Reference values for nickel (Callister, at 20 °C):**
- $\rho_{20} = 6.99\times10^{-7}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}$
- $\alpha = 0.006\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}^{-1}$

**Unit conversion:** Since $1\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m} = 100\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{cm}$, a conductivity in $(\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m})^{-1}$ is multiplied by $0.01$ to get $(\Omega\cdot\mathrm{cm})^{-1}$.

**At −50 °C** ($\Delta T = -70$):
$$\rho = 6.99\times10^{-7}[1+0.006(-70)] = 6.99\times10^{-7}(0.58)=4.054\times10^{-7}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}$$
$$\sigma = \frac{1}{\rho}\times0.01 = 2.467\times10^{4}\ (\Omega\cdot\mathrm{cm})^{-1}$$

**At +500 °C** ($\Delta T = 480$):
$$\rho = 6.99\times10^{-7}[1+0.006(480)] = 6.99\times10^{-7}(3.88)=2.712\times10^{-6}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}$$
$$\sigma = \frac{1}{\rho}\times0.01 = 3.687\times10^{3}\ (\Omega\cdot\mathrm{cm})^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
(24665.78, 3687.15)
