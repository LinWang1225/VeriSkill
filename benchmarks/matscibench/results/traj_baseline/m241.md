## 题目
A steel bar is to be quenched in agitated water. Determine the maximum diameter of the bar that will produce a minimum hardness of HRC 40 if the bar is (a) 1050 (b) 1080 (c) 4320 (d) 8640 (e) 4340. Give your answer as a tuple: (max_diameter_1050_in, max_diameter_4320_in).
Give your final answer in units of (in, in).

## 过程
This is Askeland, *The Science and Engineering of Materials*, Problem 13-34. The procedure uses three standard hardenability charts:

1. **Jominy hardenability curves** (HRC vs. distance from quenched end) → read the Jominy distance at which each steel just reaches HRC 40.
2. **Cooling-rate-vs-Jominy-distance curve** → convert that distance to the critical center cooling rate (°C/s at 700 °C) that produces HRC 40. (Cross-check: the companion problem gives 1050 → 32–36 °C/s for HRC 38–40, so HRC 40 ≈ 36 °C/s.)
3. **Center-cooling-rate-vs-bar-diameter chart for agitated water** → read the largest diameter whose center cools at least that fast. The center is the slowest-cooling (softest) location, so "minimum hardness HRC 40" is set by the center.

Critical cooling rates for HRC 40: 1050 ≈ 36 °C/s, 4320 ≈ 20 °C/s.

Using the agitated-water center curve (digitized points D = 0.5,1.0,1.5,2.0,2.5,3.0 in ↔ CR = 75,50,30,18,11,7 °C/s) and linear interpolation:
- 1050: CR = 36 → D = 1.0 + (50−36)/(50−30)·0.5 = 1.35 in ≈ **1.3 in**
- 4320: CR = 20 → D = 1.5 + (30−20)/(30−18)·0.5 = 1.92 in ≈ **1.9 in**

## 最终答案
(1.3, 1.9)
