## 题目
For a 99.65 wt% Fe-0.35 wt% C alloy at a temperature just below the eutectoid, determine the following: (a) The fractions of total ferrite and cementite phases (b) The fractions of the proeutectoid ferrite and pearlite (c) The fraction of eutectoid ferrite. 

Give your answer as a tuple (total ferrite fraction, cementite fraction, proeutectoid ferrite fraction, pearlite fraction, eutectoid ferrite fraction).

## 过程
For the Fe–0.35 wt% C alloy just below the eutectoid, use the lever rule with the tie-line endpoints: ferrite $C_\alpha=0.022$, eutectoid $C_e=0.76$, cementite $C_{\theta}=6.67$ wt% C, and alloy $C_0=0.35$ wt% C.

**(a) Total ferrite and cementite** (tie line from 0.022 to 6.67):
$$W_\alpha=\frac{6.67-0.35}{6.67-0.022}=0.9507,\quad W_\theta=\frac{0.35-0.022}{6.67-0.022}=0.0493$$

**(b) Proeutectoid ferrite and pearlite** (tie line from 0.022 to 0.76, since 0.35 < 0.76 is a hypoeutectoid steel):
$$W_{\alpha'}=\frac{0.76-0.35}{0.76-0.022}=0.5556,\quad W_p=\frac{0.35-0.022}{0.76-0.022}=0.4444$$

**(c) Eutectoid ferrite** = total ferrite − proeutectoid ferrite:
$$W_{\alpha,e}=0.9507-0.5556=0.3951$$
(Equivalently, $W_p\times\frac{6.67-0.76}{6.67-0.022}=0.4444\times0.8894=0.3951$.)

## 最终答案
(0.9507, 0.0493, 0.5556, 0.4444, 0.3951)
