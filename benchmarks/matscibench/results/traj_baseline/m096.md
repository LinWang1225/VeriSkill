## 题目
Carbon is allowed to diffuse through a steel plate 10 mm thick.  The concentrations of carbon at the two faces are 0.85 and 0.40 kg C/m³ Fe (maintained constant).  If the diffusion pre-exponential factor and activation energy are D₀ = 5.0×10⁻⁷ m²/s and Q = 77 000 J/mol, respectively, compute the temperature at which the diffusion flux is J = 6.3×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m²·s).
Give your final answer in units of K.

## 过程
Fick's first law for steady-state diffusion through a plate of thickness L:

$$J = -D\frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x} = D_0 e^{-Q/(RT)}\frac{C_1-C_2}{L}$$

Solving for T:

$$e^{-Q/(RT)} = \frac{JL}{D_0(C_1-C_2)} \;\Rightarrow\; T = -\frac{Q}{R\ln\!\left(\frac{JL}{D_0(C_1-C_2)}\right)}$$

With L = 0.01 m, ΔC = 0.85 − 0.40 = 0.45 kg/m³, D₀ = 5.0×10⁻⁷ m²/s, Q = 77000 J/mol, J = 6.3×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m²·s), R = 8.314 J/(mol·K):

- JL/(D₀ΔC) = (6.3×10⁻¹⁰)(0.01)/((5.0×10⁻⁷)(0.45)) = 2.80×10⁻⁵
- ln(2.80×10⁻⁵) = −10.4833
- T = −77000 / (8.314 × (−10.4833)) = 883.45 K

## 最终答案
883.45
