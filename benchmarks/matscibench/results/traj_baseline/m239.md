## 题目
A 1030 steel is given an improper quench and temper heat treatment, producing a final structure composed of $60 \%$ martensite and $40 \%$ ferrite. Estimate the carbon content of the martensite and the austenitizing temperature that was used. What austenitizing temperature would you recommend? Give your answer as a tuple: (carbon_content_percent, austenitizing_temperature_used_C, recommended_austenitizing_temperature_C).
Give your final answer in units of (%, °C, °C).

## 过程
A 1030 steel contains C₀ = 0.30 wt% C. The improper austenitizing treatment was done in the intercritical (α+γ) region, so at the austenitizing temperature the steel was a mixture of ferrite and austenite. On quenching, the austenite → martensite and the ferrite remains ferrite. Therefore:

- W_ferrite = W_α = 0.40
- W_martensite = W_γ = 0.60

**Lever rule** in the (α+γ) two-phase field (ferrite carbon on the GP line is negligible, C_α ≈ 0):

$$W_\gamma = \frac{C_0 - C_\alpha}{C_\gamma - C_\alpha} \;\Rightarrow\; 0.60 = \frac{0.30}{C_\gamma} \;\Rightarrow\; C_\gamma = 0.50\;\text{wt\%}$$

The martensite inherits the austenite's carbon, so **martensite carbon ≈ 0.50%**.

**Austenitizing temperature used:** This is the temperature on the A₃ (GS) line at C_γ = 0.50%. Using the GS line linearized between (912 °C, 0% C) and (727 °C, 0.77% C):

$$T = 912 - \frac{185}{0.77}C \;\Rightarrow\; T(0.50) \approx 792\;°C \;\approx\; 790\;°C$$

**Recommended austenitizing temperature:** For full austenitization the steel must be heated above A₃ for 0.30% C. A₃(0.30) ≈ 840 °C; adding ~30–50 °C gives a recommended austenitizing temperature of about **870 °C**.

## 最终答案
(0.50, 790, 870)
