## 题目
Estimate the maximum and minimum thermal conductivity values for a cermet that contains 90 vol\% titanium carbide (TiC) particles in a nickel matrix. Assume thermal conductivities of 27 and $67 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{m} \cdot \mathrm{K}$ for TiC and Ni, respectively.

Give your answer as a tuple (maximum, minimum).
Give your final answer in units of (W/mK, W/mK).

## 过程
For a two-phase cermet, the thermal conductivity lies between the **Voigt (upper)** and **Reuss (lower)** bounds.

Given: $V_{TiC}=0.90$, $V_{Ni}=0.10$, $k_{TiC}=27$, $k_{Ni}=67\ \mathrm{W/m\cdot K}$.

**Maximum (Voigt / parallel model):**
$$k_{\max}=V_{TiC}k_{TiC}+V_{Ni}k_{Ni}=0.90(27)+0.10(67)=24.3+6.7=31.0$$

**Minimum (Reuss / series model):**
$$\frac{1}{k_{\min}}=\frac{V_{TiC}}{k_{TiC}}+\frac{V_{Ni}}{k_{Ni}}=\frac{0.90}{27}+\frac{0.10}{67}=0.03333+0.001493=0.034827$$
$$k_{\min}=\frac{1}{0.034827}\approx 28.71$$

## 最终答案
(31.0, 28.71)
