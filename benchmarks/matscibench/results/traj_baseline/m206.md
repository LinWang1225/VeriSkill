## 题目
Determine the planar density and packing fraction for FCC nickel in the (100), (110), and (111) planes. Which, if any, of these planes are close packed? Give your answer as a tuple: (planar_density_100, packing_fraction_100, planar_density_110, packing_fraction_110, planar_density_111, packing_fraction_111, close_packed_planes).
Give your final answer in units of (atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , atoms/cm², , ).

## 过程
FCC nickel: lattice parameter $a = 0.352\,\text{nm} = 3.52\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$, atomic radius $R = a/(2\sqrt2)$. Planar density $= n/A_\text{plane}$; packing fraction $= n\pi R^2/A_\text{plane}$, where $n$ is the equivalent number of atoms whose centers lie in the plane.

**(100) plane** — square of side $a$, area $a^2$. 4 corners (¼ each) + 1 face-center = 2 atoms.
- $PD_{100} = 2/a^2 = 1.614\times10^{15}\,\text{atoms/cm}^2$
- $PF_{100} = 2\pi R^2/a^2 = \pi/4 = 0.785$

**(110) plane** — rectangle $a\times a\sqrt2$, area $a^2\sqrt2$. 4 corners (¼ each) + 2 face-centers (½ each) = 2 atoms.
- $PD_{110} = 2/(a^2\sqrt2) = 1.141\times10^{15}\,\text{atoms/cm}^2$
- $PF_{110} = \pi/(4\sqrt2) = 0.555$

**(111) plane** — equilateral triangle, side $a\sqrt2$, area $\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a^2$. 3 corners (1/6 each) + 3 edge-centers (½ each) = 2 atoms.
- $PD_{111} = 4/(\sqrt3\,a^2) = 1.864\times10^{15}\,\text{atoms/cm}^2$
- $PF_{111} = \pi/(2\sqrt3) = 0.907$

The (111) plane has the highest packing fraction (0.907, the theoretical 2D maximum), so it is the close-packed plane; (100) and (110) are not.

## 最终答案
(1.614e15, 0.785, 1.141e15, 0.555, 1.864e15, 0.907, (111))
