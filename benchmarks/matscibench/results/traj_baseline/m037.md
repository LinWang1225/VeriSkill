## 题目
Specify an aging temperature for a $95 \mathrm{Al}-5 \mathrm{Cu}$ alloy that will produce a maximum of $5 \mathrm{wt} \% \theta$ precipitate. Give your answer as the value of aging_temperature.
Give your final answer in units of °C.

## 过程
For a 95Al–5Cu alloy, the equilibrium fraction of θ (Al₂Cu) precipitate that forms on aging is fixed by the lever rule between α (Cu dissolved in Al) and θ. We want this fraction to be at most 5 wt%, so we set it equal to 0.05 to find the limiting aging temperature.

**1. Composition of θ = Al₂Cu**

$$C_\theta=\frac{M_{Cu}}{2M_{Al}+M_{Cu}}\times100=\frac{63.55}{2(26.98)+63.55}\times100=54.1\ \text{wt\% Cu}$$

**2. Required α composition (lever rule)**

With overall $C_0=5$ wt% Cu and $W_\theta=0.05$:

$$W_\theta=\frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_\theta-C_\alpha}\;\Rightarrow\; C_\alpha=\frac{C_0-W_\theta C_\theta}{1-W_\theta}=\frac{5-0.05(54.1)}{0.95}=2.42\ \text{wt\% Cu}$$

**3. Aging temperature from the Al-rich solvus**

The α-solvus follows $C=A\exp(-Q/RT)$. Fitting the assessed Al–Cu solvus data (548°C→5.65, 500°C→3.8, 450°C→2.3, 400°C→1.3, 350°C→0.6 wt% Cu) and solving for the temperature at $C_\alpha=2.42$ wt% Cu gives $T\approx 729.7\ \text{K}\approx 456.5^{\circ}\text{C}$, i.e. about **450°C** by phase-diagram reading. Aging at or above this temperature keeps the equilibrium θ fraction ≤ 5 wt%.

## 最终答案
450
