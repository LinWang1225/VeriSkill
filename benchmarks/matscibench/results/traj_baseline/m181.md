## 题目
A piece of metal is subjected to a cyclic stress of 80 MPa. If the phase angle $\delta = 0.1^\circ$ and no heat is transferred to the surroundings, what will be the temperature rise after 100,000 cycles? Data: $E = 80 \text{ GPa}$, $C = 800 \text{ J} / (\text{kg} \cdot \text{K})$, and $\rho = 4.2 \text{ Mg} / \text{m}^3$.
Give your final answer in units of $ {}^{\circ} C$.

## 过程
For a material under cyclic stress with a phase lag δ, the energy dissipated per unit volume per cycle equals the hysteresis loop area:

$$\Delta U = \pi \sigma_0 \varepsilon_0 \sin\delta = \frac{\pi \sigma_0^2 \sin\delta}{E}$$

With no heat transferred to the surroundings, all dissipated energy heats the material over $N$ cycles:

$$N\,\Delta U = \rho C \Delta T \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta T = \frac{N\pi\sigma_0^2\sin\delta}{E\rho C}$$

Substituting ($\sigma_0=80\,\text{MPa}$, $\delta=0.1^\circ=1.7453\times10^{-3}\,\text{rad}$, $E=80\,\text{GPa}$, $C=800\,\text{J/(kg·K)}$, $\rho=4200\,\text{kg/m}^3$, $N=10^5$):

$$\Delta T = \frac{10^5 \cdot \pi \cdot (80\times10^6)^2 \cdot \sin(0.1^\circ)}{80\times10^9 \cdot 4200 \cdot 800} \approx 13.06\;^\circ\text{C}$$

## 最终答案
13.06
