## 题目
An unalloyed steel tool used for machining aluminum automobile wheels has been found to work well, but the purchase records have been lost and you do not know the steel's composition. The microstructure of the steel is tempered martensite. Assume that you cannot estimate the composition of the steel from the structure. Design a treatment that may help determine the steel's carbon content. Give your answer as a tuple (carbon content lower bound, carbon content upper bound).
Give your final answer in units of (%, %).

## 过程
对一把用于切削、淬透后硬度已达饱和的高碳工具钢，淬火后测得硬度落在平台区（≈64–66 HRC），由此只能把碳含量界定在平台区间内，即约 **0.6%–1.0% C**。这就是该处理"有助于确定"碳含量、但只能给出区间的原因。

## 最终答案
(0.6, 1.0)
