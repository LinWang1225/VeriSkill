## 题目
The unit cell for $\mathrm{Fe}{3} \mathrm{O}{4}\left(\mathrm{FeO}-\mathrm{Fe}{2} \mathrm{O}{3}\right)$ has cubic symmetry with a unit cell edge length of 0.839 nm . If the density of this material is $5.24 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, compute its atomic packing factor. For this computation, you will need to use the ionic radii listed in  table 3.4 . The atomic weights of iron and oxygen $\left(A{\mathrm{Fe}}\right.$ and $\left.A{\mathrm{O}}\right)$ are $55.85 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ and $16.00 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.

## 过程
Fe₃O₄ = FeO·Fe₂O₃, so each formula unit contains 1 Fe²⁺, 2 Fe³⁺, and 4 O²⁻ ions.

**1. Molecular weight**
$$A_{\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4}=3(55.85)+4(16.00)=231.55\ \text{g/mol}$$

**2. Unit cell volume**
$$V_c = (0.839\times10^{-7}\ \text{cm})^3 = 5.906\times10^{-22}\ \text{cm}^3$$

**3. Formula units per unit cell** (from $\rho = nA/(V_c N_A)$)
$$n=\frac{\rho V_c N_A}{A}=\frac{(5.24)(5.906\times10^{-22})(6.022\times10^{23})}{231.55}\approx 8.05\approx 8$$

This confirms the inverse-spinel structure with 8 formula units per unit cell.

**4. Total ionic volume** (radii from Table 3.4: Fe²⁺=0.077 nm, Fe³⁺=0.069 nm, O²⁻=0.140 nm)
$$V_{\text{ions}}=n\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi\left[1\,r_{\text{Fe}^{2+}}^3+2\,r_{\text{Fe}^{3+}}^3+4\,r_{\text{O}^{2-}}^3\right]$$
$$=8\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi\left[(0.077)^3+2(0.069)^3+4(0.140)^3\right]\text{nm}^3=4.076\times10^{->^{-22}\ \text{cm}^3$$

**5. Atomic packing factor**
$$\text{APF}=\frac{V_{\text{ions}}}{V_c}=\frac{4.076\times10^{-22}}{5.906\times10^{-22}}\approx 0.690$$

## 最终答案
0.690
