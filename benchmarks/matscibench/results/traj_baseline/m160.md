## 题目
The transmissivity $T$ of a transparent material 15 mm thick to normally incident light is 0.80 . If the index of refraction of this material is 1.5, compute the thickness of material that will yield a transmissivity of 0.70. All reflection losses should be considered.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
With $R=0.04$, $(1-R)^2=0.9216$:

$$\beta = -\frac{1}{15}\ln\!\left(\frac{0.80}{0.9216}\right) = 0.009433\;\text{mm}^{-1}$$

$$l_2 = -\frac{\ln(0.70/0.9216)}{\beta} = -\frac{\ln(0.7595)}{0.009433} = 29.16\;\text{mm}$$

## 最终答案
29.16
