## 题目
The fatigue data for a steel alloy are given as follows:

| Stress Amplitude [MPa (ksi)] | Cycles to Failure |
| :--: | :--: |
| $470(68.0)$ | $10^{4}$ |
| $440(63.4)$ | $3 \times 10^{4}$ |
| $390(56.2)$ | $10^{5}$ |
| $350(51.0)$ | $3 \times 10^{5}$ |
| $310(45.3)$ | $10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $3 \times 10^{6}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{7}$ |
| $290(42.2)$ | $10^{8}$ |

(a) What is the fatigue limit for this alloy?
(b) Determine fatigue lifetimes at stress amplitudes of 415 MPa (60,000 psi).
(c) Estimate fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ and $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles.\

Give your answer as a tuple (fatigue limit, fatigue lifetimes of 415 MPa, fatigue strengths at $2 \times 10^{4}$ cycles, fatigue strengths at $6 \times 10^{5}$ cycles).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, cycles, MPa, MPa).

## 过程
**(a) Fatigue limit.** The stress amplitude 290 MPa produces runout (no failure) at $10^6$, $10^7$, and $10^8$ cycles, while 310 MPa fails at $10^6$. The stress below which failure does not occur is therefore the plateau value:

$$S_f = 290\ \text{MPa}$$

**(b) Lifetime at 415 MPa.** 415 MPa lies in the finite-life region between the data points $(440,\ 3\times10^4)$ and $(390,\ 10^5)$. Assuming Basquin/log–log linearity, interpolate $\log S$ vs $\log N$:

$$\log N = \log N_1 + \frac{\log S - \log S_1}{\log S_2 - \log S_1}(\log N_2 - \log N_1)$$

$$\log N = 4.4771 + \frac{2.6180 - 2.6435}{2.5911 - 2.6435}(5.0000 - 4.4771) = 4.7307$$

$$N \approx 10^{4.7307} \approx 5.38\times10^{4}\ \text{cycles}$$

**(c) Fatigue strengths.** Invert the same log–log relation.

At $N = 2\times10^4$ (between $10^4$ and $3\times10^4$):

$$\log S = 2.6721 + \frac{4.3010 - 4.0000}{4.4771 - 4.0000}(2.6435 - 2.6721) = 2.6541$$

$$S \approx 10^{2.6541} \approx 450.8\ \text{MPa}$$

At $N = 6\times10^5$ (between $3\times10^5$ and $10^6$):

$$\log S = 2.5441 + \frac{5.7782 - 5.4771}{6.0000 - 5.4771}(2.4914 - 2.5441) = 2.5138$$

$$S \approx 10^{2.5138} \approx 326.4\ \text{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
(290, 5.38e4, 450.8, 326.4)
