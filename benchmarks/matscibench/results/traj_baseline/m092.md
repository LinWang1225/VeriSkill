## 题目
At what temperature will the diffusion coefficient for the diffusion of zinc in copper have a value of $2.6 \times$ $10^{-16} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$? Use the diffusion data in  table 6.2 .

## 过程
For interdiffusion of Zn in Cu (FCC), Table 6.2 gives $D_0 = 2.4\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$ and $Q_d = 189{,}000\,\text{J/mol}$.

The Arrhenius relation is
$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$$

Solving for $T$:
$$T = \frac{Q_d}{R\ln(D_0/D)}$$

Substituting $D = 2.6\times10^{-16}$, $R = 8.31\,\text{J/(mol·K)}$:
$$T = \frac{189000}{8.31\,\ln\!\left(\frac{2.4\times10^{-5}}{2.6\times10^{-16}}\right)} \approx \frac{189000}{8.31 \times 25.39} \approx 900.8\,\text{K}$$

## 最终答案
901
