## 题目
Calculate the equilibrium number of vacancies per cubic meter for copper at 1000℃. The energy for vacancy formation is 0.9 eV/atom; the atomic weight and density (at 1000℃) for copper are 63.5 g/mol and 8.4 g/cm³ respectively.
Give your final answer in units of vacancies/m³.

## 过程
The equilibrium vacancy concentration is

$$N_v = N \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{kT}\right)$$

**1. Number of atomic sites per m³:**

$$N = \frac{N_A \rho}{A} = \frac{(6.022\times10^{23})(8.4\times10^{6})}{63.5} = 7.966\times10^{28}\ \text{sites/m}^3$$

(density $\rho = 8.4\ \text{g/cm}^3 = 8.4\times10^{6}\ \text{g/m}^3$).

**2. Temperature and Boltzmann factor:**

$$T = 1000 + 273 = 1273\ \text{K},\quad k = 8.617\times10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}$$

$$\frac{Q_v}{kT} = \frac{0.9}{(8.617\times10^{-5})(1273)} = 8.206$$

$$\exp(-8.206) = 2.734\times10^{-4}$$

**3. Vacancy concentration:**

$$N_v = (7.966\times10^{28})(2.734\times10^{-4}) = 2.178\times10^{25}\ \text{vacancies/m}^3$$

## 最终答案
2.18e25
