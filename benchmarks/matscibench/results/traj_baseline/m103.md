## 过程
For elastic deformation, stress and strain are related by Hooke's law:

$$\varepsilon = \frac{\sigma}{E} = \frac{F}{A_0 E}$$

**Cross-sectional area:**
$$A_0 = 15.2 \times 19.1 = 290.32 \ \mathrm{mm^2} = 2.9032 \times 10^{-4} \ \mathrm{m^2}$$

**Stress:**
$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A_0} = \frac{44{,}500 \ \mathrm{N}}{2.9032 \times 10^{-4} \ \mathrm{m^2}} = 1.5328 \times 10^{8} \ \mathrm{Pa} = 153.28 \ \mathrm{MPa}$$

**Modulus of elasticity for copper (Table 7.1):**
$$E = 110 \ \mathrm{GPa} = 1.10 \times 10^{5} \ \mathrm{MPa}$$

**Strain:**
$$\varepsilon = \frac{153.28 \ \mathrm{MPa}}{110 \times 10^{3} \ \mathrm{MPa}} = 1.393 \times 10^{-3}$$

## 最终答案
1.393e-3
