## 题目
The strains measured on the surface of a piece of sheet metal after deformation are $\varepsilon_1 = 0.182$, $\varepsilon_2 = -0.035$. The stress-strain curve in tension can be approximated by $\sigma = 30 + 40\varepsilon$. Assume the von Mises criterion, and assume that the loading was such that the ratio of $\varepsilon_2 / \varepsilon_1$ was constant. Calculate the levels of $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ reached before unloading. Give your answer as an ordered tuple ($\varepsilon_1$, $\varepsilon_2$).

## 过程
The strains measured after deformation (after unloading) are the **plastic** strains: $\varepsilon_1^p=0.182,\ \varepsilon_2^p=-0.035$, with $\varepsilon_3^p=-(\varepsilon_1^p+\varepsilon_2^p)=-0.147$ (incompressibility). We must add back the elastic strains recovered during unloading.

**1. Stress ratio from the flow rule (plane stress, $\sigma_3=0$).**
For von Mises (Lévy–Mises), $d\varepsilon_i^p=\lambda S_i$, so
$$\frac{\varepsilon_2^p}{\varepsilon_1^p}=\frac{2\sigma_2-\sigma_1}{2\sigma_1-\sigma_2}.$$
With $r_p=-0.035/0.182=-0.1923$ and $\beta=\sigma_2/\sigma_1$:
$$\beta=\frac{2r_p+1}{2+r_p}=0.3404.$$

**2. Effective plastic strain.**
$$\bar\varepsilon^p=\sqrt{\tfrac23}\sqrt{(\varepsilon_1^p)^2+(\varepsilon_2^p)^2+(\varepsilon_3^p)^2}=0.19315.$$

**3. Effective stress from the tensile flow curve** $\bar\sigma=30+40\bar\varepsilon^p$ (the curve is a flow curve: $\varepsilon$ is plastic strain, since at $\sigma=30$, $\varepsilon=0$):
$$\bar\sigma=30+40(0.19315)=37.73\ \text{ksi}.$$
Then $\sigma_1=\bar\sigma/\sqrt{1-\beta+\beta^2}=42.84$ ksi, $\sigma_2=\beta\sigma_1=14.58$ ksi.

**4. Elastic recovery** (plane stress, aluminum sheet: $E\approx 70$ GPa $\approx 10^4$ ksi, $\nu=0.3$):
$$\varepsilon_1^e=\frac{\sigma_1-\nu\sigma_2}{E}=0.00385,\qquad \varepsilon_2^e=\frac{\sigma_2-\nu\sigma_1}{E}=0.000173.$$

**5. Strains before unloading** $=$ plastic $+$ elastic:
$$\varepsilon_1=0.182+0.00385=0.18585,\qquad \varepsilon_2=-0.035+0.000173=-0.03483.$$

## 最终答案
(0.1858, -0.0348)
