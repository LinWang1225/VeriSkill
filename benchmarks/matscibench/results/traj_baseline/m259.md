## 题目
A current density of $0.1 \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{cm}^2$ is applied to the iron in an iron-zinc corrosion cell. Calculate the weight loss of zinc per hour (a) if the zinc has a surface area of $10 \mathrm{~cm}^2$ and the iron has a surface area of $100 \mathrm{~cm}^2$ and (b) if the zinc has a surface area of $100 \mathrm{~cm}^2$ and the iron has a surface area of $10 \mathrm{~cm}^2$. 

Give your answer as a tuple: (weight loss zinc per hour in (a), weight loss zinc  per hour in (b)).
Give your final answer in units of (g/h, g/h).

## 过程
In an iron–zinc galvanic corrosion cell, zinc is the anode (Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻) and iron is the cathode. The applied current density (0.1 A/cm²) is on the iron surface, so the total cell current is

$$I = i_{\text{Fe}} \cdot A_{\text{Fe}}$$

This same current flows through the zinc anode, so the zinc weight loss is governed by I, not by the zinc area:

$$\Delta m = \frac{I \, t \, M_{\text{Zn}}}{nF},\quad M_{\text{Zn}}=65.38\,\text{g/mol},\; n=2,\; F=96485\,\text{C/mol},\; t=3600\,\text{s}$$

**(a)** A_Fe = 100 cm² → I = 0.1 × 100 = 10 A
$$\Delta m_a = \frac{10 \times 3600 \times 65.38}{2 \times 96485} = 12.20\ \text{g/h}$$

**(b)** A_Fe = 10 cm² → I = 0.1 × 10 = 1 A
$$\Delta m_b = \frac{1 \times 3600 \times 65.38}{2 \times 96485} = 1.22\ \text{g/h}$$

The zinc surface area only affects the zinc current density, not the total current (which is set by the iron cathode), so it does not enter the mass-loss calculation.

## 最终答案
(12.20, 1.22)
