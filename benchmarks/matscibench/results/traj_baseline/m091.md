## 题目
Consider a diffusion couple composed of two cobalt-iron alloys; one has a composition of $75 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Co-25 wt $\%$ Fe; the other alloy composition is $50 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Co-50 wt $\%$ Fe. If this couple is heated to a temperature of $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ (1073 K) for $20,000 \mathrm{~s}$, determine how far from the original interface into the $50 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Co-50 wt $\%$ Fe alloy the composition has increased to $52 \mathrm{wt} \%$ Co-48 wt $\%$ Fe. For the diffusion coefficient, assume values of $6.6 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$ and $247,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$, respectively, for the preexponential and activation energy.

## 过程
For a diffusion couple with initial compositions $C_1 = 75$ wt% Co (at $x<0$) and $C_2 = 50$ wt% Co (at $x>0$), the solution to Fick's second law is

$$C(x,t)=\frac{C_1+C_2}{2}-\frac{C_1-C_2}{2}\,\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

Set $C(x,t)=52$ wt% Co and solve for the error-function argument $\eta = x/(2\sqrt{Dt})$:

$$\operatorname{erf}(\eta)=\frac{(C_1+C_2)/2 - C_x}{(C_1-C_2)/2}=\frac{62.5-52}{12.5}=\frac{10.5}{12.5}=0.84$$

From Table 6.1, $\operatorname{erf}(0.95)=0.8209$ and $\operatorname{erf}(1.0)=0.8427$. Linear interpolation gives

$$\eta \approx 0.95+\frac{0.84-0.8209}{0.8427-0.8209}(0.05)\approx 0.994$$

The diffusion coefficient follows from the Arrhenius relation $D=D_0\exp(-Q/RT)$:

$$D=(6.6\times10^{-6})\exp\!\left[-\frac{247000}{(8.31)(1073)}\right]\approx 6.15\times10^{-18}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

Finally, with $t=20000$ s:

$$x = 2\eta\sqrt{Dt}=2(0.994)\sqrt{(6.15\times10^{-18})(20000)}\approx 6.97\times10^{-7}\ \text{m}$$

## 最终答案
0.70
