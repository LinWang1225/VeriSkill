## 题目
Estimate the resistivity of a copper $-0.06 \mathrm{wt} \%$ phosphorus alloy at $200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Give your answer as the value of resistivity.
Give your final answer in units of Ω·m.

## 过程
This is a Callister-style dilute-alloy resistivity problem, solved with **Matthiessen's rule**:

$$\rho_{\text{total}}(T)=\rho_{\text{Cu}}(T)+\rho_{\text{impurity}}$$

The impurity (residual) resistivity is essentially temperature-independent; only the copper lattice term is corrected for temperature.

**1. Pure copper at 200 °C** (reference 20 °C, Table 18.1 values: $\rho_{20}=1.7\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$, $\alpha=0.00393\ ^\circ\text{C}^{-1}$):

$$\rho_{\text{Cu}}(200)=\rho_{20}\bigl[1+\alpha(200-20)\bigr]$$
$$=1.7\times10^{-8}\bigl[1+0.00393\times180\bigr]=1.7\times10^{-8}\times1.7074=2.903\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

**2. Phosphorus impurity contribution** (dilute linear slope from the Cu–solute resistivity table: $d\rho/dc = 6.7\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m per wt\%}$ for P):

$$\rho_{\text{imp}}=(6.7\times10^{-8})(0.06)=0.402\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

**3. Total:**

$$\rho_{\text{total}}=2.903\times10^{-8}+0.402\times10^{-8}=3.305\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

## 最终答案
3.3e-8
