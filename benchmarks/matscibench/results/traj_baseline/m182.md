## 题目
A bar of copper with randomly oriented grains was deformed in tension to a stress, $\sigma_{\text{av}}$, which caused plastic deformation and then the load was released. Determine whether a grain oriented with [100] parallel to the tensile axis would be left in a state of residual tension or residual compression. For [100], the Taylor factor, $M=2.449$ and $E=66 \text{ GPa}$. For randomly oriented copper, $M=3.067$ and $E=128 \text{ GPa}$. Give your answer as 1 if it would be left in a state of residual tension, 2  if residual compression, and 3 otherwise.

## 过程
The yield stress of a grain scales with its Taylor factor: $\sigma_y = M\tau_c$.

**Compare the [100] grain to the polycrystal average:**
- $M_{[100]} = 2.449 < M_{\text{av}} = 3.067$, so $\sigma_{y,[100]} = 0.7985\,\sigma_{y,\text{av}}$.
- The [100] grain is a **soft orientation**: it yields first and, during plastic flow, accumulates more plastic strain than the average grain.

**Stress state during plastic flow (Taylor/iso-strain model):**
All grains share the same total strain. A soft grain that has yielded and is flowing carries a lower stress than a hard (not-yet-yielded or barely-yielded) grain. Hence during loading at the macroscopic stress $\sigma_{\text{av}}$:

$$\sigma_{[100]} < \sigma_{\text{av}}$$

**After unloading:** The load is removed by subtracting the average stress $\sigma_{\text{av}}$ elastically from every grain. The residual stress in the [100] grain is

$$\sigma_{\text{res},[100]} = \sigma_{[100]} - \sigma_{\text{av}} < 0.$$

A negative residual stress means **residual compression**. (Physically: the soft [100] grain stretched plastically more than its neighbors; on elastic unloading it is "too long" relative to them and is squeezed into compression.)

## 最终答案
2
