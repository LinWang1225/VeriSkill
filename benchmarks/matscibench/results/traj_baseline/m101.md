## 题目
For the predeposition heat treatment of a semiconducting device, gallium atoms are to be diffused into silicon at a temperature of $1150^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 2.5 h . If the required concentration of Ga at a position $2 \mu \mathrm{~m}$ below the surface is $8 \times 10^{23}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{m}^{3}$, compute the required surface concentration of Ga. Assume the following:
(i) The surface concentration remains constant
(ii) The background concentration is $2 \times 10^{19} \mathrm{Ga}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{m}^{3}$
(iii) Preexponential and activation energy values are $3.74 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$ and $3.39 \mathrm{eV} /$ atom, respectively.

Give your final answer in units of Ga atoms/m^3.

## 过程
For predeposition (constant surface concentration $C_0$, background $C_b$), the solution to Fick's second law is:

$$C(x,t)=C_0-(C_0-C_b)\,\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**1. Diffusivity at $T=1150^{\circ}\mathrm{C}=1423\ \mathrm{K}$:**

$$D=D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{kT}\right)=3.74\times10^{-5}\exp\!\left(-\frac{3.39}{8.62\times10^{-5}\cdot1423}\right)$$

$$D=3.72\times10^{-17}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$$

**2. Argument of the error function** ($t=2.5\ \mathrm{h}=9000\ \mathrm{s}$, $x=2\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m}$):

$$z=\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}=\frac{2\times10^{-6}}{2\sqrt{(3.72\times10^{-17})(9000)}}=1.729$$

From Table 6.1, interpolating between $z=1.7\ (0.9838)$ and $z=1.8\ (0.9891)$ gives $\operatorname{erf}(z)\approx0.9855$ (exact `erf` = 0.98550).

**3. Solve for $C_0$:**

$$C(x,t)=C_0(1-\operatorname{erf}z)+C_b\operatorname{erf}z$$

$$C_0=\frac{C(x,t)-C_b\operatorname{erf}z}{1-\operatorname{erf}z}
=\frac{8\times10^{23}-(2\times10^{19})(0.9855)}{1-0.9855}$$

$$C_0\approx 5.52\times10^{25}\ \mathrm{atoms/m^3}$$

## 最终答案
5.52e25
