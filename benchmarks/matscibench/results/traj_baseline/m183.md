## 题目
A metal composite composed of metal M reinforced by parallel wires of metal W. The volume fraction of W is 50%, and W has a yield stress of 200 MPa and a modulus of 210 GPa. Metal M has a yield stress of 50 MPa and a modulus of 30 GPa. What is the maximum stress that the composite can carry without yielding?
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
For parallel (continuous) wires loaded along their length, the isostrain condition applies: ε_c = ε_W = ε_M, and σ_c = V_W σ_W + V_M σ_M with V_W = V_M = 0.5.

Each constituent yields at its own elastic strain:
- ε_W,y = σ_W,y / E_W = 200 MPa / 210 GPa = 9.52×10⁻⁴
- ε_M,y = σ_M,y / E_M = 50 MPa / 30 GPa = 1.67×10⁻³

W yields at the smaller strain, so the composite yields when W reaches 200 MPa. At that strain:
- σ_W = 200 MPa
- σ_M = E_M · ε_W,y = 30 GPa × 9.52×10⁻⁴ = 28.57 MPa

σ_c = 0.5(200) + 0.5(28.57) = 114.29 MPa

## 最终答案
114.29
