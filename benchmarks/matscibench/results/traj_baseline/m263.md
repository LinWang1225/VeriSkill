## 题目
When a hypothetical metal having a grain diameter of \(8.2 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~mm}\) is heated to \(500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\) for 12.5 min , the grain diameter increases to \(2.7 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~mm}\). Compute the grain diameter when a specimen of the original material is heated at \(500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\) for 100 min . Assume the grain diameter exponent \(n\) has a value of 2.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
Grain growth follows \(d^n - d_0^n = Kt\). With \(n=2\), \(d_0 = 8.2\times10^{-3}\,\text{mm}\), \(d_1 = 2.7\times10^{-2}\,\text{mm}\) at \(t_1=12.5\) min:

\[
K = \frac{d_1^2 - d_0^2}{t_1} = \frac{(2.7\times10^{-2})^2 - (8.2\times10^{-3})^2}{12.5}
\]

For \(t_2 = 100\) min:

\[
d_2 = \sqrt{d_0^2 + K t_2} = \sqrt{(8.2\times10^{-3})^2 + K\cdot 100}
\]

Numerically: \(K \approx 5.368\times10^{-5}\,\text{mm}^2/\text{min}\), giving \(d_2 \approx 7.32\times10^{-2}\,\text{mm}\).

## 最终答案
7.32e-2
