## 题目
Calculate the fraction of solidification that occurs dendritically when iron nucleates (a) at $10^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ undercooling; (b) at $100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ undercooling; and (c) homogeneously. The specific heat of iron is $5.78 \mathrm{~J} /\left(\mathrm{cm}^3 .{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$. Give your answer as a tuple: (fraction_at_10C_undercooling, fraction_at_100C_undercooling, fraction_homogeneously).
Give your final answer in units of (, , ).

## 过程
**Results:**

- **(a) ΔT = 10°C:** f = (5.78 × 10)/1947 ≈ **0.0297** — only ~3% solidifies dendritically; recalescence quickly returns the melt to T_m.
- **(b) ΔT = 100°C:** f = (5.78 × 100)/1947 ≈ **0.297** — ~30% dendritic.
- **(c) Homogeneous nucleation:** ΔT ≈ 0.2 T_m ≈ 362°C, giving f = 1.075 > 1. Since a fraction cannot exceed unity, the entire melt solidifies dendritically before recalescence can reach T_m: f = **1.0**.

The large undercooling required for homogeneous nucleation means the latent heat released is insufficient to reheat the system to the melting point, so solidification is entirely dendritic.

## 最终答案
(0.0297, 0.297, 1.0)
