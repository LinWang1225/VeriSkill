## 题目
Zinc undergoes corrosion in an acidic solution according to the reaction:
\[
\mathrm{Zn} + 2\mathrm{H}^{+} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Zn}^{2+} + \mathrm{H}_{2}
\]

The rates of both oxidation and reduction half-reactions are controlled by activation polarization.

(a) Compute the rate of oxidation of Zn (in $\mathrm{mol/cm^{2}\cdot s}$), given the following activation polarization data:

\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|}
    \hline
    \textbf{For Zn} & \textbf{For Hydrogen} \\
    \hline
    V_{\left(\mathrm{Zn}/\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\right)} = -0.763\,\mathrm{V} & V_{\left(\mathrm{H}^{+}/\mathrm{H}_{2}\right)} = 0\,\mathrm{V} \\
    \hline
    i_{0} = 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} & i_{0} = 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{A/cm^{2}} \\
    \hline
    \beta = +0.09 & \beta = -0.08 \\
    \hline
\end{array}
\]


(b) Compute the value of the corrosion potential.

Give your answer as a tuple (rate of oxidation in (a), the value of the corrosion potential in (b)).

Give your final answer in units of (mol/(cm${}^{2}$s), V).

## 过程
At the corrosion potential (mixed-potential theory), the oxidation current density equals the reduction current density, both equal to $i_{\text{corr}}$, and the net current is zero.

For activation polarization, $\eta = \beta\log_{10}(i/i_0)$:

- Zn (anodic/oxidation): $V_{\text{corr}} - V_{\text{Zn}} = \beta_{\text{Zn}}\log_{10}(i_{\text{corr}}/i_{0,\text{Zn}})$
- H⁺/H₂ (cathodic/reduction): $V_{\text{corr}} - V_{\text{H}} = \beta_{\text{H}}\log_{10}(i_{\text{corr}}/i_{0,\text{H}})$

Subtracting eliminates $V_{\text{corr}}$:

$$V_{\text{H}}-V_{\text{Zn}} = \beta_{\text{Zn}}\log_{10}\!\frac{i_{\text{corr}}}{i_{0,\text{Zn}}} - \beta_{\text{H}}\log_{10}\!\frac{i_{\text{corr}}}{i_{0,\text{H}}}$$

$$0.763 = 0.09\log_{10}\!\frac{i_{\text{corr}}}{10^{-7}} + 0.08\log_{10}\!\frac{i_{\text{corr}}}{10^{-10}}$$

Solving numerically (Brent's method): $i_{\text{corr}} \approx 1.19\times10^{-4}\ \text{A/cm}^2$.

Back-substituting gives $V_{\text{corr}} = -0.763 + 0.09\log_{10}(1.19\times10^{-4}/10^{-7}) \approx -0.486\ \text{V}$ (consistent from both half-reactions).

The oxidation rate of Zn (n = 2 electrons, F = 96485 C/mol):

$$r = \frac{i_{\text{corr}}}{nF} = \frac{1.19\times10^{-4}}{2(96485)} \approx 6.18\times10^{-10}\ \text{mol/(cm}^2\!\cdot\text{s)}$$

## 最终答案
(6.18e-10, -0.486)
