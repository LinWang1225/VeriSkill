## 题目
A copper-nickel ( $35 \mathrm{wt} \%-65 \mathrm{wt} \%$ ) alloy is corroded in an oxygen-concentration cell using boiling water. What volume of oxygen gas (at 1 atm ) must be consumed at the cathode to corrode 10 g of the alloy? (Assume only divalent ions are produced.)
Give your final answer in units of m^3.

## 过程
The alloy (10 g) contains 35 wt% Cu and 65 wt% Ni:
- $m_{Cu}=3.5\text{ g},\; m_{Ni}=6.5\text{ g}$

Using molar masses $M_{Cu}=63.55$, $M_{Ni}=58.69$ g/mol:
- $n_{Cu}=3.5/63.55=0.05508$ mol
- $n_{Ni}=6.5/58.69=0.11075$ mol

Both metals oxidize to divalent ions ($M\to M^{2+}+2e^-$), so total electrons released:
$$n_e = 2(n_{Cu}+n_{Ni}) = 2(0.05508+0.11075)=0.33165\text{ mol}$$

Cathodic oxygen reduction in water: $O_2+2H_2O+4e^-\to 4OH^-$, so 1 mol $O_2$ consumes 4 mol $e^-$:
$$n_{O_2}=n_e/4=0.08291\text{ mol}$$

Boiling water ⇒ $T=373.15$ K, $P=1$ atm. Ideal gas law $V=nRT/P$:
$$V=\frac{0.08291\times 0.08206\times 373.15}{1}=2.539\text{ L}=2.54\times10^{-3}\text{ m}^3$$

## 最终答案
2.54e-3
