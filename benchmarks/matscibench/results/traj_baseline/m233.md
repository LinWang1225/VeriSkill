## 题目
A steel is heated until $40 \%$ austenite, with a carbon content of $0.5 \%$, forms. Estimate the temperature and the overall carbon content of the steel. Give your answer as a tuple: (temperature, overall_carbon_content).
Give your final answer in units of (°C, %).

## 过程
In the α+γ two-phase region of the Fe–Fe₃C diagram, the austenite composition lies on the **A₃ line** (γ/(α+γ) boundary) and the ferrite composition lies on the **α solvus** (α/(α+γ) boundary).

**1. Temperature from the A₃ line.** The A₃ line runs from (0% C, 912 °C) for pure iron to the eutectoid point (0.76% C, 727 °C). Setting the austenite composition to 0.5% C:

$$T = 912 + \frac{0.5}{0.76}(727-912) \approx 790.3\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

**2. Ferrite composition at this temperature.** The α solvus runs from (0% C, 912 °C) to (0.022% C, 727 °C):

$$C_\alpha = 0.022\,\frac{912-T}{912-727} \approx 0.0145\%$$

**3. Overall carbon content via lever rule.** With 40% austenite (0.5% C) and 60% ferrite (0.0145% C):

$$C_0 = 0.40(0.5) + 0.60(0.0145) \approx 0.2087\%$$

## 最终答案
(790.3, 0.209)
