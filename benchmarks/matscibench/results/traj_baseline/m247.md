## 题目
Five kilograms of continuous boron fibers are introduced in a unidirectional orientation into 8 kg of an aluminum matrix. Calculate (a) the density of the composite, (b) the modulus of elasticity parallel to the fibers, and (c) the modulus of elasticity perpendicular to the fibers. Give your answer as a tuple: (density_g_per_cm3, modulus_parallel_psi, modulus_perpendicular_psi).
Give your final answer in units of (g/cm³, psi, psi).

## 过程
**Given:** 5 kg boron fibers (unidirectional, continuous) + 8 kg aluminum matrix.

**Standard properties (Callister):**
- Boron fiber: ρ_f = 2.36 g/cm³, E_f = 400 GPa = 58.0×10⁶ psi
- Aluminum: ρ_m = 2.70 g/cm³, E_m = 69 GPa = 10.0×10⁶ psi

**Step 1 — Volume fractions** (mass/density; units cancel in the ratio):

$$V_f = \frac{m_f/\rho_f}{m_f/\rho_f + m_m/\rho_m} = \frac{5/2.36}{5/2.36 + 8/2.70} = 0.4169$$

$$V_m = 1 - V_f = 0.5831$$

**Step 2 (a) Density — rule of mixtures:**

$$\rho_c = \rho_f V_f + \rho_m V_m = 2.36(0.4169) + 2.70(0.5831) = 2.558 \approx 2.56\ \text{g/cm}^3$$

**Step 3 (b) Modulus parallel — rule of mixtures:**

$$E_{cl} = E_f V_f + E_m V_m = 58.0(0.4169) + 10.0(0.5831) = 30.02\times10^6\ \text{psi}$$

$$\Rightarrow 30{,}023{,}135\ \text{psi}$$

**Step 4 (c) Modulus perpendicular — inverse rule of mixtures:**

$$E_{ct} = \frac{E_f E_m}{E_f V_m + E_m V_f} = \frac{(58.0)(10.0)}{58.0(0.5831) + 10.0(0.4169)}\times10^6 = 15.28\times10^6\ \text{psi}$$

$$\Rightarrow 15{,}278{,}920\ \text{psi}$$

## 最终答案
(2.56, 30023135, 15278920)
