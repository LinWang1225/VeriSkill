## 题目
A thick steel sheet of area 100 in. ${ }^{2}$ is exposed to air near the ocean. After a one-year period it was found to experience a weight loss of 485 g due to corrosion. To what rate of corrosion, in both mpy and $\mathrm{mm} / \mathrm{yr}$, does this correspond? The density of steel is $7.9 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$.

Give your answer as a tuple (the rate of corrosion in mpy, the rate of corrosion in mm/yr).
Give your final answer in units of (mpy, mm/yr).

## 过程
The corrosion penetration depth equals the volume of metal lost divided by the exposed area.

1. **Volume lost** = mass/density = 485 g / 7.9 g/cm³ = 61.39 cm³.
2. **Area in cm²** = 100 in² × (2.54 cm/in)² = 645.16 cm².
3. **Penetration depth** = 61.39 / 645.16 = 0.09516 cm over 1 year.

Convert:
- **mm/yr** = 0.09516 cm × 10 = 0.9516 mm/yr.
- **mpy** = (0.09516 cm / 2.54 cm/in) / 0.001 in/mil = 37.46 mils/yr.

## 最终答案
(37.46, 0.9516)
