## 题目
Nickel containing $2 \mathrm{wt} \%$ thorium is produced in powder form, consolidated into a part, and sintered in the presence of oxygen, causing all of the thorium to produce $\mathrm{ThO}_2$ spheres 80 nm in diameter. Calculate the number of spheres per $\mathrm{cm}^3$. The density of $\mathrm{ThO}_2$ is $9.69 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Give your answer as the value of number_of_spheres_per_cm3.
Give your final answer in units of per cm³.

## 过程
Take a basis of 100 g of the Ni–Th alloy: 2 g Th and 98 g Ni.

**1. Mass of ThO₂ formed.** All Th → ThO₂:
- $M_{\text{Th}} = 232.04$, $M_{\text{ThO}_2}=232.04+2(16.00)=264.04$ g/mol
- $m_{\text{ThO}_2}=2\times\frac{264.04}{232.04}=2.276$ g

**2. Volumes.**
- $V_{\text{ThO}_2}=2.276/9.69=0.2349$ cm³
- $V_{\text{Ni}}=98/8.90=11.011$ cm³
- $V_{\text{total}}=V_{\text{Ni}}+V_{\text{ThO}_2}=11.246$ cm³

**3. Volume of one sphere.** $d=80\text{ nm}=80\times10^{-7}$ cm:
$$V_s=\frac{4}{3}\pi\left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^3=2.681\times10^{-16}\text{ cm}^3$$

**4. Number of spheres.**
$$N=\frac{V_{\text{ThO}_2}}{V_s}=\frac{0.2349}{2.681\times10^{-16}}=8.76\times10^{14}$$

**5. Number per cm³.**
$$\frac{N}{V_{\text{total}}}=\frac{8.76\times10^{14}}{11.246}=7.79\times10^{13}\text{ cm}^{-3}$$

## 最终答案
7.79e13
