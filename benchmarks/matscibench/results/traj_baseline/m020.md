## 题目
Suppose that a ductile iron (100-70-03, air-quenched) has a tensile strength of 700 MPa . What diameter impression would you expect the $3,000-\mathrm{kg}$ load to produce with the $10-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter ball? Give your answer as the value of diameter_impression.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
This is a Brinell hardness test problem. Two steps: (1) convert tensile strength → Brinell hardness number (HB) using the Callister hardness–tensile-strength correlation figure for ductile iron; (2) invert the Brinell equation to get the indentation diameter d.

**Step 1 — Tensile strength → Brinell hardness**

The grade 100-70-03 ductile iron has a nominal tensile strength of 100 ksi ≈ 689 MPa (the problem rounds this to 700 MPa). On Callister's tensile-strength-vs-Brinell-hardness figure, the ductile (nodular) iron line lies to the right of the steel line (TS = 3.45·HB): for a given tensile strength, ductile iron has a higher hardness than steel because the graphite nodules depress strength more than hardness. Reading the ductile-iron line at TS ≈ 700 MPa gives

HB ≈ 250

(For reference, the steel correlation would give HB = 700/3.45 ≈ 203; the ductile-iron line gives the larger value ≈ 250, consistent with the specified 100-70-03 grade whose typical hardness is 241–302 HB.)

**Step 2 — Brinell equation → indentation diameter**

The Brinell hardness number is

$$HB = \frac{2P}{\pi D\!\left(D-\sqrt{D^{2}-d^{2}}\right)}$$

with P = 3000 kgf, D = 10 mm. Solving for d:

$$D-\sqrt{D^{2}-d^{2}} = \frac{2P}{\pi D\,HB} \;\Rightarrow\; d = \sqrt{D^{2}-\left(D-\frac{2P}{\pi D\,HB}\right)^{2}}$$

Numerically (verified with Python):

- 2P/(π·D·HB) = 6000/(π·10·250) = 0.7639
- √(D²−d²) = 10 − 0.7639 = 9.2361
- d² = 100 − 85.305 = 14.695
- d = 3.833 mm

## 最终答案
3.83
