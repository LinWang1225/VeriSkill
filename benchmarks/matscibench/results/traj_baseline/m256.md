## 题目
Suppose we just dip a 1 -cm-diameter, 10cm -long rod of aluminum into one liter of water at $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The other end of the rod is in contact with a heat source operating at $400^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Determine the length of time required to heat the water to $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ if $75 \%$ of the heat is lost by radiation from the bar.
Give your final answer in units of min.

## 过程
This is a steady-state conduction problem with a lumped radiation-loss factor.

**1. Geometry & heat required**

Rod: diameter $d=1\,\text{cm}=0.01\,\text{m}$, length $L=0.10\,\text{m}$.
Cross-section: $A=\pi(0.005)^2=7.854\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2$.

Water: $m=1\,\text{kg}$, $c_p=4186\,\text{J/(kg·K)}$, $\Delta T_w=25-20=5\,\text{K}$.
$$Q = mc_p\Delta T_w = (1)(4186)(5)=20\,930\ \text{J}$$

**2. Conduction rate through the Al rod**

Using $k_{\text{Al}}=247\ \text{W/(m·K)}$ and $\Delta T = 400-20=380\ \text{K}$:
$$\dot Q_{\text{cond}}=\frac{kA\Delta T}{L}
=\frac{(247)(7.854\times10^{-5})(380)}{0.10}=73.7\ \text{W}$$

**3. Account for 75 % radiation loss**

Only 25 % of the conducted heat reaches the water:
$$\dot Q_{\text{water}}=0.25\times 73.7=18.4\ \text{W}$$

**4. Time to deliver $Q$**

$$t=\frac{Q}{\dot Q_{\text{water}}}=\frac{20\,930}{18.4}=1136\ \text{s}=18.9\ \text{min}$$

(The water temperature rises only 5 °C, so $\Delta T$ across the rod stays ≈375–380 K; integrating exactly gives 19.05 min — essentially the same.)

## 最终答案
19
