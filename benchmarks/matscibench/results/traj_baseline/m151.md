## 题目
(a) If a rod of brass 0.35 m (13.8 in.) long is heated from $15^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $85^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\left(60^{\circ} \mathrm{F}\right.$ to $\left.185^{\circ} \mathrm{F}\right)$ while its ends are maintained rigid, determine the magnitude of stress that develops. Assume that at $15^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ the rod is stressfree.
(b) If the rod in part (a) is cooled from $15^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $-15^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\left(60^{\circ} \mathrm{F}\right.$ to $\left.5^{\circ} \mathrm{F}\right)$, what magnitude of stress results?

Give your answer as a tuple (the magnitude of stress in (a), the magnitude of stress in (b)).

## 过程
When a rod is fully constrained (ends rigid), it cannot expand or contract freely. The thermal strain that would occur freely is $\varepsilon_{th} = \alpha_l \Delta T$. Since the ends are fixed, the total strain is zero, so a mechanical strain of equal magnitude and opposite sign is induced:

$$\sigma = E\,\varepsilon_{mech} = -E\,\alpha_l\,\Delta T$$

The magnitude is $|\sigma| = E\,\alpha_l\,|\Delta T|$.

From Table 17.1, for brass (70Cu-30Zn): $\alpha_l = 20.0\times10^{-6}\,(^\circ\mathrm{C})^{-1}$. The modulus of elasticity for brass is $E = 100\,\mathrm{GPa}=100\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

**(a)** Heating from $15^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $85^\circ\mathrm{C}$: $\Delta T = 70^\circ\mathrm{C}$ (compressive stress)
$$|\sigma_a| = (100\times10^{9})(20.0\times10^{-6})(70) = 140\times10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}=140\,\mathrm{MPa}$$

**(b)** Cooling from $15^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $-15^\circ\mathrm{C}$: $|\Delta T| = 30^\circ\mathrm{C}$ (tensile stress)
$$|\sigma_b| = (100\times10^{9})(20.0\times10^{-6})(30) = 60\times10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
(140.0, 60.0)
