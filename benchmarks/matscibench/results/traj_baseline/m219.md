## 题目
What temperature is required to obtain $0.50 \% \mathrm{C}$ at a distance of 0.5 mm beneath the surface of a $0.20 \%$ C steel in 2 h , when $1.10 \% \mathrm{C}$ is present at the surface? Assume that the iron is FCC.
Give your final answer in units of °C.

## 过程
For carburization of a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration, Fick's second law gives:

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \text{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given:** $C_s = 1.10\%$, $C_0 = 0.20\%$, $C_x = 0.50\%$, $x = 0.5\,\text{mm}=5\times10^{-4}\,\text{m}$, $t = 2\,\text{h}=7200\,\text{s}$.

For FCC (austenite) iron, carbon diffusion data: $D_0 = 2.3\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 148{,}000\,\text{J/mol}$.

**Step 1 — Find the erf argument:**
$$\frac{1.10-0.50}{1.10-0.20} = \frac{0.60}{0.90} = 0.6667 = \text{erf}(z) \;\Rightarrow\; z = \text{erf}^{-1}(0.6667) = 0.6841$$

**Step 2 — Solve for D:**
$$D = \frac{x^2}{4z^2 t} = \frac{(5\times10^{-4})^2}{4(0.6841)^2(7200)} = 1.855\times10^{-11}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Step 3 — Solve for T from $D = D_0 e^{-Q_d/RT}$:**
$$T = \frac{Q_d}{R\ln(D_0/D)} = \frac{148000}{8.314\,\ln(2.3\times10^{-5}/1.855\times10^{-11})} = 1268.8\,\text{K}$$

$$T = 1268.8 - 273.15 = 995.6\,^\circ\text{C}$$

## 最终答案
995.6
