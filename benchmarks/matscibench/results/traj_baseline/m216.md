## 题目
A 4 -cm-diameter, $0.5-\mathrm{mm}$-thick spherical container made of BCC iron holds nitrogen at $700^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The concentration at the inner surface is 0.05 atomic percent and at the outer surface is 0.002 atomic percent. Calculate the number of grams of nitrogen that are lost from the container per hour. Give your answer as the value of grams_lost_per_hour.
Give your final answer in units of g/h.

## 过程
This is steady-state diffusion of N through a spherical BCC-iron shell. For a spherical shell, the total outward mass flow rate is constant:

$$\dot m = -D\,4\pi r^{2}\frac{dC}{dr}=\text{const}$$

Integrating between inner radius $r_1$ and outer radius $r_2$:

$$\dot m = \frac{4\pi D\,(C_1-C_2)}{\dfrac{1}{r_1}-\dfrac{1}{r_2}}$$

**Geometry:** $r_1 = 2.0\,\text{cm}=0.020\,\text{m}$, thickness $=0.5\,\text{mm}$, so $r_2=0.0205\,\text{m}$.

**Diffusivity of N in α-Fe (Callister Table 5.2):** $D_0=3.0\times10^{-7}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d=76{,}150\,\text{J/mol}$.

$$D = D_0 e^{-Q_d/RT},\quad T=973\,\text{K}$$

**Concentration conversion (at% → kg/m³):** For 0.05 at% N, mass fraction $=\frac{0.05(14.007)}{0.05(14.007)+99.95(55.845)}=1.255\times10^{-4}$, so $C_1=1.255\times10^{-4}\times7870=0.987\,\text{kg/m}^3$. Likewise $C_2=0.0395\,\text{kg/m}^3$.

Plugging in (evaluated in Python):

- $D = 2.438\times10^{-11}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$
- $C_1-C_2 = 0.948\,\text{kg/m}^3$
- $\dot m = 2.381\times10^{-10}\,\text{kg/s}$
- Per hour: $\dot m\times3600\times1000 = 8.57\times10^{-4}\,\text{g/h}$

## 最终答案
8.57e-4
