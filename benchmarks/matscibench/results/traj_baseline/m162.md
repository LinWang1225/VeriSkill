## 题目
A rotating shaft in a power‐generation system has been inspected; nondestructive tests can only reveal surface cracks larger than 2 mm. The shaft is made of AISI 4340 (T = 260 °C) steel with fracture toughness K_IC = 50 MPa·m^1/2. Under service it sees an alternating stress Δσ = 200 MPa. Using a surface‐crack geometry factor β = 1.0 and Paris’ law with C = 1.0×10^–12 and n = 3, estimate the fatigue life (number of cycles) of the shaft.
Give your final answer in units of cycles.

## 过程
- $a_f = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{50}{200}\right)^2 = \frac{0.0625}{\pi} \approx 0.01989\text{ m} \approx 19.9\text{ mm}$

**Step 3 — Numerical evaluation:**

$$N_f = \frac{2}{(10^{-12})(200)^3\,\pi^{3/2}}\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{0.002}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{0.01989}}\right] \approx 6.856\times10^{5}\text{ cycles}$$

## 最终答案
6.856e5
