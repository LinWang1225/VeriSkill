## 题目
The following data are obtained for a nickel-iron alloy during the generation of a steady-state ferromagnetic hysteresis loop: \begin{tabular}{cc} \hline $\boldsymbol{H}$ (amperes/m) & $\boldsymbol{B}\left(\right.$ weber $\left./ \mathbf{m}^{\mathbf{2}}\right)$ \\ \hline 50 & 0.95 \\ 25 & 0.94 \\ 0 & 0.92 \\ -10 & 0.90 \\ -15 & 0.75 \\ -20 & -0.55 \\ -25 & -0.87 \\ -50 & -0.95 \\ \hline \end{tabular} (a) Plot the data. (b) What is the remanent induction? (c) What is the coercive field? Give your answer as a tuple: (remanent_induction, coercive_field).
Give your final answer in units of (Wb/m^2, A/m).

## 过程
**(a) Plot:** The data trace the descending branch of a steady-state hysteresis loop. $B$ stays near saturation (≈0.95) for large positive $H$, gradually decreases, then drops steeply through zero between $H=-15$ and $H=-20$, and approaches negative saturation (≈−0.95) by $H=-50$.

**(b) Remanent induction** is the induction remaining when $H=0$:
$$B_r = B(H=0) = 0.92\ \text{Wb/m}^2$$

**(c) Coercive field** is the field required to reduce $B$ to zero. From the table, $B$ changes sign between $H=-15$ ($B=0.75$) and $H=-20$ ($B=-0.55$). Linear interpolation between these two points:
$$H_c = H_1 + \frac{0 - B_1}{B_2 - B_1}(H_2 - H_1) = -15 + \frac{-0.75}{-1.30}(-5) = -17.88\ \text{A/m}$$

## 最终答案
(0.92, -17.88)
