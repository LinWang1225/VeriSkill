## 题目
Calculate the density of a honeycomb structure composed of the following elements: The two 2 -mm-thick cover sheets are produced using an epoxy matrix prepreg containing $55 \mathrm{vol} \% \mathrm{E}$-glass fibers. The aluminum honeycomb is 2 cm thick; the cells are in the shape of 0.5 cm squares and the walls of the cells are 0.1 mm thick. The density of the epoxy is $1.25 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Compare the weight of a $1 \mathrm{~m} \times 2 \mathrm{~m}$ panel of the honeycomb with a solid aluminum panel of the same dimensions. at $100 \%$ water content. Give your answer as a tuple: (density_g_per_cm3, honeycomb_weight_kg, solid_aluminum_weight_kg).
Give your final answer in units of (g/cm³, kg, kg).

## 过程
**1. Face-sheet composite density (rule of mixtures, volume basis):**
- E-glass fiber density ρ_glass = 2.54 g/cm³, epoxy ρ = 1.25 g/cm³, 55 vol% glass / 45 vol% epoxy.
- ρ_face = 0.55×2.54 + 0.45×1.25 = 1.9595 g/cm³.

**2. Honeycomb core density (square cells):**
- Cell side a = 0.5 cm, wall thickness t = 0.1 mm = 0.01 cm.
- Each square cell shares its 4 walls with neighbors, so solid volume per cell = ½·4·(a·t·h) = 2 a t h; cell volume = a²h.
- Relative density = 2t/a = 2(0.01)/0.5 = 0.04.
- ρ_core = 0.04 × 2.70 = 0.108 g/cm³.

**3. Sandwich panel (1 m × 2 m = 100 cm × 200 cm, area = 20 000 cm²):**
- Two face sheets: total thickness 2×0.2 = 0.4 cm; core thickness 2.0 cm; total = 2.4 cm.
- Mass_face = 20 000 × 0.4 × 1.9595 = 15 676 g.
- Mass_core = 20 000 × 2.0 × 0.108 = 4 320 g.
- Mass_total = 19 996 g → 19.996 kg.
- Panel volume = 20 000 × 2.4 = 48 000 cm³.
- ρ_panel = 19 996 / 48 000 = 0.4166 g/cm³.

**4. Solid aluminum panel (same 2.4 cm thickness):**
- Mass = 20 000 × 2.4 × 2.70 = 129 600 g = 129.6 kg.

## 最终答案
(0.4166, 19.996, 129.6)
