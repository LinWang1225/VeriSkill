## 题目
At what temperature will the diffusion coefficient for the diffusion of nickel in copper have a value of $4.0 \times$ $10^{-17} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$ ? Use the diffusion data in  table 6.2 .

## 过程
For the interdiffusion of Ni in Cu (FCC), Table 6.2 gives:

$$D_0 = 1.9 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}, \quad Q_d = 230{,}000\ \mathrm{J/mol}$$

The diffusion coefficient follows the Arrhenius relation

$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$$

Solving for $T$:

$$T = \frac{Q_d}{R \ln(D_0/D)}$$

Substituting $D = 4.0 \times 10^{-17}\ \mathrm{m^2/s}$ and $R = 8.314\ \mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$:

$$T = \frac{230{,}000}{8.314\ \ln\!\left(\dfrac{1.9\times10^{-4}}{4.0\times10^{-17}}\right)}$$

Numerical evaluation gives $T \approx 947.76\ \mathrm{K}$.

## 最终答案
948
