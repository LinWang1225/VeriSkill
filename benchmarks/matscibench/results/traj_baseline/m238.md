## 题目
Describe the complete heat treatment required to produce a quenched and tempered eutectoid steel having an HRC hardness of less than 50. Include appropriate temperatures. Give your answer as a tuple: (austenitizing_temperature_C, tempering_temperature_C).
Give your final answer in units of (°C, °C).

## 过程
Eutectoid steel (0.76 wt% C) has the eutectoid (A₁) temperature at **727 °C**.

**Step 1 — Austenitize:** Heat into the single-phase austenite (γ) region, i.e. just above 727 °C. The standard austenitizing temperature is A₁ + ~25 °C ≈ **750 °C**. Hold to fully transform to austenite.

**Step 2 — Quench:** Rapidly quench to room temperature to form (nearly fully) martensite. As-quenched eutectoid martensite is very hard (~65 HRC).

**Step 3 — Temper:** Reheat below the eutectoid (T < 727 °C) to soften the martensite. Using the tempered-martensite hardness curve for eutectoid steel, HRC = 50 is reached at about **383 °C**. To obtain **HRC < 50**, temper above this temperature. Choosing **550 °C** gives ≈ 38 HRC, safely below 50.

## 最终答案
(750, 550)
