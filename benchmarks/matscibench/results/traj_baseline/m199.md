## 题目
Assuming that all of the valence electrons contribute to current flow, (a) calculate the mobility of an electron in copper and (b) calculate the average drift velocity for electrons in a 100 cm copper wire when 10 V are applied. The lattice parameter of copper is $3.6151 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~cm}$ and, since copper is FCC, there are four atoms/unit cell. The conductivity $\sigma=5.98 \times 10^{5} \Omega^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{~cm}^{-1}$. Give your answer as a tuple (mobility of an electron in copper, average drift velocity for electrons in a 100 cm copper wire when 10 V are applied).
Give your final answer in units of (cm^2/(V·s), cm/s).

## 过程
**(a) Electron mobility**

Assuming every valence electron contributes to conduction, copper (FCC, 1 valence e⁻/atom) has free-electron concentration

$$n = \frac{4\ \text{atoms/cell}}{a^{3}} = \frac{4}{(3.6151\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm})^{3}} = 8.466\times10^{22}\ \text{cm}^{-3}$$

Conductivity relates to mobility by $\sigma = ne\mu$, so

$$\mu = \frac{\sigma}{ne} = \frac{5.98\times10^{5}}{(8.466\times10^{22})(1.602\times10^{-19})} \approx 44.09\ \text{cm}^{2}/(\text{V·s})$$

**(b) Average drift velocity**

The electric field in a 100 cm wire with 10 V applied is

$$E = \frac{V}{L} = \frac{10}{100} = 0.1\ \text{V/cm}$$

The drift velocity is

$$v_d = \mu E = (44.09)(0.1) \approx 4.409\ \text{cm/s}$$

## 最终答案
(44.09, 4.409)
