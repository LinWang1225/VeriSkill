## 题目
(a) For the solidification of nickel, calculate the critical radius $r$ and the activation free energy $\Delta G$ if nucleation is homogeneous. Values for the latent heat of fusion and surface free energy are $-2.53 \times 10^{9} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ and $0.255 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{2}$, respectively. Use the supercooling value found in  table 11.1 . The melting temperature for nickel is $1455^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$.
(b) Now calculate the number of atoms found in a nucleus of critical size. Assume a lattice parameter of 0.360 nm for solid nickel at its melting temperature.

 table 11.1 
Degree of Supercooling $\Delta T$ (Homogeneous Nucleation) for Several Metals
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\hline Metal & $\Delta \boldsymbol{T}\left({ }^{\circ} \boldsymbol{C}\right)$ \\
\hline Antimony & 135 \\
\hline Germanium & 227 \\
\hline Silver & 227 \\
\hline Gold & 230 \\
\hline Copper & 236 \\
\hline Iron & 295 \\
\hline Nickel & 319 \\
\hline Cobalt & 330 \\
\hline Palladium & 332 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}


Give your answer as a tuple (the critical radius $r$, the activation free energy $\Delta G$, the number of atoms).
Give your final answer in units of (nm, J, ).

## 过程
For homogeneous nucleation, the critical radius and activation free energy are

$$r^*=\frac{2\gamma T_m}{|\Delta H_f|\Delta T}, \qquad \Delta G^*=\frac{16\pi\gamma^3 T_m^2}{3\Delta H_f^2\Delta T^2}$$

**Given values:**
- $|\Delta H_f|=2.53\times10^{9}\ \mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $\gamma=0.255\ \mathrm{J/m^2}$
- $\Delta T=319\ \mathrm{K}$ (Table 11.1, nickel)
- $T_m=1455+273=1728\ \mathrm{K}$

**(a) Critical radius:**
$$r^*=\frac{2(0.255)(1728)}{(2.53\times10^{9})(319)}=1.09\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{m}=1.09\ \mathrm{nm}$$

**Activation free energy:**
$$\Delta G^*=\frac{16\pi(0.255)^3(1728)^2}{3(2.53\times10^{9})^2(319)^2}=1.27\times10^{-18}\ \mathrm{J}$$

**(b) Number of atoms in a nucleus of critical size:**

Nickel is FCC (4 atoms/unit cell), lattice parameter $a=0.360\ \mathrm{nm}$.

$$V_{\text{nucleus}}=\frac{4}{3}\pi(r^*)^3,\quad V_{\text{cell}}=a^3$$
$$N=\frac{V_{\text{nucleus}}}{V_{\text{cell}}}\times 4=\frac{\frac{4}{3}\pi(1.092\times10^{-9})^3}{(0.360\times10^{-9})^3}\times 4\approx 468$$

## 最终答案
(1.09, 1.27e-18, 468)
