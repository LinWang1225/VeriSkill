## 题目
Gold (Au) forms a substitutional solid solution with silver (Ag). Compute the weight percent of gold that must be added to silver to yield an alloy that contains $5.5 \times 10^{21} \mathrm{Au}$ atoms per cubic centimeter. The densities of pure Au and Ag are 19.32 and $10.49 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, respectively.
Give your final answer in units of wt%.

## 过程
For a substitutional solid solution, assume volume additivity (Callister inverse rule of mixtures for alloy density). Let $C'_{\mathrm{Au}}$ be the wt% of Au.

The alloy density is

$$\rho_{\text{avg}}=\frac{100}{\dfrac{C'_{\mathrm{Au}}}{\rho_{\mathrm{Au}}}+\dfrac{100-C'_{\mathrm{Au}}}{\rho_{\mathrm{Ag}}}}.$$

The number of Au atoms per cm³ is

$$N_{\mathrm{Au}}=\frac{C'_{\mathrm{Au}}\,\rho_{\text{avg}}\,N_A}{100\,M_{\mathrm{Au}}}=5.5\times10^{21}.$$

Substituting $\rho_{\mathrm{Au}}=19.32$, $\rho_{\mathrm{Ag}}=10.49$, $M_{\mathrm{Au}}=196.97$, $N_A=6.023\times10^{23}$ and solving numerically for $C'_{\mathrm{Au}}$:

- $\rho_{\text{avg}}\approx 11.31\ \text{g/cm}^3$
- $C'_{\mathrm{Au}}\approx 15.90$

Verification: $N_{\mathrm{Au}}=\dfrac{15.90\times 11.31\times 6.023\times10^{23}}{100\times196.97}\approx 5.50\times10^{21}$ ✓

## 最终答案
15.90
