## 题目
You are asked to measure nondestructively the yield strength and tensile strength of an annealed 65-45-12 cast iron structural member. Fortunately, a small hardness indentation in this structural design will not impair its future usefulness, which is a working definition of nondestructive. A $10-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter tungsten carbide sphere creates a $4.26-\mathrm{mm}$-diameter impression under a $3,000-\mathrm{kg}$ load. What are the yield and tensile strengths? Give your answer as a tuple: (yield_strength, tensile_strength).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa).

## 过程
**1. Brinell hardness number (BHN):**

$$HB = \frac{P}{\frac{\pi}{2} D\left(D - \sqrt{D^2 - d^2}\right)}$$

With $P = 3000$ kgf, $D = 10$ mm, $d = 4.26$ mm:

$$HB = \frac{3000}{\frac{\pi}{2}(10)\left(10 - \sqrt{100 - 18.148}\right)} = \frac{3000}{\frac{\pi}{2}(10)(0.9528)} \approx 200.45$$

**2. Hardness → strength correlations for ductile cast iron:**

For ductile (nodular) cast iron, the standard empirical correlations (ASM Handbook / ASTM A536) are:

$$\sigma_{\text{tensile}}\;(\text{ksi}) = 0.325 \times HB$$
$$\sigma_{\text{yield}}\;(\text{ksi}) = 0.225 \times HB$$

**3. Compute strengths:**

- Tensile: $0.325 \times 200.45 = 65.15$ ksi $= 65.15 \times 6.895 = 449.2$ MPa
- Yield: $0.225 \times 200.45 = 45.10$ ksi $= 45.10 \times 6.895 = 311.0$ MPa

These are consistent with the 65-45-12 grade spec (65 ksi UTS, 45 ksi YS), confirming the measurement.

## 最终答案
(311.0, 449.2)
