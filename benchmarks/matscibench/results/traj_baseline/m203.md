## 题目
Suppose that in a corrosion cell composed of copper and zinc, the current density at the copper cathode is $0.05 \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{cm}^{2}$. The area of both the copper and zinc electrodes is $100 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$. Calculate (a) the corrosion current, (b) the current density at the zinc anode, and (c) the zinc loss per hour. The atomic mass of zinc is $65.38 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$. Give your answer as a tuple (corrosion current, current density at zinc anode, zinc loss per hour).
Give your final answer in units of (A, A/cm^2, g/h).

## 过程
In a Cu–Zn corrosion cell the same current flows through both electrodes (series circuit).

**(a) Corrosion current**

$$I = i_{\text{Cu}} \cdot A = 0.05\;\text{A/cm}^2 \times 100\;\text{cm}^2 = 5\;\text{A}$$

**(b) Current density at the zinc anode**

The total current is the same at the anode, and the areas are equal:

$$i_{\text{Zn}} = \frac{I}{A} = \frac{5}{100} = 0.05\;\text{A/cm}^2$$

**(c) Zinc loss per hour**

Zinc dissolves as $\text{Zn} \to \text{Zn}^{2+} + 2e^-$, so $n=2$. Using Faraday's law with $t = 3600$ s:

$$m = \frac{I \cdot M_{\text{Zn}} \cdot t}{nF} = \frac{5 \times 65.38 \times 3600}{2 \times 96500} \approx 6.098\;\text{g/h}$$

## 最终答案
(5.0, 0.05, 6.10)
