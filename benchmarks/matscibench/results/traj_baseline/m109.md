## 题目
The following true stresses produce the corresponding true plastic strains for a brass alloy:

| True Stress (psi) | True Strain |
| :--: | :--: |
| 60,000 | 0.15 |
| 70,000 | 0.25 |

What true stress is necessary to produce a true plastic strain of 0.21 ?
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
In the plastic region, true stress and true plastic strain follow the Hollomon (power-law) relation:

$$\sigma_T = K\,\varepsilon_T^{\,n}$$

Using the two data points to eliminate $K$:

$$\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_2}=\left(\frac{\varepsilon_1}{\varepsilon_2}\right)^n
\;\Rightarrow\;
\frac{60000}{70000}=\left(\frac{0.15}{0.25}\right)^n$$

$$n=\frac{\ln(6/7)}{\ln(0.6)}\approx 0.3018$$

Then $K=\sigma_1/\varepsilon_1^n \approx 106360\ \text{psi}$.

For $\varepsilon_T=0.21$:

$$\sigma_T = K(0.21)^n \approx 66412\ \text{psi}$$

Converting to MPa ($1\ \text{psi}=0.00689476\ \text{MPa}$):

$$\sigma_T \approx 66412\times 0.00689476 \approx 457.9\ \text{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
457.9
