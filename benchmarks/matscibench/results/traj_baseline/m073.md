## 题目
Using the ionic radii in  table 3.4 , compute the theoretical density of CsCl. (Hint: Use a modification of $$
a=\frac{4 R}{\sqrt{3}}
$$.)

## 过程
CsCl has the cesium chloride structure: Cl⁻ ions at the cube corners and Cs⁺ at the body center. The ions touch along the body diagonal, so

$$a\sqrt{3}=2(R_{\mathrm{Cs}}+R_{\mathrm{Cl}})\;\Rightarrow\; a=\frac{2(R_{\mathrm{Cs}}+R_{\mathrm{Cl}})}{\sqrt{3}}$$

This is the hinted modification of $a=4R/\sqrt{3}$ (replace $2R$ with $R_{\mathrm{Cs}}+R_{\mathrm{Cl}}$).

With $R_{\mathrm{Cs}}=0.170$ nm and $R_{\mathrm{Cl}}=0.181$ nm:

$$a=\frac{2(0.170+0.181)}{\sqrt{3}}\text{ nm}=0.4053\text{ nm}=4.053\times10^{-8}\text{ cm}$$

The unit cell contains $n=1$ CsCl formula unit. With $A=132.91+35.45=168.36$ g/mol:

$$\rho=\frac{nA}{N_A a^3}=\frac{1(168.36)}{(6.022\times10^{23})(4.053\times10^{-8})^3}=4.20\text{ g/cm}^3$$

## 最终答案
4.20
