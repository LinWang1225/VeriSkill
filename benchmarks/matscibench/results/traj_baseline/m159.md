## 题目
Zinc selenide has a band gap of 2.58 eV . Over what range of wavelengths of visible light is it transparent?

Give your answer as a tuple (the lower bound, the upper bound).
Give your final answer in units of (\mu m, \mu m).

## 过程
A semiconductor is transparent to photons with energy below the band gap (no electron–hole pair can be created), i.e. for wavelengths longer than the cutoff

$$\lambda_c = \frac{hc}{E_g}.$$

With $hc = 1240\ \text{eV·nm}$ and $E_g = 2.58\ \text{eV}$:

$$\lambda_c = \frac{1240}{2.58} \approx 480.6\ \text{nm} = 0.481\ \mu\text{m}.$$

Visible light spans roughly $0.40$–$0.70\ \mu\text{m}$. Since ZnSe only absorbs photons with $\lambda < 0.481\ \mu\text{m}$, it is transparent to the visible portion with $\lambda > 0.481\ \mu\text{m}$, up to the red limit of the visible spectrum at $0.70\ \mu\text{m}$.

## 最终答案
(0.481, 0.70)
