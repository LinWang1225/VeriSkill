## 题目
We wish to produce a rod composed of a single crystal of pure aluminum, which has a critical resolved shear stress of 148 psi. We would like to orient the rod in such a manner that, when an axial stress of 500 psi is applied, the rod deforms by slip in a $45^{\circ}$ direction to the axis of the rod and actuates a sensor that detects the overload. Design the rod and a method by which it might be produced. Give your answer as a tuple: (λ_deg, φ_deg).
Give your final answer in units of (\circ, \circ).

## 过程
Schmid's law relates the resolved shear stress to the axial stress:

$$\tau_R = \sigma \cos\phi \cos\lambda$$

where $\lambda$ is the angle between the rod (tensile) axis and the **slip direction**, and $\phi$ is the angle between the rod axis and the **slip-plane normal**.

Slip is initiated when $\tau_R$ reaches the critical resolved shear stress $\tau_c = 148\ \text{psi}$.

**Step 1 — Determine $\lambda$.**  
The problem specifies that the rod deforms by slip in a $45^\circ$ direction to the rod axis. Since $\lambda$ is precisely the angle between the tensile axis and the slip direction,

$$\lambda = 45^\circ$$

**Step 2 — Apply Schmid's law to find $\phi$.**  
Setting $\tau_R = \tau_c$ with $\sigma = 500\ \text{psi}$:

$$500 \cos\phi \cos 45^\circ = 148$$

$$\cos\phi = \frac{148}{500\cos 45^\circ} = \frac{0.296}{0.7071} = 0.4186$$

$$\phi = \arccos(0.4186) \approx 65.25^\circ$$

**Step 3 — Design/production method.**  
Grow a single crystal of pure Al (e.g., by Bridgman/Czochralski directional solidification from a seeded melt) and orient the rod axis so it makes $\lambda = 45^\circ$ with the active $\langle 110\rangle$ slip direction and $\phi \approx 65.25^\circ$ with the $\{111\}$ slip-plane normal, then cut/fixture the rod at that orientation. Under a 500 psi axial load the resolved shear stress on that system equals 148 psi, triggering slip at $45^\circ$ to the axis and actuating the overload sensor.

## 最终答案
(45.0, 65.25)
