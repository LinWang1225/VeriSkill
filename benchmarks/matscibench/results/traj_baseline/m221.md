## 题目
A $0.80 \% \mathrm{C}$ steel must operate at $950^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in an oxidizing environment for which the carbon content at the steel surface is zero. Only the outermost 0.02 cm of the steel part can fall below $0.75 \% \mathrm{C}$. What is the maximum time that the steel part can operate?
Give your final answer in units of s.

## 过程
This is a decarburization problem modeled by Fick's second law for a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration:

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given:** $C_0 = 0.80\%$, $C_s = 0\%$, $C_x = 0.75\%$ (limit at $x = 0.02\text{ cm} = 2.0\times10^{-4}\text{ m}$), $T = 950^\circ\text{C} = 1223\text{ K}$.

**Diffusivity.** At 950 °C the steel is austenite (γ-Fe), so use carbon-in-austenite data (Callister Table 5.2): $D_0 = 1.28\times10^{-5}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 148\text{ kJ/mol}$.

$$D = D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right) = 1.28\times10^{-5}\exp\!\left(-\frac{148000}{8.31\cdot1223}\right) \approx 6.06\times10^{-12}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Error-function argument.**

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \frac{0 - 0.75}{0 - 0.80} = 0.9375$$

$$z = \operatorname{erf}^{-1}(0.9375) \approx 1.3172$$

**Solve for time** with $x/(2\sqrt{Dt}) = z$:

$$t = \frac{x^2}{4Dz^2} = \frac{(2.0\times10^{-4})^2}{4(6.06\times10^{-12})(1.3172)^2} \approx 950\text{ s}$$

## 最终答案
950.4
