## 题目
A niobium alloy is produced by introducing tungsten substitutional atoms into the BCC structure; eventually an alloy is produced that has a lattice parameter of 0.32554 nm and a density of $11.95 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Calculate the fraction of the atoms in the alloy that are tungsten. Give your answer as as the value of number_of tungsten_fraction.

## 过程
For a BCC structure there are $n=2$ atoms per unit cell. Let $x$ be the fraction of atoms that are tungsten; the average atomic mass per atom is

$$\bar M = x M_W + (1-x) M_{Nb}.$$

The density relates to the unit-cell volume $V=a^3$ by

$$\rho = \frac{n\bar M}{N_A a^3}\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \bar M = \frac{\rho N_A a^3}{n}.$$

With $a=0.32554\,\text{nm}=3.2554\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$, $\rho=11.95\,\text{g/cm}^3$, $N_A=6.022\times10^{23}$, $M_{Nb}=92.906$, $M_W=183.84\,\text{g/mol}$:

$$\bar M = \frac{11.95\cdot 6.022\times10^{23}\cdot(3.2554\times10^{-8})^3}{2}=124.13\,\text{g/mol}.$$

Then

$$x=\frac{\bar M-M_{Nb}}{M_W-M_{Nb}}=\frac{124.13-92.906}{183.84-92.906}=0.3434.$$

## 最终答案
0.3434
