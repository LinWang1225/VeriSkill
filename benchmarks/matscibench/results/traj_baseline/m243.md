## 题目
Compare the percent increase in the yield strength of commercially pure annealed aluminum, magnesium, and copper by strain hardening. Explain the differences observed. Give your answer as a tuple: (percent_increase_Al, percent_increase_Mg, percent_increase_Cu).
Give your final answer in units of (%, %, %).

## 过程
This problem uses Callister's Table 7.2, which tabulates yield strength (YS) for several metals in the **annealed** condition and after **75% cold work** (a standard strain-hardening reference state).

**Reference data (Callister Table 7.2, yield strength in MPa):**

| Metal | Annealed YS | 75% CW YS |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| 1100 Aluminum (commercially pure) | 34 | 159 |
| Magnesium | 100 | 170 |
| Copper (commercially pure, C11000) | 33 | 280 |

**Percent increase formula:**

$$\%\,\text{increase} = \frac{\sigma_{y,\,\text{CW}} - \sigma_{y,\,\text{ann}}}{\sigma_{y,\,\text{ann}}}\times 100$$

**Evaluation (Python):**

- **Al:** (159 − 34)/34 × 100 = 125/34 × 100 = **367.65%**
- **Mg:** (170 − 100)/100 × 100 = 70/100 × 100 = **70.00%**
- **Cu:** (280 − 33)/33 × 100 = 247/33 × 100 = **748.48%**

**Explanation of differences:**

The percent increase tracks the crystal structure and number of available slip systems. **Al and Cu are FCC**, which possess 12 slip systems that remain active at room temperature, allowing extensive dislocation generation and pile-up → large strain hardening (Cu hardens most because its higher strain-hardening exponent, n ≈ 0.30 vs. ≈ 0.20 for Al, gives steeper σ–ε rise). **Mg is HCP**, with only 3 independent basal slip systems active at room temperature; the limited slip systems restrict dislocation motion and pile-up, so it strain hardens far less (and tends to twin/fracture rather than cold work heavily). Hence: Cu (748%) > Al (368%) ≫ Mg (70%).

## 最终答案
(367.65, 70.00, 748.48)
