## 题目
Beryllium (Be) has an HCP unit cell for which the ratio of the lattice parameters c/a is 1.568. If the radius of the Be atom is 0.1143 nm , (a) determine the unit cell volume, and (b) calculate the theoretical density of Be and compare it with the literature value.

Give your answer as a tuple (the unit cell volume,  the theoretical density)
Give your final answer in units of (cm^3/unit cell, g/cm^3).

## 过程
For HCP, atoms touch along the basal edge, so $a = 2r = 2(0.1143) = 0.2286$ nm. Given $c/a = 1.568$, $c = 1.568a = 0.3584$ nm.

The HCP unit-cell volume is
$$V = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^2 c$$
Evaluating: $V = 0.04867$ nm³ $= 4.867\times10^{-23}$ cm³/unit cell.

For density, HCP has $n=6$ atoms/cell, atomic weight of Be $A=9.0122$ g/mol, $N_A=6.022\times10^{23}$:
$$\rho = \frac{nA}{V N_A} = \frac{6(9.0122)}{(4.867\times10^{-23})(6.022\times10^{23})} = 1.845\ \text{g/cm}^3$$

This agrees closely with the literature value of ≈1.85 g/cm³ for Be.

## 最终答案
(4.87e-23, 1.85)
