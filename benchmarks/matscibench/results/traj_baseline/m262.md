## 题目
A tensile stress is to be applied along the long axis of a cylindrical brass rod that has a diameter of 10 mm. Brass has Young’s modulus E = 97 GPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34. Determine the magnitude of the load required to produce a 2.5×10⁻³ mm decrease in diameter if the deformation is entirely elastic.
Give your final answer in units of N.

## 过程
Poisson's ratio relates lateral and axial strain: ν = −ε_lateral / ε_axial.

Lateral (transverse) strain from the diameter decrease:
ε_lat = Δd / d₀ = (−2.5×10⁻³ mm) / (10 mm) = −2.5×10⁻⁴

Axial strain:
ε_ax = −ε_lat / ν = (2.5×10⁻⁴) / 0.34 = 7.3529×10⁻⁴

Hooke's law gives the axial stress:
σ = E·ε_ax = (97×10⁹ Pa)(7.3529×10⁻⁴) = 7.1324×10⁷ Pa

Cross-sectional area:
A = π(d/2)² = π(5×10⁻³ m)² = 7.854×10⁻⁵ m²

Load:
F = σ·A = (7.1324×10⁷)(7.854×10⁻⁵) ≈ 5601.7 N

## 最终答案
5601.74
