## 题目
In a plot of the logarithm (to the base 10) of the diffusion coefficient versus reciprocal of absolute temperature for the diffusion of copper in gold, two points are  $1 / T_{1}=0.8 \times$ $10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $1 / T_{2}=1.1 \times 10^{-3}(\mathrm{~K})^{-1}$ and $ \log D_{1}=-12.40$ and $ \log D_{2}=-15.45$. Determine values for the activation energy and the preexponential. Give your answer as a tuple (activation energy, preexponential).
Give your final answer in units of (kJ/mol, m^2/s).

## 过程
The Arrhenius relation is $D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q}{RT}\right)$. Taking $\log_{10}$:

$$\log D = \log D_0 - \dfrac{Q}{2.303\,R}\cdot\dfrac{1}{T}$$

So a plot of $\log D$ vs $1/T$ is linear with slope $=-Q/(2.303R)$ and intercept $=\log D_0$.

Using the two points $(1/T_1,\log D_1)=(0.8\times10^{-3},-12.40)$ and $(1/T_2,\log D_2)=(1.1\times10^{-3},-15.45)$:

$$\text{slope}=\frac{-15.45-(-12.40)}{1.1\times10^{-3}-0.8\times10^{-3}}=\frac{-3.05}{0.3\times10^{-3}}=-10166.7\ \text{K}$$

$$Q = -\text{slope}\cdot 2.303\cdot R = 10166.7\times 2.303\times 8.314 = 1.9466\times10^{5}\ \text{J/mol}\approx 194.66\ \text{kJ/mol}$$

Intercept:

$$\log D_0 = \log D_1 - \text{slope}\cdot(1/T_1) = -12.40 - (-10166.7)(0.8\times10^{-3}) = -4.2667$$

$$D_0 = 10^{-4.2667}\approx 5.41\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

## 最终答案
(194.66, 5.41e-05)
