## 题目
C₀ (eutectoid composition) ≃ 0.77 wt % C  
Cα (ferrite solubility) ≃ 0.02 wt % C  
Cθ (cementite Fe₃C) ≃ 6.67 wt % C  

Calculate the relative widths of the ferrite and carbide platelets using the lever law. Give the value of thickness ratio.

## 过程
For a eutectoid steel (pearlite) the lever rule gives the weight fractions of the two phases at C₀ = 0.77 wt% C:

- Ferrite (α): Wα = (Cθ − C₀)/(Cθ − Cα) = (6.67 − 0.77)/(6.67 − 0.02) = 5.90/6.65
- Cementite (θ): Wθ = (C₀ − Cα)/(Cθ − Cα) = (0.77 − 0.02)/(6.67 − 0.02) = 0.75/6.65

Since the α and θ lamellae share the same interfacial area, their thicknesses scale with their phase fractions. The ferrite-to-carbide thickness ratio is therefore

tα/tθ = Wα/Wθ = (Cθ − C₀)/(C₀ − Cα) = 5.90/0.75 = 7.8667

So the ferrite platelet is about 7.87 times thicker than the cementite platelet (≈ 7.9 : 1).

## 最终答案
7.87
