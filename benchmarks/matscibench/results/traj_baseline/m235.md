## 题目
A eutectoid steel has been heated to $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 1 h , quenched to $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and held for 750 s , and finally quenched to room temperature. What is the hardness?
Give your final answer in units of HRC.

## 过程
**Step 1 — Austenitization:** The steel is heated to 800°C (above the eutectoid temperature A₁ = 727°C) for 1 h, producing fully homogeneous austenite (γ).

**Step 2 — Identify the transformation regime at 350°C:**  
For eutectoid steel (0.76 wt% C), the martensite start temperature is:
$$M_s \approx 539 - 423(0.76) \approx 218°\text{C}$$

Since 350°C lies **above** M_s (~218°C) and **below** the nose of the TTT diagram (~550°C), the isothermal transformation at 350°C falls in the **bainite** region. Specifically, 350°C is in the **lower bainite** range.

**Step 3 — Check completeness of transformation (TTT diagram):**  
On the isothermal transformation diagram for eutectoid steel, at 350°C:
- Transformation **starts** at ~10¹·⁵ ≈ 30 s
- Transformation **finishes** at ~10²·⁷ ≈ 500 s

The hold time is 750 s, and log₁₀(750) ≈ 2.88, which is **past the finish curve**. Therefore, the austenite → bainite transformation is **100% complete**.

**Step 4 — Final quench to room temperature:** Since the austenite has already fully transformed to bainite at 350°C, the final quench produces no further transformation. The microstructure is **100% lower bainite** formed at 350°C.

**Step 5 — Hardness:** Reading from the hardness-versus-isothermal-transformation-temperature curve for eutectoid steel (Callister, Figure 10.24), lower bainite formed at 350°C has a hardness of approximately **40 HRC**.

## 最终答案
40
