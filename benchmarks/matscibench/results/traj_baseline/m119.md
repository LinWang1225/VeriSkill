## 题目
The following yield strength, grain diameter, and heat treatment time (for grain growth) data were gathered for an iron specimen that was heat treated at $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Using these data, compute the yield strength of a specimen that was heated at $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 3 h . Assume a value of 2 for $n$, the grain diameter exponent.

| Grain diameter $(\mathbf{m m})$ | Yield Strength $(\mathbf{M P a})$ | Heat Treating Time $(\boldsymbol{h})$ |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 0.028 | 300 | 10 |
| 0.010 | 385 | 1 |
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
**1. Hall-Petch relation** $\sigma_y = \sigma_0 + k_y/\sqrt{d}$

Using the two data points:
- $300 = \sigma_0 + k_y/\sqrt{0.028}$
- $385 = \sigma_0 + k_y/\sqrt{0.010}$

Subtracting: $85 = k_y(1/\sqrt{0.010}-1/\sqrt{0.028}) = k_y(10-5.976)$, giving $k_y = 21.124$ MPa·mm$^{1/2}$ and $\sigma_0 = 173.76$ MPa.

**2. Grain-growth law** $d^n - d_0^n = Kt$ with $n=2$:
- $0.028^2 - d_0^2 = 10K$
- $0.010^2 - d_0^2 = K$

Solving: $d_0^2 = 2.4\times10^{-5}$ mm$^2$, $K = 7.6\times10^{-5}$ mm$^2$/h.

**3. Diameter at $t=3$ h:**
$d^2 = d_0^2 + 3K = 2.4\times10^{-5} + 2.28\times10^{-4} = 2.52\times10^{-4}$
$d = 0.015875$ mm.

**4. Yield strength:**
$\sigma_y = 173.76 + 21.124/\sqrt{0.015875} = 173.76 + 167.66 = 341.42$ MPa.

## 最终答案
341.4
