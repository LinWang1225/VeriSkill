## 题目
Unusual combinations of properties can be obtained by producing a steel with a microstructure containing $50 \%$ ferrite and $50 \%$ martensite. The martensite provides strength, and the ferrite provides ductility and toughness. Design a heat treatment to produce a dual phase steel in which the composition of the martensite is $0.60 \% \mathrm{C}$.

**Give your answer as a tuple: (steel_carbon_content, heat_treatment_temperature).**
Give your final answer in units of (%, °C).

## 过程
**Strategy: intercritical annealing.** Heat the steel into the α + γ two-phase region (between A₁ and A₃), hold to equilibrate, then quench. The austenite present at temperature transforms to martensite; the ferrite remains ferrite. We need the austenite to contain 0.60 wt% C (martensite inherits its parent austenite's carbon), and the austenite fraction to be 0.50.

**Step 1 — Find the intercritical temperature.** The austenite composition in the α + γ field is set by the A₃ line (the γ/(α+γ) boundary). Using the standard Fe–C A₃ relation

$$T_{A_3} = 910 - 203\sqrt{C_\gamma}$$

with $C_\gamma = 0.60$:

$$T = 910 - 203\sqrt{0.60} = 910 - 157.2 \approx 752.8^\circ\text{C}$$

So hold at **≈ 750 °C**.

**Step 2 — Ferrite composition at this T.** The α/(α+γ) solubility line runs from (0 %C, 912 °C) to (0.022 %C, 727 °C):

$$C_\alpha = 0.022\frac{912 - T}{912 - 727} = 0.022\frac{159.2}{185} \approx 0.019\%\text{C}$$

**Step 3 — Lever rule for 50 % austenite.** With $f_\gamma = 0.50$:

$$0.50 = \frac{C_0 - C_\alpha}{C_\gamma - C_\alpha}
\;\Rightarrow\; C_0 = C_\alpha + 0.50(C_\gamma - C_\alpha) = \tfrac{1}{2}(C_\gamma + C_\alpha)$$

$$C_0 = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.60 + 0.019) \approx 0.31\%\text{C}$$

Since $C_\alpha \ll C_\gamma$, this is essentially $C_0 \approx 0.30\%\text{C}$.

**Heat treatment:** Austenitize a ~0.30 %C steel fully, cool to ≈ 750 °C (intercritical hold) to establish 50 % ferrite + 50 % austenite(0.60 %C), then quench to convert the austenite to martensite.

## 最终答案
(0.30, 750)
