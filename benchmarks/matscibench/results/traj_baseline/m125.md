## 题目
The following creep data were taken on an aluminum alloy at $480^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\left(900^{\circ} \mathrm{F}\right)$ and a constant stress of 2.75 MPa (400 psi). Determine the steady-state or minimum creep rate. Note: The initial and instantaneous strain is not included.

| Time $(\mathbf{m i n})$ | Strain | Time $(\mathbf{m i n})$ | Strain |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 0 | 0.00 | 18 | 0.82 |
| 2 | 0.22 | 20 | 0.88 |
| 4 | 0.34 | 22 | 0.95 |
| 6 | 0.41 | 24 | 1.03 |
| 8 | 0.48 | 26 | 1.12 |
| 10 | 0.55 | 28 | 1.22 |
| 12 | 0.62 | 30 | 1.36 |
| 14 | 0.68 | 32 | 1.53 |
| 16 | 0.75 | 34 | 1.77 |
Give your final answer in units of min^-1.

## 过程
The creep curve has three stages: primary (decreasing slope), secondary/steady-state (approximately linear, minimum slope), and tertiary (increasing slope). The steady-state creep rate is the slope of the linear secondary region.

First, compute the slope between each pair of consecutive data points to locate the secondary stage:

| Segment (min) | Slope (min⁻¹) | Segment (min) | Slope (min⁻¹) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | 0.110 | 18–20 | 0.030 |
| 2–4 | 0.060 | 20–22 | 0.035 |
| 4–6 | 0.035 | 22–24 | 0.040 |
| 6–8 | 0.035 | 24–26 | 0.045 |
| 8–10 | 0.035 | 26–28 | 0.050 |
| 10–12 | 0.035 | 28–30 | 0.070 |
| 12–14 | 0.030 | 30–32 | 0.085 |
| 14–16 | 0.035 | 32–34 | 0.120 |
| 16–18 | 0.035 | | |

The slope falls during primary creep (0–4 min), is approximately constant and minimal during secondary creep (≈4–22 min, slope ≈ 0.035), then rises during tertiary creep (22–34 min). The minimum creep rate is the slope of this linear secondary region.

A least-squares linear fit over the steady-state window t = 4–22 min gives slope ≈ 0.0338 min⁻¹; over t = 4–24 min, slope ≈ 0.0340 min⁻¹. Using endpoint pairs in the secondary region confirms this: (0.95 − 0.34)/(22 − 4) = 0.61/18 = 0.0339 min⁻¹.

Thus the minimum (steady-state) creep rate ≈ 3.4 × 10⁻² min⁻¹.

## 最终答案
0.034
