## 题目
Compute the rate of some reaction that obeys Avrami kinetics, assuming that the constants $n$ and $k$ have values of 2.0 and $5 \times 10-4$, respectively, for time expressed in seconds.
Give your final answer in units of $s^{-1}$.

## 过程
The Avrami equation is $y = 1 - \exp(-k t^n)$, where $y$ is the fraction transformed. The reaction rate is

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = n k t^{n-1} \exp(-k t^n).$$

With $n = 2.0$ and $k = 5\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}^{-2}$, the rate is time-dependent, so it is evaluated at $t = 100\ \text{s}$ (the value for which $k t^n = 5\times10^{-4}\cdot 100^2 = 5$ exactly):

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = (2)(5\times10^{-4})(100)^{1}\exp(-5) = 0.1\,e^{-5}.$$

Numerically, $0.1\,e^{-5} = 6.7379\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}^{-1}$.

## 最终答案
6.74e-4
