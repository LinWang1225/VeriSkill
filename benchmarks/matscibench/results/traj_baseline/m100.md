## 过程
For carburization of a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration, Fick's second law gives:

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Step 1 — Evaluate the erf argument.**

$$\frac{C_s - C_x}{C_s - C_0} = \frac{1.10 - 0.30}{1.10 - 0.10} = \frac{0.80}{1.00} = 0.80$$

So $\operatorname{erf}(z) = 0.80$. Interpolating Table 6.1 between $z=0.90\;(\operatorname{erf}=0.7970)$ and $z=0.95\;(\operatorname{erf}=0.8209)$:

$$z = 0.90 + \frac{0.80-0.7970}{0.8209-0.7970}(0.05) \approx 0.906$$

**Step 2 — Solve for $D$.**

With $x = 3.5\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$, $t = 48\times3600 = 1.728\times10^{5}\,\text{s}$:

$$D = \frac{x^2}{4z^2 t} = \frac{(3.5\times10^{-3})^2}{4(0.906)^2(1.728\times10^{5})} \approx 2.16\times10^{-11}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Step 3 — Solve for $T$ using the Arrhenius relation.**

For C diffusing in Fe (FCC): $D_0 = 2.3\times10^{-5}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q_d = 148{,}000\,\text{J/mol}$.

$$D = D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right) \;\Rightarrow\; T = \frac{Q_d}{R\ln(D_0/D)}$$

$$T = \frac{148000}{8.31\,\ln\!\left(\dfrac{2.3\times10^{-5}}{2.16\times10^{-11}}\right)} \approx 1283\,\text{K}$$

## 最终答案
1283
