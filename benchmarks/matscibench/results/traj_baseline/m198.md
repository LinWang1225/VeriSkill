## 题目
Suppose $2 \mathrm{wt} \% \mathrm{ThO}_{2}$ is added to nickel. Each $\mathrm{ThO}_{2}$ particle has a diameter of $1000 \AA$. How many particles are present in each cubic centimeter? The densities of $\mathrm{ThO}_{2}$ and nickel are 9.69 and $8.9 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, respectively.
Give your final answer in units of particles / cm^3.

## 过程
Take a basis of 100 g of composite: 2 g ThO₂ and 98 g Ni.

Volumes:
- $V_{\mathrm{ThO_2}} = 2/9.69 = 0.2064\ \mathrm{cm^3}$
- $V_{\mathrm{Ni}} = 98/8.9 = 11.011\ \mathrm{cm^3}$
- $V_{\text{total}} = 11.218\ \mathrm{cm^3}$

Particle diameter $d = 1000\ \text{Å} = 1000\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{cm} = 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{cm}$.

Volume of one particle:
$$V_p = \frac{4}{3}\pi\left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(5\times10^{-6})^3 = 5.236\times10^{-16}\ \mathrm{cm^3}$$

Number of particles in the 100 g batch:
$$N = \frac{V_{\mathrm{ThO_2}}}{V_p} = \frac{0.2064}{5.236\times10^{-16}} = 3.941\times10^{14}$$

Particles per cm³:
$$\frac{N}{V_{\text{total}}} = \frac{3.941\times10^{14}}{11.218} = 3.514\times10^{13}$$

## 最终答案
3.514e13
