## 题目
The planar density of the (112) plane in BCC iron is $9.94 \times 10^{14}$ atoms $/ \mathrm{cm}^{2}$. Calculate (a) the planar density of the (110) plane and (b) the interplanar spacings for both the (112) and (110) planes. The lattice parameter of BCC iron is 0.2866 nm or $2.866 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~cm}$. Give your answer as a tuple (planar density of (110) plane, interplanar spacing of (112) plane, interplanar spacing of (110) plane).
Give your final answer in units of (atoms/cm^2, cm, cm).

## 过程
For a cubic lattice (BCC iron, cubic), the interplanar spacing is
$$d_{hkl}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}},\qquad a=2.866\times10^{-8}\text{ cm}.$$

**(a) Planar density of (110).** The (110) plane in a BCC cell is a rectangle of area $a\cdot a\sqrt{2}=a^{2}\sqrt{2}$ containing 2 effective atoms (4 corners × 1/4 + 1 body-center):
$$\text{PD}_{110}=\frac{2}{a^{2}\sqrt{2}}=\frac{2}{(2.866\times10^{-8})^{2}\sqrt{2}}=1.7217\times10^{15}\text{ atoms/cm}^{2}.$$

Check on the given (112) value: the (112) plane (parallelogram of area $a^{2}\sqrt{3/2}$) carries 1 effective atom, giving $1/[a^{2}\sqrt{3/2}]=9.94\times10^{14}$ atoms/cm², matching the stated value.

**(b) Interplanar spacings.**
$$d_{112}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{1+1+4}}=\frac{2.866\times10^{-8}}{\sqrt{6}}=1.170\times10^{-8}\text{ cm},$$
$$d_{110}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{1+1+0}}=\frac{2.866\times10^{-8}}{\sqrt{2}}=2.027\times10^{-8}\text{ cm}.$$

## 最终答案
(1.7217e15, 1.1700e-8, 2.0266e-8)
