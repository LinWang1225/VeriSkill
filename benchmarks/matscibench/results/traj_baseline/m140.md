## 题目
A large-particle composite consisting of tungsten particles within a copper matrix is to be prepared. If the volume fractions of tungsten and copper are 0.70 and 0.30, respectively, estimate the upper limit for the specific stiffness of this composite, given the data that follow.

|  | Specific Gravity | Modulus of Elasticity (GPa) |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| Copper | 8.9 | 110 |
| Tungsten | 19.3 | 407 |
Give your final answer in units of GPa.

## 过程
For a large-particle composite, the **upper limit** (Voigt / rule of mixtures) is used for both the modulus of elasticity and the density.

Upper-bound modulus:
$$E_c = E_W V_W + E_{Cu} V_{Cu} = 407(0.70) + 110(0.30) = 284.9 + 33.0 = 317.9 \text{ GPa}$$

Composite specific gravity (rule of mixtures, using specific gravity as relative density):
$$\rho_c = \rho_W V_W + \rho_{Cu} V_{Cu} = 19.3(0.70) + 8.9(0.30) = 13.51 + 2.67 = 16.18$$

Specific stiffness = modulus ÷ density:
$$\frac{E_c}{\rho_c} = \frac{317.9}{16.18} \approx 19.65 \text{ GPa}$$

## 最终答案
19.65
