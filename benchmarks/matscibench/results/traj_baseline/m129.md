## 题目
It is known that the kinetics of recrystallization for some alloy obeys the Avrami equation, and that the value of $n$ in the exponential is 5.0. If, at some temperature, the fraction recrystallized is 0.30 after 100 min , determine the rate of recrystallization at this temperature.
Give your final answer in units of $(\min )^{-1}$.

## 过程
The Avrami equation is

$$y = 1 - \exp(-k t^n)$$

with $n = 5.0$. Given $y = 0.30$ at $t = 100\ \text{min}$, first solve for $k$:

$$1-y = \exp(-k t^n) \;\Rightarrow\; -k t^n = \ln(1-y)$$

$$k = -\frac{\ln(1-y)}{t^n} = -\frac{\ln(0.70)}{100^5}$$

The rate of recrystallization is $\dfrac{dy}{dt}$:

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = n k t^{n-1}\exp(-k t^n) = n k t^{n-1}(1-y)$$

Substituting $n=5$, $t=100$, $y=0.30$:

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = 5 \cdot k \cdot 100^{4} \cdot 0.70$$

Numerically this evaluates to $0.0124836\ \text{min}^{-1}$.

## 最终答案
0.01248
