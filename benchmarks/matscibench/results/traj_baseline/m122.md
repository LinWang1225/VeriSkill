## 题目
A structural component in the form of a wide plate is to be fabricated from a steel alloy that has a planestrain fracture toughness of $98.9 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{~m}}(90 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\text { in. }})$ and a yield strength of 860 MPa (125,000 psi). The flaw size resolution limit of the flaw detection apparatus is 3.0 mm ( 0.12 in .). If the design stress is one-half the yield strength and the value of $Y$ is 1.0, determine whether a critical flaw for this plate is subject to detection.
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
Substituting:

$$a_c = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{98.9}{1.0 \times 430}\right)^2 = 0.01684\ \mathrm{m} = 16.84\ \mathrm{mm}$$

The critical flaw size ($\approx 16.8$ mm) is well above the flaw detection resolution limit of 3.0 mm, so **a critical flaw for this plate is subject to detection**.

## 最终答案
16.84
