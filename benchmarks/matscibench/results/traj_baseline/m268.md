## 题目
Consider a single crystal of BCC iron oriented such that a tensile stress is applied along a [010] direction. (a) Compute the resolved shear stress along a (110) plane and in a [111] direction when a tensile stress of 52 MPa (7500 psi) is applied. (b) If slip occurs on a (110) plane and in a [111] direction, and the critical resolved shear stress is 30 MPa (4350 psi), calculate the magnitude of the applied tensile stress necessary to initiate yielding. Give your answer as a tuple (resolved shear stress in part (a), applied tensile stress in part (b)).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa).

## 过程
By Schmid's law, the resolved shear stress is τ_R = σ cos φ cos λ, where φ is the angle between the tensile axis and the slip-plane normal, and λ is the angle between the tensile axis and the slip direction.

Tensile axis: [010]; slip plane (110) → normal [110]; slip direction [111].

cos φ = ([010]·[110])/(|[010]||[110]|) = 1/(1·√2) = 1/√2
cos λ = ([010]·[111])/(|[010]||[111]|) = 1/(1·√3) = 1/√3

So cos φ cos λ = 1/√6.

(a) τ_R = 52 × (1/√6) = 52/√6 ≈ 21.23 MPa.

(b) Yielding initiates when τ_R = τ_c = 30 MPa, so σ_y = τ_c/(cos φ cos λ) = 30√6 ≈ 73.48 MPa.

## 最终答案
(21.23, 73.48)
