## 题目
A charge of $2.0 \times 10^{-10} \mathrm{C}$ is to be stored on each plate of a parallel-plate capacitor having an area of 650 $\mathrm{mm}^{2}\left(1.0 \mathrm{in} .^{2}\right.$ ) and a plate separation of 4.0 mm ( 0.16 in.).
(a) What voltage is required if a material having a dielectric constant of 3.5 is positioned within the plates?
(b) What voltage would be required if a vacuum were used?
(c) What are the capacitances for parts (a) and (b)?
(d) Compute the dielectric displacement for part (a).
(e) Compute the polarization for part (a).

Give your answer as a tuple (voltage in (a), voltage in (b), capacitances for (a), capacitances for (b), dielectric displacement for part (a), polarization for part (a)).
Give your final answer in units of (V, V, F, F, C/m^2, C/m^2).

## 过程
Given: $Q=2.0\times10^{-10}\,\text{C}$, $A=650\,\text{mm}^2=6.5\times10^{-4}\,\text{m}^2$, $d=4.0\,\text{mm}=4.0\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$, $\varepsilon_r=3.5$, $\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\,\text{F/m}$.

Capacitance of parallel-plate capacitor: $C=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A}{d}$, and $V=\dfrac{Q}{C}=\dfrac{Qd}{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A}$.

(a) $V_a=\dfrac{Qd}{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A}\approx 39.73\,\text{V}$

(b) Vacuum $\varepsilon_r=1$: $V_b=\dfrac{Qd}{\varepsilon_0 A}=\varepsilon_r V_a\approx 139.07\,\text{V}$

(c) $C_a=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A}{d}\approx 5.03\times10^{-12}\,\text{F}$; $C_b=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}\approx 1.44\times10^{-12}\,\text{F}$

(d) Dielectric displacement $D=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r E=\dfrac{Q}{A}\approx 3.08\times10^{-7}\,\text{C/m}^2$

(e) Polarization $P=D-\varepsilon_0 E=\varepsilon_0(\varepsilon_r-1)E$, with $E=V_a/d$: $P\approx 2.20\times10^{-7}\,\text{C/m}^2$

## 最终答案
(39.73, 139.07, 5.03e-12, 1.44e-12, 3.08e-07, 2.20e-07)
