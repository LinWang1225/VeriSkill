## 题目

Compute the time required for a specimen of this same material (i.e., $d{0}=1.7 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~mm}$ ) to achieve a grain diameter of $8.7 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~mm}$ while being heated at $450^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Assume the $n$ grain diameter exponent has a value of 2.1.
Give your final answer in units of min.
