## 题目
The constant $A$ in equation (C_v=A T^3) is $12 \pi^{4} R / 5 \theta{D}^{3}$, where $R$ is the gas constant and $\theta{D}$ is the Debye temperature (K). Estimate $\theta{D}$ for aluminum, given that the specific heat is $4.60 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}-\mathrm{K}$ at 15 K . $A^{\prime}=26.98 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.
Give your final answer in units of K.

## 过程
$C_v = 0.124108\ \mathrm{J/mol\cdot K}$, giving $A = 3.677\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{J/mol\cdot K^4}$ and

$$\theta_D = \left(\frac{12\pi^4(8.314)}{5(3.677\times10^{-5})}\right)^{1/3} \approx 375\ \mathrm{K}$$

This matches the known Debye temperature of aluminum.

## 最终答案
375
