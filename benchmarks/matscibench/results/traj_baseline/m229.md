## 题目
Analysis of a nickel casting suggests that $28 \%$ of the solidification process occurred in a dendritic manner. Calculate the temperature at which nucleation occurred. The specific heat of nickel is $4.1 \mathrm{~J} /\left(\mathrm{cm}^3 \cdot{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$. Give your answer as the value of nucleation_temperature.
Give your final answer in units of °C.

## 过程
During dendritic solidification, the liquid undercools below the melting point $T_m$ before nucleation. The latent heat released by the fraction that solidifies dendritically is consumed as sensible heat warming the liquid back toward $T_m$ (recalescence). The heat balance is

$$f_s H_f = c_p \Delta T_n \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Delta T_n = \frac{f_s H_f}{c_p}$$

where $f_s = 0.28$ is the dendritic fraction, $c_p = 4.1\ \mathrm{J/(cm^3\cdot{}^\circ C)}$, and $H_f$ is the volumetric latent heat of fusion of nickel.

**Volumetric latent heat of Ni** (using $H_f^{\text{molar}}=17.2\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$, $M=58.69\ \mathrm{g/mol}$, $\rho=8.90\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$):

$$H_f = \frac{17200}{58.69}\times 8.90 = 2608.3\ \mathrm{J/cm^3}$$

**Undercooling:**

$$\Delta T_n = \frac{0.28 \times 2608.3}{4.1} = 178.1\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

**Nucleation temperature** (with $T_m(\mathrm{Ni}) = 1455^\circ\mathrm{C}$):

$$T_n = T_m - \Delta T_n = 1455 - 178.1 = 1276.9^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

## 最终答案
1276.87
