## 题目
For an aluminum alloy under constant load, the creep rate increased by a factor of 2 when the temperature was increased from $71\,^\circ\text{C}$ to $77\,^\circ\text{C}$. Find the activation energy.
Give your final answer in units of kJ/mole.

## 过程
Creep rate follows an Arrhenius law: $\dot{\varepsilon}=A\exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q}{RT}\right)$.

Taking the ratio at the two temperatures ($T_1=71+273=344$ K, $T_2=77+273=350$ K):

$$\frac{\dot{\varepsilon}_2}{\dot{\varepsilon}_1}=\exp\!\left[-\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)\right]=2$$

Solving for $Q$:

$$Q=\frac{R\ln 2}{\dfrac{1}{T_1}-\dfrac{1}{T_2}}$$

With $R=8.314$ J/mol·K:

$$Q=\frac{8.314\ln 2}{\frac{1}{344}-\frac{1}{350}}=1.156\times10^{5}\ \text{J/mol}\approx 115.6\ \text{kJ/mol}$$

## 最终答案
115.64
