## 题目
A cylindrical rod 120 mm long and having a diameter of 15.0 mm is to be deformed using a tensile load of $35,000 \mathrm{~N}$. It must not experience either plastic deformation or a diameter reduction of more than $1.2 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~mm}$. Calculate the change in diameter of the candidate materials.

| Material | Modulus of Elasticity (GPa) | Yield Strength (MPa) | Poisson's Ratio |
| :-- | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Aluminum alloy | 70 | 250 | 0.33 |
| Titanium alloy | 105 | 850 | 0.36 |
| Steel alloy | 205 | 550 | 0.27 |

Give your answer as a tuple (Aluminum alloy,  Steel alloy, Titanium alloy).
Give your final answer in units of (mm, mm, mm).

## 过程
**1. Applied stress** (same for all materials, using original cross-section):

$$A_0 = \frac{\pi d_0^2}{4} = \frac{\pi(15.0\times10^{-3})^2}{4}=1.767\times10^{-4}\,\text{m}^2$$

$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A_0}=\frac{35000}{1.767\times10^{-4}}=198.06\,\text{MPa}$$

This is below every yield strength (250, 850, 550 MPa), so **no plastic deformation** occurs for any material — all deformation is elastic.

**2. Elastic axial strain** $\varepsilon = \sigma/E$, **lateral strain** $\varepsilon_{\text{lat}}=-\nu\varepsilon$, and **diameter change** $\Delta d = \varepsilon_{\text{lat}}\,d_0 = -\nu\dfrac{\sigma}{E}d_0$:

| Material | $E$ (GPa) | $\nu$ | $\varepsilon$ | $\Delta d = -\nu(\sigma/E)d_0$ (mm) | $|\Delta d|\le 1.2\times10^{-2}$? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminum alloy | 70 | 0.33 | $2.829\times10^{-3}$ | $-1.401\times10^{-2}$ | **No** (fails) |
| Steel alloy | 205 | 0.27 | $9.661\times10^{-4}$ | $-3.913\times10^{-3}$ | Yes |
| Titanium alloy | 105 | 0.36 | $1.886\times10^{-3}$ | $-1.019\times10^{-2}$ | Yes |

All three remain elastic (σ < σ_y). Aluminum, however, exceeds the allowed diameter reduction of $1.2\times10^{-2}$ mm, so it is rejected as a candidate; Steel and Titanium satisfy both constraints.

## 最终答案
(-0.014005634992086792, -0.003912882503332452, -0.010185916357881302)
