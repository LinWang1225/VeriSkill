## 题目
Suppose a 24 -in.-long round bar is to support a load of 400 lb without any permanent deformation. Calculate the minimum diameter of the bar if it is made from (a) AZ80A-T5 magnesium alloy and (b) 6061-T6 aluminum alloy. Calculate the weight of the bar and the approximate cost (based on pure Mg and $\mathrm{Al})$ in each case. Give your answer as a tuple: (min_diameter_AZ80A_in, weight_AZ80A_lb, cost_AZ80A_USD, min_diameter_6061_in, weight_6061_lb, cost_6061_USD).
Give your final answer in units of (in, lb, USD, in, lb, USD).

## 过程
To avoid permanent deformation the axial stress must not exceed the yield strength:

$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A}=\frac{F}{\pi d^{2}/4}\le \sigma_y \;\Rightarrow\; d_{\min}=\sqrt{\frac{4F}{\pi\sigma_y}}$$

with \(F=400\;\text{lb}\), \(L=24\;\text{in}\).

**Material properties (Callister appendix tables):**
- AZ80A‑T5 Mg: \(\sigma_y=276\;\text{MPa}=40{,}000\;\text{psi}\), \(\rho=1.74\;\text{g/cm}^3=0.06286\;\text{lb/in}^3\)
- 6061‑T6 Al: \(\sigma_y=276\;\text{MPa}=40{,}000\;\text{psi}\), \(\rho=2.70\;\text{g/cm}^3=0.09754\;\text{lb/in}^3\)
- Pure‑metal prices: Mg \$3.18/lb, Al \$1.10/lb

Both alloys have the same yield strength, so both require the same minimum area \(A=F/\sigma_y=400/40000=0.0100\;\text{in}^2\):

$$d_{\min}=\sqrt{\frac{4(0.0100)}{\pi}}=0.1128\;\text{in}$$

**Weight** \(W=\rho A L=\rho(0.0100)(24)=0.240\,\rho\):
- Mg: \(0.240(0.06286)=0.01509\;\text{lb}\)
- Al: \(0.240(0.09754)=0.02341\;\text{lb}\)

**Cost** \(=W\times\text{price}\):
- Mg: \(0.01509(3.18)=\$0.0480\)
- Al: \(0.02341(1.10)=\$0.0258\)

The Mg bar is lighter but, owing to Mg's higher unit price, costs more; the Al bar is heavier but cheaper.

## 最终答案
(0.1128, 0.0151, 0.0480, 0.1128, 0.0234, 0.0258)
