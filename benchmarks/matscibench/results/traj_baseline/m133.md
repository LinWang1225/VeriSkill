## 题目
(a) Using the data in  table 12.1 , compute the resistance of an aluminum wire 5 mm ( 0.20 in.) in diameter and $5 m$ (200 in.) in length.
(b) What would be the current flow if the potential drop across the ends of the wire is 0.04 V ?
(c) What is the current density?
(d) What is the magnitude of the electric field across the ends of the wire?

Give your answer as a tuple (the resistance in (a), the current flow in (b), the current density in (c), the magnitude of the electric field in (d)).

Reference:
"Table 12.1
Room-Temperature Electrical Conductivities for Nine Common Metals and Alloys
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline Metal & Electrical Conductivity [ $(\Omega \cdot \boldsymbol{m})^{-1}$ ] \\
\hline Silver & $6.8 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Copper & $6.0 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Gold & $4.3 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Aluminum & $3.8 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Brass ( $70 \mathrm{Cu}-30 \mathrm{Zn}$ ) & $1.6 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Iron & $1.0 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Platinum & $0.94 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Plain carbon steel & $0.6 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline Stainless steel & $0.2 \times 10^7$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}"
Give your final answer in units of ($\Omega$, A, A/m^2, V/m).

## 过程
Given for aluminum: conductivity $\sigma = 3.8\times10^{7}\;(\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1}$, diameter $d=5\;\text{mm}=5\times10^{-3}\;\text{m}$, length $L=5\;\text{m}$, potential drop $V=0.04\;\text{V}$.

Cross-sectional area:
$$A=\pi\left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^2=\pi(2.5\times10^{-3})^2\approx1.9635\times10^{-5}\;\text{m}^2$$

(a) Resistance:
$$R=\frac{\rho L}{A}=\frac{L}{\sigma A}=\frac{5}{(3.8\times10^{7})(1.9635\times10^{-5})}\approx6.70\times10^{-3}\;\Omega$$

(b) Current (Ohm's law):
$$I=\frac{V}{R}=\frac{0.04}{6.70\times10^{-3}}\approx5.97\;\text{A}$$

(c) Current density:
$$J=\frac{I}{A}=\frac{5.97}{1.9635\times10^{-5}}\approx3.04\times10^{5}\;\text{A/m}^2$$

(d) Electric field:
$$\mathcal{E}=\frac{V}{L}=\frac{0.04}{5}=8.0\times10^{-3}\;\text{V/m}$$

## 最终答案
(0.00670, 5.97, 304000.0, 0.008)
