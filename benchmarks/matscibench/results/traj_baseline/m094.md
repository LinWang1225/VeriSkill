## 题目
The diffusion coefficients for nickel in iron are given at two temperatures, as follows:

\begin{tabular}{cc}
\hline$T(\mathrm{~K})$ & $D\left(\mathrm{~m}^2 / \mathrm{s}\right)$ \\
\hline 1473 & $2.2 \times 10^{-15}$ \\
1673 & $4.8 \times 10^{-14}$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

(a) Determine the values of $D_{0}$ and the activation energy $Q_{d}$.
(b) What is the magnitude of $D$ at $1300^{\circ} \mathrm{C}(1573 \mathrm{~K})$ ?

Give your answer as a tuple ($D_{0}$, $Q_{d}$, D at $1300^{\circ} \mathrm{C}(1573 \mathrm{~K})
Give your final answer in units of (m^2/s, J/mol, m^2/s).

## 过程
The diffusion coefficient follows the Arrhenius relation:

$$D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$$

Taking natural logs gives a linear relation in $1/T$:

$$\ln D = \ln D_0 - \frac{Q_d}{R}\cdot\frac{1}{T}$$

Using the two data points $(T_1,D_1)=(1473,\,2.2\times10^{-15})$ and $(T_2,D_2)=(1673,\,4.8\times10^{-14})$:

$$m = \frac{\ln D_2 - \ln D_1}{1/T_2 - 1/T_1} = -\frac{Q_d}{R}, \qquad \ln D_0 = \ln D_1 - m/T_1$$

Numerically (with $R=8.31$ J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹):
- slope $m = -Q_d/R \Rightarrow Q_d \approx 315651$ J/mol
- $D_0 = \exp(\ln D_0) \approx 3.48\times10^{-4}$ m²/s

For part (b) at $T=1573$ K:

$$D(1573) = D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{R\cdot1573}\right) \approx 1.13\times10^{-14}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

## 最终答案
(0.000348, 315651, 1.13e-14)
