## 题目
A bar of annealed $85 \mathrm{Cu}-15 \mathrm{Zn}$ (12-mm diameter) is cold-drawn through a die with a diameter of 10 mm . What are (a) the tensile strength and (b) the ductility of the resulting bar? Give your answer as a tuple: (tensile_strength, ductility).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, %).

## 过程
Cold-work percentage based on area reduction:

$$CW = \frac{A_0 - A_f}{A_0}\times 100 = \frac{d_0^2 - d_f^2}{d_0^2}\times 100 = \frac{12^2 - 10^2}{12^2}\times 100 = \frac{44}{144}\times 100 \approx 30.6\%$$

For annealed 85Cu‑15Zn brass, the tensile‑strength and ductility vs. %cold‑work curves (Callister, Fig. 7.8) are read at CW ≈ 30.6%:

- (a) Tensile strength ≈ 440 MPa
- (b) Ductility (% elongation) ≈ 15%

## 最终答案
(440, 15)
