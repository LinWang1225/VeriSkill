## 题目
Design of a More Economical Heat TreatmentnnWe find that 10 hours are required to successfully carburize a batch of 500 steel gears at $900^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, where the iron has the FCC structure. It costs $\$1000$ per hour to operate the carburizing furnace at $900^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and $\$1500$ per hour to operate the furnace at $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. For carbon diffusing in FCC iron, the activation energy is $32,900 \mathrm{cal} / \mathrm{mol}$. Is it economical to increase the carburizing temperature to $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$?

 **Give your answer as a tuple: (required_time_at_1000C, cost_per_part_at_900C, cost_per_part_at_1000C).*
Give your final answer in units of (hours, dollars/part, dollars/part).

## 过程
Carburizing is governed by diffusion, so to reach the same carbon profile we need the same diffusion exposure: $D_1 t_1 = D_2 t_2$.

With $D = D_0 \exp(-Q/RT)$, the $D_0$ cancels:

$$t_2 = t_1 \frac{D_1}{D_2} = t_1 \exp\!\left[\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)\right]$$

Given $Q = 32900\ \mathrm{cal/mol}$, $R = 1.987\ \mathrm{cal/(mol\cdot K)}$, $T_1 = 900+273 = 1173\ \mathrm{K}$, $T_2 = 1000+273 = 1273\ \mathrm{K}$, $t_1 = 10\ \mathrm{h}$:

$$\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \exp\!\left[\frac{32900}{1.987}\left(\frac{1}{1273}-\frac{1}{1173}\right)\right] \approx 0.3299$$

$$t_2 = 10 \times 0.3299 \approx 3.30\ \mathrm{h}$$

Cost per part:
- At $900^\circ\mathrm{C}$: $\dfrac{\$1000/\mathrm{h}\times 10\ \mathrm{h}}{500} = \$20.00/\mathrm{part}$
- At $1000^\circ\mathrm{C}$: $\dfrac{\$1500/\mathrm{h}\times 3.30\ \mathrm{h}}{500} \approx \$9.90/\mathrm{part}$

Since $\$9.90 < \$20.00$, raising the temperature to $1000^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is economical.

## 最终答案
(3.30, 20.00, 9.90)
