## 题目
The fraction recrystallized-time data for the recrystallization at $350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ of a previously deformed aluminum are tabulated here. Assuming that the kinetics of this process obey the Avrami relationship, determine the fraction recrystallized after a total time of 116.8 min .

| Fraction Recrystallized | Time $(\mathbf{m i n})$ |
| :--: | :--: |
| 0.30 | 95.2 |
| 0.80 | 126.6 |

## 过程
The Avrami relationship is

$$X = 1 - \exp(-k t^{n})$$

Rearranging and taking logarithms twice:

$$\ln\!\left[\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{1-X}\right)\right] = \ln k + n \ln t$$

Using the two data points (X₁=0.30, t₁=95.2) and (X₂=0.80, t₂=126.6):

$$n = \frac{\ln\!\ln\!\frac{1}{1-X_2}-\ln\!\ln\!\frac{1}{1-X_1}}{\ln t_2 - \ln t_1}$$

Evaluating: y₁ = ln[ln(1/0.70)] = −0.1856, y₂ = ln[ln(1/0.20)] = 0.3266.

$$n = \frac{0.3266-(-0.1856)}{\ln 126.6-\ln 95.2} = \frac{0.5122}{0.2842} = 5.286$$

Then ln k = y₁ − n ln t₁ = −0.1856 − 5.286·ln(95.2) = −25.011, so k = 1.239×10⁻¹¹.

At t = 116.8 min:

$$X = 1 - \exp\!\left[-(1.239\times10^{-11})(116.8)^{5.286}\right] = 1 - \exp(-1.869)$$

$$X \approx 0.6505$$

## 最终答案
0.6505
