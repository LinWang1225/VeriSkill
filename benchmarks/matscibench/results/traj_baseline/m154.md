## 题目
It is possible to express the magnetic susceptibility $\chi{m}$ in several different units. For the discussion of this chapter, $\chi{m}$ was used to designate the volume susceptibility in SI units-that is, the quantity that gives the magnetization per unit volume $\left(m^{3}\right)$ of material when multiplied by $H$. The mass susceptibility $\chi{m}(\mathrm{~kg})$ yields the magnetic moment (or magnetization) per kilogram of material when multiplied by $H$; similarly, the atomic susceptibility $\chi{m}(a)$ gives the magnetization per kilogram-mole. The last two quantities are related to $\chi{m}$ through the following relationships:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \chi{m}=\chi{m}(k g) \times \text { mass density }\left(\text { in } \mathrm{kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}\right) \\
& \chi{m}(a)=\chi{m}(k g) \times \text { atomic weight (in } \mathrm{kg})
\end{aligned}
$$

When using the cgs-emu system, comparable parameters exist, which may be designated by $\chi{m}^{\prime}, \chi{m}^{\prime}(g)$, and $\chi{m}^{\prime}(a)$; the $\chi{m}$ and $\chi{m}^{\prime}$ are related in accordance with  table 18.1 . From  table 18.2 , $\chi{m}$ for copper is $-0.96 \times 10^{-5}$; convert this value into the other five susceptibilities. Give your answer as a tuple: ($\chi_m(\mathrm{~kg})$, $\chi_m(a), \chi_m^{\prime}, \chi_m^{\prime}(\mathrm{g})$, $\chi_m^{\prime}(\mathrm{a})$)

Give your final answer in units of (\mathrm{m}^3 / \mathrm{kg}, \mathrm{m}^3 / \mathrm{mol}\right.$, unitless $\left., \mathrm{cm}^3 / \mathrm{g}, \mathrm{cm}^3 / \mathrm{mol}\right).

## 过程
Given $\chi_m = -0.96\times10^{-5}$ (SI volume susceptibility) for copper. Copper properties: density $\rho = 8.96\;\text{g/cm}^3 = 8960\;\text{kg/m}^3$, atomic weight $A = 63.55\;\text{g/mol} = 0.06355\;\text{kg/mol}$.

**SI mass susceptibility** (m³/kg): divide volume susceptibility by density in kg/m³:
$$\chi_m(\mathrm{kg}) = \frac{\chi_m}{\rho} = \frac{-0.96\times10^{-5}}{8960} = -1.07\times10^{-9}$$

**SI atomic susceptibility** (m³/mol): multiply mass susceptibility by atomic weight in kg:
$$\chi_m(a) = \chi_m(\mathrm{kg})\times A_{\text{kg}} = (-1.0714\times10^{-9})(0.06355) = -6.81\times10^{-11}$$

**cgs-emu volume susceptibility** (unitless): use conversion $\chi_m = 4\pi\chi_m^{\prime}$:
$$\chi_m^{\prime} = \frac{\chi_m}{4\pi} = \frac{-0.96\times10^{-5}}{4\pi} = -7.64\times10^{-7}$$

**cgs-emu mass susceptibility** (cm³/g): divide by density in g/cm³:
$$\chi_m^{\prime}(\mathrm{g}) = \frac{\chi_m^{\prime}}{\rho_{\text{g/cm}^3}} = \frac{-7.639\times10^{-7}}{8.96} = -8.53\times10^{-8}$$

**cgs-emu atomic susceptibility** (cm³/mol): multiply by atomic weight in g:
$$\chi_m^{\prime}(\mathrm{a}) = \chi_m^{\prime}(\mathrm{g})\times A_{\text{g}} = (-8.526\times10^{-8})(63.55) = -5.42\times10^{-6}$$

## 最终答案
(-1.07e-09, -6.81e-11, -7.64e-07, -8.53e-08, -5.42e-06)
