## 题目
The dielectric constant of MgO is 9.83, and its molar volume $(V_{\mathrm{m}})$ is $18.69 \AA^{3}$. The dielectric constant of $\mathrm{Al}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$ is estimated to be 10.126, and its molar volume $(V_{\mathrm{m}})$ is $42.45 \AA^{3}$. What is the dielectric constant of $\mathrm{MgAl}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{4}$? The molar volume $(V_{\mathrm{m}})$ of $\mathrm{MgAl}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{4}$ is $66.00 \AA^{3}$.
Note: No supplementary information needed.

## 过程
Using the **Clausius–Mossotti relation**, the molar polarizability is

$$P_m \;=\; \frac{3V_m}{4\pi}\,\frac{\varepsilon_r - 1}{\varepsilon_r + 2}$$

Molar polarizability is **additive** over constituent oxides. Since

$$\mathrm{MgAl_2O_4} = \mathrm{MgO} + \mathrm{Al_2O_3},$$

we have $P_m(\text{spinel}) = P_m(\text{MgO}) + P_m(\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3)$. The common factor $\tfrac{3}{4\pi}$ cancels, giving

$$V_m^{\text{sp}}\frac{\varepsilon_{\text{sp}}-1}{\varepsilon_{\text{sp}}+2}
= V_m^{\text{MgO}}\frac{\varepsilon_{\text{MgO}}-1}{\varepsilon_{\text{MgO}}+2}
+ V_m^{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3}\frac{\varepsilon_{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3}-1}{\varepsilon_{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3}+2}.$$

Evaluating each side:

- MgO: $18.69 \times \frac{9.83-1}{9.83+2} = 13.9504$
- Al₂O₃: $42.45 \times \frac{10.126-1}{10.126+2} = 31.9478$
- Sum $C = 45.8981$

So $R = \dfrac{C}{V_m^{\text{sp}}} = \dfrac{45.8981}{66.00} = 0.69543$, and

$$\varepsilon_{\text{sp}} = \frac{1+2R}{1-R} = \frac{1+2(0.69543)}{1-0.69543} \approx 7.850.$$

## 最终答案
7.85
