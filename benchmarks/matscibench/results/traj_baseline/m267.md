## 题目
Question: Average and Standard Deviation Computations

The following tensile strengths were measured for four specimens of the same steel alloy:


\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Sample Number} & \textbf{Tensile Strength (MPa)} \\
\hline
1 & 520 \\
2 & 512 \\
3 & 515 \\
4 & 522 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}



(a) Compute the average tensile strength.
(b) Determine the standard deviation.
Give your answer as a tuple (average tensile strength, standard deviation).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa).

## 过程
The four measurements are 520, 512, 515, 522 MPa.

(a) Average:
$$\bar{x}=\frac{520+512+515+522}{4}=\frac{2069}{4}=517.25\ \text{MPa}$$

(b) Standard deviation (sample, using the Callister convention with $n-1$ in the denominator):
$$s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}}$$

Deviations from the mean: $520-517.25=2.75$, $512-517.25=-5.25$, $515-517.25=-2.25$, $522-517.25=4.75$.

Squared deviations: $7.5625,\ 27.5625,\ 5.0625,\ 22.5625$, sum $=62.75$.

$$s=\sqrt{\frac{62.75}{3}}=\sqrt{20.9167}\approx 4.57\ \text{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
(517.25, 4.57)
