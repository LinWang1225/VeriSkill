## 题目
Calculate the concentration of vacancies in copper at room temperature $\left(25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\right)$. What temperature will be needed to heat treat copper such that the concentration of vacancies produced will be 1000 times more than the equilibrium concentration of vacancies at room temperature? Assume that 20,000 cal are required to produce a mole of vacancies in copper. The lattice parameter of FCC copper is 0.36151 nm. Give your answer as a tuple (concentration, temperature)
Give your final answer in units of (vacancies/cm³, K).

## 过程
For FCC copper, there are 4 atoms per unit cell. The atomic density is

$$N = \frac{4}{a^3}, \quad a = 0.36151\,\text{nm} = 3.6151\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}$$

$$N = \frac{4}{(3.6151\times10^{-8})^3} = 8.466\times10^{22}\,\text{atoms/cm}^3$$

The equilibrium vacancy concentration is

$$N_v = N\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{RT}\right)$$

with $Q_v = 20000\,\text{cal/mol}$, $R = 1.987\,\text{cal/(mol·K)}$, $T = 25+273 = 298\,\text{K}$.

$$N_v = 8.466\times10^{22}\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{20000}{1.987\times298}\right) = 1.814\times10^{8}\,\text{vacancies/cm}^3$$

For a concentration 1000 times larger:

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{RT}\right) = 1000\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{RT_0}\right)$$

$$\frac{Q_v}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_0}-\frac{1}{T}\right)=\ln 1000 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{1}{T}=\frac{1}{298}-\frac{R}{Q_v}\ln 1000$$

$$T = 374.6\,\text{K}$$

## 最终答案
(1.814e8, 374.6)
