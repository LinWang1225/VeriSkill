## 题目
The density of a sample of FCC palladium is $11.98 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$, and its lattice parameter is $3.8902 \AA$. Calculate(a) the fraction of the lattice points that contain vacancies; and(b) the total number of vacancies in a cubic centimeter of Pd. Give your answer as a tuple: (vacancy_fraction, vacancies_per_cm3).
Give your final answer in units of (, vacancies/cm3).

## 过程
For FCC, the ideal unit cell has 4 atoms. The measured density reflects the actual number of atoms present per cell:

$$n' = \frac{\rho V_c N_A}{A}$$

with $V_c = a^3$, $a = 3.8902\times10^{-8}$ cm, $A_{\text{Pd}} = 106.42$ g/mol, $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}$.

- $V_c = (3.8902\times10^{-8})^3 = 5.886\times10^{-23}$ cm³
- $n' = \frac{11.98 \times 5.886\times10^{-23}\times 6.022\times10^{23}}{106.42} \approx 3.9911$

**(a) Vacancy fraction:**

$$f_v = \frac{4 - n'}{4} = \frac{4 - 3.9911}{4} \approx 2.23\times10^{-3}$$

**(b) Vacancies per cm³:** Number of unit cells per cm³ is $1/V_c \approx 1.699\times10^{22}$; lattice points per cm³ $= 4/V_c \approx 6.794\times10^{22}$.

$$N_v = \frac{4}{V_c}f_v \approx 6.794\times10^{22}\times 2.23\times10^{-3} \approx 1.52\times10^{20}\ \text{cm}^{-3}$$

## 最终答案
(0.00223, 1.52e20)
