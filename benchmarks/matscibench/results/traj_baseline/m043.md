## 题目
The Feature Box in this chapter introduced spider silk as an impressive natural material with a specific strength greater than structural steel. Given a tensile strength of a particular spider silk as $1.2 \times 10^3 \mathrm{MPa}$ with a density of $1.30 \mathrm{Mg} / \mathrm{m}^3$, calculate its specific strength and compare that result with the value for 1040 steel in Table. Give your answer as the value of specific_strength.
(Table:
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline \multicolumn{3}{|l|}{Specific Strengths (Strength/Density)} \\
\hline Group & Material & Specific strength [mm (in.)] \\
\hline \multirow[t]{4}{*}{Noncomposites} & 1040 steel ${ }^{\text {a }}$ & $9.9 \times 10^6\left(0.39 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & 2048 plate aluminum ${ }^{\text {a }}$ & $16.9 \times 10^6\left(0.67 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & Ti-5Al-2.5Sn ${ }^{\text {a }}$ & $19.7 \times 10^6\left(0.78 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & Epoxy ${ }^{\text {b }}$ & $6.4 \times 10^6\left(0.25 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline \multirow[t]{5}{*}{Composites} & E-glass (73.3 vol \%) in epoxy (parallel loading of continuous fibers) ${ }^{\text {c }}$ & $77.2 \times 10^6\left(3.04 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & $\mathrm{Al}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ whiskers (14 vol \%) in epoxy ${ }^{\text {c }}$ & $48.8 \times 10^6\left(1.92 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & $\mathrm{C}(67 \mathrm{vol} \%)$ in epoxy (parallel loading) ${ }^{\text {d }}$ & $76.9 \times 10^6\left(3.03 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & $\operatorname{Kevlar}^{\mathrm{e}}$ (82 vol \%) in epoxy (parallel loading) ${ }^{\text {d }}$ & $112 \times 10^6\left(4.42 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline & Douglas fir, kiln-dried to $12 \%$ moisture (loaded in bending) ${ }^{\text {c }}$ & $18.3 \times 10^6\left(0.72 \times 10^6\right)$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
)
Give your final answer in units of mm.

## 过程
Specific strength is strength divided by density. Because the table reports specific strength as a **length** (mm, with inch values that convert consistently to the same length), the quantity is $\sigma/(\rho g)$, which has units of meters:

$$
\text{specific strength}=\frac{\sigma}{\rho g}
$$

Given:
- $\sigma = 1.2\times10^{3}\,\text{MPa}=1.2\times10^{9}\,\text{Pa}$
- $\rho = 1.30\,\text{Mg/m}^{3}=1.30\times10^{3}\,\text{kg/m}^{3}$
- $g = 9.81\,\text{m/s}^{2}$

$$
\frac{\sigma}{\rho g}=\frac{1.2\times10^{9}}{(1.30\times10^{3})(9.81)}
=9.41\times10^{4}\,\text{m}
$$

Converting to mm ($\times10^{3}$):

$$
\text{specific strength}=9.41\times10^{7}\,\text{mm}
$$

Comparison: 1040 steel $=9.9\times10^{6}\,\text{mm}$, so the spider silk's specific strength is

$$
\frac{9.41\times10^{7}}{9.9\times10^{6}}\approx 9.5
$$

times that of 1040 steel — consistent with the Feature Box's claim that spider silk's specific strength exceeds that of structural steel.

## 最终答案
9.4e7
