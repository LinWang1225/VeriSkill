## 题目
Consider a parallel-plate capacitor having an area of $3225 \mathrm{~mm}^{2}$ (5 in. ${ }^{2}$ ), a plate separation of 1 mm (0.04 in.), and a material having a dielectric constant of 3.5 positioned between the plates.
(a) What is the capacitance of this capacitor?
(b) Compute the electric field that must be applied for $2 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{C}$ to be stored on each plate.

Give your answer as a tuple (capacitance of this capacitor in (a), electric field in (b)).
Give your final answer in units of (F, V/m).

## 过程
For a parallel-plate capacitor with a dielectric:

$$C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r A}{d}$$

Converting units: $A = 3225\,\text{mm}^2 = 3225\times10^{-6}\,\text{m}^2 = 3.225\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}^2$, $d = 1\,\text{mm} = 10^{-3}\,\text{m}$, $\varepsilon_r = 3.5$, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85\times10^{-12}\,\text{F/m}$.

(a) 
$$C = \frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(3.5)(3.225\times10^{-3})}{10^{-3}} = 9.99\times10^{-11}\,\text{F} \approx 1.0\times10^{-10}\,\text{F}$$

(b) The charge–voltage relation is $Q = CV$, so $V = Q/C$. The electric field is $E = V/d = Q/(Cd)$:

$$E = \frac{2\times10^{-8}}{(9.99\times10^{-11})(10^{-3})} = 2.00\times10^{5}\,\text{V/m}$$

## 最终答案
(9.99e-11, 2.00e5)
