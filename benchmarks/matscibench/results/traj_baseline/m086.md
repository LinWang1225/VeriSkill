## 题目
Calculate the number of vacancies per cubic meter at $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for a metal that has an energy for vacancy formation of $1.22 \mathrm{eV} /$ atom, a density of $6.25 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, and an atomic weight of $37.4 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.
Give your final answer in units of m^-3.

## 过程
The equilibrium number of vacancies is

$$N_v = N \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{kT}\right)$$

where the number of atomic sites per m³ is

$$N = \frac{\rho N_A}{A}$$

- $\rho = 6.25\ \text{g/cm}^3 = 6.25\times10^6\ \text{g/m}^3$
- $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}\ \text{atoms/mol}$
- $A = 37.4\ \text{g/mol}$
- $Q_v = 1.22\ \text{eV/atom}$
- $k = 8.617\times10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}$
- $T = 1000+273 = 1273\ \text{K}$

Atomic site density:

$$N = \frac{(6.25\times10^6)(6.022\times10^{23})}{37.4} \approx 1.006\times10^{29}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$

Exponential factor:

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{1.22}{(8.617\times10^{-5})(1273)}\right) \approx 1.479\times10^{-5}$$

Thus

$$N_v \approx (1.006\times10^{29})(1.479\times10^{-5}) \approx 1.49\times10^{24}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$

## 最终答案
1.49e24
