## 题目
Consider a single crystal of some hypothetical metal that has the BCC crystal structure and is oriented such that a tensile stress is applied along a [121] direction. If slip occurs on a (101) plane and in a [ $\overline{1} 11]$ direction, compute the stress at which the crystal yields if its critical resolved shear stress is 2.4 MPa .
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
By Schmid's law, yielding occurs when the resolved shear stress equals the CRSS:

$$\tau_R = \sigma \cos\phi \cos\lambda = \tau_c$$

where $\phi$ is the angle between the tensile axis $[121]$ and the slip-plane normal $[101]$, and $\lambda$ is the angle between the tensile axis $[121]$ and the slip direction $[\bar{1}11]$.

**Cos φ:**
$$\cos\phi = \frac{[121]\cdot[101]}{|[121]||[101]|} = \frac{1\cdot1+2\cdot0+1\cdot1}{\,5sqrt{6}\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{12}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$$

**Cos λ:**
$$\cos\lambda = \frac{[121]\cdot[\bar{1}11]}{|[121]||[\bar{1}11]|} = \frac{-1+2+1}{\sqrt{6}\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{18}} = \frac{2}{3\sqrt{2}}$$

**Schmid factor:**
$$m = \cos\phi\cos\lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{2}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2}{3\sqrt{6}} \approx 0.2722$$

**Yield stress:**
$$\sigma_y = \frac{\tau_c}{m} = \frac{2.4}{2/(3\sqrt{6})} = 3.6\sqrt{6} \approx 8.818 \text{ MPa}$$

## 最终答案
8.82
