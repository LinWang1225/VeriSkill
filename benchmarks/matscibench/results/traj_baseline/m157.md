## 题目
“Samarium iron garnet has the formula Sm₃Fe₅O₁₂ which can be written Sm₃ᶜ Fe₂ᵃ Fe₃ᵈ O₁₂, where the subscripts ᵃ, ᶜ, and ᵈ denote the crystallographic a, c, and d sites.  The 2 Fe³⁺ ions on the a‐sites have their spins aligned parallel to those of the 3 Sm³⁺ ions on the c‐sites, and these are antiparallel to the 3 Fe³⁺ ions on the d‐sites.  Given that (1) each cubic unit cell (edge 1.2529 nm) contains eight Sm₃Fe₅O₁₂ formula units, (2) the saturation magnetization Mₛ=1.35×10⁵ A/m, and (3) each Fe³⁺ carries 5 μ_B, compute the number of Bohr magnetons μ_Sm carried by each Sm³⁺ ion.”
Give your final answer in units of BM.

## 过程
The garnet formula unit Sm₃Fe₅O₁₂ = Sm₃ᶜ Fe₂ᵃ Fe₃ᵈ O₁₂ has the spin arrangement: a‑site Fe (2×5 μ_B) and c‑site Sm (3·μ_Sm) parallel, both antiparallel to d‑site Fe (3×5 μ_B). Taking the a/c direction as positive, the net moment per formula unit is

$$m_{\text{fu}} = (2\cdot5 + 3\mu_{Sm}) - (3\cdot5) = 3\mu_{Sm} - 5 \quad [\mu_B].$$

Convert the given saturation magnetization to a moment per formula unit. The cubic cell volume is $V=a^3=(1.2529\times10^{-9}\,\text{m})^3$, and with 8 formula units per cell:

$$m_{\text{fu}} = \frac{M_s V}{8}.$$

In Bohr magnetons ($\mu_B=9.274\times10^{-24}\,\text{A·m}^2$):

$$\frac{m_{\text{fu}}}{\mu_B} = \frac{(1.35\times10^5)(1.2529\times10^{-9})^3}{8(9.274\times10^{-24})} \approx 3.579.$$

Set $3\mu_{Sm}-5 = 3.579$ and solve:

$$\mu_{Sm} = \frac{3.579+5}{3} \approx 2.86.$$

## 最终答案
2.86
