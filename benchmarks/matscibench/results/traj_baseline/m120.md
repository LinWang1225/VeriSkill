## 题目
A specimen of a 4340 steel alloy with a plane strain fracture toughness of $54.8 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{~m}}(50 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\mathrm{in}}$. ) is exposed to a stress of 1030 MPa (150,000 psi). Will this specimen experience fracture if the largest surface crack is $0.5 \mathrm{~mm}(0.02 \mathrm{in}$.) long? Why or why not? Assume that the parameter Y has a value of 1.0.
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
For a surface crack, the stress intensity factor is

$$K_I = Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a}$$

with $Y=1.0$, $\sigma=1030\ \text{MPa}$, and $a=0.5\ \text{mm}=5\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}$.

Evaluating:

$$K_I = (1.0)(1030)\sqrt{\pi(5\times10^{-4})}=40.8\ \text{MPa}\sqrt{\text{m}}$$

The plane-strain fracture toughness is $K_{Ic}=54.8\ \text{MPa}\sqrt{\text{m}}$.

Since $K_I = 40.8\ \text{MPa}\sqrt{\text{m}} < K_{Ic}=54.8\ \text{MPa}\sqrt{\text{m}}$, the crack tip stress intensity is below the toughness, so the specimen will **not** fracture.

Equivalently, the critical (fracture) stress is

$$\sigma_c = \frac{K_{Ic}}{Y\sqrt{\pi a}}=\frac{54.8}{1.0\sqrt{\pi(5\times10^{-4})}}=1382.7\ \text{MPa}$$

Since the applied stress $1030\ \text{MPa} < 1382.7\ \text{MPa}=\sigma_c$, no fracture occurs.

## 最终答案
1382.7
