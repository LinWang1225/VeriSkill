## 题目
Calculate the total amount of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and the amount of each microconstituent in a $\mathrm{Pb}-50 \% \mathrm{Sn}$ alloy at $182^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. What fraction of the total $\alpha$ in the alloy is contained in the eutectic microconstituent? Give your answer as a tuple: (total_alpha_percent, total_beta_percent, primary_alpha_percent, eutectic_percent, fraction_eutectic_alpha).
Give your final answer in units of (%, %, %, %, ).

## 过程
For the Pb–Sn system at 182 °C (just below the eutectic at 183 °C), use the phase compositions:
- $C_\alpha = 18.3\%$ Sn, $C_\beta = 97.8\%$ Sn, $C_E = 61.9\%$ Sn, $C_0 = 50\%$ Sn.

**Total phases** (lever rule across $\alpha$–$\beta$):
$$W_\alpha = \frac{C_\beta-C_0}{C_\beta-C_\alpha}=\frac{97.8-50}{97.8-18.3}=60.13\%$$
$$W_\beta = \frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_\beta-C_\alpha}=\frac{50-18.3}{79.5}=39.87\%$$

**Microconstituents** (lever rule across primary-$\alpha$ / eutectic, using $C_E$):
$$W_{\alpha_p}=\frac{C_E-C_0}{C_E-C_\alpha}=\frac{61.9-50}{61.9-18.3}=27.29\%$$
$$W_e=\frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_E-C_\alpha}=\frac{31.7}{43.6}=72.71\%$$

**Fraction of total $\alpha$ in the eutectic:** The eutectic liquid (61.9) decomposes into $\alpha(18.3)$ and $\beta(97.8)$, so the $\alpha$ fraction within the eutectic is $\frac{97.8-61.9}{79.5}=0.4516$. Thus $\alpha$ in eutectic $= 72.71\times0.4516 = 32.83\%$ of the alloy, and
$$\text{fraction}=\frac{32.83}{60.13}=0.546$$

## 最终答案
(60.13, 39.87, 27.29, 72.71, 0.546)
