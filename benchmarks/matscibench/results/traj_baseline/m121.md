## 题目
Suppose that a wing component on an aircraft is fabricated from an aluminum alloy that has a plane-strain fracture toughness of $26.0 \mathrm{MPa} \sqrt{\mathrm{m}}(23.7 \mathrm{ksi} \sqrt{\text { in. }}$ ). It has been determined that fracture results at a stress of 112 MPa (16,240 psi) when the maximum internal crack length is 8.6 mm ( 0.34 in .). For this same component and alloy, compute the stress level at which fracture will occur for a critical internal crack length of 6.0 mm ( 0.24 in.).
Give your final answer in units of MPa.

## 过程
For plane-strain fracture, the governing relation is

$$K_{Ic}=Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a}$$

where for an **internal** crack the total crack length is $2a$, so $a$ is half the stated crack length.

**Step 1 — Determine $Y$ from the known fracture condition.**
Given $K_{Ic}=26.0\ \mathrm{MPa\sqrt m}$, $\sigma_1=112\ \mathrm{MPa}$, and internal crack length $2a_1=8.6\ \mathrm{mm}=0.0086\ \mathrm{m}$, so $a_1=0.0043\ \mathrm{m}$:

$$Y=\frac{K_{Ic}}{\sigma_1\sqrt{\pi a_1}}=\frac{26.0}{112\sqrt{\pi(0.0043)}}\approx 1.997\approx 2.0$$

This confirms the standard geometry factor $Y\approx 2$ for an internal crack.

**Step 2 — Compute the new fracture stress.**
For the critical internal crack length $2a_2=6.0\ \mathrm{mm}=0.0060\ \mathrm{m}$, so $a_2=0.0030\ \mathrm{m}$:

$$\sigma_2=\frac{K_{Ic}}{Y\sqrt{\pi a_2}}=\frac{26.0}{1.997\sqrt{\pi(0.0030)}}$$

Numerical evaluation gives

$$\sigma_2 \approx 134.1\ \mathrm{MPa}$$

## 最终答案
134.1
