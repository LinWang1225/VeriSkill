## 题目
An isothermally transformed eutectoid steel is found to have a yield strength of 410 MPa. Estimate (a) the transformation temperature and (b) the interlamellar spacing in the pearlite. Give your answer as a tuple: (transformation_temperature_C, interlamellar_spacing_cm).
Give your final answer in units of (°C, cm).

## 过程
For isothermally transformed eutectoid steel, Callister gives two empirical relations for pearlite:

**1. Yield strength vs. interlamellar spacing** (Hall-Petch form):
$$\sigma_y = 139 + \frac{92.8}{\sqrt{\lambda}} \quad (\sigma_y\ \text{MPa},\ \lambda\ \mu\text{m})$$

**2. Interlamellar spacing vs. undercooling**:
$$\lambda = \frac{8.02\times10^{-4}}{\Delta T} \quad (\lambda\ \text{cm},\ \Delta T\ \text{K})$$
with $\Delta T = T_E - T = 727 - T$.

**(b) Interlamellar spacing** — solve Eq. 1 with $\sigma_y = 410$ MPa:

$$\sqrt{\lambda} = \frac{92.8}{410 - 139} = \frac{92.8}{271} = 0.3424$$
$$\lambda = (0.3424)^2 = 0.1173\ \mu\text{m} = 1.173\times10^{-5}\ \text{cm}$$

**(a) Transformation temperature** — from Eq. 2:

$$\Delta T = \frac{8.02\times10^{-4}}{1.173\times10^{-5}} = 68.4\ \text{K}$$
$$T = 727 - 68.4 = 658.6\ °\text{C}$$

Verification: $139 + 92.8/\sqrt{0.1173} = 410.0$ MPa ✓

## 最终答案
(658.61, 1.17e-05)
