## 题目
If the atomic radius of lead is 0.175 nm, calculate the volume of its unit cell in cubic meters.
Give your final answer in units of m^3.

## 过程
Lead (Pb) has an FCC crystal structure. For FCC, the lattice parameter relates to the atomic radius by:

$$a = 2R\sqrt{2}$$

Given $R = 0.175\ \text{nm} = 0.175\times10^{-9}\ \text{m}$:

$$a = 2(0.175\times10^{-9})\sqrt{2} = 4.9497\times10^{-10}\ \text{m}$$

Unit cell volume:

$$V = a^3 = \left(2R\sqrt{2}\right)^3 = 16R^3\sqrt{2}$$

$$V = 16(0.175\times10^{-9})^3\sqrt{2} \approx 1.2127\times10^{-28}\ \text{m}^3$$

## 最终答案
1.2127e-28
