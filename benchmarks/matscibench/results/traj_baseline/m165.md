## 题目
If the range of strength of an alloy is given as $800-1200 \mathrm{MPa}$, what is the mean strength, the standard deviation, and coefficient of variation? Give your answer as a tuple: (mean_strength, standard_deviation, coefficient_of_variation).
Give your final answer in units of (MPa, MPa,).

## 过程
The strength range $800-1200\ \mathrm{MPa}$ is treated as a uniform distribution over $[a,b]=[800,1200]$.

For a uniform distribution:
- Mean: $\mu=\dfrac{a+b}{2}=\dfrac{800+1200}{2}=1000\ \mathrm{MPa}$
- Standard deviation: $\sigma=\dfrac{b-a}{\sqrt{12}}=\dfrac{400}{\sqrt{12}}\approx 115.47\ \mathrm{MPa}$
- Coefficient of variation: $CV=\dfrac{\sigma}{\mu}=\dfrac{115.47}{1000}\approx 0.1155$

## 最终答案
(1000.0, 115.47, 0.1155)
