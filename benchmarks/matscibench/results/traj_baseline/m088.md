## 题目
When $\alpha$-iron is subjected to an atmosphere of nitrogen gas, the concentration of nitrogen in the iron, $C{N}$ (in weight percent), is a function of hydrogen pressure, $p{N{2}}$ (in MPa), and absolute temperature (T) according to

$$
C{\mathrm{N}}=4.90 \times 10^{-3} \sqrt{p{\mathrm{N}{2}}} \exp \left(-\frac{37,600 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right)
$$

Furthermore, the values of $D{0}$ and $Q{d}$ for this diffusion system are $5.0 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}$ and $77,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$, respectively. Consider a thin iron membrane $1.5-\mathrm{mm}$ thick that is at $300^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Compute the diffusion flux through this membrane if the nitrogen pressure on one side of the membrane is 0.10 MPa ( 0.99 atm ) and that on the other side is 5.0 MPa (49.3 atm).
Give your final answer in units of kg/m^2s.

## 过程
The diffusion flux follows Fick's first law in steady state across the thin membrane:

$$J = -D\frac{C_2 - C_1}{\Delta x} = D\frac{C_1 - C_2}{\Delta x}$$

where $C_1, C_2$ are the surface concentrations at the two faces (set by the gas pressures), and $D = D_0\exp(-Q_d/RT)$.

**Temperature:** $T = 300 + 273 = 573\ \text{K}$.

**Surface concentrations (wt%):** Both faces share the same Arrhenius factor $\exp(-37600/RT)$:

- $C_1 = 4.90\times10^{-3}\sqrt{0.10}\,\exp(-37600/(8.31\cdot573)) = 5.77\times10^{-7}\ \text{wt\%}$
- $C_2 = 4.90\times10^{-3}\sqrt{5.0}\,\exp(-37600/(8.31\cdot573)) = 4.08\times10^{-6}\ \text{wt\%}$

**Convert wt% → kg/m³** using iron density $\rho_{\text{Fe}}=7.87\times10^{3}\ \text{kg/m}^3$:
$$C\,[\text{kg/m}^3] = \frac{C\,[\text{wt\%}]}{100}\,\rho_{\text{Fe}}$$
- $c_1 = 4.54\times10^{-5}\ \text{kg/m}^3$
- $c_2 = 3.21\times10^{-4}\ \text{kg/m}^3$

**Diffusivity:**
$$D = 5.0\times10^{-7}\exp\!\left(-\frac{77000}{8.31\cdot573}\right) = 4.74\times10^{-14}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

**Flux** ($\Delta x = 1.5\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}$):
$$J = D\frac{c_1 - c_2}{\Delta x} = (4.74\times10^{-14})\frac{4.54\times10^{-5}-3.21\times10^{-4}}{1.5\times10^{-3}} = -8.71\times10^{-15}\ \text{kg/m}^2\text{s}$$

The negative sign indicates flow from the high-pressure (5.0 MPa) side toward the low-pressure (0.10 MPa) side. Reporting the magnitude:

## 最终答案
8.71e-15
