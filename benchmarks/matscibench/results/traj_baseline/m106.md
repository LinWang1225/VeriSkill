## 题目
A cylindrical rod 500 mm (20.0 in.) long and having a diameter of 12.7 mm ( 0.50 in.) is to be subjected to a tensile load. If the rod is to experience neither plastic deformation nor an elongation of more than 1.3 mm ( 0.05 in.) when the applied load is $29,000 \mathrm{~N}\left(6500 \mathrm{lb}{2}\right)$, compute $\Delta l$ of the candidate materials.

| Material | Modulus of Elasticity (GPa) | Yield Strength (MPa) | Tensile Strength (MPa) |
| :-- | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Aluminum alloy | 70 | 255 | 420 |
| Brass alloy | 100 | 345 | 420 |
| Steel alloy | 207 | 450 | 550 |

Give your answer as a tuple (Aluminum alloy, Brass alloy, Steel alloy)
Give your final answer in units of (mm, mm, mm).

## 过程
Given:
- Length $L = 500$ mm, diameter $d = 12.7$ mm, load $F = 29000$ N
- Cross-sectional area: $A = \pi(d/2)^2 = \pi(6.35)^2 = 126.68\ \text{mm}^2$
- Stress: $\sigma = F/A = 29000/126.68 = 228.93$ MPa

Elongation (elastic): $\Delta l = \dfrac{FL}{AE} = \dfrac{\sigma L}{E}$

Checking yield (no plastic deformation): $\sigma = 228.93$ MPa is below all three yield strengths (255, 345, 450 MPa), so all deformations are elastic.

Evaluating with $E$ in MPa (= N/mm²):
- Aluminum ($E=70$ GPa): $\Delta l = \dfrac{29000 \times 500}{126.68 \times 70000} = 1.635$ mm
- Brass ($E=100$ GPa): $\Delta l = \dfrac{29000 \times 500}{126.68 \times 100000} = 1.145$ mm
- Steel ($E=207$ GPa): $\Delta l = \dfrac{29000 \times 500}{126.68 \times 207000} = 0.553$ mm

(Note: Aluminum's 1.635 mm exceeds the 1.3 mm elongation limit, so it would be rejected on that criterion; Brass and Steel satisfy both criteria. The computed $\Delta l$ values are reported as asked.)

## 最终答案
(1.635, 1.145, 0.553)
