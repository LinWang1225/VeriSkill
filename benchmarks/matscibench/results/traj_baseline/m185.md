## 题目
For potassium chloride $(\mathrm{KCl})$, (a) calculate the radius ratio of $\mathrm{K}^{+}$ to $\mathrm{Cl}^{-}$ and (b) calculate the packing factor for the compound. The ionic radii are $r_{\mathrm{K}^{+}}=0.133 \mathrm{~nm}$ and $r_{\mathrm{Cl}^{-}}=0.181 \mathrm{~nm}$. Give your answer as a tuple (radius ratio, packing factor).
Give your final answer in units of (, ).

## 过程
**(a) Radius ratio**

$$\frac{r_{\mathrm{K}^+}}{r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}} = \frac{0.133}{0.181} = 0.735$$

**(b) Packing factor**

KCl crystallizes in the NaCl (rock salt) structure — an FCC lattice with 4 K⁺ and 4 Cl⁻ ions per unit cell. The lattice parameter is set by ions touching along an edge:

$$a = 2(r_{\mathrm{K}^+} + r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-})$$

The atomic packing factor (packing factor) is the fraction of the unit-cell volume occupied by ions:

$$\mathrm{PF} = \frac{4\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi r_{\mathrm{K}^+}^3 + 4\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}^3}{a^3} = \frac{\frac{16\pi}{3}(r_{\mathrm{K}^+}^3 + r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}^3)}{[2(r_{\mathrm{K}^+}+r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-})]^3}$$

Substituting $r_{\mathrm{K}^+}=0.133$ nm, $r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}=0.181$ nm:

- $a = 2(0.133+0.181) = 0.628$ nm
- $V_{\text{ions}} = \frac{16\pi}{3}(0.133^3 + 0.181^3) = 0.1386$ nm³
- $V_{\text{cell}} = 0.628^3 = 0.2477$ nm³
- $\mathrm{PF} = 0.1386/0.2477 = 0.560$

## 最终答案
(0.735, 0.560)
