## 题目
(a) Calculate the magnetic moment of a unit cell of manganese ferrite. (b) Calculate the corresponding saturation magnetization, given a lattice parameter of 0.850 nm . Give your answer as a tuple: (magnetic_moment, saturation_magnetization).
Give your final answer in units of (\mu_{\mathrm{B}}, A/m).

## 过程
Manganese ferrite is **MnFe₂O₄**, a normal spinel. In a normal spinel the divalent cation occupies tetrahedral (A) sites and the trivalent cations occupy octahedral (B) sites:

- **A sublattice:** Mn²⁺ → 3d⁵ high-spin → 5 μ_B
- **B sublattice:** 2 × Fe³⁺ → 2 × (3d⁵ high-spin) → 2 × 5 = 10 μ_B

The two sublattices are antiparallel, so the net moment per formula unit is

$$n_B = |M_B - M_A| = |10 - 5| = 5\;\mu_B$$

**(a) Unit-cell magnetic moment.** A spinel unit cell contains 8 formula units:

$$\mu_{\text{uc}} = 8 \times 5 = 40\;\mu_B$$

**(b) Saturation magnetization.** Converting to A·m² and dividing by the unit-cell volume $V = a^3$:

$$M_s = \frac{40\,\mu_B}{a^3} = \frac{40 \times 9.274\times10^{-24}}{(0.850\times10^{-9})^3}$$

Numerically: $M_s \approx 6.04\times10^{5}\;\text{A/m}$.

## 最终答案
(40, 604047.06)
