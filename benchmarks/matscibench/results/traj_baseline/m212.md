## 题目
The density of BCC iron is $7.882 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$, and the lattice parameter is 0.2866 nm when hydrogen atoms are introduced at interstitial positions. Calculate (a) the atomic fraction of hydrogen atoms; and (b) the number of unit cells on average that contain hydrogen atoms. Give your answer as a tuple: (atomic_fraction_of_hydrogen, number_of_unit_cells_on_average_that_contain_hydrogen_atoms.).
Give your final answer in units of (, ).

## 过程
For BCC iron there are 2 Fe atoms per unit cell. With hydrogen occupying interstitial sites, let $n_H$ be the number of H atoms per unit cell. The density is

$$\rho = \frac{n_{Fe}A_{Fe} + n_H A_H}{V N_A}, \qquad V = a^3$$

Solving for $n_H$:

$$n_H = \frac{\rho V N_A - n_{Fe}A_{Fe}}{A_H}$$

Using $\rho = 7.882\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$, $a = 0.2866\ \mathrm{nm} = 2.866\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{cm}$, $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}$, $A_{Fe}=55.845$, $A_H=1.008$, $n_{Fe}=2$:

- $V = a^3 = 2.352\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{cm^3}$
- $\rho V N_A = 7.882 \cdot 2.352\times10^{-23}\cdot 6.022\times10^{23} = 111.70$
- $n_{Fe}A_{Fe} = 2(55.845) = 111.69$
- $n_H = (111.70 - 111.69)/1.008 \approx 0.0489$ H atoms per unit cell

**(a) Atomic fraction of hydrogen:**

$$x_H = \frac{n_H}{n_{Fe}+n_H} = \frac{0.0489}{2+0.0489} \approx 0.0238$$

**(b) Number of unit cells on average that contain hydrogen atoms:**

Since $n_H \ll 1$, the probability that a given unit cell contains a hydrogen atom is $\approx n_H$. The reciprocal gives the average number of unit cells per hydrogen-containing cell:

$$\frac{1}{n_H} = \frac{1}{0.0489} \approx 20.47$$

So on average, 1 out of every ≈ 20.47 unit cells contains a hydrogen atom.

## 最终答案
(0.0238, 20.47)
