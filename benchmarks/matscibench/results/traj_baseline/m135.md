## 题目
A hypothetical metal is known to have an electrical resistivity of $3.3 \times 10^{-8}(\Omega-\mathrm{m})$. A current of 25 A is passed through a specimen of this metal 15 mm thick. When a magnetic field of 0.95 tesla is simultaneously imposed in a direction perpendicular to that of the current, a Hall voltage of $-2.4 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~V}$ is measured. Compute the following:
(a) the electron mobility for this metal
(b) the number of free electrons per cubic meter.
Give your answer a tuple: (answer to (a), answer to (b)).
Give your final answer in units of ($\mathrm{m}^2 / \mathrm{V}-\mathrm{s}$, $\mathrm{m}^{-3}$).

## 过程
Hall effect relations:
- Hall coefficient: $R_H = \dfrac{V_H d}{I B}$, with $d=15\text{ mm}=1.5\times10^{-2}\text{ m}$ (thickness along $\mathbf{B}$).
- For electron conduction: $R_H = -\dfrac{1}{ne}$, so $|R_H|=\dfrac{1}{ne}$.
- Mobility: $\mu = \dfrac{|R_H|}{\rho}=\dfrac{1}{ne\rho}$, and conductivity $\sigma=\dfrac{1}{\rho}=ne\mu$.

Numerically:
$$R_H = \frac{(-2.4\times10^{-7})(1.5\times10^{-2})}{(25)(0.95)} = -1.516\times10^{-10}\ \Omega\text{-m/C}$$

(a) $\mu = \dfrac{|R_H|}{\rho} = \dfrac{1.516\times10^{-10}}{3.3\times10^{-8}} \approx 4.59\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2/\text{V-s}$

(b) $n = \dfrac{1}{|R_H|e} = \dfrac{1}{(1.516\times10^{-10})(1.602\times10^{-19})} \approx 4.12\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^{-3}$

## 最终答案
(4.59e-3, 4.12e28)
