## 题目
Given that at the eutectoid temperature the ferrite phase contains 0.0218 %C and the cementite phase (Fe₃C) contains 6.67 %C,  use the lever rule to calculate the weight fractions of ferrite and cementite in pearlite, which has an overall composition of 0.77 %C. 

Give your answer as (ferrite_fraction, cementite_fraction).
Give your final answer in units of (%, %).

## 过程
Using the lever rule with overall composition $C_0=0.77$, ferrite $C_\alpha=0.0218$, and cementite $C_{\theta}=6.67$:

$$W_\alpha = \frac{C_{\theta}-C_0}{C_{\theta}-C_\alpha}=\frac{6.67-0.77}{6.67-0.0218}=\frac{5.90}{6.6482}=0.88746$$

$$W_{\theta} = \frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_{\theta}-C_\alpha}=\frac{0.77-0.0218}{6.6482}=\frac{0.7482}{6.6482}=0.11254$$

Check: $W_\alpha+W_\theta=1.0$. ✓

## 最终答案
(88.75, 11.25)
