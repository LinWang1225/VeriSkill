## 题目
Steady-state creep rate data are given in the following table for some alloy taken at $200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}(473 \mathrm{~K})$ :

| $\dot{\varepsilon}{s}\left(\mathrm{~h}^{-1}\right)$ | $\sigma[\mathrm{MPa}(p s i)]$ |
| :-- | :-- |
| $2.5 \times 10^{-3}$ | $55(8000)$ |
| $2.4 \times 10^{-2}$ | $69(10,000)$ |

If it is known that the activation energy for creep is $140,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$, compute the steady-state creep rate at a temperature of $250^{\circ} \mathrm{C}(523 \mathrm{~K})$ and a stress level of $48 \mathrm{MPa}(7000 \mathrm{psi})$.
Give your final answer in units of h^-1.

## 过程
Steady-state creep follows:

$$\dot{\varepsilon}_s = K\,\sigma^{\,n}\,e^{-Q/(RT)}$$

**Step 1 — Find stress exponent $n$** (both points at $T_1=473$ K, so the Arrhenius factor cancels):

$$\frac{\dot{\varepsilon}_{s2}}{\dot{\varepsilon}_{s1}}=\left(\frac{\sigma_2}{\sigma_1}\right)^n \;\Rightarrow\; n=\frac{\ln(2.4\times10^{-2}/2.5\times10^{-3})}{\ln(69/55)}=\frac{\ln(9.6)}{\ln(1.2545)}\approx 9.97$$

**Step 2 — Find $K$** using point 1 ($Q=140{,}000$ J/mol, $R=8.31$ J/mol·K):

$$K=\frac{\dot{\varepsilon}_{s1}}{\sigma_1^{\,n}e^{-Q/(RT_1)}}=\frac{2.5\times10^{-3}}{55^{9.97}\,e^{-140000/(8.31\cdot473)}}\approx 3.23\times10^{-5}$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate at $T_2=523$ K, $\sigma=48$ MPa:**

$$\dot{\varepsilon}_s = K\,(48)^{9.97}\,e^{-140000/(8.31\cdot523)} \approx 1.94\times10^{-2}\ \text{h}^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
1.94e-2
