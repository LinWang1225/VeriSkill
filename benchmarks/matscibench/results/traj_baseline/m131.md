## 题目
The kinetics of the austenite-to-pearlite transformation obeys the Avrami relationship. Using the fraction transformed-time data given here, determine the total time required for $95 \%$ of the austenite to transform to pearlite:

| Fraction Transformed | Time $(\mathrm{s})$ |
| :--: | :--: |
| 0.2 | 280 |
| 0.6 | 425 |
Give your final answer in units of s.

## 过程
The Avrami relationship is

$$y = 1 - \exp(-k t^n)$$

Rearranging: $\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{1-y}\right) = k t^n$.

Let $u=\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{1-y}\right)$.

From the two data points:
- $u_1=\ln(1/0.8)=\ln 1.25=0.22314$ at $t_1=280$ s
- $u_2=\ln(1/0.4)=\ln 2.5=0.91629$ at $t_2=425$ s

Dividing eliminates $k$:

$$\frac{u_2}{u_1}=\left(\frac{t_2}{t_1}\right)^n \;\Rightarrow\; n=\frac{\ln(u_2/u_1)}{\ln(t_2/t_1)}=\frac{\ln(0.91629/0.22314)}{\ln(425/280)}\approx 3.385$$

Then $k=u_1/t_1^n \approx 1.162\times10^{-9}$.

For $y=0.95$: $u_3=\ln(1/0.05)=\ln 20=2.9957$, so

$$t_{95}=\left(\frac{u_3}{k}\right)^{1/n}\approx 603.08\ \text{s}$$

## 最终答案
603.08
