## 题目
An electrical contact material is produced by infiltrating copper into a porous tungsten carbide (WC) compact. The density of the final composite is $12.3 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$. Assuming that all of the pores are filled with copper, calculate (a) the volume fraction of copper in the composite, (b) the volume fraction of pores in the WC compact prior to infiltration, and (c) the original density of the WC compact before infiltration. Give your answer as a tuple: (volume_fraction_copper, volume_fraction_pores, original_density_WC).
Give your final answer in units of (, , g/cm³).

## 过程
Let $V$ be the total volume (same for the WC compact and the final composite, since infiltration fills pores without changing external dimensions). Let $f$ be the volume fraction of pores (= volume fraction of copper, since all pores are filled with Cu).

Mass balance on the composite:
$$\rho_{\text{comp}} = \rho_{\text{WC}}(1-f) + \rho_{\text{Cu}}\,f$$

Using standard densities $\rho_{\text{WC}}=15.77\ \text{g/cm}^3$, $\rho_{\text{Cu}}=8.96\ \text{g/cm}^3$:

$$12.3 = 15.77(1-f)+8.96\,f = 15.77-6.81\,f$$
$$f = \frac{15.77-12.3}{15.77-8.96}=\frac{3.47}{6.81}\approx 0.5095$$

- **(a)** Volume fraction of copper $= f \approx 0.510$.
- **(b)** Volume fraction of pores in the WC compact prior to infiltration $= f \approx 0.510$ (pores become copper).
- **(c)** Original density of WC compact $=$ (mass of WC)/$V = \rho_{\text{WC}}(1-f)=15.77(1-0.5095)\approx 7.73\ \text{g/cm}^3$.

## 最终答案
(0.510, 0.510, 7.73)
