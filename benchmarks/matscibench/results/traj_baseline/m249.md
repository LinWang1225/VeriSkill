## 题目
We apply 10 V to an aluminum wire 2 mm in diameter and 20 m long. If $10 \%$ of the valence electrons carry the electrical charge, calculate the average drift velocity of the electrons in $\mathrm{km} / \mathrm{h}$ and miles $/ \mathrm{h}$. Give your answer as a tuple: (average_drift_velocity_km_per_h, average_drift_velocity_miles_per_h).
Give your final answer in units of (km/h, miles/h).

## 过程
**Drift velocity** is given by $v_d = \dfrac{I}{n\,e\,A}$, where $n$ is the mobile charge-carrier density.

**1. Geometry & area**
$$A = \pi\left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^2 = \pi(1\times10^{-3})^2 = 3.1416\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^2$$

**2. Current via Ohm's law + resistance**
$$R = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{(2.65\times10^{-8})(20)}{3.1416\times10^{-6}} = 0.1687\ \Omega$$
$$I = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{10}{0.1687} = 59.28\ \text{A}$$

**3. Mobile electron density**

Aluminum: density $2.70\ \text{g/cm}^3$, atomic weight $26.98\ \text{g/mol}$, valence $=3$, $10\%$ mobile.

$$n_{\text{atoms}} = \frac{\rho_{\text{mass}} N_A}{M} = \frac{(2.70\times10^3)(6.022\times10^{23})}{0.02698} = 6.0265\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$
$$n = 0.10 \times 3 \times n_{\text{atoms}} = 1.808\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$

**4. Drift velocity**
$$v_d = \frac{I}{n\,e\,A} = \frac{59.28}{(1.808\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})(3.1416\times10^{-6})} = 6.514\times10^{-3}\ \text{m/s}$$

**5. Unit conversion**
$$v_d = 6.514\times10^{-3}\times3.6 = 0.02345\ \text{km/h}$$
$$v_d = \frac{0.02345}{1.609344} = 0.01457\ \text{miles/h}$$

## 最终答案
(0.0235, 0.0146)
