## 题目
A metal alloy is known to have electrical conductivity and electron mobility values of $1.2 \times 10^{7}(\Omega-\mathrm{m})^{-1}$ and $0.0050 \mathrm{~m}^{2} / V-\mathrm{s}$, respectively. A current of 40 A is passed through a specimen of this alloy that is 35 mm thick. What magnetic field would need to be imposed to yield a Hall voltage of $-3.5 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~V}$ ?
Give your final answer in units of tesla.

## 过程
The Hall voltage relates to the Hall coefficient by

$$V_H = R_H \frac{I B}{d}$$

For an electron conductor, $R_H = -\dfrac{1}{ne}$. Using $\sigma = ne\mu_e$, we get $ne = \dfrac{\sigma}{\mu_e}$, so

$$R_H = -\frac{\mu_e}{\sigma}$$

Substituting into the Hall-voltage expression and solving for $B$:

$$V_H = -\frac{\mu_e}{\sigma}\frac{I B}{d} \;\Rightarrow\; B = -\frac{V_H\, d\, \sigma}{\mu_e\, I}$$

With $V_H = -3.5\times10^{-7}\,\text{V}$, $d = 35\,\text{mm} = 0.035\,\text{m}$, $\sigma = 1.2\times10^{7}\,(\Omega\cdot\text{m})^{-1}$, $\mu_e = 0.0050\,\text{m}^2/\text{V·s}$, $I = 40\,\text{A}$:

$$B = -\frac{(-3.5\times10^{-7})(0.035)(1.2\times10^{7})}{(0.0050)(40)} = 0.735\,\text{T}$$

## 最终答案
0.735
