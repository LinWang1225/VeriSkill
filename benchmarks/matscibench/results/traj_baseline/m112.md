## 题目
A three-point bending test was performed on an aluminum oxide specimen having a circular cross section of radius 5.0 mm ( 0.20 in.); the specimen fractured at a load of $3000 \mathrm{~N}\left(675 \mathrm{lb}\right.$ ) when the distance between the support points was 40 mm (1.6 in.). Another test is to be performed on a specimen of this same material, but one that has a square cross section of 15 mm ( 0.6 in.) length on each edge. At what load would you expect this specimen to fracture if the support point separation is maintained at 40 mm (1.6 in.)?
Give your final answer in units of N.

## 过程
For three-point bending, the maximum flexural stress is $\sigma = Mc/I$ with $M=FL/4$.

- Circular cross section: $I=\pi R^4/4$, $c=R$, so $\sigma = \dfrac{FL}{\pi R^3}$.
- Square cross section (edge $a$): $I=a^4/12$, $c=a/2$, so $\sigma = \dfrac{3FL}{2a^3}$.

The support span $L=40\,\text{mm}$ is unchanged and the material (fracture stress) is the same, so equate the two expressions ($L$ cancels):

$$\frac{F_1}{\pi R^3}=\frac{3F_2}{2a^3}$$

$$F_2 = F_1\frac{2a^3}{3\pi R^3}=3000\cdot\frac{2(15)^3}{3\pi(5)^3}=3000\cdot\frac{18}{\pi}=\frac{54000}{\pi}$$

Numerically: $F_2 \approx 17188.7\,\text{N}$.

## 最终答案
17188.7
