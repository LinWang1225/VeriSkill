## 题目
Calculate the concentration of conduction electrons in (a) Al and (b) Si. Assume that the densities of Al and Si are $2.7 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$ and $2.33 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$, respectively. The atomic masses for Al and Si are $\sim 27$ and $28$, respectively. Assuming that the electron mobility values are similar, what will be the expected concentration of electrons in terms of the values of conductivity of Al and Si? 

Give your answer as a tuple (concentration in Al, concentration in Si).
Give your final answer in units of (electrons/cm^3, electrons/cm^3).
Note: *   Valence of Aluminum (Al) is 3.
*   Valence of Silicon (Si) is 4.

## 过程
The concentration of conduction electrons (number of free electrons per unit volume) is

$$n = \frac{\rho\, N_A\, v}{A}$$

where $\rho$ is density, $N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\ \text{atoms/mol}$, $v$ is the valence, and $A$ is the atomic mass.

**(a) Al** ($\rho=2.7\ \text{g/cm}^3$, $A=27$, $v=3$):

$$n_{\text{Al}}=\frac{2.7\times6.022\times10^{23}\times3}{27}
=1.8066\times10^{23}\ \text{electrons/cm}^3$$

**(b) Si** ($\rho=2.33\ \text{g/cm}^3$, $A=28$, $v=4$):

$$n_{\text{Si}}=\frac{2.33\times6.022\times10^{23}\times4}{28}
=2.0045\times10^{23}\ \text{electrons/cm}^3$$

**Conductivity relation:** Since $\sigma = n|e|\mu$, and the mobilities are assumed similar, $n\propto\sigma$. Thus the expected electron concentration ratio equals the conductivity ratio: $n_{\text{Si}}/n_{\text{Al}}=\sigma_{\text{Si}}/\sigma_{\text{Al}}$. In reality Al's conductivity is far higher than Si's, so this simple valence-based estimate greatly overestimates Si's *free* conduction electrons (Si's intrinsic carrier concentration is only $\sim10^{10}\ \text{cm}^{-3}$); the calculation gives the total valence-electron count, most of which are bound in covalent bonds in the semiconductor.

## 最终答案
(1.81e+23, 2.00e+23)
