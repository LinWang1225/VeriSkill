## 题目
Calculate the energy for vacancy formation in nickel (Ni), given that the equilibrium number of vacancies at $850^{\circ} \mathrm{C}(1123 \mathrm{~K})$ is $4.7 \times 10^{22} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$. The atomic weight and density (at $850^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ) for Ni are, respectively, $58.69 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ and $8.80 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$.
Give your final answer in units of eV/atom.

## 过程
The equilibrium vacancy concentration follows

$$N_v = N\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_v}{kT}\right)$$

so

$$Q_v = kT\ln\!\left(\frac{N}{N_v}\right)$$

**1. Atomic site density** $N = \dfrac{\rho N_A}{A}$:

$\rho = 8.80\;\text{g/cm}^3 = 8.80\times10^6\;\text{g/m}^3$, $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}\;\text{mol}^{-1}$, $A = 58.69\;\text{g/mol}$

$$N = \frac{(8.80\times10^6)(6.022\times10^{23})}{58.69} \approx 9.03\times10^{28}\;\text{m}^{-3}$$

**2. Ratio:** $N_v/N = \dfrac{4.7\times10^{22}}{9.03\times10^{28}} \approx 5.21\times10^{-7}$

**3. Formation energy** with $k = 8.617\times10^{-5}\;\text{eV/K}$, $T = 1123\;\text{K}$:

$$Q_v = (8.617\times10^{-5})(1123)\ln\!\left(\frac{9.03\times10^{28}}{4.7\times10^{22}}\right) \approx 1.40\;\text{eV/atom}$$

## 最终答案
1.40
