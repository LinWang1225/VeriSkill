## 题目
A 4-in.-diameter sphere of liquid copper is allowed to solidify, producing a spherical shrinkage cavity in the center of the casting. Compare the volume and diameter of the shrinkage cavity in the copper casting to that obtained when a 4 in. sphere of liquid iron is allowed to solidify. Give your answer as a tuple: (diameter_copper_in, diameter_iron_in).
Give your final answer in units of (in, in).

## 过程
A shrinkage cavity forms because the metal contracts on solidifying. With no superheat specified, the cavity volume equals the **solidification (volumetric) shrinkage** fraction times the original sphere volume. The post-solidification solid contraction does not create a cavity (it shrinks uniformly).

Original sphere: D = 4 in, V₀ = (4/3)π(2)³ = 33.51 in³.

Volumetric solidification shrinkage (standard tabulated values):
- Copper: f_Cu = 4.5% = 0.045
- Iron:   f_Fe = 3.0% = 0.030

Cavity volume and (spherical) cavity diameter:

V_cav = f·V₀,  and since the cavity is spherical, d_cav = D·f^(1/3).

**Copper:**
- V_cav = 0.045 × 33.51 = 1.51 in³
- d_cav = 4 × (0.045)^(1/3) = 1.42 in

**Iron:**
- V_cav = 0.030 × 33.51 = 1.01 in³
- d_cav = 4 × (0.030)^(1/3) = 1.24 in

The copper cavity is larger because copper's solidification shrinkage (4.5%) exceeds iron's (3.0%); the diameter ratio is (0.045/0.030)^(1/3) ≈ 1.15.

## 最终答案
(1.42, 1.24)
