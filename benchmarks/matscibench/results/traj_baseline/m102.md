## 题目
A gas mixture is found to contain two diatomic $A$ and $B$ species ( $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ ), the partial pressures of both of which are 0.1013 MPa ( 1 atm ). This mixture is to be enriched in the partial pressure of the $A$ species by passing both gases through a thin sheet of some metal at an elevated temperature. The resulting enriched mixture is to have a partial pressure of 0.051 MPa ( 0.5 atm ) for gas $A$ and 0.0203 MPa ( 0.2 atm ) for gas $B$. The concentrations of $A$ and $B\left(C_{A}\right.$ and $C_{B}$, in $\left.mol / m^{3}\right)$ are functions of gas partial pressures $\left(p_{A_{2}}\right.$ and $p_{B_{2}}$, in MPa) and absolute temperature according to the following expressions:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& C_{\mathrm{A}}=1.5 \times 10^{3} \sqrt{p_{\mathrm{A}_{2}}} \exp \left(-\frac{20,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right) \\
& C_{\mathrm{B}}=2.0 \times 10^{3} \sqrt{p_{\mathrm{B}_{2}}} \exp \left(-\frac{27,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

Furthermore, the diffusion coefficients for the diffusion of these gases in the metal are functions of the absolute temperature as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& D_{\mathrm{A}}\left(\mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}\right)=5.0 \times 10^{-7} \exp \left(-\frac{13,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right) \\
& D_{\mathrm{B}}\left(\mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{s}\right)=3.0 \times 10^{-6} \exp \left(-\frac{21,000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}}{R T}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

In addition that the permeate must be produced at a flux of 1.0×10⁻⁵ mol m⁻² s⁻¹ (total of A₂ + B₂) under a counter‐current sweep on the permeate side, determine the temperature at which the ratio of partial pressures 0.051 MPa∶0.0203 MPa can be maintained in steady state.
Give your final answer in units of K.

## 过程
This is a membrane permeation problem. The metal sheet separates a high-pressure feed (both $A_2$, $B_2$ at 0.1013 MPa) from a permeate side swept counter-currently, where the partial pressures are $p_{A_2}=0.051$ MPa and $p_{B_2}=0.0203$ MPa.

**Flux model.** For each dissolved species, Fick's law through a sheet of thickness $L$:
$$J_i = \frac{D_i\big(C_{i,\text{feed}}-C_{i,\text{perm}}\big)}{L}$$

Using the given Sieverts-law concentrations and Arrhenius diffusivities, the product $D_i\Delta C_i$ separates into a pressure prefactor and a single exponential:

$$D_A\Delta C_A = (5.0\times10^{-7})(1.5\times10^{3})\big(\sqrt{0.1013}-\sqrt{0.051}\big)\exp\!\Big(\!-\tfrac{33000}{RT}\Big)$$
$$D_B\Delta C_B = (3.0\times10^{-6})(2.0\times10^{3})\big(\sqrt{0.1013}-\sqrt{0.0203}\big)\exp\!\Big(\!-\tfrac{48000}{RT}\Big)$$

**Steady-state ratio condition.** With a counter-current sweep, each permeated species is removed proportionally to its flux, so at steady state
$$\frac{J_A}{J_B}=\frac{p_{A_2,\text{perm}}}{p_{B_2,\text{perm}}}=\frac{0.051}{0.0203}=2.5123$$

The thickness $L$ cancels in this ratio, leaving an equation for $T$ alone:
$$\frac{J_A}{J_B}=\frac{\text{coeff}_A}{\text{coeff}_B}\exp\!\Big(\frac{48000-33000}{RT}\Big)=2.5123$$

Solving,
$$\frac{15000}{RT}=\ln\!\left(2.5123\,\frac{\text{coeff}_B}{\text{coeff}_A}\right)=\ln(38.22)=3.643$$

$$T=\frac{15000}{8.314\times 3.643}\approx 495.2\ \text{K}$$

**Check.** At $T=495.2$ K, $J_A/J_B=2.5123$ ✓. The total-flux condition $J_A+J_B=1.0\times10^{-5}$ then fixes $L\approx 3.20$ mm (a reasonable sheet thickness), with $J_A=7.15\times10^{-6}$ and $J_B=2.85\times10^{-6}$ mol m⁻² s⁻¹.

## 最终答案
495.2
