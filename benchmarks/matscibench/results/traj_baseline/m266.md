## 题目
It is known that the kinetics of recrystallization for some alloy obeys the Avrami equation and that the value of n is 3.1. If the fraction recrystallized is 0.30 after 20 min, determine the rate of recrystallization.
Give your final answer in units of (min)^-1.

## 过程
The Avrami equation is:

$$y = 1 - \exp(-k\,t^n)$$

**Step 1 — Solve for k using the given data** (n = 3.1, y = 0.30, t = 20 min):

$$0.30 = 1 - \exp(-k\cdot 20^{3.1})$$
$$\exp(-k\cdot 20^{3.1}) = 0.70$$
$$k = \frac{-\ln(0.70)}{20^{3.1}} = 3.304\times10^{-5}\ \text{min}^{-3.1}$$

**Step 2 — Find the time for 50% recrystallization (t₀.₅)**, since the rate of recrystallization is defined as the reciprocal of the time to reach 50% completion:

$$0.50 = 1 - \exp(-k\,t_{0.5}^{3.1})$$
$$t_{0.5} = \left(\frac{-\ln(0.50)}{k}\right)^{1/3.1} = 24.78\ \text{min}$$

**Step 3 — Rate of recrystallization:**

$$\text{rate} = \frac{1}{t_{0.5}} = \frac{1}{24.78} = 0.0404\ \text{min}^{-1}$$

## 最终答案
0.0404
