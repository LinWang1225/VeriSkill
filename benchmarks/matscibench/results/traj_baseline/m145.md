## 题目
Nickel experiences corrosion in an acid solution according to the reaction

$$
\mathrm{Ni}+2 \mathrm{H}^{+} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ni}^{2+}+\mathrm{H}{2}
$$

The rates of both oxidation and reduction half-reactions are controlled by activation polarization.
(a) Compute the rate of oxidation of Ni (in $\mathrm{mol} / \mathrm{cm}^{2}-\mathrm{s}$ ), given the following activation polarization data:

For Nickel

$$
\begin{aligned}
V{\left(\mathrm{Ni} / \mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\right)}=-0.25 \mathrm{~V} & V{\left(\mathrm{H}^{+} / \mathrm{H}{2}\right)}=0 \mathrm{~V} \\
i{0}=10^{-8} \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{cm}^{2} & i{0}=6 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{cm}^{2} \\
\beta=+0.12 & \beta=-0.10
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute the rate of oxidation and value of the corrosion potential.

Give your answer as a tuple (the rate of oxidation, the corrosion potential).
Give your final answer in units of (mol/cm^2s, V).

## 过程
At the corrosion potential the net current is zero, so the oxidation (anodic) current density equals the reduction (cathodic) current density, both equal to the corrosion current $i_c$.

Using activation (Tafel) polarization, $\eta = \beta\log_{10}(i/i_0)$ and $V = V_0 + \eta$:

- Anodic (Ni): $V_c = V_{\mathrm{Ni/Ni^{2+}}} + \beta_{\mathrm{Ni}}\log_{10}(i_c/i_{0,\mathrm{Ni}})$
- Cathodic (H): $V_c = V_{\mathrm{H^+/H_2}} + \beta_{\mathrm{H}}\log_{10}(i_c/i_{0,\mathrm{H}})$

Setting the two expressions for $V_c$ equal:

$$-0.25 + 0.12\log_{10}(i_c/10^{-8}) = 0 + (-0.10)\log_{10}(i_c/6\times10^{-7})$$

Solving numerically gives $i_c = 8.80\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{A/cm^2}$ and $V_c = -0.0166\ \mathrm{V}$ (verified by substituting back into both half-cell equations).

The oxidation rate is $r = i_c/(nF)$ with $n=2$ and $F=96485\ \mathrm{C/mol}$:

$$r = \frac{8.80\times10^{-7}}{2(96485)} = 4.56\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{mol/cm^2\cdot s}$$

## 最终答案
(4.56e-12, -0.0166)
