## 题目
Calculate the number of Schottky defects per cubic meter in potassium chloride at 500℃. The energy required to form each Schottky defect is 2.6 eV, whereas the density for KCl (at 500℃) is 1.955 g/cm³. 
Give your final answer in units of defects/m³.

## 过程
For a Schottky defect in an MX compound (KCl), the equilibrium number of defects per unit volume is

$$N_s = N \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_s}{2kT}\right)$$

where $N$ is the number of cation (or anion) lattice sites per m³, $Q_s = 2.6\ \text{eV}$ is the formation energy per defect pair, $k = 8.617\times10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}$, and $T = 500+273 = 773\ \text{K}$.

**Lattice site density:** KCl molar mass $M = 39.10 + 35.45 = 74.55\ \text{g/mol}$; density $\rho = 1.955\ \text{g/cm}^3$. Each formula unit gives one K site and one Cl site, so

$$N = \frac{\rho N_A}{M}\times 10^6 = \frac{1.955\times 6.022\times10^{23}}{74.55}\times 10^6 = 1.579\times10^{28}\ \text{sites/m}^3$$

**Exponential factor:**

$$\exp\!\left(-\frac{2.6}{2(8.617\times10^{-5})(773)}\right) = \exp(-19.534) = 3.341\times10^{-9}$$

**Result:**

$$N_s = (1.579\times10^{28})(3.341\times10^{-9}) = 5.28\times10^{19}\ \text{defects/m}^3$$

## 最终答案
5.28e19
