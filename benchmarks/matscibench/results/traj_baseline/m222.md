## 题目
What time is required to nitride a $0.002 \% \mathrm{~N}$ steel to obtain $0.12 \% \mathrm{~N}$ at a distance of 0.002 in . beneath the surface at $625^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ? The nitrogen content at the surface is $0.15 \%$.
Give your final answer in units of s.

## 过程
For diffusion into a semi-infinite solid with constant surface concentration, Fick's second law gives:

$$\frac{C(x,t)-C_0}{C_s-C_0}=1-\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$

**Given:** $C_0=0.002\%$, $C_s=0.15\%$, $C(x,t)=0.12\%$, $x=0.002\text{ in}=0.00508\text{ cm}$, $T=625^{\circ}\mathrm{C}=898\text{ K}$.

**Diffusion data for N in α-Fe:** $D_0=0.0047\ \mathrm{cm^2/s}$, $Q=18{,}300\ \mathrm{cal/mol}$, $R=1.987\ \mathrm{cal/(mol\cdot K)}$.

$$D=D_0\exp\!\left(-\frac{Q}{RT}\right)=0.0047\exp\!\left(-\frac{18300}{1.987\times 898}\right)=1.652\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{cm^2/s}$$

**Error-function argument:**

$$\frac{C_x-C_0}{C_s-C_0}=\frac{0.12-0.002}{0.15-0.002}=0.7973$$

$$\operatorname{erf}(z)=1-0.7973=0.2027\ \Rightarrow\ z=\operatorname{erf}^{-1}(0.2027)=0.1816$$

**Solve for time** using $z=x/(2\sqrt{Dt})$:

$$t=\frac{x^2}{4z^2D}=\frac{(0.00508)^2}{4(0.1816)^2(1.652\times10^{-7})}\approx 1184\ \mathrm{s}$$

## 最终答案
1184
