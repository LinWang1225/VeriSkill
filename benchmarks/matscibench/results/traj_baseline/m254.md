## 题目
An $\mathrm{Fe}-49 \% \mathrm{Ni}$ alloy has a maximum permeability of 64,000 when a magnetic field of 0.125 oersted is applied. What inductance is obtained and what current is needed to obtain this inductance in a 200-turn, 3-cm-long coil? Give your answer as a tuple: (inductance_G, current_mA).
Give your final answer in units of (G, mA).

## 过程
The maximum permeability relates flux density to field: $\mu = B/H$, so

$$B = \mu_{\max} H = 64{,}000 \times 0.125 = 8{,}000\ \text{G}.$$

This flux density is the "inductance obtained" (in gauss).

For the current, use the CGS solenoid field formula (practical-current form)

$$H\ (\text{Oe}) = 0.4\pi\,\frac{N}{l}\,I\ (\text{A}),$$

with $N=200$, $l=3\ \text{cm}$, $H=0.125\ \text{Oe}$. Solving for $I$:

$$I = \frac{H\,l}{0.4\pi\,N} = \frac{0.125 \times 3}{0.4\pi \times 200} = 1.492\times10^{-3}\ \text{A} = 1.49\ \text{mA}.$$

## 最终答案
(8000.0, 1.49)
