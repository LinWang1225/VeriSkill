## 题目
Confirm that there are 1.72 Bohr magnetons associated with each cobalt atom, given that the saturation magnetization is $1.45 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{m}$, that cobalt has an HCP crystal structure with an atomic radius of 0.1253 nm and a c/a ratio of 1.623 .
Give your final answer in units of Bohr magnetons/atom.

## 过程
For HCP cobalt, the unit cell contains 6 atoms. With atomic radius $R=0.1253\,\text{nm}$:

- Lattice parameter: $a = 2R = 0.2506\,\text{nm}$
- $c = (c/a)\cdot a = 1.623 \times 0.2506 = 0.4067\,\text{nm}$
- HCP unit cell volume: $V = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^{2}c = 6.636\times10^{-29}\,\text{m}^3$

Number of atoms per unit volume:

$$n = \frac{6}{V} = 9.041\times10^{28}\,\text{atoms/m}^3$$

Magnetic moment per atom:

$$\mu_{\text{atom}} = \frac{M_s}{n} = \frac{1.45\times10^{6}}{9.041\times10^{28}} = 1.604\times10^{-23}\,\text{A·m}^2$$

Converting to Bohr magnetons ($\mu_B = 9.27\times10^{-24}\,\text{A·m}^2$):

$$\beta = \frac{\mu_{\text{atom}}}{\mu_B} = \frac{1.604\times10^{-23}}{9.27\times10^{-24}} = 1.73$$

This confirms the stated value of 1.72 Bohr magnetons/atom (difference due to rounding).

## 最终答案
1.73
