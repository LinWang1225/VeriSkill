## 题目
At $800^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, iron oxidizes at a rate of $0.014 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^2$ per hour; at $1000^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, iron oxidizes at a rate of $0.0656 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^2$ per hour. Assuming a parabolic oxidation rate, determine the maximum temperature at which iron can be held if the oxidation rate is to be less than $0.005 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^2$ per hour.
Give your final answer in units of °C.

## 过程
The oxidation rate constant follows an Arrhenius relation $k = A\exp\!\left(-\dfrac{Q}{RT}\right)$, consistent with a thermally activated parabolic oxidation process. With two data points we eliminate $A$:

$$\ln\frac{k_2}{k_1} = -\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right)$$

Using $T_1=1073\,\text{K},\; k_1=0.014$ and $T_2=1273\,\text{K},\; k_2=0.0656$:

$$\frac{Q}{R}=\frac{\ln(0.0656/0.014)}{-(1/1273-1/1073)}=10548.5\,\text{K}$$

Now require $k=0.005\,\text{g/cm}^2\!\cdot\!\text{h}$ and solve for $T$:

$$\ln\frac{0.005}{0.014}=-\frac{Q}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T}-\frac{1}{1073}\right)$$

$$\frac{1}{T}=\frac{1}{1073}-\frac{\ln(0.005/0.014)}{10548.5}=1.0296\times10^{-3}\,\text{K}^{-1}$$

$$T = 971.3\,\text{K}\;\Rightarrow\; T_{^\circ\text{C}}=971.3-273=698.3^{\circ}\text{C}$$

## 最终答案
698.3
