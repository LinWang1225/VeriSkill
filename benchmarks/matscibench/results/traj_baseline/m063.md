## 题目
The force of attraction between a divalent cation and a divalent anion is $1.67 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~N}$. If the ionic radius of the cation is 0.080 nm , what is the anion radius?
Give your final answer in units of nm.

## 过程
The attractive force between two ions is given by Coulomb's law:

$$F = \frac{z_1 z_2 e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}$$

where $r = r_{\text{cation}} + r_{\text{anion}}$ is the interionic separation, and for a divalent cation/anion $z_1 = z_2 = 2$.

Solving for $r$:

$$r = \sqrt{\frac{z_1 z_2 e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 F}}$$

Using $e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}\,\text{F/m}$, $F = 1.67\times10^{-8}\,\text{N}$:

$$r = \sqrt{\frac{(2)(2)(1.602\times10^{-19})^2}{4\pi(8.854\times10^{-12})(1.67\times10^{-8})}} \approx 2.35\times10^{-10}\,\text{m} = 0.235\,\text{nm}$$

Then:

$$r_{\text{anion}} = r - r_{\text{cation}} = 0.235 - 0.080 = 0.155\,\text{nm}$$

## 最终答案
0.155
