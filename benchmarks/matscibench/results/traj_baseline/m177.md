## 题目
A tensile bar is machined so that the diameter at one location is 1% smaller than the rest of the bar. The bar is tested at high temperature, so strain hardening is negligible, but the strain rate exponent is 0.25. When the strain in the reduced region is 0.20, what is the strain in the larger region?

## 过程
Let the reduced region (subscript $r$) have initial diameter $0.99d$ and the larger region (subscript $L$) diameter $d$. With volume conservation, current area $A=A_0 e^{-\varepsilon}$. At high temperature with negligible strain hardening, the flow stress is purely rate-sensitive:

$$\sigma = K\dot{\varepsilon}^{\,m},\qquad m=0.25$$

Force equilibrium between the two regions at every instant:

$$\sigma_r A_r = \sigma_L A_L \;\Rightarrow\; \dot\varepsilon_r^{\,m} A_{r0}e^{-\varepsilon_r}=\dot\varepsilon_L^{\,m} A_{L0}e^{-\varepsilon_L}$$

With $f=A_{r0}/A_{L0}=0.99^2=0.9801$ and $u=\varepsilon_r-\varepsilon_L$:

$$\frac{\dot\varepsilon_r}{\dot\varepsilon_L}=\left(\frac{1}{f}e^{u}\right)^{1/m}\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{d\varepsilon_L}{d\varepsilon_r}=f^{1/m}e^{-u/m}$$

Integrating from $(0,0)$ to $\varepsilon_r=0.20$ (verified two ways: RK4 and the equivalent integral $\int_0^{u_f}\frac{du}{a e^{u/m}-1}=0.20-u_f$ with $a=(1/f)^{1/m}$):

$$\varepsilon_L \approx 0.17738$$

The strain localizes in the reduced section ($\varepsilon_r=0.20 > \varepsilon_L$), consistent with $m=0.25$ giving only moderate rate-sensitive resistance to localization.

## 最终答案
0.1774
