## 题目
The chemical formula for copper ferrite may be written as $\left(\mathrm{CuFe}{2} \mathrm{O}{4}\right){8}$ because there are eight formula units per unit cell. If this material has a saturation magnetization of $1.35 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~A} / \mathrm{m}$ and a density of $5.40 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$. estimate the number of Bohr magnetons associated with each $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ ion.
Give your final answer in units of Bohr magnetons $/ \mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ ion.

## 过程
Saturation magnetization relates to the Bohr magneton number per formula unit $n_B$ by

$$M_s = \frac{n_B \mu_B N_A \rho}{M}$$

where the 8 formula units/unit cell cancel (moment per cell $=8n_B\mu_B$, cell volume $=8M/(\rho N_A)$). With 1 Cu²⁺ per formula unit, $n_B$ per formula unit = $n_B$ per Cu²⁺ ion.

Using $M(\mathrm{CuFe_2O_4}) = 63.55 + 2(55.85) + 4(16.00) = 239.25$ g/mol, $\rho = 5.40\times10^{6}$ g/m³, $\mu_B = 9.27\times10^{-24}$ A·m², $N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}$:

$$n_B = \frac{M_s M}{\mu_B N_A \rho} = \frac{(1.35\times10^{5})(239.25)}{(9.27\times10^{-24})(6.022\times10^{23})(5.40\times10^{6})} \approx 1.07$$

## 最终答案
1.07
