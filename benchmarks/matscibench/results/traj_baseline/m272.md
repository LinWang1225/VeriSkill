## 题目
Computation of Critical Nucleus Radius and Activation Free Energy\n\n(a) For the solidification of pure gold, calculate the critical radius \(r^{*}\) and the activation free energy \(\Delta G^{*}\) if nucleation is homogeneous. Values for the latent heat of fusion and surface free energy are \(-1.16 \times 10^{9} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}\) and \(0.132 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{2}\), respectively. Use the supercooling value in Table 10.1.\n(b) Now, calculate the number of atoms found in a nucleus of critical size. Assume a lattice parameter of 0.413 nm for solid gold at its melting temperature.\nGive your answer as a tuple (critical radius, activation free energy, number of atoms).
Give your final answer in units of (nm, J, ).

## 过程
For homogeneous nucleation, the volume free-energy change is

$$\Delta G_v = \frac{\Delta H_f \,\Delta T}{T_m}$$

with $\Delta H_f = -1.16\times10^{9}\,\text{J/m}^3$, $\gamma = 0.132\,\text{J/m}^2$, gold $T_m = 1064+273 = 1337\,\text{K}$, and from Table 10.1 the supercooling for gold is $\Delta T = 230\,\text{K}$.

$$\Delta G_v = \frac{(-1.16\times10^{9})(230)}{1337} = -1.996\times10^{8}\,\text{J/m}^3$$

**(a) Critical radius and activation free energy**

$$r^{*} = -\frac{2\gamma}{\Delta G_v} = -\frac{2(0.132)}{-1.996\times10^{8}} = 1.323\times10^{-9}\,\text{m} = 1.323\,\text{nm}$$

$$\Delta G^{*} = \frac{16\pi\gamma^{3}}{3\Delta G_v^{2}} = \frac{16\pi(0.132)^3}{3(-1.996\times10^{8})^2} = 9.68\times10^{-19}\,\text{J}$$

**(b) Number of atoms in the critical nucleus**

Gold is FCC (4 atoms/unit cell), $a = 0.413\,\text{nm}$:

$$V_{\text{nucleus}} = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi(r^{*})^3 = 9.70\times10^{-27}\,\text{m}^3$$

$$V_{\text{cell}} = a^3 = 7.04\times10^{-29}\,\text{m}^3$$

$$N = \frac{V_{\text{nucleus}}}{V_{\text{cell}}}\times 4 \approx 551$$

## 最终答案
(1.323, 9.68e-19, 551)
