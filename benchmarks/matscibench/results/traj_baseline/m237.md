## 题目
A steel containing $0.8 \% \mathrm{C}$ is quenched to produce all martensite. Estimate the volume change that occurs, assuming that the lattice parameter of the austenite is $3.6 \AA$.  What is the volume change percent?
Give your final answer in units of %.

## 过程
**Austenite (FCC)** — given $a_\gamma = 3.6\,\text{Å}$, 4 atoms/cell:

$$V_\gamma = a_\gamma^3 = 3.6^3 = 46.656\;\text{Å}^3 \quad (4\text{ atoms})$$

**Martensite (BCT)** — 2 atoms/cell. Use the standard Roberts (1953) lattice-parameter relations (wt% C):

$$a_m = 2.8610 - 0.0130\,(\%\text{C}) = 2.8610 - 0.0104 = 2.8506\;\text{Å}$$

$$c_m = 2.8610 + 0.1160\,(\%\text{C}) = 2.8610 + 0.0928 = 2.9538\;\text{Å}$$

Volume per BCT cell (2 atoms):

$$V_{m} = a_m^2\,c_m = (2.8506)^2(2.9538) = 24.006\;\text{Å}^3$$

**Compare on the same 4-atom basis** (2 BCT cells vs 1 FCC cell):

$$V_\gamma = 46.656\;\text{Å}^3,\qquad V_m = 2\times 24.006 = 48.005\;\text{Å}^3$$

$$\frac{\Delta V}{V} = \frac{48.005 - 46.656}{46.656} = 0.02891$$

$$\boxed{\%\Delta V \approx 2.89\%}$$

## 最终答案
2.89
